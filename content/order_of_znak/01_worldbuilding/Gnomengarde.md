# ⚙️ Gnomengarde

## 🛡️ Overview & Geography
* **Location:** A secluded settlement of rock gnomes built within a network of caves and grottos surrounding a misty waterfall in the **Sword Mountains**, south of [[Phandalin]].
* **Architecture & Atmosphere:** Filled with whirring gears, experimental acoustic contraptions, steam-powered walkways, and ingenious mechanical lifts. The gnomes harvest and brew magical wild mushrooms and fungal delicacies in the damp river gorge.
* **Monarchy:** Ruled jointly by dual kings **[[Gnerkli]]** and **[[Korboz]]**.

---

## 👥 Notable Artificers & Leadership
* **The Dual Kings:** [[Gnerkli]] and [[Korboz]], whose partnership governs the enclave.
* **Chief Inventors:** [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]], renowned for eccentric inventions including the *Sanity Ray*, clockwork armor, and alchemical siege explosives.
* **Artisan Enclave:** Home to numerous specialized gnomes including [[Anverth]], [[Facktoré]], [[Caramip]], [[Delebean]], [[Dimble]], [[Jabby]], [[Joybell]], [[Nyx]], [[Pallabar]], [[Panana]], [[Pinchwit Wigglehoof]], [[Pog]], [[Quippy]], [[Tervaround]], [[Ulla]], [[Uppendown]], and [[Zook (gnome)]].

## ⚔️ Campaign Milestones & Party History
* **Full Enclave Tour, Apprehension & The Facktoré Crossbow Incident (Level 3):** Arriving at Gnomengarde at **Level 3**, [[The Order of Znak]] explored the entire cavern complex and **met every single resident gnome** (all 21 named inhabitants). The party remained deeply **apprehensive** throughout their visit—especially after [[Facktoré]] opened fire with her experimental autoloading heavy crossbow contraption and shot [[Rhynok]]. Cooler heads prevailed before she could do further harm, and the party was escorted to chief inventors [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]]—though [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] manhandled one or two gnome guards along the way.
* **Slaying the Mimic in the Barrel Room (Act 1 — *Dragon of Icespire Peak*):** Investigating the mysterious disappearances that had driven [[Korboz|King Korboz]] into paranoid madness (locking himself and his husband [[Gnerkli|King Gnerkli]] inside the royal bedchamber), the party tracked the shapechanger to the **wine barrel storage room** and **slew the Mimic** as per the module, freeing Gnerkli and restoring calm to the throne.
* **Kings' Bedroom & Treasury Rewards:** In gratitude for slaying the mimic and saving the kings, the party was rewarded with the magical treasures from the kings' bedchamber and the Gnomengarde treasury: the ***Clockwork Amulet***, ***Pole of Collapsing***, ***Hat of Wizardry***, and ***Wand of Pyrotechnics***.
* **Siege Support (Act 4 — *Divine Contention*):** Later in the campaign, the inventors of Gnomengarde crafted experimental weaponry—including alchemical *Gnomengarde grenades* transported via [[Dumathoin's Gulch]]—to aid the Order during the climactic defense of [[Leilon]] against the forces of [[Ularan Mortus]] and [[Fheralai Stormsworn]].
