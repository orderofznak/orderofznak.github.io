# 🗺️ Leilon (The Town of the High Road)

## 📜 In-Universe Lore & History
* **Strategic Crossroads:** An ancient settlement situated along the High Road between Neverwinter and Waterdeep, nestled near the eastern edge of the Mere of Dead Men and beneath the western ridges of the Sword Mountains.
* **The Reconstruction Effort:** Following decades of abandonment and devastation, the town was formally reclaimed and rebuilt under the authority of the **Lords' Alliance** (spearheaded by Neverwinter's Lord Protector Dagult Neverember) to secure trade transit and revive coastal commerce.
* **Civic Rebuilding Leadership:** Rebuilding was directed by gruff dwarf master builder **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]** ("The Growler"), commissioned by Neverember to direct work crews and dispense tenday wages. Official defense was garrisoned by Neverwinter soldiers under the slovenly, corrupt **[[Hazz Yorrum|Sergeant Hazz Yorrum]]**, who carried a Neverwinter shield but routinely shirked duty by deferring decisions to wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]**.

## 🏛️ Key Districts & Landmarks

### 1. The Fishery & Shorelines
The primary lifeblood and economic engine of the town, harvesting fish from the coastal shallows. Site of the tragic kraken cult raid where fisher parents were sacrificed and young [[Smithwell and Burnice]] were orphaned before their rescue by the Order.

### 2. Shrine of Lathander & The First Temple
Dedicated to the Morninglord, offering solace, healing, and spiritual guidance. Led by the amiable, universally beloved halfling priestess **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]**, who conducts services each day at dawn and secured the charter for Leilon's first permanent temple. Merrygold and her acolytes personally assist in feeding and caring for Grizzelda's laborers outside work shifts.

### 3. House of Thalivar
An ominous, ruined wizard's tower dominating the town center, containing lingering arcane wards and planar hazards. Stationed in study H3 is the short-tempered Rashemi wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]**, who suffers from exhaustion caused by nightly possession by the **ghost of Thalivar**, beseeching the Order to cleanse the tower's spectral manifestations.

### 4. Aubrey's Peculiarities, Puck's Apothecary & The Swords of Leilon
A quirky antiquities and magic curio shop run by the eccentric **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]**. Unknown to the populace, Aubrey was secretly an ancient **ghost** of the historic **Swords of Leilon**, visible only to the champions of the Order until his shop's destruction in *Divine Contention* revealed his painted portrait among the ancient heroes. Nearby, the nervous elven Tymoran acolyte **[[Puck Caribdas]]** peddled alchemical potions—most famously selling **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** the ***Potion of Growth*** that (stacked with [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s *Enlarge* spell) gave Krageer the Huge stature to wrestle the Fire Giant Queen in *"Keeping the Faith"*, inspiring Krageer's two punny endorsement quotes on Puck's bottle labels.

### 5. The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn
A lively town tavern and inn built around the hollowed carapace of an ancient umber hulk, run by friendly, gossip-loving innkeeper **[[Alion Malwyn]]**. Serving as the social nerve center of the settlement, it was here in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* (September 1, 2020) that Alion shared the defining rumor of the bronze dragon carrying a dolphin in its claws, steering the Order toward the **Bronze Shrine** and [[Lhammaruntosz]].

### 6. Aufox Manor & Annex (The Order of Znak Headquarters)
The fortified timber-and-stone estate and permanent headquarters of [[The Order of Znak]] constructed with Grizzelda's masonry crews, christened **Aufox Manor** in honor of mentor and friend **David**. Managed by trusted human majordomo **[[Leopold]]**, the estate houses the heroes, their adopted wards ([[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]], [[Yasmina]]), and visiting lizardfolk allies ([[Graxxoamo]], [[Shriss]]).
* **Triangular Architecture:** Built with a distinctive **triangular footprint**, a steep **wooden shake roof** (warm cedar/pine tones rather than civic slate), and a **single prominent stone chimney** rising on the southern pitch. Situated just north of Leilon's northeastern wagon entrance (taking the place of the building originally indicated by the Knight's Goblet Inn pointer, with the inn relocated deeper into town).
* **The Annex & Dojo:** Accompanied by the two-story timber **Aufox Annex** to the northeast (acolyte quarters) and the post-and-rail fenced gravel **courtyard dojo** behind the manor, where [[Tvyordiy Znak]] trained the acolytes and planar Aarakocra disciples (see [[Aufox Manor]]).
* **Wards:** Protected by Sector Delta of the [[Leilon Defense Grid & Abjuration Wards]] (*Forbiddance* and *Symbol* threshold glyphs).

### 7. The Temple of the Twin Signs (Temple of the Gods)
A radiant 120-foot square divine sanctuary manifested via the 7th-level *Temple of the Gods* spell by cleric **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, situated on the grassy knoll north of Aufox Manor along the northern timber palisade (partially obscured from the south by the elevation of the House of Thalivar).
![[temple_of_the_twin_signs.jpg]]
*The Temple of the Twin Signs — Seamless Unmortared White Marble Portico Engraved with the Cyrillic Glyphs Ъ and Ь in Gold Leaf*
* **Architecture & Divinity:** Crafted from seamless, unmortared white marble with classical fluted columns, golden Cyrillic glyphs (**Ъ** and **Ь**) above the architrave, twin burning bronze braziers, and stained-glass clerestory windows glowing with dawn light. Dedicated jointly to Gond, Lathander, and the doctrine of the Signs.
* **Sanctuary Wards:** Permanently consecrated under *Hallow* (daylight illumination, courage aura, impervious to non-magical destruction, fiends and undead barred).

### 8. The Leilon Cemetery & Cemetery Hill
The newly established, stone-walled municipal graveyard situated on the flat meadow immediately outside the northwest gate, directly south of **Cemetery Hill**.
* **Hallowed Rest:** Warded under a permanent *Hallow* field anchored by Myagkiy Znak to guarantee that any humanoid interred within can never be raised as undead or desecrated by swamp ghouls.

## 🗺️ Campaign Significance & Major Sagas
* **The Kraken Cult Raid (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*):** Arriving in Leilon to confer with the town leadership on the High Road, the Order was thrust into immediate crisis when cult alarms sounded, scattering local children toward the marsh fringes. Using tracking, athletic leaps, and gentle coaxing, the heroes secured the children—including ten-year-old village friends [[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]], whose parents were working at the coastal fishery. Just as the children were safe behind barricades, Dark Tide Knights riding living water steeds and pulling an archer wagon breached the High Road entrance. Repelling the cavalry, the battle escalated to the quayside where tiefling Kraken Priest **[[Nixoxious]]** sacrificed captive fishers to summon water elementals and water weirds. Though the Order crushed the cult, both sets of Smithwell and Burnice's parents were among the casualties, prompting the Order to adopt the newly orphaned children into the Znak family upon being awarded **Aufox Manor**.
* **The High Road Alliances:** Secured a mutual protection and trade pact with the **Wayside Inn**, trading town goods for livestock and produce from former bandit captain **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]** and spicy cook **[[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]]**. Simultaneously established a southern swamp truce with Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk chieftain **[[Grixxugroaz]]**.
* **Dragon Cult Incursions (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** During Act 3, Leilon served as the frontline defense against dragon cultists and necrotic monstrosities threatening the High Road, beginning with breaking the sahuagin siege of satellite outpost **[[Leilon Point]]** and tracking threats from the **Umber Hulk's Shell Inn**.
* **The Primordial Siege (*Divine Contention*):** In Act 4, Leilon faced a multi-front coordinated invasion from Myrkulite death legions and Talosian storm cultists, during which ancient green dragon **Claugiyliamatar** assaulted the town and destroyed Aubrey's Peculiarities, prompting the Order to secure anti-siege munitions from [[Dumathoin's Gulch]] and confront [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] at sea.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Official D&D 5e Setting Context:** Leilon serves as the central hub and home base for the entire *Beyond the Dragon of Icespire Peak* expansion trilogy (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, *Divine Contention*). The modules detail its district-by-district reconstruction and escalating attacks by competing religious factions.
* **Tabletop Campaign Hearth & Aufox Manor:** For Peter and Gary, Leilon was not merely a quest board—it became the true emotional heart and hearth of the campaign where the party lived, adopted and raised their wards, formed enduring friendships, and rallied the Sword Coast against destruction. Christening the headquarters **Aufox Manor** served as a heartfelt tribute to workplace coworker and mentor **David**.
* **🇫🇷 Table Pronunciation: "Lee-LON" (The Super-Nasal French Inflection):** While official Forgotten Realms sourcebooks typically suggest an Anglicized pronunciation ("LAY-lon" or "LEE-lon"), Peter (as DM) and Gary established an enduring, comedic running table tradition: pronouncing the town with an exaggerated French accent as **"Lee-LON"** (phonetically *[li.lɔ̃]*). The first syllable is a crisp, bright *"Lee"*, followed by an aggressively nasal, drawn-out *"-lon"* (reminiscent of the French nasal vowel in *bon*, *pardon*, or *chanson*). Whenever the Order arrived from the wilderness, coordinated defense with Sergeant Hazz Yorrum, or checked up on Grizzelda's masonry progress at Aufox Manor, this signature pronunciation immediately signaled the comfort and camaraderie of returning to their adopted hometown.
