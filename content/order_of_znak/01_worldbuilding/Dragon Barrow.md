# 🛡️ Dragon Barrow

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A massive, dragon-shaped earthen burial mound located in the grassy hills northwest of the **Neverwinter Wood** and northeast of Leilon.
* **Historical Significance:** The final resting place of **Lady Tanamere Alagondar**, a renowned Neverwintian royal warrior who gave her life to defeat a rampaging green dragon centuries ago. The mound covers the intertwined remains of Lady Tanamere and her draconic steed.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Dragonslayer Blade:** Seeking a weapon capable of penetrating the icy scales of white dragon [[Cryovain]], [[The Order of Znak]] excavated the ancient barrow during Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*).
* **Barrow Guardians:** Navigating spectral guardians, skeletal defenses, and hidden sarcophagi, the champions recovered Lady Tanamere's magical ***Dragonslayer*** longsword, which proved instrumental in their assault on Icespire Hold.
