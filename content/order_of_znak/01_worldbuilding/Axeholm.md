# 🏰 Axeholm

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A subterranean dwarven fortress carved into the base of a mountain fifteen miles south of [[Phandalin]].
* **The Banshee's Curse:** Abandoned and sealed centuries ago after moon elf ambassador **[[Vyldara]]** attempted to foment civil unrest, was slain during an escape attempt, and rose as a **banshee**—turning the dwarves who succumbed to her terrifying wails into flesh-eating **ghouls**.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Pre-Expedition Prep — Silvered Arsenal:** Before setting out for Axeholm, [[The Order of Znak]] stopped in [[Phandalin]] where **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] silvered his Greataxe** (vowing never to be powerless against his enemies again after the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] wererats), **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** silvered his ***Qiang* spear**, and **[[Rhynok]]** silvered his **Shortsword**.
* **Chimney Insertion with *Guidance*:** Finding the main dwarven gates sealed, the party scaled the mountain and entered Axeholm by climbing down the **chimney**, with every single champion making the perilous descent unscathed thanks to their athletic prowess and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]'s prayerful *Guidance***!
* **Surprise Ambush on the Giant Spiders & Purging the Lower Halls:** Descending from the chimney, the party caught the **Giant Spiders** in a lethal surprise round—killing several spiders immediately as [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] raged, [[Myagkiy Znak]] summoned his floating *Spiritual Weapon*, and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] unleashed a *Flurry of Blows*, while [[Rhynok]] loosed six arrows to pick off the surviving spiders as they shot webs and climbed the 15-foot ceiling. From there, the champions battled waves of **Ghouls** and **Stirges** as they pushed through the **West Hall** and the **Smithy**, before crossing to the **East Hall** and the **Ghouls' Den** to eradicate the remaining ghouls.
* **Slaying [[Vyldara]] the Banshee:** Ascending the stairs to the upper level, the party wandered into the **Haunted Hall** and confronted the moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**. Bolstered by their ironclad fortitude, **none of the party succumbed to her deadly *Wail***, and with divine magic and silvered/magical weapons on their side, they swiftly destroyed her.
* **Castellan's Bedchamber, Secret Fireplace Vault & Castellan Ghoul:** Moving into the **Castellan's Bedchamber**, the champions dispatched another flock of **Stirges**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]'s sharp eyes** spotted the hidden iron vault concealed inside the fireplace:
  * **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** claimed and attuned the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power*** (infusing his frame with prodigious giant-kin strength).
  * **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** claimed the ***Dread Helm*** (giving his eyes a menacing crimson glow beneath the shadowed steel faceplate).
  * Finally, the party fought and **defeated the ghoul of the dwarf Castellan** before leaving Axeholm behind, completely cleared—spotting **[[Cryovain]]** prowling Axeholm on their return to [[Phandalin]] and strafing the **Starmetal Hills** as they rode back out of town!
