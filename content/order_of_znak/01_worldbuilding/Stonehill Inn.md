# 🍺 Stonehill Inn

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A comfortable, two-story fieldstone and timber roadhouse located prominently in the center of town in **[[Phandalin]]**.
* **Proprietor:** Owned and run by friendly human innkeeper **[[Toblen Stonehill]]**, whose warmth and hospitality made the taproom the central community gathering place for locals, miners, and visiting adventurers.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Level 1 Arrival & Frontier Headquarters:** Upon arriving in [[Phandalin]] at Level 1 at the start of Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*), [[The Order of Znak]] met [[Toblen Stonehill]] and made the Stonehill Inn their primary gathering hearth, rumor clearinghouse, and resting sanctuary throughout their quests across the Triboar Trail, Neverwinter Wood, and the Sword Mountains—celebrating their final Level 7 triumph here after slaying [[Cryovain]] at [[Icespire Hold]].
