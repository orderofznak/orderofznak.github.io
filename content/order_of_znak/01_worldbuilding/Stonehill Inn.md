# 🍺 Stonehill Inn

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A comfortable, two-story fieldstone and timber roadhouse located prominently in the center of town in **Phandalin**.
* **Proprietor:** Owned and run by friendly shield dwarf/human innkeeper **[[Toblen Stonehill]]**, whose warmth and hospitality made the taproom the central community gathering place for locals, miners, and visiting adventurers.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Frontier Headquarters:** During Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*), the Stonehill Inn served as the primary gathering hearth, rumor clearinghouse, and resting sanctuary for [[The Order of Znak]] as they undertook quests across the Triboar Trail and Sword Mountains.
