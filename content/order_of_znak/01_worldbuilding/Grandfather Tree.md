# 🌳 Grandfather Tree

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **The Living Colossus:** A legendary, colossal oak tree standing at the heart of the **High Forest**, revered as an ancient sacred site of Eaerlanni dryads and the ancestral mound of the **Tree Ghost Uthgardt** barbarians.
* **Inhabitants & Guardians:**
  - **Chieftain:** Boorvald Orcbane.
  - **Huntress:** **[[Athela Orcbane]]**, wielding an ancient elven *Oathbow*.
  - **Spirit-Shaman:** **[[Elder Kaya]]**, the blind seer who communes with the roots and forest spirits.
  - **Fey Allies:** Guardian dryads who tend to the hollow root-caverns and mossy glades.
* **The Longfire of 1500 DR:** Visited by **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** during his expedition to the Star Mounts. Beneath its gargantuan root-caverns, Elder Kaya diagnosed his contracted wereboar curse and the necrotic frost of **[[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]]**, teaching him to harmonize with the beast within before ascending Mount Nimbus (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 2]]).
