# 🔮 The Ruinstone

## 🛡️ Overview & Arcane Profile
* **Artifact Type:** Mythic Netherese Planar Relic
* **Origin:** Ancient Netheril / Discovered on the Ethereal Plane
* **Current Status:** Recovered and secured by [[The Order of Znak]] in [[Leilon]]
* **Primary Arc:** *Divine Contention* (Act 4)

---

## 📜 Canonical Lore & History
**The Ruinstone** is an ancient, reality-warping artifact forged during the zenith of the Netherese Empire. Millennia later, the eccentric wizard **[[Thalivar]]** discovered the relic within the Ethereal Plane and transported it into the prime material plane atop the [[High Tower of Thalivar]] in Leilon.

Upon the stone's arrival, a catastrophic wild magic surge disabled the protective planar wards anchoring the tower's research subjects. Extradimensional horrors broke free, collapsed the tower, and slew Thalivar, binding his restless spirit to the ruins for centuries.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Significance in *The Order of Znak*

### The Beacon Incursion & Recovery (*Divine Contention*)
Following their expedition to [[Dumathoin's Gulch]], [[The Order of Znak]] rushed to Thalivar's Beacon to contain a catastrophic planar breach. The beacon's arcane array had flared, pulling horrors from the Far Realm into the tower:
* The Order fought a harrowing battle against deadly **Star Spawn Manglers**, with [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] braving their psychic assault—the single form of damage his Barbarian rage could not flinch off.
* Upon vanquishing the star spawns, the Order reached the apex of the beacon and recovered **The Ruinstone**.
* Traumatized by the horrors of the tower and the reality-altering menace of the stone, Neverwinter's appointed mage **[[Gallio Elibro]]** resigned his commission and returned permanently to Neverwinter, leaving the artifact in the safekeeping of The Order of Znak.

### The Catalyst of the Siege of Leilon
The presence of The Ruinstone in Leilon transformed the frontier settlement into the supreme battleground of the Sword Coast:
* **The Cult of Myrkul:** Led by the dread necromancer [[Ularan Mortus]], the death cult marched legions of undead upon Leilon to seize the stone to enact mass resurrection and destruction.
* **The Anchorites of Talos:** Led by High Priestess [[Fheralai Stormsworn]], storm cultists launched a devastating naval and land assault to claim the stone in honor of Talos.
* Both rival factions clashed in the cataclysmic three-way **Battle of Leilon**, while The Order of Znak defended the town and guarded the stone with their lives.
