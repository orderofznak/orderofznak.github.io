# ⛏️ Mountain's Toe Gold Mine

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A lucrative gold mine tunneled into the rugged foothills of the **Sword Mountains**, fifteen miles northeast of [[Phandalin]].
* **Wererat Occupation:** Acquired by a consortium of businessfolk in Neverwinter who hired **[[DonJon Raskin]]** as overseer, the mine had been seized by **[[Zeleen Varnaster]]** and her **Whiskered Gang** of wererat bandits after an orc attack drove them from their previous lair at the [[Shrine of Savras]].

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Unfooled by [[Zeleen Varnaster]]:** Escorting **[[DonJon Raskin]]** to his new post, [[The Order of Znak]] entered the mine tunnels and confronted **[[Zeleen Varnaster]]**—who attempted to pass herself off as the legitimate mine overseer. The party **was not fooled for a second** by her deceit.
* **The Wererat Immunity Ordeal & [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s Escape:** Despite seeing through Zeleen's bluff, the party was only **half-prepared to fight wererats** (who are completely immune to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical, unsilvered weapons):
  * **Ineffectual Frontline:** Because the party had not yet delved into [[Dragon Barrow]] for the *Dragon Slayer* Longsword, both **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** (wielding his wooden-and-steel *Qiang* spear) and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** (swinging his mundane Greataxe) were **completely ineffectual in hurting the immune lycanthropes**!
  * **Carried by Rhynok & Myagkiy:** The entire battle was carried by **[[Rhynok]]**—firing his magical **+1 Longbow** (from the [[Tower of Storms]] crab)—and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, who tore through the wererats with ***Spirit Guardians***, ***Spiritual Weapon***, and ***Sacred Flame***! Routing the Whiskered Gang, the party **ran off the surviving wererats as [[Zeleen Varnaster]] escaped from the mine**, restoring the operation to DonJon and dividing the gang's coin stash (`50 gp, 40 sp, 200 cp`) and curios.
* **Aftermath — Silvering Weapons in [[Phandalin]]:** The frustration of being unable to harm the wererats prompted [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] to immediately return to [[Phandalin]] and **silver his Greataxe** (alongside Tvyordiy silvering his *Qiang* spear and Rhynok silvering a Shortsword), vowing never to be powerless against his enemies again!
