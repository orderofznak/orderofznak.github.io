# ⚒️ The Right Hand of Gond

> [!NOTE]
> **Keeper:** [[Flanning Teldorer]], Master of the forge in the Crags
> **Item Type:** Wondrous Item (Smithing Hammer), Holy Relic of [[Gond]]
> **Rarity / Properties:** Rare. Lore only; no game statistics have been set.

---

## 🛡️ Overview
The **Right Hand of Gond**, also called the **Hammer of Gond**, is the most sacred relic of the Gondar faith in the North. It is an ancient smithing hammer of dark, star-flecked iron. It is kept at a consecrated mountain forge high in the Crags, north-east of Neverwinter. In the hands of a cleric working in [[Gond]]'s name, it can shape metal far beyond what mortal craft allows. That includes great implements of war, which is why the church guards it so closely.

---

## 📜 Origin: The First Hammer
The faithful say the hammer was made by **Ippen**, the first great cleric of Gond, long before there was a church to name him. No record proves it, but no Gondar who has held the hammer doubts it.

The tale says that Ippen was a wandering smith. One winter a fire fell from the sky over the hills north-east of Neverwinter, and he followed it. In a smoking crater in what are now called the **Starmetal Hills**, he found a lump of sky-iron that rang when he touched it, though nothing had struck it. He carried it up into the Crags, built a forge over a vent where the mountain breathed fire, and worked the metal for a year and a day.

On the last night, he said, the Wonderbringer stood behind him at the anvil. Gond said nothing about what the metal should become. He only wanted to see what Ippen would make of it. That night Ippen understood the nature of his god: Gond cares for the making, not for what is made.

### The Two Hammers and the First Unmaking
Ippen got two heads from the star-metal, perfectly alike. Faced with two of the same thing, he did what the Gondar have done ever since. He hid one away. He carried the other to the forge mouth and burned it in the mountain's fire as an offering. The church holds this to be the **first Sacred Unmaking** and the reason for the rite.

The Gondar call the burned twin the ***Left Hand***: it unmade, so that the *Right Hand* could make. For this, the tale goes, Gond named Ippen the first **Artificer**, the title the church still reserves for those the god personally rewards for special service.

## 🔥 Powers & Peril
The hammer has never been known to fail a smith who works in Gond's name. Siege engines, war machines, and armor fit for giants have all been credited to it in the church's tales.

The church has always seen the danger in that. Gond does not care what his gifts are used for, so the faithful have to care for him. The hammer has never left the Crags by the church's choice.

---

## 🏔️ The Keepers of the Crags
Ippen's forge became a holy site, and the cleric who tends it holds the rank of **Master**. Each Master swears an oath to work with the Right Hand and never to lend, sell, or carry it away. The keepers have come and gone over the centuries. During the **Ippensheir**, the twelve days after Greengrass that honor Ippen, the faithful climb to the forge to see the hammer and show off their own inventions.

* **Current Master:** [[Flanning Teldorer]], a Forge Domain cleric.
* **Successor in Training:** [[Dayne Beltran]], Flanning's prodigy apprentice.

---

## 👹 Surtur's Want
The fire giants have their own smith-god in **Surtur**, and they have long resented that the finest hammer in the North belongs to a human god. Legends say giants have tried for the Right Hand before. [[Kevlaan the Merciless]] was the first to hold it, and only through a hag's dealings.

## ⚔️ Campaign History
### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **The Theft:** Shadowy thieves broke into Flanning's forge, desecrated it, and stole the hammer. Flanning sent an urgent plea to [[The Order of Znak]].
* **The Broker:** The trail led from Neverwinter, through the Starmetal Hills, to the Night Hag [[Balheelsa]], who had sold the relic to Fire Giant Queen [[Kevlaan the Merciless]].
* **The Recovery:** The Order slew Kevlaan in the caldera of Mount Hotenow and recovered the hammer.
* **The Return:** The Order brought the hammer back to the Crags. In gratitude, Flanning granted each champion a bonus feat by Gond's favor: ***Crusher*** for [[Tvyordiy Znak]], ***Resilient (Constitution)*** for [[Myagkiy Znak]], ***Lucky*** for [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], and ***Poisoner*** for [[Rhynok]].

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Rumored Provenance:** The tie to Ippen is deliberately left as rumor in-world, so it stays open for future revision.
* **Canon vs. Table Lore:** Ippen, the Sacred Unmaking, the Ippensheir, and the Master and Artificer ranks come from Forgotten Realms lore on Gond. The star-metal origin, the twin hammers, the "Left Hand", the keepers' oath, and the fire giants' earlier attempts on the hammer were invented for this campaign.
* **Rarity:** Listed as a rare magic item for now. Its game statistics are intentionally undefined.
