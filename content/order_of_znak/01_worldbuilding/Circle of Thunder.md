# 🌩️ Circle of Thunder

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A ring of ancient standing stones atop a storm-lashed hill in southern **Neverwinter Wood**, situated atop a network of caves dug into the hillside.
* **Cult Ritual Site:** Controlled by the [[Anchorites of Talos]]—specifically half-orc ritualists **[[Flenz]]**, **[[Narux]]**, and **[[Yargath]]**—who performed blood sacrifices and dances in rotted boar-head masks to summon storms and invoke **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, a primal spirit of [[Talos]].

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **March with [[Xanth]] & Crushing [[Yargath]]'s Patrol:** After delving into [[Dragon Barrow]] to claim Krageer's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** and enlisting the centaur **[[Xanth]]**, [[The Order of Znak]] marched into Neverwinter Wood—passing the ransacked ruins of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] before pressing on toward the Circle of Thunder. En route through the forest trails, the party and **[[Xanth]]** intercepted **[[Yargath]]** and her warband of **10 Orcs**, wiping out Yargath and all ten orcs in a fierce woodland clash!
* **Epic Showdown Against [[Gorthok]], 10 Twig Blights, & 2 Anchorites ([[Flenz]] & [[Narux]]):** Storming the hilltop standing stones, an epic battle erupted amidst thundering hooves and crackling lightning tusks from **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, *Lightning Bolts* from the **2 [[Anchorites of Talos]]** (**[[Flenz]]** and **[[Narux]]**), **10 Twig Blights**, and constricting **Vine Blights**—matched against **[[Xanth]]**, the might of **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** and his blade, the lethal volleys of **[[Rhynok]]**'s enchanted +1 Longbow, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**'s nerve-locking *Stunning Strikes*, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**'s crowd-shredding *Spirit Guardians*!
* **Satisfying [[Xanth]], Myagkiy's *+1 Shield* & Woodland Manse Cleanup:** With Gorthok, Flenz, and Narux slain, **[[Xanth]] the Centaur was satisfied**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**—as the party's sole shield-bearer—claimed the ***+1 Shield*** from the anchorite cave hoard, before the party traveled back to the [[Woodland Manse]] to clean out [[Grannoc]], his anchorite cohort, 12 twig blights, 5 vine blights, 12 stirges, and 8 needle blights.
