# 🌩️ Circle of Thunder

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A ring of ancient standing stones atop a storm-lashed hill in southern **Neverwinter Wood**, situated atop a network of caves dug into the hillside.
* **Cult Ritual Site:** Controlled by the [[Anchorites of Talos]]—specifically half-orc ritualists **[[Flenz]]**, **[[Narux]]**, and **[[Yargath]]**—who performed blood sacrifices and dances in rotted boar-head masks to summon storms and invoke **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, a primal spirit of [[Talos]].

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **March with [[Xanth]] & The Custom Roll20 Map vs. [[Yargath]]:** After returning to [[Dragon Barrow]] to claim Krageer's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, enlisting the centaur **[[Xanth]]**, finding [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] ransacked by a giant boar, and collecting [[Rhynok]]'s ***Boots of Elvenkind*** from [[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]] in [[Phandalin]], [[The Order of Znak]] marched on the Circle of Thunder. En route, on a **custom Roll20 map created by Peter**, the party and **[[Xanth]]** intercepted **[[Yargath]]** and her patrol of **10 Orcs**, wiping out Yargath and all ten orcs!
* **Epic Showdown Against [[Gorthok]], [[Flenz]], [[Narux]] & Blights:** Storming the hilltop standing stones, an epic battle erupted amidst thundering hooves and crackling lightning tusks from **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, *Lightning Bolts* from **[[Flenz]]** and **[[Narux]]**, pesky **Twig Blights**, and constricting **Vine Blights**—matched against **[[Xanth]]**, the might of **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** and his silvered greataxe, the lethal twang of **[[Rhynok]]**'s +1 Longbow (*Sharpshooter*), **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**'s *Stunning Strikes*, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**'s crowd-shredding *Spirit Guardians*!
* **Myagkiy's *+1 Shield* & Woodland Manse Cleanup:** Emerging victorious over Gorthok and the anchorites, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**—as the party's sole shield-bearer—claimed the ***+1 Shield*** from the anchorite cave hoard, before the party traveled back to the [[Woodland Manse]] to clean out [[Grannoc]], the remaining blights, and the stirges.
