# 🍺 The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn

## 📜 Overview & Atmosphere
* **Location:** The commercial district of rebuilt [[Leilon]], situated near the town square along the High Road.
* **Proprietor:** **[[Alion Malwyn]]**, a friendly, gossip-loving Illuskan tavernkeeper who eagerly collects rumors, overland tales, and news from merchant caravans to share with guests each evening.
* **Architecture & Decor:** The tavern is named for the enormous, hollowed-out carapace of an ancient umber hulk that forms the centerpiece of the taproom. Lanterns cast a warm, amber glow over sturdy trestle tables where off-duty laborers, town guards, fishers, and traveling merchants gather to drink local ales and swap frontier gossip.

## 🗺️ Campaign Significance & The Order of Znak

### 🐲 Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake Kickoff
Following their return to Leilon from *Shadow's Run* and their successful liberation of **[[Leilon Point]]** from sahuagin raiders (August 28 – September 1, 2020), **[[The Order of Znak]]** made regular rounds through the town taverns to gather intelligence on regional threats.

Stopping into the Umber Hulk's Shell on the morning of **September 1, 2020**, innkeeper [[Alion Malwyn]] served the champions and shared the defining rumor of the tenday:

> *"Did you hear about the bronze dragon? Apparently, a few travelers from Neverwinter saw one fly north along the coast with a dolphin in its claws. They say those metallic dragons are good. I hope they’re right."*

This pivotal rumor provided the party with their first lead regarding the ancient bronze dragon **[[Lhammaruntosz]]** ("The Claws of the Coast"), setting the stage for the Order's subsequent pilgrimage to the **Bronze Shrine** of Bahamut later in the campaign.
