# 🪓 Ultima Exodus

> [!NOTE]
> **Owner & Wielder:** [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]  
> **Item Type:** Weapon (Greataxe), Two-Handed  
> **Rarity / Properties:** +2 Bonus to Attack and Damage Rolls, Heavy, Two-Handed, Adamantine & Silvered  

---

## 🛡️ Overview & Physical Description
**Ultima Exodus** is the signature weapon wielded by the Goliath barbarian **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**. The greataxe is a massive two-handed weapon forged from blackened adamantine and silver inlay, capable of cleaving through magical defenses, reinforced structures, and primordial beasts.

The centerpiece of the axehead features a sculpted horned fiend evoking the demonic entity Exodus, whose clawed arms clasp the weapon's haft and whose bat-like wings curve to form the inner supports of twin sweeping crescent blades. Both outer cutting edges are engraved with runic inscriptions that glow with molten orange light.

## ⚔️ Combat Role & Table History
* **Martial Synergy:** Krageer paired Ultima Exodus with the **Great Weapon Master (GWM)** feat and the Champion Fighter archetype's expanded critical range (19–20), capitalizing on adamantine properties and maximum martial DPR.
* **Fire Giant Queen Encounter:** Carried by Krageer during the climactic duel inside the dormant volcano in *"Keeping the Faith"*, where he drank [[Puck Caribdas]]'s *Potion of Growth* and was simultaneously *Enlarged* by [[Myagkiy Znak]] ("Double-Enlarge" to Huge size) to wrestle the Fire Giant Queen.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Homage:** Named after the classic 1983 RPG *Ultima III: Exodus*, serving as an intentional tribute and inside joke between Gary and Peter as devoted fans of the *Ultima* franchise.
