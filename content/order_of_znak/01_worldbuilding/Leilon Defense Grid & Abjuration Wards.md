# 🛡️ Leilon Defense Grid & Abjuration Wards

## 📜 Strategic Overview: The Shield of the Twin Signs (1496–1500 DR)
Following the climactic defeat of the cults of Talos and Myrkul during *Divine Contention*, [[Leilon]] was left physically battered and vulnerable to coastal raiders, planar horrors, and vengeance strikes from surviving cult cells.

During the five-year era of reconstruction (1496–1500 DR), **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** (cleric of the Life Domain, Level 13) joined forces with local priestess **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]**, town architect **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** to transform Leilon from an open frontier outpost into the most formidable, sacredly warded bastion on the Sword Coast. 

By systematically dedicating high-level spell slots, liquid party wealth (~4,500 gp in crushed rubies, holy oils, and rare incenses), and continuous daily ritual castings, Myagkiy established permanent abjuration zones that shield the populace from undead, fiends, and magical infiltration.

## ⚡ The Abjuration Layers

### 1. The Forbiddance Grid (*Forbiddance*, 6th-Level Abjuration)
* **Mechanics (*PHB* p. 243):** Cast daily in the same 40,000 sq ft area for 30 consecutive days, making the spell effect **permanent until dispelled**.
* **Area Coverage:** Four contiguous, non-overlapping sectors (each covering up to 40,000 square feet, extending 30 feet high from the ground):
  1. **Sector Alpha (The High Road Gates & Northern Palisade):** Encloses the primary overland entrance, wagon checkpoints, and eastern approach.
  2. **Sector Beta (The Fishery & Quayside Docks):** Covers the tidal basin, marsh quays, and fish-processing shacks, preventing aquatic undead or kraken cult infiltrators.
  3. **Sector Gamma (Town Square & House of Thalivar Perimeter):** Envelops the central markets, town hall, and the surrounding radius of Thalivar's haunted tower.
  4. **Sector Delta (Aufox Manor Estate, The Temple Meadow & Subterranean Bedrock Vault):** Protects the Order’s headquarters, the Annex, the training dojo, the sacred knoll, and extends thirty feet down into the bedrock to completely encase the doorless 15×15×10 ft treasury vault (preventing ethereal, planar, or teleportation bypass into the Order's riches).
* **Planar & Teleportation Denial:**
  - Creatures cannot enter the warded area by means of teleportation (*Misty Step*, *Dimension Door*, *Teleport*, *Word of Recall*) or using interplanar portals (e.g., *Gate*, *Plane Shift*, or ethereal travel).
  - Any attempt to magically teleport into the grid instantly fails, stranding the caster at their origin.
* **Antipathy to Fiends & Undead:**
  - **Damage:** When a **Fiend** or **Undead** enters the warded area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it takes **`5d10 Radiant Damage`** (DC 18 Charisma save for half damage).
  - **Password:** The verbal pass-phrase (*"The Soft Sign bends, the Hard Sign endures"*) allows designated visiting celestials or allied extraplanar emissaries to enter unharmed.

### 2. The Hallow Consecrations (*Hallow*, 5th-Level Evocation)
* **Mechanics (*PHB* p. 249):** 24-hour ritual casting utilizing 1,000 gp of rare herbs, oils, and frankincense per site, lasting permanently until dispelled.
* **Protected Sanctuaries:**
  1. **The Temple of the Twin Signs:**
     - A 120-foot radius sphere of consecrated ground.
     - **Extra Effect — Daylight:** The entire inner sanctuary and portico are illuminated with soft, radiant sunlight, dispelling any darkness of 5th level or lower.
     - **Extra Effect — Courage:** Allies within the temple cannot become frightened.
  2. **The Leilon Cemetery (South of Cemetery Hill):**
     - Wards the newly constructed, stone-walled graveyard on the flat meadow outside the northwest gates.
     - **Extra Effect — Dead Cannot Rise:** Any humanoid buried within this hallowed ground cannot be transformed into undead by spells like *Animate Dead*, *Create Undead*, or natural necromantic blights.
     - Prevents grave-robbing ghouls or wandering spirits from rising along the marsh border.

---

### 3. The Symbol & Glyph Network (*Symbol*, 7th-Level Abjuration)
* **Mechanics (*PHB* p. 280):** Inscribed with mercury-infused diamond ink into granite threshold pavers (1,000 gp focus per glyph), remaining dormant indefinitely until triggered.
* **Threshold Deployments:**
  * **The Gates of Aufox Manor:**
    - **Glyph 1 — Stun (DC 18 CON Save):** Triggers if any hostile force breaches the perimeter post-and-rail fence with drawn weapons or lethal intent; targets are stunned for 1 minute.
    - **Glyph 2 — Hopelessness (DC 18 CHA Save):** Overwhelms attackers with despair, rendering them unable to attack or take harmful actions for 1 minute.
  * **The High Road North Gate & South Gate:**
    - **Glyph — Death (DC 18 CON Save):** Hidden beneath the center gate arch; triggers on organized military formations or undead warbands; detonates for **`10d10 Necrotic Damage`** (half on save) in a 60-foot radius sphere.
  * **The Quayside Fishery Bridge:**
    - **Glyph — Sleep (DC 18 WIS Save):** Triggers if intruders infiltrate the marsh canals under cover of darkness without speaking the night warden pass-phrase; puts creatures into magical sleep for 10 minutes, allowing the town guard to apprehend saboteurs without civilian casualties.
* **Keyholders & Passwords:**
  - Passwords are held by: **[[Leopold]]** (Majordomo of Aufox Manor), **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]** (Shrine of Lathander), **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]** (Town Architect), and the grown Acolytes (**[[Burnice Znak]]**, **[[Smithwell Znak]]**, **[[Yasmina]]**).

### 4. The Temple of the Gods (*Temple of the Gods*, 7th-Level Conjuration)
* **Location:** The elevated grassy knoll north of Aufox Manor along the northern timber palisade.
* **Architecture:** A 120-foot square, two-story radiant white marble cathedral created by divine manifestation:
  - Unmortared, seamless white marble blocks glowing with faint celestial luminesce.
  - Dedicated jointly to **Gond** (craft, defense, and architecture), **Lathander** (renewal and dawn), and the philosophical doctrine of **The Signs** ($\mathbf{Ъ \quad Ь}$).
  - Features twin burning bronze braziers flanking the portico, grand stained-glass clerestory windows, and private living quarters for visiting acolytes and traveling pilgrims.
* **Defensive Properties:**
  - The temple walls are completely impervious to all non-magical damage and resist magical destruction.
  - Undead and fiends cannot enter the temple walls unless explicitly invited by the high priest.
