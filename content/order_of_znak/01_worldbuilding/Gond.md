# ⚙️ Gond, the Wonderbringer

> [!NOTE]
> **Portfolio:** Blacksmiths, woodworkers, inventors, and engineers
> **Other Names:** **Nebelun** (among gnomes) and **Zionil** (in Durpar, Estagund, and Var the Golden)
> **Holy Relic in the Campaign:** [[Right Hand of Gond]]

---

## 🛡️ Overview
**Gond** is the god of craft and invention. He turns ideas into solid form and inspires mortals to make new things, and he is worshipped by humans and gnomes alike. He cares only for the act of creation and pays little heed to what his inventions do once they are loose in the world.

He treats magic as just another tool for building new devices, which puts him at odds with some in the church of Mystra. He always needs strange components and raw materials, so he may overlook shady sponsors if they pay in materials, useful knowledge, or future favors. He can be distracted, businesslike, sarcastic, or patronizing, but also brilliant and generous. He is devoted to his faithful. He may be slow to answer because he is busy, but he always makes sure their needs are met.

## ⛪ The Church of Gond
* **Reach & Reputation:** The faith is tolerated across Faerûn, in good and evil realms alike, and gnome worshippers are increasingly common. Merchants court the clergy for their latest inventions. Those same inventions sometimes upset markets and draw hostility from other faiths.
* **Lantan:** The island nation of Lantan holds Gond's worship, Gondarism, as its state religion and is a hotbed of invention.
* **Smoke Powder:** The church has angered rulers across Faerûn by introducing smoke powder and firearms. Its priests guard the formulas closely and sell small sealed jars of powder, sealants, and lubricants as they travel.
* **Builders:** Priests of Gond are in demand as architects, especially for the vaulted temples of other gods, and these contracts bring the faith wealth and influence.

### 🧰 Vestments
Gondar clergy wear saffron vestments with a crimson collar and stole, plus a leather sash dotted with tools, gears, wire, locks, and other useful oddments, ending in a large pouch. They favor enormous sunhats and belts of linked metal medallions. Their holy symbol is worn as a pendant of bone, brass, bronze, or ivory. In the field, most carry knuckleduster rings, hidden knives (boot-heel blades are a favorite), lockpicks, and often a sealed flask of smoke powder.

### 📜 Hierarchy
The clergy call themselves the *Consecrated of Gond*. In ascending order, the ranks are:
* *Wonderer* (novice)
* *Seeker Postulant* (priest in training)
* *Seeker after Small Things* (confirmed priest)
* *Greater Seeker*
* *Seeker of the Twelfth Order* down to *Seeker of the First Order*
* *High Seeker* (all senior clergy)
* ***Master*** (leader of a religious community or keeper of a holy site)
* ***Artificer*** (one personally rewarded and named by Gond)
* *High Artificer* (the supreme priest of the faith)

Obedience to a superior is absolute.

### 🏛️ Temples
Temples of Gond are boxy stone buildings ringed with stout-pillared porticoes, plain inside except for displays of the clergy's creations. The central altar is always a massive anvil surrounded by the endlessly spinning cogs of a great machine. The back rooms are workshops.

## 🔥 Rites & Festivals
* **Daily Prayer:** Short prayers on rising and retiring, a longer prayer of thanks at the main meal, and a special dedication before starting any new creation.
* **The Ippensheir:** The faith's one holy festival, named for **Ippen**, the first great cleric of Gond. For the twelve days after Greengrass, the faithful gather at a site where a famed inventor once worked to feast and share new inventions. Some travel between gatherings using the church's network of portals.
* **The Sacred Unmaking:** When the Gondar discover a new machine or tool, they make two copies if they can. One is hidden away to show fellow Gondar later. The other is smashed or burned as an offering, affirming Gond's mastery over both making and unmaking. In this campaign, the rite's origin is tied to the [[Right Hand of Gond]].

### 🏅 Honorary Orders
The church has no knightly orders but many honorary societies, such as the *Order of Puissant Stonemasons and Stonecarvers*, the *Holy Order of Most Skilled Architects and Bridgemakers*, the *Armorers of the Wonderbringer*, and the *Most Arcane Order of Gearmakers, Clockmakers, and Automationists*.

## ⚔️ Gond in the Campaign
* **[[Flanning Teldorer]]:** A Forge Domain cleric and Master of the holy forge in the Crags, keeper of the [[Right Hand of Gond]].
* **[[Dayne Beltran]]:** Flanning's prodigy apprentice and a Forge Domain cleric.
* **Surtur's Enmity:** The fire giants' smith-god Surtur is an old rival of Gond. His devotee [[Kevlaan the Merciless]] bought the stolen Right Hand to forge weapons of war.
* **Gond's Blessings:** After the Order recovered the hammer in [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|"Keeping the Faith"]], Flanning granted each champion a bonus feat by Gond's favor.
