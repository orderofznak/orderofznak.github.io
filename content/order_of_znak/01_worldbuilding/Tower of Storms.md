# ⚡ Tower of Storms

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A solitary stone lighthouse perched atop a jagged sea cliff protruding into the **Sea of Swords**, southwest of [[Phandalin]] and northwest of [[Leilon]].
* **Corrupted Beacon:** Originally built to guide coastal ships around treacherous offshore reefs, the lighthouse was seized by the [[Anchorites of Talos]]. Its magical beacon was warped into an eerie green beacon of destruction that summoned storms and lured seafaring vessels onto the rocks.
* **The Undead Keeper:** Guarded by the half-orc anchorite **[[Moesko]]**, whose disembodied, still-beating heart was impaled atop the pinnacle of the tower, granting him necrotic immortality until destroyed.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Awakened Giant Crab & Banshee Pact:** Arriving on the beach below the cliffs, [[The Order of Znak]] calmed and befriended the friendly **awakened giant crab** through gentle wilderness empathy, then entered the sea cave to parley with the sea elf banshee **[[Miraal]]**—agreeing to recover her stolen opalescent conch shell spellcasting focus from her murderer, **[[Moesko]]**.
* **Lured by the Harpies:** Trekking up the narrow cliffside path toward the lighthouse, several members of the party succumbed to the enchanting pull of the **Harpies' *Luring Song*** and were drawn toward the sheer ledges multiple times before shaking off the siren enchantment and slaying the harpies in their aerie.
* **Shrine of Talos, Slaying [[Moesko]] & Destroying His Heart:** Inside the lighthouse, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** recognized the desecrated altar and iconography of **Talos**, god of storms. The party fought and killed **[[Moesko]]**, ascended to the pinnacle of the beacon to **destroy his pulsating heart**, and **returned the opalescent conch shell to [[Miraal]]**, putting the banshee's spirit to rest.
* **The Crab's Gift (+1 Longbow) & Unexplored Wrecks:** Fulfilling its promise after the party helped [[Miraal]], the **awakened giant crab** retrieved a magical weapon from the sunken shipwreck offshore—an enchanted **+1 Longbow** claimed by **[[Rhynok]]**! Because the party never dove to explore the shipwrecks themselves, neither the hunter sharks nor **[[Daggermaw]]** were ever encountered.
