# 🌲 Woodland Manse

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** An overgrown stone-and-timber manor house nestled deep within the shadowed interior of **Neverwinter Wood**, southeast of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]].
* **Cult Occupation:** Seized and desecrated by half-orc ritualist **[[Grannoc]]** and his [[Anchorites of Talos]], who planted a **Gulthias Tree** in the central courtyard well to spawn twig, needle, and vine blights.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **First Expedition — Rhynok's Gruumsh Blood-Ruse & Underleveled Retreat:**
  * **Psychological Warfare:** Arriving at the manse while still underleveled for the dungeon, **[[Rhynok]]** used his Favored Enemy knowledge of **Orcs** to stage a brilliant ruse outside—killing a wild animal, displaying its bloody remains, and writing a message in blood proclaiming that **Gruumsh** was angry with them, which terrified a large number of the stationed orcs into fleeing!
  * **Overwhelmed at the Gulthias Tree:** Pushing inside to the central courtyard housing the **Gulthias Tree** and **[[Grannoc]]**, the party was rapidly outnumbered by swarms of blights, wild boars, and *Lightning Bolts* from the [[Anchorites of Talos]].
  * **Krageer's Double Down & Tvyordiy's "Flight" Rescue:** As the party fled, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** fell unconscious **twice**. He was saved the first time by a clutch **Medicine check**, but as the others escaped off the map, Krageer lingered at the edge and was dropped a second time by a final salvo of **needle blight needles**. Swooping back in from his **flight** (*Aarakocra pun intended!*), **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** picked up the pin-cushioned Goliath and dragged him away to safety.
* **Second Expedition — Post-[[Circle of Thunder]] Cleanup:**
  * After defeating [[Yargath]]'s patrol and slaying [[Gorthok]], [[Flenz]], and [[Narux]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]], the Level 5+ party traveled back to the Woodland Manse to settle the score—cleaning out the **Stirges** in the upper halls, slaying **[[Grannoc]]** and the remaining **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**, and eradicating the Gulthias blights once and for all.
