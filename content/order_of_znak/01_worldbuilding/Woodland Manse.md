# 🌲 Woodland Manse

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** An overgrown stone-and-timber manor house nestled deep within the shadowed interior of the **Neverwinter Wood**, northwest of Falcon's Hunting Lodge.
* **Cult Occupation:** Seized and desecrated by the [[Anchorites of Talos]], who transformed the overgrown courtyards and library into a ritual site to spawn destructive twig, needle, and vine blights.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Blight Infestation:** In Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*), Anchorite leader **[[Grannoc]]** rallied his beast-cultists within the manse to launch coordinated attacks against the frontier.
* **The Order's Assault:** Guiding their forces from Falcon's Hunting Lodge with ranger [[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]], [[The Order of Znak]] infiltrated the manse, engaged Grannoc's anchorites, eradicated the blight swarms, and broke the cult's territorial grip on the Neverwinter Wood.
