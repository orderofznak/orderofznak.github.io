# 🌲 Woodland Manse

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** An overgrown stone-and-timber manor house nestled deep within the shadowed interior of **Neverwinter Wood**, southeast of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]].
* **Cult Occupation:** Seized and desecrated by half-orc ritualist **[[Grannoc]]** and his [[Anchorites of Talos]], who planted a **Gulthias Tree** in the central courtyard well to spawn twig, needle, and vine blights.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **First Expedition — Rhynok's Gruumsh Blood-Ruse & Narrow Retreat:**
  * **Psychological Warfare:** Arriving at the manse while still untested against the full strength of the cult, **[[Rhynok]]** used his deep knowledge of **Orcs** to stage a brilliant ruse outside—killing a wild animal, displaying its bloody remains, and writing a message in blood proclaiming that **Gruumsh** was angry with them, which terrified a large number of the stationed orcs into fleeing!
  * **Overwhelmed at the Gulthias Tree:** Pushing inside to the central courtyard housing the **Gulthias Tree** and **[[Grannoc]]**, the party was rapidly outnumbered by swarms of blights, wild boars, and *Lightning Bolts* from the [[Anchorites of Talos]].
  * **Krageer's Double Down & Tvyordiy's "Flight" Rescue:** As the party fought a desperate fighting retreat, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** fell unconscious **twice**. He was revived from the brink the first time by emergency battlefield triage, but as the others broke clear into the trees, Krageer lingered at the edge of the clearing and was dropped a second time by a final salvo of **needle blight needles**. Swooping back in from his **flight** (*Aarakocra pun intended!*), **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** hoisted the pin-cushioned Goliath and carried him away to safety.
* **Second Expedition — Post-[[Circle of Thunder]] Cleanup:**
  * After defeating [[Yargath]]'s patrol and slaying [[Gorthok]], [[Flenz]], and [[Narux]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]], the seasoned party traveled back to the Woodland Manse to settle the score—cleaning out the **Stirges** in the upper halls, slaying **[[Grannoc]]** and the remaining **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**, and eradicating the Gulthias blights once and for all.
