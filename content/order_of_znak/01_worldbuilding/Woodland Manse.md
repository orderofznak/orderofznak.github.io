# 🌲 Woodland Manse

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** An overgrown stone-and-timber manor house nestled deep within the shadowed interior of **Neverwinter Wood**, southeast of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]].
* **Cult Occupation:** Seized and desecrated by half-orc ritualist **[[Grannoc]]** and his [[Anchorites of Talos]], who planted a **Gulthias Tree** in the central courtyard well to spawn twig, needle, and vine blights.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **First Expedition — The Tattooed One-Eyed Goat of Gruumsh & Narrow Retreat:**
  * **Psychological Warfare:** Arriving at the manse after departing [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] (where [[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]] rode out to offer his ***Boots of Elvenkind***), the party brought a **shaved, one-eyed goat** in tow to play a trick on the superstitious orc garrison. **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Stoneface]] tattooed a prophetic warning in Orcish directly onto the shaved one-eyed goat:**
    > *"Take this goat as an offering of sustenance. For if you do not take it and flee from this mansion and cease your worship of false and weak gods then my wrath will come upon thee. My blood spear is unforgiving and though I only have one eye... it sees all. — He Who Watches, He Who Never Sleeps, The One Eyed God, Gruumsh"*
    Setting the goat loose outside the estate terrified a large contingent of the stationed orcs into fleeing on the spot as [[Rhynok]] opened the assault from ambush!
  * **Overwhelmed at the Gulthias Tree:** Pushing inside to the central courtyard housing the **Gulthias Tree** and **[[Grannoc]]**, the party was rapidly outnumbered by swarms of blights, wild boars, and *Lightning Bolts* from the [[Anchorites of Talos]].
  * **Krageer's Double Down & Tvyordiy's "Flight" Rescue:** As the party fought a desperate fighting retreat, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** fell unconscious **twice**. He was revived from the brink the first time by emergency battlefield triage, but as the others broke clear into the trees, Krageer lingered at the edge of the clearing and was dropped a second time by a final salvo of **needle blight needles**. Swooping back in from his **flight** (*Aarakocra pun intended!*), **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** hoisted the pin-cushioned Goliath and carried him away to safety.
* **Second Expedition — Post-[[Circle of Thunder]] Five-Round Courtyard Battle & Purge:**
  * After defeating [[Yargath]]'s patrol and slaying [[Gorthok]], [[Flenz]], and [[Narux]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]] alongside [[Xanth]], the seasoned party traveled back to the Woodland Manse to settle the score—fighting a fierce five-round courtyard battle against **12 Twig Blights**, **5 Vine Blights** (pushing through their *Entangling Plants* while [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s ***Spirit Guardians*** shredded the blights), and **2 [[Anchorites of Talos]]** (including **[[Grannoc]]**), before clearing out **12 Stirges** in the upper halls and **8 Needle Blights** on the grounds!
