# 🏹 Falcon's Hunting Lodge

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A fortified log palisade compound, guest house, and two-story main lodge standing along the eastern bank of the Neverwinter River deep inside **Neverwinter Wood**.
* **Household:** Owned and maintained by veteran tracker and master ranger **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]]**, who hosted wealthy nobles from Neverwinter on wilderness hunting excursions alongside his weary aged cook **[[Corwin]]**, 12-year-old mute stablehand **[[Pell]]**, and prized gray riding stallion **[[Baatorius]]**.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **First Visit — Skirmish Against [[Cryovain]] & Praise for the Manse Ruse:** Arriving at the lodge after clearing the [[Loggers' Camp]], [[The Order of Znak]] walked into a brief skirmish where **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** was actively fighting **[[Cryovain]]**, with the party jumping in to help drive the white dragon off. Inside, the champions met Falcon, [[Corwin]], [[Pell]], and saw [[Baatorius]]. As the quest giver seeking a strike against the woodland orcs and anchorites, Falcon was **duly impressed by [[Rhynok]]'s earlier Gruumsh blood-note subterfuge** at the [[Woodland Manse]]. Rhynok immediately set his sights on Falcon's offered reward—a pair of ***Boots of Elvenkind***—which later inspired him to hunt down the matching ***Cloak of Elvenkind*** in a subsequent adventure.
* **Retaliatory Ransacking by [[Gorthok]] & Stopover Amidst the Wreckage:** Shortly after the party departed the lodge following their first failed foray into the [[Woodland Manse]], the [[Anchorites of Talos]] launched a retaliatory strike—unleashing **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar** and a horde of twenty orcs to ransack the compound and drive Falcon's household back to [[Phandalin]]. Later, after clearing [[Dragon Barrow]] and enlisting centaur ally **[[Xanth]]**, the party reunited with the evacuated **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** in [[Phandalin]], learned that his lodge had been overrun, and received [[Rhynok]]'s promised ***Boots of Elvenkind***. Setting out into Neverwinter Wood, the party stopped over at the ruined lodge along their way, witnessing the tusk-shattered palisades and heavy **orcsign** firsthand before marching to the [[Circle of Thunder]] and returning to purge the [[Woodland Manse]].
