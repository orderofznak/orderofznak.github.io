# 🏹 Falcon's Hunting Lodge

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A fortified log palisade compound, guest house, and two-story main lodge standing along the eastern bank of the Neverwinter River deep inside **Neverwinter Wood**.
* **Household:** Owned and maintained by veteran tracker and master ranger **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]]**, who hosted wealthy nobles from Neverwinter on wilderness hunting excursions alongside his weary aged cook **[[Corwin]]**, 12-year-old mute stablehand **[[Pell]]**, and prized gray riding stallion **[[Baatorius]]**.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **First Visit — A Bottle of Wine, Skirmish Against [[Cryovain]] & The Promise of Elven Boots:** Acting on a tavern rumor heard in [[Phandalin]] (*"Bottle of wine for room and board — deep in the forest near the river's edge"*), [[The Order of Znak]] traveled to the lodge after clearing the [[Loggers' Camp]]. Arriving in the middle of a brief skirmish where **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** was actively fighting **[[Cryovain]]**, the party jumped in to help drive the white dragon off and met Falcon, [[Corwin]], [[Pell]], and [[Baatorius]]. As the party departed the lodge heading southeast toward the [[Woodland Manse]], **Falcon rode out on his horse and called out:** *"Wait! If you are headed where I think you are headed, then you would be doing me a service. If you can cull the evil that resides there, I have a pair of magical boots that might serve you on your journeys."* One look at the ***Boots of Elvenkind*** was enough for the party to know they had **[[Rhynok]]**'s name written all over them (later inspiring him to hunt down a matching ***Cloak of Elvenkind***).
* **Retaliatory Ransacking by [[Gorthok]] & Reward in [[Phandalin]]:** Shortly after the party's first foray and retreat from the [[Woodland Manse]], the [[Anchorites of Talos]] launched a retaliatory strike—unleashing **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar** and a horde of twenty orcs to ransack the hunting lodge and drive Falcon, Corwin, and Pell south to shelter in [[Phandalin]]. After the Order later slew Gorthok at the [[Circle of Thunder]] and returned to purge the [[Woodland Manse]], they reunited with the evacuated **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** in [[Phandalin]] and claimed [[Rhynok]]'s promised ***Boots of Elvenkind***!
