# 🍷 The Wayside Inn

## 🛡️ Overview & Architecture
* **Location:** A historic, fortified coaching inn situated strategically along the **High Road** midway between Waterdeep and Neverwinter, just north of the Mere of Dead Men and south of Leilon.
* **Layout:** Built of heavy timber and dressed fieldstone with a walled courtyard, stables, vegetable gardens, a large common taproom, private guest rooms, and a roaring central hearth.
* **Proprietor:** Owned and managed with shrewd commercial efficiency by former bandit captain **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]**.
* **Kitchen & Hearth:** Commanded by half-orc head cook **[[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]]**, celebrated across the Sword Coast for her alarmingly spicy stews and signature pork pies.

## ⚔️ Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **The Cult Siege (Act 2 — *Storm Lord's Wrath*):** The inn was besieged by Talos cultists and undead marauders during a violent thunderstorm. [[The Order of Znak]] intervened, broke the siege, and secured the safety of the staff and patrons.
* **The Party's Sanctuary & Hearth:** Following the battle, the inn became the favored regional rest stop and unofficial home hearth of the party along the High Road. Goliath barbarian **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]** developed a deep infatuation with Cooragh's spicy cooking, sparking an enduring table romance.
