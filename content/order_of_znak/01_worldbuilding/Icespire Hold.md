# 🏔️ Icespire Hold

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A frozen stone fortress perched atop the northeastern spur of **Icespire Peak**, the tallest snow-capped mountain in the **Sword Mountains**.
* **Dragon's Lair:** Built centuries ago by warlord trespassing warbands and later occupied by the orc tribe of war chief **Kra**, the fortress was seized by the young white dragon **[[Cryovain]]**, who slew Kra, drove the surviving orcs down into the foothills and Neverwinter Wood, and claimed the icy rooftop as his lair.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Hobgoblin Wilderness Clash & Silvered Weapons:** Ascending the frozen mountain trails toward Icespire Hold, [[The Order of Znak]] fought off a wilderness warband of **Hobgoblins**—with **[[Rhynok]]** wielding his **Silvered Shortsword** in close quarters alongside **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s Silvered *Qiang* spear** and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s Silvered Greataxe**.
* **Subduing the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]] & Stirges:** Entering the lower stone chambers of Icespire Hold, the party encountered the opportunistic mercenary band known as the **[[Stone-Cold Reavers]]** ([[Brakkis Elspaar]], [[Dobin Noreth]], [[Jabarl]], [[Runa Vokdottir]], and [[Syleen Wintermoon]]) and dispatched them in their usual no-nonsense fashion before clearing out a flock of **Stirges**.
* **Climactic Rooftop Showdown Against [[Cryovain]] & 4 Ice Mephits:** Scaling the fortress to the icy rooftop, the party confronted **[[Cryovain]]** alongside **4 Ice Mephits** lairing amid the frozen battlements!
  * **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** invoked ***Bless*** upon the party and summoned his ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat down the **4 Ice Mephits**, while the champions weathered and dodged Cryovain's freezing *Cold Breath*.
  * **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** landed **clutch *Stunning Strikes* directly on the dragon**, locking Cryovain down on the ice and enabling **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** to unload devastating salvos with his ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, its ancient runes flaring with dragon-slaying fury on every stroke.
  * **[[Rhynok]]** rained deadly, pinpoint arrows into the wyrm with his **+1 Longbow**, slaying **[[Cryovain]]** and ending the white dragon's reign of terror!
* **Heroic Triumph & Sword Coast Renown:** Returning to [[Phandalin]] as celebrated heroes of the frontier, the Order's renown spread all the way to **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Dagult Neverember]]** in Neverwinter and the **Lords of Waterdeep**, setting the stage for their Act 2 commission in **[[Leilon]]**.
