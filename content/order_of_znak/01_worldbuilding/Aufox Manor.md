# 🏰 Aufox Manor

## 🛡️ Overview & History
* **The Seat of the Order:** The fortified timber-and-stone estate and permanent headquarters of **[[The Order of Znak]]** in [[Leilon]], constructed with the aid of town architect [[Grizzelda Copperwraught|Grizzelda]]'s masonry crews following the defense of the town during the Kraken Cult raid.
* **Dedication & Name:** Named as a heartfelt tribute to workplace coworker, mentor, and friend **David**.
* **Household & Inhabitants:**
  - **Majordomo:** Managed by trusted human estate steward **[[Leopold]]**.
  - **Adopted Wards & Acolytes:** Raised the orphaned village children **[[Burnice Znak]]** and **[[Smithwell Znak|Smithwell]]**, along with young lizardfolk monk **[[Yasmina]]**.
  - **Planar Dojo (1502–1505 DR):** Following his return from the Plane of Air, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** and his sister **[[Klee'errk]]** established the gravel courtyard dojo of the Hard Sign, training the grown acolytes alongside four planar Aarakocra disciples: **[[Kree-uk]]**, **[[Qeerik]]**, **[[Arak-tai]]**, and **[[Sool-arrk]]** (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 6]]).
  - **Library:** Contains the sealed journals of the champions, ancestral heirlooms, and [[Yeri]]'s leather-bound notebook of proverbs inscribed with the three Cyrillic signs: $\mathbf{Ъ \quad Ы \quad Ь}$.

## 🏛️ Estate Architecture & Grounds

### 1. The Main Manor House
* **Triangular Geometry:** Unlike the boxy rectangular construction typical of frontier towns, Aufox Manor was built with a distinctive **triangular footprint**, nestled comfortably into the earthen embankment just north of Leilon's northeastern wagon entrance. (On the official module town map, this building was originally indicated by the Knight's Goblet Inn pointer before the inn was relocated deeper into town).
* **Roofline & Chimney:** Sheltered under a steeply pitched **wooden shake and timber-shingle roof** (warm weathered pine and cedar, rather than civic grey slate), crowned by a **single prominent stone masonry chimney** rising along the south-facing pitch from the great hearth within.
* **Entryway:** Heavy double doors of iron-banded bog oak, master-carved by dwarven artisans with the twin Cyrillic glyphs of the order: **Ъ** (The Hard Sign) and **Ь** (The Soft Sign).

### 2. The Aufox Annex
* Situated twenty paces northeast of the main manor, the Annex is a sturdy, two-story rectangular timber lodge constructed to accommodate the expanding household.
* Served as the private dormitory and study halls for teenage wards [[Burnice Znak]], [[Smithwell Znak]], and [[Yasmina]], as well as guest quarters for visiting lizardfolk exchange allies ([[Graxxoamo]], [[Shriss]]).

### 3. The Courtyard Dojo & Training Yard
* Enclosed behind the manor and annex within a rustic, post-and-rail split-log fence.
* Surfaced with crushed white river gravel to instill discipline and balance.
* Furnished with hardwood sparring pell posts, straw archery targets, and weapon racks holding practice staffs, shields, and wooden blades.
* From 1502 to 1505 DR, Master Tvyordiy convened dawn katas here, refining the unarmed techniques of the Acolytes and his Aarakocra disciples beneath the shadow of the northern palisade.

### 4. The Subterranean Bedrock Vault
* **Dimensions & Excavation:** Situated fifteen feet directly beneath the great stone hearth of the main manor, this hidden **15 ft × 15 ft × 10 ft** subterranean vault was excavated from solid coastal granite bedrock. Created and reinforced by [[Myagkiy Znak]] through repeated castings of ***Stone Shape***, its floor, walls, and ceiling form a seamless, unmortared monolithic stone cube.
* **Doorless Architecture:** Under normal circumstances, there is **no physical entrance**, staircase, ladder, hatch, or connecting tunnel. To all physical inspection, tapping, or mundane excavation, the ground beneath the manor floor is impenetrable, solid bedrock.
* **Passage Protocol:** Entry and exit are accessible solely via the 3rd-level divine spell ***Meld into Stone*** (*PHB* p. 259), allowing a cleric or druid to step directly into the solid rock of the hearth floor and emerge inside the sealed vault. In an extreme crisis, *Stone Shape* can temporarily reshape a passage.
* **Planar & Teleportation Immunity:** Because the vault lies within the subterranean vertical envelope of **Forbiddance Sector Delta**, magical entry via *Misty Step*, *Dimension Door*, *Teleport*, *Gaseous Form*, or *Etherealness* is strictly barred; any attempt fails automatically, and hostile fiends or undead take 5d10 radiant damage upon entry.
* **Treasury Holdings:** Secured inside iron-banded dwarven chests are the Order’s accumulated wealth, hundreds of platinum trade ingots, liquid coinage, diamond caches for emergency resurrection (*Revivify*, *Raise Dead*), rare incense for *Legend Lore*, dragon spoils from the Sword Mountains, and ancestral ledgers.

## ⚡ Wards & Defensive Enchantments
As the heart of the Order of Znak's civilian presence, Aufox Manor is heavily warded under the [[Leilon Defense Grid & Abjuration Wards]]:
* **Forbiddance Sector Delta:** Completely encompasses the manor, annex, and training grounds, dealing **5d10 Radiant Damage** to unauthorized fiends or undead and forbidding all forms of magical teleportation or planar breach.
* **Symbol of Stunning:** Inscribed into the granite threshold beneath the front gate arch (DC 18 CON save), stunning intruders who force entry with drawn weapons.
* **Symbol of Hopelessness:** Inscribed into the central dojo flagstone (DC 18 CHA save), disarming and pacifying any infiltration attempt.
