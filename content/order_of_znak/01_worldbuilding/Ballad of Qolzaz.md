# 🎵 The Ballad of Qolzaz

> [!NOTE]
> **Performance Note:** Set to be performed by a bard on lute and drums.
> **Lore Context:** Honors the legendary battle against Matriarch **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]**, the joint defense against invading undead trolls, and the tattoo etched onto **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**'s chest in mutual respect. Features **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** (monk), **[[Rhynok]]** (elf ranger), **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** (life cleric), and Krageer.

---

## 📜 Full Lyrics & Performance Cues

### Intro
*(Spoken, quiet, reverent)*
> *A tale of blood, of honor, and the mark left by strength.*
> *This is the ballad of Qolzaz, Matron of Scale and Storm.*

### Verse 1
*(Soft lute, minor key. Slow tempo.)*
In the marsh where the moon meets the mire,
Where the reeds hum a song of the deep,
Dwelt [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]], the Matron of Fire,
Whose eyes made the bravest hearts weep.
With her claws she carved shelter from sorrow,
With her hiss, she commanded the dawn—
A mother, a war-chief, a shadow,
And now, by the sword, she is gone.

*(Lute pause. Let silence hang. One heartbeat. Then continue.)*

### Verse 2
*(Add faint drumbeat, building tension.)*
Came [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]], the goliath of thunder,
With vengeance and purpose like flame.
With him, the swift monk of the mountain ([[Tvyordiy Znak]]),
An elf with death's eye and sure aim ([[Rhynok]]).
The cleric's faith shone beside them (*[[Myagkiy Znak]]*),
All hearts for the town of Leilon.
They came for the stolen and helpless—
By honor and wrath, they were drawn.

### Chorus
*(Drum and lute together, mid-tempo, somber chant)*
> **Raise the reed, sound the scale,**
> **For the matron who will not pale.**
> **Blade and fang, death and flame,**
> **Qolzaz lives on in name.**

### Verse 3
*(Strum hard on first line. Intensify emotion.)*
In the clash, the first blood was her final,
[[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]] met the might of the storm.
No spell nor steel could unbind her,
But [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] struck true, harsh and warm.
She fell with a cry like the tempest,
Her brood raised a dirge for their kin—
Yet the ground shook anew with a horror,
As dead trolls crawled up from the fen.

### Verse 4
*(Faster rhythm. Urgency in tone.)*
Then foes stood as shields for each other,
Steel and scale in a desperate line.
The monk ([[Tvyordiy Znak]]) danced through death with a feather,
The elf's ([[Rhynok]]) arrows sang into spine.
[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] broke bones with his fury,
The cleric's prayers (*[[Myagkiy Znak]]*) held the breach.
And [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]], though gone from the battle,
Watched still from the edge, out of reach.

### Chorus
*(Softer than before, more reflective)*
> **Raise the reed, sound the scale,**
> **For the matron who will not pale.**
> **Blade and fang, death and flame,**
> **Qolzaz lives on in name.**

### Verse 5
*(Drop drum, only lute, slow again.)*
When silence returned to the water,
And moonlight struck blood on the leaves,
The victors gave honor to [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]],
Whose strength still rode under their greaves.
[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]], with respect in his sinew,
Etched her face on the breadth of his chest.
For even in slaying, he knew her—
A matron, a warrior, blessed.

### Verse 6
*(Finale. A cappella or single lute note per line. Let the words carry.)*
Now her name flows with wind through the cattails,
Her tale etched in marrow and mud.
Her kin teach the hatchlings of [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]],
The one who died fighting for blood.
And [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]], when asked of his markings,
Will say with a reverent nod:
"She died as a queen 'neath the thunder—
And I bear her, in ink, like a god."

### Final Chorus
*(Slower than before, barely above a whisper.)*
> **Raise the reed, sound the scale,**
> **For the matron who will not pale.**
> **Blade and fang, death and flame,**
> **Qolzaz lives on in name.**

### Closing
*(Optional Spoken Eulogy)*
> *Strength need not be gentle to be good.*
> *And death, when faced with honor, need not be an end.*

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Origins of the Ballad:** Written to immortalize the transition of the Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk tribe from initial foes to steadfast allies of [[The Order of Znak]].
* **The Chest Tattoo:** Commemorates Gary's in-character decision to have Krageer tattoo Qolzaz's visage across his chest in accordance with Goliath warrior traditions honoring formidable adversaries.
