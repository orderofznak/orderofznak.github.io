# 🏘️ Phandalin

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A rugged frontier mining and farming settlement nestled in the foothills of the **Sword Mountains**, south of Neverwinter Wood along the Triboar Trail.
* **Frontier Hub:** Built atop the ruins of an older settlement sacked centuries ago by orc hordes, Phandalin served as the birthplace and Act 1 operational headquarters of **[[The Order of Znak]]** during *Dragon of Icespire Peak*.

---

## 👥 Key Townsfolk & Local Establishments
* **[[Stonehill Inn]]:** The central roadhouse and social hearth of Phandalin, operated by friendly innkeeper **[[Toblen Stonehill]]**.
* **Townmaster's Hall:** Seat of local governance where cowardly, paranoid Townmaster **[[Harbin Wester]]** ("everyone's favorite jerk") posted job notices from behind a locked door.
* **Barthen's Provisions:** The primary general store run by **[[Elmar Barthen]]** alongside his young clerks **[[Ander (Phandalin)|Ander]]** and **[[Thistle (clerk)|Thistle]]**.
* **Lionshield Coster:** Frontier trading post and armory managed by sharp-tongued master **[[Linene Graywind]]**.
* **Phandalin Miner's Exchange:** Guildhall and assay office overseen by calculating Zhentarim operative **[[Halia Thornton]]**.
* **Shrine of Luck:** Small stone shrine dedicated to Tymora, tended by scholarly Harper elf acolyte **[[Garaele|Sister Garaele]]**.

## ⚔️ Campaign Milestones & Party History
* **Founding of the Order & Town Tour (Level 1):** Arriving in Phandalin at Level 1, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], [[Rhynok]], [[Myagkiy Znak]], and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] toured the frontier town, met every local proprietor—from [[Toblen Stonehill]] at the [[Stonehill Inn]] to [[Elmar Barthen]], [[Ander (Phandalin)|Ander]], [[Thistle (clerk)|Thistle]], [[Linene Graywind]], [[Halia Thornton]], [[Garaele|Sister Garaele]], and Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]]—and took up the job board postings to protect the region from white dragon [[Cryovain]].
* **Loggers' Camp Resupply:** After escaping their underleveled first foray into the [[Woodland Manse]], the party returned to Phandalin to pick up an ox-drawn supply cart (pulled by **[[Vincent (ox)|Vincent]]**) from [[Elmar Barthen]] for delivery to Harbin's half-brother [[Tibor Wester]] at the [[Loggers' Camp]].
* **Silvering the Party's Arsenal:** Following the frustrating battle against immune wererats at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], the Order stopped in Phandalin before heading to [[Axeholm]]. Vowing **never to be powerless against his enemies again**, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** had his favored **Greataxe silvered**, while **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** silvered his ***Qiang* spear** and **[[Rhynok]]** silvered a **Shortsword**.
* **Evacuation of Falcon & The *Boots of Elvenkind*:** After discovering [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] ransacked by [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar, the party returned to Phandalin and reunited with evacuated hunter **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]**, who awarded [[Rhynok]] his coveted ***Boots of Elvenkind*** before the Order marched on the [[Circle of Thunder]].
* **Heroes of Phandalin & Sword Coast Renown (Level 7):** After slaying [[Cryovain]] atop [[Icespire Hold]], the Order of Znak returned to Phandalin as celebrated Level 7 heroes. Word of their deeds spread rapidly across the Sword Coast, reaching **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Dagult Neverember]]** in Neverwinter and the **Lords of Waterdeep**—leading directly to their commission to oversee the rebuilding of **[[Leilon]]** in Act 2.
