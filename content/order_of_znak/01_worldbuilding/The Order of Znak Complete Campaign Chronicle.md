# 🐉 The Order of Znak — Complete Campaign Chronicle

## 📜 The Legend of The Order of Znak
***The Order of Znak*** is an epic Sword Coast saga charting the rise of four legendary champions—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**. Beginning as humble sellswords uniting in the frontier town of [[Phandalin]] beneath the shadow of [[Cryovain]], the Order evolved into the chartered protectors of [[Leilon]], raising adopted heirs and repelling dragon cults, planar fiends, and elemental deities across an interconnected seven-part chronicle.

## 🗺️ Chapter Summaries

### 1. [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
Uniting in the frontier boomtown of [[Phandalin]], the four sellswords answered the townsfolk's pleas as the arrival of the white dragon **[[Cryovain]]** drove monsters and mountain orcs across the Sword Mountains and Neverwinter Wood. Across fifteen frontier expeditions—from driving the manticore from [[Umbrage Hill]], surviving the trapped [[Temple of Abbathor]], and slaying the mimic of [[Gnomengarde]], to staging [[Rhynok]]'s blood-note of Gruumsh at the [[Woodland Manse]], destroying [[Moesko]]'s heart at the [[Tower of Storms]], fighting alongside [[DonJon Raskin]] at the [[Shrine of Savras]] and [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], silvering their arsenal to cleanse [[Axeholm]], riding with centaur ally [[Xanth]] from [[Dragon Barrow]] to crush [[Yargath]] and [[Gorthok]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]], and finally stunning and slaying **[[Cryovain]]** atop the icy roof of **[[Icespire Hold]]**—the companions forged their legend across the Sword Coast.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]**

### 2. [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
Commissioned to protect the rebuilding settlement of **[[Leilon]]**, the Order arrived in the middle of a Kraken Priest raid led by [[Nixoxious]]—shattering the dark ritual and adopting orphaned ten-year-olds **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** into the Znak family under the care of their unflappable majordomo **[[Leopold]]**. While building their fortified manor with [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]] and befriending [[Merrygold Brightshine]] and mysterious curio dealer [[Aubrey Silverspun]], [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] courted half-orc cook **[[Cooragh]]** over her fiery pork pies at [[The Wayside Inn]], and the party turned a bloody clash with [[Matriarch Qolzaz]] into a lasting peace treaty with lizardfolk sovereign **[[Grixxugroaz]]** (immortalized in the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***).
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]**

### 3. [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
Venturing deep into a lightless subterranean cavern network between the opening storm raids and the escalating dragon cult war, the Order relied on [[Rhynok]]'s mastery of the dark to stalk shadow-warped horrors and vanquish a lethal **Shadow Dragon** in its subterranean lair.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]**

### 4. [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]
Seeking answers in Kryptgarden Forest, the Order infiltrated **Deeping Cave**—crossing boiling acid pools via a *Water Walk* relay (and Krageer's headlong barbarian charge) to parley with the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]**. After hunting down undead warlock **[[Viantha Cruelhex]]**, the champions witnessed the dracolich spirit of **[[Chardansearavitriol]]** (Ebondeath) possess Old Gnawbone, fought through spectral sword wraiths, extracted the dragon's poison-trapped hoard using Krageer's grit and [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s monastic immunity to poison, and bluffed **[[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]]** druids on their way out.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]**

### 5. [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
Answering the call of Forge Cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**, the Order hunted down a predatory Night Hag—rescuing young **[[Yasmina]]** and recovering Dayne's stolen +2 Plate Armor for [[Myagkiy Znak]]—before descending into a dormant volcano where Myagkiy cast *Enlarge* on [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] so the Goliath could wrestle the **Fire Giant Queen** amidst rivers of molten lava.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]**

### 6. [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
In the climactic war for [[Leilon]], [[Tvyordiy Znak]] contracted and embraced **wereboar lycanthropy** during the expedition to [[Dumathoin's Gulch]], the party slew Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers** atop the House of Thalivar to claim **[[The Ruinstone]]**, and Krageer's unlikely diplomacy aboard Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s drow caravels *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath]]* won Lord [[Dagult Neverember]]'s full military aid before the Order sank [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships at sea. After repelling [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s siege of Leilon (discovering [[Aubrey Silverspun]]'s true identity in the ancient Swords of Leilon portrait), the Order sailed *Twinkle* into a divine hurricane and boarded the Talosite flagship dreadnaught—defeating **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an Air Elemental Myrmidon, and a Nalfeshnee demon beneath the towering apparition of **[[Talos]]**.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]**

## 💡 Behind the Screen: Campaign Genesis, Mechanics & Table Trivia

### 🇷🇺 The workplace Connection & Linguistic Origin of "Znak"
The naming of the party and its core character concepts originated from real-world conversations at a former tech workplace, where Peter and Gary worked with Russian colleague **Grigory**. While exploring Russian Cyrillic language mechanics, Peter and Gary became intrigued by the linguistic concept of **Znak (Знак — sign/mark)**, specifically the alphabet's two silent modifying signs:
* **Твёрдый знак (Ъ — Tvyordiy Znak / The Hard Sign):** Signifying firmness, hardness, and unyielding impact. This became Peter's high-mobility, wereboar Aarakocra Monk: **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] (Ъ)** (Level 13 Drunken Master Monk; took +2 DEX ASI at Level 4; wielded a spear flavored as a ***Qiang*** as an homage to earlier D&D edition *Oriental Adventures* sourcebooks).
* **Мягкий знак (Ь — Myagkiy Znak / The Soft Sign):** Signifying softness, soothing, and life-preserving grace. This became Peter's fearless 24 AC battle cleric: **[[Myagkiy Znak]] (Ь)** (Level 13 Variant Human Life Domain Cleric; took +1 CON / +1 WIS ASI at Level 4).

Alongside Gary's two powerhouses—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** (Level 13 Goliath Bear Totem Barbarian / Champion Fighter; took +2 CON ASI at Level 4 and *Great Weapon Master*) and **[[Rhynok]]** (Level 13 Wood Elf Gloom Stalker Ranger / Assassin Rogue; took the *Sharpshooter* feat at Level 4)—the party took the name **The Order of Znak**, with their character sheet names proudly incorporating their Cyrillic symbols **(Ъ)** and **(Ь)**.

### 🎭 Dual-PC Co-Op & Alternating DM Screen
Rather than playing the expansion trilogy (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, *Divine Contention* — titled *"C'mon, Listen"* in D&D Beyond) as an uninterrupted block, Peter and Gary ran a 1-on-1 dual-character setup and alternated DM duties:
* **Peter** ran *Dragon of Icespire Peak* (Levels 1–7) and the three Leilon trilogy modules (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, and *Divine Contention*). Because Myagkiy and Tvyordiy functioned as DMPCs while Peter was behind the screen, Peter intentionally steered key story spotlights—such as touching the divinatory altar at the [[Shrine of Savras]] or choosing the *+1 Longbow* reward at the [[Tower of Storms]]—directly to Gary's characters.
* **Gary** interleaved custom homebrew dungeon crawls and boss encounters: Interlude 1 (*"Shadow's Run"*) between Acts 2 and 3, Interlude 2 (*"Keeping the Faith"*) between Acts 3 and 4, and the upcoming *Bridge Campaign*.

### ⚔️ DM Encounter Scaling, Custom Maps & Dice Highlights
Gary is an avid character optimizer who min-maxed Krageer and Rhynok for extreme DPR and survivability. Operating as DM required up-leveling published encounters, building custom battlemaps, and adding extra threats to maintain intense combat stakes:
* **Krageer's Double-Fail with *Danger Sense* (Act 1):** At the [[Temple of Abbathor]], right after unlocking *Danger Sense* at Level 2 (granting Advantage on Dexterity saves against traps), Krageer pried at the trapped statue gem and rolled **under 15 on both d20s** against the DC 15 save!
* **Custom Roll20 Map for [[Yargath]]'s Patrol (Act 1):** En route to the [[Circle of Thunder]], Peter built a custom Roll20 wilderness battlemap where the party and centaur [[Xanth]] ambushed [[Yargath]] and ten Talosian orcs.
* **Rooftop Ice Mephits with [[Cryovain]] (Act 1 Finale):** To elevate the final *Dragon of Icespire Peak* rooftop boss fight against a Level 6 party armed with a *Dragon Slayer* Longsword, Peter added a pack of **Ice Mephits** alongside [[Cryovain]], while Tvyordiy landed clutch *Stunning Strikes* on the dragon.
* **High-Tier Planar Reinforcements (Act 4):** In the *Divine Contention* finale, Peter reinforced [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] with an Air Elemental Myrmidon and a Nalfeshnee demon.

### 🍕 Table Running Gags & Homages
* **DonJon's Cajun Accent:** Voiced by Peter with an exaggerated Louisiana Cajun drawl, improvising endless monologues about wild youth escapades and grievances concerning his ex-wives.
* **The Wayside Inn Fiery Meat Pies & Tattoos:** Krageer's romance with half-orc head cook [[Cooragh]] Struckt was an ongoing comedy highlight driven by his obsession with her alarmingly well-spiced stews and special fiery pork pies. Whenever asked about the curved dagger tattoos beneath her ears, Cooragh would deadpan that she got them purely to spite her human mother from Luskan as youthful folly.
* **[[Ultima Exodus]] Greataxe:** Krageer's signature axe was named after *Ultima III: Exodus*, a shared inside joke between Peter and Gary as lifelong *Ultima* fans.
* **"Shadow's Run" Retro Homage:** Interlude 1 was named *"Shadow's Run"* by Gary as a deliberate nod to *[[Shadowrun]]*, the classic PC game Peter and Gary played together long ago.
* **Salvatore Drow Lore & The Twin Ships:** Peter's deep familiarity with R.A. Salvatore's 39 *Legend of Drizzt* audiobooks inspired the portrayal of Jarlaxle Baenre's Bregan D'aerthe privateer ships *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle and Icingdeath]]*. Rhynok pierced the illusory glamour cloaking Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s drow crew, forging a friendly surface alliance that culminated in Deloz boarding the dreadnaught alongside the Order.
* **Krageer as the Unlikely "Party Face":** Despite playing a Goliath barbarian with an abysmal Charisma of 6 (-2 penalty), Gary frequently volunteered Krageer to speak for the party during tense diplomatic negotiations. Krageer succeeded a surprising number of times against impossible odds—most famously when he swayed Lord Protector [[Dagult Neverember]] into sending full Neverwinter aid to Leilon after every other party member had failed.
* **The "Sacrificial Lamb" Hoard Breach:** During the Deeping Cave dungeon crawl in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, the party tackled the poison gas chamber trapped behind a *Wall of Force* by sending Krageer in first as the "sacrificial lamb" to soak the initial poison damage. Once Krageer scrambled out and was healed, Peter realized Tvyordiy's Level 10 Monk feature *Purity of Body* made him completely immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition, allowing Tvyordiy to casually stroll through the deadly fumes without taking a single point of damage to secure the dragon's entire hoard.
* **Krageer's Naval Archery Duel:** During the fleet battle against [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships, Krageer decided that if Rhynok could snipe, he could too—setting aside his legendary greataxe *Ultima Exodus* to enthusiastically trade arrow volleys across the waves using his own longbow.
* **The "Rope Trick" Extradimensional Sniper Pocket:** One of Gary's favorite tactical optimization tricks for Rhynok was casting *Rope Trick*, climbing up, pulling the rope into the extradimensional space, and raining down poisoned arrows from complete cover with absolute impunity.
* **Krageer's Only Unflinching Pain (Psychic Damage):** As a Bear Totem Barbarian, Krageer resisted all physical, elemental, necrotic, and radiant damage while raging. The Star Spawn Manglers at Thalivar's Beacon introduced the party to pure psychic damage—the single agony Krageer's barbarian constitution could not flinch off, leading to much roleplay grumbling about "brain pain."
* **Rhynok's Snake Venom Milking (Downtime):** During campaign downtime intervals, Gary would send Rhynok out into the wilderness specifically to hunt and milk giant poisonous snakes, ensuring his Poisoner feat was continuously supplied with lethal fresh toxins for every battle.
