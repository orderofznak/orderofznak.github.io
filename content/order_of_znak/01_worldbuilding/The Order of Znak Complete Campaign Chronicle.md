# 🐉 The Order of Znak — Complete Campaign Chronicle

## 📜 The Legend of The Order of Znak
***The Order of Znak*** is an epic Sword Coast saga charting the rise of four legendary champions—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**. Beginning as humble sellswords uniting in the frontier town of [[Phandalin]] beneath the shadow of [[Cryovain]], the Order evolved into the chartered protectors of [[Leilon]], raising adopted heirs and repelling dragon cults, planar fiends, and elemental deities across an interconnected seven-part chronicle.

## 🗺️ Chapter Summaries

### 1. [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
Uniting in the frontier boomtown of [[Phandalin]] after an early raid by the white dragon **[[Cryovain]]** claimed the life of [[Turkos Doomheart]], the four sellswords answered the townsfolk's pleas across the Sword Mountains and Neverwinter Wood. Beginning their saga on November 10, 2019 by delving into [[Gnomengarde]] to slay the barrel mimic alongside [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch|Dabbledop]] (using Gary's tactical 10-foot collapsible pole), the companions braved the acidic splitting jellies and trapped altar at the [[Temple of Abbathor]], drove the manticore from [[Umbrage Hill]], purged twelve orcs at [[Butterskull Ranch]] before looking up to witness Cryovain brutally shred hunting manticores mid-air, survived a harrowing retreat from the blights of [[Woodland Manse]] after Krageer's tattooed one-eyed goat ruse, rescued loggers from ankhegs, defended [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]], destroyed [[Moesko]]'s disembodied heart at the [[Tower of Storms]], hauled away the 2,500 gp gold bell from the [[Shrine of Savras]], learned the necessity of silvered weapons against wererats at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], rappelled down the soot-black chimney to purge [[Axeholm]], claimed the ancient *Dragon Slayer* blade at [[Dragon Barrow]], allied with centaur [[Xanth]] to smash [[Yargath]] and [[Gorthok]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]], and ultimately stunned and struck down **[[Cryovain]]** atop the frozen roof of **[[Icespire Hold]]**—forging their legend across the Sword Coast.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]**

### 2. [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
Commissioned to protect the rebuilding settlement of **[[Leilon]]**, the Order arrived in the middle of a Kraken Priest raid led by [[Nixoxious]]—shattering the dark ritual and adopting orphaned ten-year-olds **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** into the Znak family under the care of their unflappable majordomo **[[Leopold]]**. While building their fortified manor with [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]] and befriending [[Merrygold Brightshine]], nervous elven potion-seller [[Puck Caribdas]], and mysterious curio dealer [[Aubrey Silverspun]], [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] courted half-orc cook **[[Cooragh]]** over her fiery pork pies at [[The Wayside Inn]], and the party turned a bloody clash with [[Matriarch Qolzaz]] into a lasting peace treaty with lizardfolk sovereign **[[Grixxugroaz]]** (immortalized in the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***).
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]**

### 3. [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
With Gary stepping behind the DM screen for the first time, the Order answered an urgent commission from Waterdhavian noble Daniel Renault—brought by servants Liefold and Joffrey—following the murder of Renault's wife and the abduction of his young child **Heracio**. Traveling 55 miles north by carriage (crushing an owlbear road ambush) and trekking 15 miles into uncharted woods, the champions infiltrated a two-tiered cult redoubt. Delving through secret tunnels, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] claimed his signature *Vicious Quarterstaff* from a locked chest, and the party recovered four ritual stones (*Blood Stone*, *Nightshade Stone*, *Shadow Stone*, and *Moon Stone*) to open the sealed Dragon Door. In a harrowing subterranean climax, the Order destroyed the cult vanguard and vanquished a lethal **Shadow Dragon**—rescuing young Heracio, casting *Raise Dead*, and securing an adamantine chain shirt that Rhynok later reforged into his iconic adamantine net.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]**

### 4. [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]
Seeking answers in Kryptgarden Forest, the Order infiltrated **Deeping Cave**—crossing boiling acid pools via a *Water Walk* relay (and Krageer's headlong barbarian charge) to parley with the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]**. After hunting down undead warlock **[[Viantha Cruelhex]]**, the champions witnessed the dracolich spirit of **[[Chardansearavitriol]]** (Ebondeath) possess Old Gnawbone, fought through spectral sword wraiths, extracted the dragon's poison-trapped hoard using Krageer's grit and [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s monastic immunity to poison, and bluffed **[[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]]** druids on their way out.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]**

### 5. [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
Answering the call of Forge Cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**, the Order hunted down a predatory Night Hag—rescuing young **[[Yasmina]]** (who shortly afterward canonically drew expressive portraits of all four champions of the Order) and recovering Dayne's stolen +2 Plate Armor for [[Myagkiy Znak]]—before descending into a dormant volcano where [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] drank a ***Potion of Growth*** purchased from **[[Puck Caribdas]]** in [[Leilon]] **and** received ***Enlarge*** from [[Myagkiy Znak]] ("Double-Enlarge" to Huge size) so the Goliath could wrestle the **Fire Giant Queen** amidst rivers of molten lava.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chapter:** **[[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]**

### 6. [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
In the climactic war for [[Leilon]], [[Tvyordiy Znak]] contracted and embraced **wereboar lycanthropy** during the expedition to [[Dumathoin's Gulch]], the party slew Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers** atop the House of Thalivar to claim **[[The Ruinstone]]**, and Krageer's unlikely diplomacy aboard Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s drow caravels *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath]]* won Lord [[Dagult Neverember]]'s full military aid before the Order sank [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships at sea. After repelling [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s siege of Leilon (discovering [[Aubrey Silverspun]]'s true identity in the ancient Swords of Leilon portrait), the Order sailed *Twinkle* into a divine hurricane and boarded the Talosite flagship dreadnaught—defeating **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an Air Elemental Myrmidon, and a Nalfeshnee demon beneath the towering apparition of **[[Talos]]**.

## 💡 Behind the Screen: Campaign Genesis, Mechanics & Table Trivia

### 🇷🇺 The workplace Connection & Linguistic Origin of "Znak"
The naming of the party and its core character concepts originated from real-world conversations at a former tech workplace, where Peter and Gary worked with Russian colleague **Grigory**. While exploring Russian Cyrillic language mechanics, Peter and Gary became intrigued by the linguistic concept of **Znak (Знак — sign/mark)**, specifically the alphabet's two silent modifying signs:
* **Твёрдый знак (Ъ — Tvyordiy Znak / The Hard Sign):** Signifying firmness, hardness, and unyielding impact. This became Peter's high-mobility, wereboar Aarakocra Monk: **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] (Ъ)** (Level 13 Drunken Master Monk; took +2 DEX ASI at Level 4, +1 DEX / +1 WIS at Level 8; wielded a spear flavored as a ***Qiang*** as an homage to earlier D&D edition *Oriental Adventures* sourcebooks).
* **Мягкий знак (Ь — Myagkiy Znak / The Soft Sign):** Signifying softness, soothing, and life-preserving grace. This became Peter's fearless 24 AC battle cleric: **[[Myagkiy Znak]] (Ь)** (Level 13 Variant Human Life Domain Cleric; took +1 CON / +1 WIS ASI at Level 4, +2 WIS and *Blessed Strikes* at Level 8).

Alongside Gary's two powerhouses—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** (Level 13 Goliath Bear Totem Barbarian / Champion Fighter; took +2 CON ASI at Level 4 and *Great Weapon Master* at Level 8) and **[[Rhynok]]** (Level 13 Wood Elf Gloom Stalker Ranger / Assassin Rogue; raised by Uncle Christos with *Heart of Darkness*, took *Sharpshooter* and *Poisoner*)—the party took the name **The Order of Znak**, with their character sheet names proudly incorporating their Cyrillic symbols **(Ъ)** and **(Ь)**.

### 🏰 Aufox Manor & David
Just as Grigory inspired the party's Cyrillic name, the party's fortified estate and headquarters in [[Leilon]]—**Aufox Manor** and the diplomatic **Aufox-Annex**—was named as a direct tribute to **David**, Peter and Gary's coworker at work (whom Gary had also known prior to workplace).

### 🇫🇷 Table Pronunciation: "Lee-LON" (The Super-Nasal French Inflection)
While official Forgotten Realms guides suggest an Anglicized pronunciation ("LAY-lon" or "LEE-lon"), Peter (as DM) and Gary established an enduring, comedic running table tradition: pronouncing the town with an exaggerated French accent as **"Lee-LON"** (phonetically *[li.lɔ̃]*). The first syllable is a crisp, bright *"Lee"*, followed by an aggressively nasal, drawn-out *"-lon"* (reminiscent of the French nasal vowel in *bon* or *pardon*). Whenever the Order arrived from the wilderness, coordinated defense with Sergeant Hazz Yorrum, or checked up on Grizzelda's masonry progress at Aufox Manor, this signature pronunciation immediately signaled the comfort and camaraderie of returning to their adopted hometown.

### 🎙️ The Origin of "C'mon, Listen" (Campaign & Discord Namesake)
The overarching campaign title and the private Discord server name—**"C'mon, Listen"**—originated as an affectionate workplace running joke between Peter and Gary at work:
* **"C'mon, c'mon" (Rapid-Fire Persuasion):** Whenever trying to persuade each other on technical architectures, script designs, or work decisions, one would rapidly fire off, *"C'mon, c'mon!"* Over time, this rapid cadence bled into their daily banter, becoming the instinctual verbal preamble spoken before initiating almost any collaborative debate.
* **"Listen" (The Mock-Authoritative Reality Check):** The *"Listen"* half was invoked whenever jokingly calling the other out, playfully asserting subject-matter authority, or establishing undisputed technical ground—immortalized in classic table exchanges such as:
  > *"Listen, I've been scripting in bash for years, Gary. I think I know how a for loop works."*

Combined, the two verbal habits naturally coalesced into **"C'mon, Listen"**, serving as their official Discord server title, their D&D Beyond campaign header, and the humorous banner under which the four champions of Znak conquered the Sword Coast.

### 🎭 Dual-PC Co-Op & Alternating DM Screen
Rather than playing the expansion trilogy (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, *Divine Contention* — titled *"C'mon, Listen"* in D&D Beyond) as an uninterrupted block, Peter and Gary ran a 1-on-1 dual-character setup and alternated DM duties:
* **Peter** ran *Dragon of Icespire Peak* (Levels 1–7) and the three Leilon trilogy modules (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, and *Divine Contention*). Because Myagkiy and Tvyordiy functioned as DMPCs while Peter was behind the screen, Peter intentionally steered key story spotlights—such as touching the divinatory altar at the [[Shrine of Savras]] or choosing the *+1 Longbow* reward at the [[Tower of Storms]]—directly to Gary's characters.
* **Gary** interleaved custom homebrew dungeon crawls and boss encounters: Interlude 1 (*"Shadow's Run"*) between Acts 2 and 3, Interlude 2 (*"Keeping the Faith"*) between Acts 3 and 4, and the upcoming *Bridge Campaign*.
* **Interlude Level Freeze & Pacing Discipline:** To preserve encounter balance and keep party levels strictly aligned with published Wizards of the Coast module math (Levels 7–8 for *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Levels 9–11 for *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, Levels 11–13 for *Divine Contention*), Peter did not allow level ups during Gary's homebrew Interludes (*"Shadow's Run"* and *"Keeping the Faith"*). The party faced high-stakes encounters—such as the Shadow Dragon and Fire Giant Queen—at their existing levels, keeping milestone progression tethered to the main storyline acts.
* **Krageer's Fighter Multiclass Timing:** Analysis of the combat logs confirms that throughout *Storm Lord's Wrath* (Act 2), Krageer fought as a pure Barbarian (reaching Level 8 with *Great Weapon Master*). He did not utilize any Fighter features (*Second Wind*, *Great Weapon Fighting* rerolls, or *Action Surge*) in Act 2; his Champion Fighter multiclass debuted during the subsequent trials of *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* (Act 3).

### ⚔️ DM Encounter Scaling, Custom Maps & Dice Highlights
Gary is an avid character optimizer who min-maxed Krageer and Rhynok for extreme DPR and survivability. Operating as DM required up-leveling published encounters, building custom battlemaps, and adding extra threats to maintain intense combat stakes:
* **The Early VTT Genesis (Theatre of the Mind &rarr; Tactical Roll20):** During the opening sessions in early November 2019 (such as the Gnomengarde kickoff), the campaign operated as a hybrid of **theatre of the mind**, map screen-sharing from D&D Beyond and early Roll20 captures, and digital dice rolls in D&D Beyond called out verbally over voice chat. The sporadic rolls in the Roll20 logs between November 10 and 13 (Goblins, Orcs, Manticores, Ochre Jellies) were Peter's sandbox test runs while mastering Roll20's macros and character sheet templates before formally transitioning to full tactical on-grid VTT combat for the *Dwarven Excavation* on November 16–17.
* **Krageer's Double-Fail with *Danger Sense* (Act 1):** At the [[Temple of Abbathor]], right after unlocking *Danger Sense* at Level 2 (granting Advantage on Dexterity saves against traps), Krageer pried at the trapped statue gem and rolled **under 15 on both d20s** against the DC 15 save!
* **Custom Roll20 Map for [[Yargath]]'s Patrol (Act 1):** En route to the [[Circle of Thunder]], Peter built a custom Roll20 wilderness battlemap where the party and centaur [[Xanth]] ambushed [[Yargath]] and ten Talosian orcs.
* **Rooftop Ice Mephits with [[Cryovain]] (Act 1 Finale):** To elevate the final *Dragon of Icespire Peak* rooftop boss fight against a Level 6 party armed with a *Dragon Slayer* Longsword, Peter added a pack of **Ice Mephits** alongside [[Cryovain]], while Tvyordiy landed clutch *Stunning Strikes* on the dragon.
* **The Stableyard Rain Debate (Act 2):** During the August 3 assault on the Wayside Inn in *Storm Lord's Wrath*, the players realized the tactical grid featured a stableyard with no ceiling, sparking the hilarious table banter: *"It's raining inside?" / "We are in a stableyard!" / "With no roof?!" / "There was no roof, we could have climbed it!"*
* **The "Joke-Jab" That Became Canon ([[Matriarch Qolzaz]]):** Peter's July 23, 2020 Discord tease (*"Krageer murdered an elderly lizardwoman ... to keep his rage"*) started as table ribbing over barbarian rage timers before the pair transformed it into Krageer's defining lore anchor, his chest tattoo, and the *Ballad of Qolzaz*.
* **Rhynok's Net Obsession:** Rhynok's successful netting of Pinchwit's giant goats and his first-round netting of Master Thief Velleen Firecrow in Phandalin (completely foiling Peter's planned sneak-attack backstabs) turned nets into Gary's signature crowd-control weapon, culminating in a future adamantine-threaded net.
* **Myagkiy's Permanent *Banishment* Lock:** In the battle against Captain Kristoffen on August 10, Myagkiy's double-*Banishment* of the Air Elementals was held unbroken for the full spell duration, turning a temporary exile into permanent planar banishment.
* **The "Rule of Cool" Double-Enlarge (`Medium` &rarr; `Large` &rarr; `Huge` in *"Keeping the Faith"*):** Because a single *Enlarge* effect only bumps a Medium Goliath to Large whereas the **Fire Giant Queen** was **Huge**, the table invoked the **Rule of Cool**: Krageer drank a ***Potion of Growth*** bought from **[[Puck Caribdas]]** in [[Leilon]] (`Medium` &rarr; `Large`) **and** **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** cast ***Enlarge*** on him (`Large` &rarr; `Huge`), stacking both transmutations so Krageer could grapple the Huge Fire Giant Queen eye-to-eye in the volcano!
* **High-Tier Planar Reinforcements (Act 4):** In the *Divine Contention* finale, Peter reinforced [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] with an Air Elemental Myrmidon and a Nalfeshnee demon.

### 🍕 Table Running Gags & Homages
* **DonJon's Cajun Accent:** Voiced by Peter with an exaggerated Louisiana Cajun drawl, improvising endless monologues about wild youth escapades and grievances concerning his ex-wives.
* **The Wayside Inn Fiery Meat Pies & Tattoos:** Krageer's romance with half-orc head cook [[Cooragh]] Struckt was an ongoing comedy highlight driven by his obsession with her alarmingly well-spiced stews and special fiery pork pies. Whenever asked about the curved dagger tattoos beneath her ears, Cooragh would deadpan that she got them purely to spite her human mother from Luskan as youthful folly.
* **Puck's *Potion of Growth* & Krageer's Punny Label Endorsements:** After buying a *Potion of Growth* from nervous elven Tymoran acolyte **[[Puck Caribdas]]** in [[Leilon]] and using it in the volcano showdown of *"Keeping the Faith"*, Peter pitched two punny endorsement quotes (right up Krageer's alley) for Krageer to give Puck for his bottle labels:
  * *"I am a huge fan of Puck's Potion of Growth."* — **Krageer**
  * *"I didn't care for the taste at first, but I grew to like it."* — **Also Krageer**
* **[[Ultima Exodus]] Greataxe:** Krageer's signature axe was named after *Ultima III: Exodus*, a shared inside joke between Peter and Gary as lifelong *Ultima* fans.
* **"Shadow's Run" Retro Homage:** Interlude 1 was named *"Shadow's Run"* by Gary as a deliberate nod to *[[Shadowrun]]*, the classic PC game Peter and Gary played together long ago.
* **Salvatore Drow Lore & The Twin Ships:** Peter's deep familiarity with R.A. Salvatore's 39 *Legend of Drizzt* audiobooks inspired the portrayal of Jarlaxle Baenre's Bregan D'aerthe privateer ships *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle and Icingdeath]]*. Rhynok pierced the illusory glamour cloaking Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s drow crew, forging a friendly surface alliance that culminated in Deloz boarding the dreadnaught alongside the Order.
* **Krageer as the Unlikely "Party Face":** Despite playing a Goliath barbarian with an abysmal Charisma of 6 (-2 penalty), Gary frequently volunteered Krageer to speak for the party during tense diplomatic negotiations. Krageer succeeded a surprising number of times against impossible odds—most famously when he swayed Lord Protector [[Dagult Neverember]] into sending full Neverwinter aid to Leilon after every other party member had failed.
* **The "Sacrificial Lamb" Hoard Breach:** During the Deeping Cave dungeon crawl in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, the party tackled the poison gas chamber trapped behind a *Wall of Force* by sending Krageer in first as the "sacrificial lamb" to soak the initial poison damage. Once Krageer scrambled out and was healed, Peter realized Tvyordiy's Level 10 Monk feature *Purity of Body* made him completely immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition, allowing Tvyordiy to casually stroll through the deadly fumes without taking a single point of damage to secure the dragon's entire hoard.
* **Krageer's Naval Archery Duel:** During the fleet battle against [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships, Krageer decided that if Rhynok could snipe, he could too—setting aside his legendary greataxe *Ultima Exodus* to enthusiastically trade arrow volleys across the waves using his own longbow.
* **The "Rope Trick" Extradimensional Sniper Pocket:** One of Gary's favorite tactical optimization tricks for Rhynok was casting *Rope Trick*, climbing up, pulling the rope into the extradimensional space, and raining down poisoned arrows from complete cover with absolute impunity.
* **Krageer's Only Unflinching Pain (Psychic Damage):** As a Bear Totem Barbarian, Krageer resisted all physical, elemental, necrotic, and radiant damage while raging. The Star Spawn Manglers at Thalivar's Beacon introduced the party to pure psychic damage—the single agony Krageer's barbarian constitution could not flinch off, leading to much roleplay grumbling about "brain pain."
* **Rhynok's Snake Venom Milking (Downtime):** During campaign downtime intervals, Gary would send Rhynok out into the wilderness specifically to hunt and milk giant poisonous snakes, ensuring his Poisoner feat was continuously supplied with lethal fresh toxins for every battle.
