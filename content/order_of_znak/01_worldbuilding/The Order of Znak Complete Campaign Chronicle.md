# 🐉 The Order of Znak — Complete Campaign Chronicle

## 📜 The Legend of The Order of Znak
***The Order of Znak*** is an epic D&D 5e campaign saga charting the rise of four legendary champions across the Sword Coast. Beginning as humble sellswords uniting in the frontier town of [[Phandalin]], the Order evolved into the saviors of [[Leilon]], repelling dragon cults, planar fiends, and elemental deities across an interconnected grand campaign.

## 📌 Campaign Phase Details

### 1. Act 1: *Dragon of Icespire Peak*
* **[[Phandalin]] Arrival, Town Tour & Starter Loadouts:** Arriving in the frontier settlement of [[Phandalin]] as untested sellswords, the four companions toured the town and met its colorful residents: [[Toblen Stonehill]] at the [[Stonehill Inn]], [[Elmar Barthen]] and his clerks [[Ander (Phandalin)|Ander]] and [[Thistle (clerk)|Thistle]], [[Linene Graywind]], [[Halia Thornton]], [[Garaele|Sister Garaele]], and everyone's favorite coward, Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]]. In those early days, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] carried a simple **Greataxe** and **Javelins**; [[Rhynok]] carried a mundane **Longbow** and **Warhammer**; [[Myagkiy Znak]]—already a devoted **Life Domain** priest—carried a **Mace** and **Shield**; and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] wielded an Eastern-style ***Qiang* spear**.
* **Quest 1 — [[Umbrage Hill]]:** Dispatched to [[Umbrage Hill]], the party fought and **drove off the Manticore** besieging midwife [[Adabra Gwynn]]'s stone windmill. Though unable to convince the stubborn acolyte to abandon her home for Phandalin, they earned her gratitude and a **Potion of Healing**. Hardened by their first frontier clash, Krageer began fighting with reckless abandon while honing an uncanny sixth sense for danger, and Rhynok awakened the primal magic of the wilds to conjure nourishing berries and volleys of thorns. *(Later, prior to the [[Woodland Manse]], the manticore and its mate returned to Umbrage Hill and were slain by [[Cryovain]] in a vicious aerial clash.)*
* **Quest 2 — [[Temple of Abbathor|Dwarven Excavation]] & The Gem Trap:** Aiding shield dwarf prospectors [[Dazlyn Grayshard]] and [[Norbus Ironrune]], the party slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** haunting the ruined temple, uncovered its secret doors, and excavated the rubble-choked tunnel to the inner **Statue of Abbathor**. When Krageer pried at the statue's glowing gem, even his newfound instinct for danger could not save him from taking the full force of the exploding trap. Though the dwarves refused to part with extra coin, the party claimed their pair of ***Sending Stones*** and slaughtered a pack of marauding **Orcs**—Rhynok's sworn quarry—waiting in ambush outside the canyon. Emerging from the ruins, each champion stepped into their signature discipline: Krageer bonded with the primal spirit of the **Bear Totem**, Rhynok embraced the dark shadows of the wilderness as a **Gloom Stalker**, Tvyordiy mastered the swaying, unpredictable footwork of the **Drunken Master**, and Myagkiy deepened his calling as a healer of the **Life Domain**.
* **Quest 3 — [[Gnomengarde]] & The Barrel Room Mimic:** Exploring the rock gnome caverns of [[Gnomengarde]] and meeting all twenty-one inhabitants, the party grew understandably wary after manic inventor [[Facktoré]] opened fire with her autoloading crossbow contraption and shot [[Rhynok]] (and Krageer roughed up sentries [[Pog]] and [[Ulla]] on the way to chief inventors [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]]). Hunting down and slaying the shapeshifting **Mimic in the barrel room** to free [[Gnerkli|King Gnerkli]] and [[Korboz|King Korboz]], the party was rewarded with the ***Clockwork Amulet***, ***Pole of Collapsing***, ***Hat of Wizardry***, and ***Wand of Pyrotechnics***.
* **Quest 4 — [[Butterskull Ranch]] & The Outhouse Ambush:** Riding along the Triboar Trail, the champions ambushed a marauding orc at the ranch **outhouse**, cleared the burning farmhouse of its orc invaders, and rescued retired sheriff **[[Alfonse Kalazorn|"Big Al" Kalazorn]]** and his prize cow **[[Petunia (cow)|Petunia]]**.
* **Quest 5 — First Expedition to [[Woodland Manse]] (Gruumsh Blood-Ruse & Escape):** Venturing into the [[Woodland Manse]] before they were truly ready for its numbers, [[Rhynok]] used his deep knowledge of orcish superstition to slay a wild animal outside and scrawl a warning in blood proclaiming that **Gruumsh** was angry—terrifying many of the stationed orcs into fleeing the estate! Inside the courtyard, however, [[Grannoc]], the **Gulthias Tree** blights, wild boars, and anchorite *Lightning Bolts* overwhelmed the party. [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] fell unconscious **twice** during the fighting withdrawal—patched up on the field the first time, then felled at the edge of the woods by a parting volley of needle blight spines, before [[Tvyordiy Znak]] swooped back from his **flight** (Aarakocra pun intended) and hauled the unconscious Goliath to safety.
* **Quest 6 & 7 — [[Loggers' Camp]] & [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]:** Picking up the ox-drawn supply wagon (pulled by [[Vincent (ox)|Vincent]]) in Phandalin, the party followed Rhynok's keen woodcraft through Neverwinter Wood—spotting a shapeshifted anchorite boar fleeing back toward the Manse—and slew the burrowing **Ankhegs** erupting around [[Tibor Wester]]'s office at the [[Loggers' Camp]]. Continuing to [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]], the champions joined master hunter **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** in a brief skirmish to drive off [[Cryovain]], met [[Corwin]], [[Pell]], and Falcon's gray stallion [[Baatorius]], and earned Falcon's admiration for their earlier blood-ruse at the Woodland Manse—with Rhynok setting his sights on Falcon's prized ***Boots of Elvenkind***. *(Unbeknownst to the party, shortly after they departed the lodge following their failed Manse foray, the vengeful [[Anchorites of Talos]] and [[Gorthok]] stormed and ransacked Falcon's compound, forcing Falcon and his household to flee to [[Phandalin]].)*
* **Quest 8 — [[Tower of Storms]] & The Crab's Enchanted Longbow:** Befriending an **awakened giant crab** on the coastal shingle below the cliffs, the party entered the sea cave and pledged to help the sea elf banshee **[[Miraal]]**. Though the haunting song of the cliffside **Harpies** momentarily entranced several companions on the ascent, the champions broke the spell, slew the harpies, recognized the desecrated altar of **Talos** through Myagkiy's clerical lore, killed the undead anchorite **[[Moesko]]** before destroying his still-beating heart atop the beacon tower, and returned the opalescent conch to Miraal. True to its word, the giant crab retrieved a sunken magical **+1 Longbow** from the offshore wreck for **[[Rhynok]]**—and with their mission complete, the party left the sunken hulks and the reef shark [[Daggermaw]] undisturbed.
* **Quest 9 & 10 — [[Shrine of Savras]] with [[DonJon Raskin]] & First Foray to [[Dragon Barrow]]:** Joining forces with the boisterous, Cajun-drawling mine overseer **[[DonJon Raskin]]**, the party fought a fierce, harrowing battle against the **Orcs and Ogres** occupying the [[Shrine of Savras]]. Laying hands upon the uncovered sanctuary altar, Rhynok and Krageer received a divinatory vision of [[Cryovain]] slumbering atop [[Icespire Hold]], while Krageer and Tvyordiy scaled the ruined belfry to pry loose the shrine's **2,500 gp solid gold bell**. An initial foray into the [[Dragon Barrow]] followed—clashing with lurking Will-o'-Wisps and an Invisible Stalker—before the party withdrew to regroup.
* **Seasoned Champions & Quest 11 — [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]:** Tempered by their trials across the frontier, the four champions grew deadlier still: Krageer's mountain-born constitution became even more rugged and unyielding; Myagkiy deepened both his physical fortitude and spiritual wisdom, calling down swirling ***Spirit Guardians*** and ***Spiritual Weapon*** to devastating effect in crowded melees; Tvyordiy grew ever more nimble and dexterous as his martial arts flowed into rapid multi-strike combinations; and Rhynok honed his bowmanship to lethal, deadeye sharpshooting precision. Escorting [[DonJon Raskin]] to [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], the party saw straight through wererat boss **[[Zeleen Varnaster]]**'s pose as mine overseer—yet when battle broke out, both Tvyordiy and Krageer discovered their mundane weapons could not scratch the immune **wererats**. Instead, the entire battle was carried on the backs of **Rhynok's enchanted Longbow** and **Myagkiy's *Spirit Guardians*, *Spiritual Weapon*, and *Sacred Flame***!
* **Quest 12 — Silvered Arsenal & Clearing [[Axeholm]]:** Vowing never again to stand powerless before a foe, Krageer had his **Greataxe silvered** in [[Phandalin]] (while Tvyordiy silvered his ***Qiang*** and Rhynok silvered a **Shortsword**). Scaling the mountain above [[Axeholm]] and rappelling cleanly down the fortress chimney under Myagkiy's prayerful *Guidance*, the party cut down the Giant Spiders, Ghouls, and Stirges across the West Hall, Smithy, East Hall, and Ghouls' Den. Ascending to the Haunted Hall, they confronted the moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**—every champion withstanding her deathly *Wail* unflinchingly—and banished her spirit with magic and silvered steel. In the Castellan's Bedchamber, after clearing out more stirges, Myagkiy's keen eye spotted the hidden vault inside the fireplace—yielding the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power*** for Myagkiy and the crimson-eyed ***Dread Helm*** for Krageer—before the party put down the undead **Castellan ghoul**.
* **Quest 13 & 14 — Return to [[Dragon Barrow]], [[Circle of Thunder]] & [[Woodland Manse]] Vengeance:** Returning to the [[Dragon Barrow]] to finish what they had started, the party slew the remaining Will-o'-Wisp in the Adventurers' Sepulcher and overcame the Invisible Stalker so Krageer could claim Lady Tanamere Alagondar's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, enlisting the noble centaur **[[Xanth]]** to ride to war at their side. Back in [[Phandalin]], the party encountered the evacuated **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]**, learned that his hunting lodge had been ransacked by a giant boar and Talosian orcs right after their first Woodland Manse retreat, and received Rhynok's promised ***Boots of Elvenkind***. Marching into Neverwinter Wood with Xanth, the companions intercepted and slaughtered **[[Yargath]]** and her patrol of **ten Orcs** in the forest, then stormed the hilltop [[Circle of Thunder]]. Amidst booming thunder, anchorite *Lightning Bolts*, twig blights, and constricting vine blights, Xanth's charging hooves, Krageer's axe, Rhynok's bow, Tvyordiy's stunning blows, and Myagkiy's *Spirit Guardians* felled **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, **[[Flenz]]**, and **[[Narux]]**—with Myagkiy claiming the ***+1 Shield*** from the caves. Stopping over at the tusk-shattered wreckage of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] along the river trail, the party marched straight back to the [[Woodland Manse]], where their newfound power and centaur ally turned the tables completely—wiping out [[Grannoc]], the blight hordes, and the stirges.
* **Quest 15 — [[Icespire Hold]] & Slaying [[Cryovain]]:** Fighting through a **Hobgoblin** warband in the mountain wilderness and cutting down the opportunistic **[[Stone-Cold Reavers]]** and stirges inside [[Icespire Hold]] with their silvered blades, the party ascended to the icy roof to face **[[Cryovain]]** and a pack of **Ice Mephits**. While Myagkiy invoked *Bless* upon the party and swatted Ice Mephits from the air with his *Spiritual Weapon*, the heroes dodged blasts of freezing breath. **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] delivered a series of clutch *Stunning Strikes* that locked the dragon in place**, enabling **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** to unleash devastating blows with his ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** while **[[Rhynok]]** hammered the wyrm with deadeye arrows. Slaying Cryovain atop the peak, the Order returned to [[Phandalin]] as celebrated heroes, their renown spreading all the way to **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Dagult Neverember]]** in Neverwinter and the **Lords of Waterdeep**.

### 2. Act 2: *Storm Lord's Wrath* (*"C'mon, Listen"* Part 1)
* **Defense of [[Leilon]] & The Kraken Raid:** Arrived in [[Leilon]] during an assault from the sea (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), where the tiefling Kraken Priest **[[Nixoxious]]** and her cultist berserkers stormed the town fishery, taking local fishers captive for sacrifice to summon water elementals and water weirds. In the panic, two 10-year-old village children and best friends, **Smithwell** (boy) and **Burnice** (girl), went missing in the marshlands.
* **Rescue & Adoption of the Znak Wards:** The Order shattered the cult's dark summoning ritual and safely rescued [[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]], but discovered their fisher parents had tragically been sacrificed. Left orphaned and without family, the children were adopted into the Order by the champions as brother and sister, given the surname **Znak**, and raised at the party's new [[Leilon]] headquarters.
* **Establishing Headquarters under Majordomo [[Leopold]]:** To maintain the fortified manor and care for the children while campaigning, the Order appointed the unflappable human majordomo **[[Leopold]]**. Leopold managed estate logistics, trade manifests, armory maintenance, and daily schooling for the young wards. Construction of the estate was coordinated with Neverember's gruff dwarf master builder **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]** ("The Growler").
* **Leilon Civic, Arcane & Garrison Life:** While halfling priestess **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]** nurtured the spiritual hearth at the Shrine of Lathander and fed Grizzelda's workers, official garrison sergeant **[[Hazz Yorrum]]** proved lazy, corrupt, and cowardly, deferring nearly all town decisions to the short-tempered Rashemi wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** in the House of Thalivar (who was secretly possessed each night by the ghost of [[Thalivar]]). Meanwhile, the heroes frequented **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]**'s curio shop, unaware the eccentric merchant was an ancient spectral champion.
* **Wayside Inn Hearth & Trade Pact:** Along the High Road, the Order allied with former bandit captain **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]** for mutual defense and farm trade. In the kitchen, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] formed an enduring romance with Martisha's confidante, head cook **[[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]]**, whose alarmingly spicy pork pies completely stole the Goliath's heart.
* **Lizardfolk Alliance & Qolzaz Tribute:** Clashed with the Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk in the Missing Patrol questline and slew Matriarch **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]**. After helping vanquish invading rot trolls in a desperate joint defense, tribal chieftain **[[Grixxugroaz]]** ratified an enduring peace treaty. Krageer tattooed Qolzaz on his chest (immortalized in ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***), and Grixxugroaz dispatched exchange envoys **[[Graxxoamo]]** and **[[Shriss]]** to train and live with the Order in Leilon.

### 3. Interlude 1: *"Shadow's Run"* — Shadow Dragon Dungeon Crawl
* **Chronological Placement:** Occurred between *Storm Lord's Wrath* and *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*.
* **Cavern Dungeon Crawl:** Plunging into a deep subterranean cavern network, the Order battled waves of shadow-warped creatures and confronted a deadly **Shadow Dragon** in its lightless lair.

### 4. Act 3: *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* (*"C'mon, Listen"* Part 2)
* **Sword Coast Conflict:** Escalating multi-front warfare around Leilon confronting dragon cults, undead incursions, and primordial forces.
* **Infiltration of Deeping Cave (Claugiyliamatar's Lair):** Venturing deep into Kryptgarden Forest to seek an audience with the ancient green dragon [[Claugiyliamatar]] ("Old Gnawbone"), the Order infiltrated via the perilous northern cave entrance, fighting their way through grasping assassin vines and teleporting phase spiders.
* **The Caustic Acid Crossing & Krageer's Rage:** Encountering massive subterranean acid pools blocking their advance, the party devised an ingenious crossing relay: [[Rhynok]] provided his *Ring of Water Walking*, while [[Myagkiy Znak]] cast the *Water Walk* spell, ferrying the magic ring back and forth across the burning caustic pools to guide each hero safely over. Impatient and headstrong, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] charged straight across the boiling acid in a Barbarian rage, requiring extensive post-crossing healing from Myagkiy once safely on the other side.
* **Audience with Old Gnawbone & The Hunt for [[Viantha Cruelhex]]:** Reaching the green dragon's inner lair, Claugiyliamatar demanded a tribute of service, tasking the Order with eliminating [[Viantha Cruelhex]], an insidious **Deathlock Mastermind** (undead warlock) operating in Kryptgarden Forest. Tracking her to a clearing filled with a rank stench and crackling chanting, the Order ambushed Viantha as she waved a bone wand to summon shrieking **Specters**, swiftly dispatching the undead warlock and securing her corpse.
* **Ebondeath's Possession & The Sword Wraith Ambush:** Returning to Deeping Cave with Viantha's corpse, the encounter escalated into catastrophe when the necrotic spirit of the dracolich [[Chardansearavitriol]] (Ebondeath) possessed Claugiyliamatar before the party's eyes. As the green wyrm writhed under foul necrotic corruption, spectral sword wraiths materialized throughout the cavern, forcing the Order to battle through waves of undead warriors.
* **The Poisoned Hoard & Monastic Immunity:** Discovering the dragon's treasure hoard trapped behind a *Wall of Force* and choked with lethal green dragon poison gas, the party relied on extreme frontline tactics. Krageer served as the initial "sacrificial lamb," braving the deadly toxic fumes to absorb the brunt of the hazard. Once Krageer fell back to safety, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] drew upon his monastic mastery of body and spirit—rendering him completely impervious to poison—to stride fearlessly into the lethal cloud, safely extracting the dragon's entire hoard unharmed.
* **Bluffing [[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]] Circle (Act 3 Finale):** Laden with dragon treasure on their exit through Deeping Cave, the Order was confronted by the inner circle of **[[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]]** druids—leader [[Argina Therrow]], alchemist [[Delis Venomcauldron]], beastwarden [[Sajia Hurwi|Saija Hurwi]], and draconic scion [[Zuri Kimyak]]. Rather than engaging in another exhausting skirmish, the champions bluffed the fanatical cultists into believing they were authorized emissaries of Claugiyliamatar, walking out of the Kryptgarden caverns completely unmolested to conclude Act 3.

### 5. Interlude 2: *"Keeping the Faith"*
* **Chronological Placement:** Occurred between *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* and *Divine Contention*.
* **Dayne's Armor & Hag Rescue:** Recovered stolen enchanted Plate Armor for Forge Domain Cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**; defeated a predatory Night Hag and rescued young **[[Yasmina]]**.
* **Volcano Climax Boss Battle:** The epic culmination of the adventure—[[Myagkiy Znak]] cast *Enlarge* on [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], allowing Krageer to wrestle the **Fire Giant Queen** in molten grapple combat inside a dormant volcano!

### 6. Act 4: *Divine Contention* (*"C'mon, Listen"* Finale)
* **Quest 1: The Perils of [[Dumathoin's Gulch]] (Opening Quest):** Hastening directly into the Sword Mountains to secure Gnomengarde's anti-siege munitions against the newly possessed green dragon, the Order walked into an ambush of feral wereboars outside the gulch. [[Tvyordiy Znak]] was knocked prone and bitten by a wereboar, contracting wereboar lycanthropy—embracing the curse's feral brawn and hybrid tusks. When sword wraiths swarmed the canyon, [[Rhynok]] conjured a ***Rope Trick***, pulled up the rope, and sniped with total cover from his extradimensional pocket. Rhynok then persuaded the dwarven eidolon [[Sister Silvergranite]] to yield the volatile **Gnomengarde grenade**, before the party brought down an allied sword wraith and a lethal **Boneclaw**.
* **Quest 2: Thalivar's Beacon & [[The Ruinstone]]:** Traveling directly from the gulch to Thalivar's Beacon atop the House of Thalivar in Leilon, the Order confronted a planar breach of Far Realm horrors: **Star Spawn Manglers**. In the brutal close-quarters struggle, Krageer discovered that their psychic assault was the single pain his Bear Totem rage could not shrug off, yet the Goliath persevered to help slaughter the aberrations. The champions claimed the legendary Netherese relic, **[[The Ruinstone]]**, while a completely exhausted, shell-shocked wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** resigned his post and fled permanently to Neverwinter.
* **Quest 3: High Seas Voyage to Neverwinter & Naval Siege Battles:** The Order embarked upon Bregan D'aerthe's privateer vessels *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]* and *Icingdeath* to petition Lord Protector [[Dagult Neverember]] for military reinforcements. Krageer famously stepped forward as the unlikely diplomat of the company, swaying the Lord Protector to grant full aid. On the return voyage, the ships repelled an ambush of Invisible Stalkers at sea, followed by a pitched naval fleet battle against [[Galas Windrage]]'s warship (*Throatcutter*) and longship (*Thunderstrike*). Trading ballista and mangonel broadsides, Rhynok sniped from the deck while Krageer drew his own bow to trade arrow volleys across the waves, annihilating the reavers and returning victorious to Leilon.
* **Quest 4: The Dragon Siege & The Swords of Leilon Portrait:** During the climactic siege of Leilon in *Divine Contention*, the green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** assaulted the settlement, destroying Aubrey's Peculiarities. Following the defeat of the dragon, Aubrey was revealed to have been a lingering spirit the entire time whom only the four champions could perceive; after the destruction of his shop, an ancient painting depicting the historic **Swords of Leilon** showed [[Aubrey Silverspun]] in the portrait.
* **Quest 5: Tempest Dreadnaught Climax & *Twinkle* Assault:** The Order called upon Captain [[Deloz Montiv]] aboard *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]* to navigate the hurricane and hunt down the Talosite fleet. In an epic flagship sea battle beneath an apparition of **Talos**, the Order and Captain Deloz boarded and scoured the dreadnaught, defeating high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an **Air Elemental Myrmidon**, and a **Nalfeshnee demon**.

## 💡 Behind the Screen: Campaign Genesis, Mechanics & Table Trivia

### 🇷🇺 The workplace Connection & Linguistic Origin of "Znak"
The naming of the party and its core character concepts originated from real-world conversations at a former tech workplace, where Peter and Gary worked with Russian colleague **Grigory**. While exploring Russian Cyrillic language mechanics, Peter and Gary became intrigued by the linguistic concept of **Znak (Знак — sign/mark)**, specifically the alphabet's two silent modifying signs:
* **Твёрдый знак (Ъ — Tvyordiy Znak / The Hard Sign):** Signifying firmness, hardness, and unyielding impact. This became Peter's high-mobility, wereboar Aarakocra Monk: **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] (Ъ)** (Level 13 Drunken Master Monk; took +2 DEX ASI at Level 4; wielded a spear flavored as a ***Qiang*** as an homage to earlier D&D edition *Oriental Adventures* sourcebooks).
* **Мягкий знак (Ь — Myagkiy Znak / The Soft Sign):** Signifying softness, soothing, and life-preserving grace. This became Peter's fearless 24 AC battle cleric: **[[Myagkiy Znak]] (Ь)** (Level 13 Variant Human Life Domain Cleric; took +1 CON / +1 WIS ASI at Level 4).

Alongside Gary's two powerhouses—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** (Level 13 Goliath Bear Totem Barbarian / Champion Fighter; took +2 CON ASI at Level 4 and *Great Weapon Master*) and **[[Rhynok]]** (Level 13 Wood Elf Gloom Stalker Ranger / Assassin Rogue; took the *Sharpshooter* feat at Level 4)—the party took the name **The Order of Znak**, with their character sheet names proudly incorporating their Cyrillic symbols **(Ъ)** and **(Ь)**.

### 🎭 Dual-PC Co-Op & Alternating DM Screen
Rather than playing the expansion trilogy (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, *Divine Contention* — titled *"C'mon, Listen"* in D&D Beyond) as an uninterrupted block, Peter and Gary ran a 1-on-1 dual-character setup and alternated DM duties:
* **Peter** ran *Dragon of Icespire Peak* (Levels 1–7) and the three Leilon trilogy modules (*Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, and *Divine Contention*). Because Myagkiy and Tvyordiy functioned as DMPCs while Peter was behind the screen, Peter intentionally steered key story spotlights—such as touching the divinatory altar at the [[Shrine of Savras]] or choosing the *+1 Longbow* reward at the [[Tower of Storms]]—directly to Gary's characters.
* **Gary** interleaved custom homebrew dungeon crawls and boss encounters: Interlude 1 (*"Shadow's Run"*) between Acts 2 and 3, Interlude 2 (*"Keeping the Faith"*) between Acts 3 and 4, and the upcoming *Bridge Campaign*.

### ⚔️ DM Encounter Scaling, Custom Maps & Dice Highlights
Gary is an avid character optimizer who min-maxed Krageer and Rhynok for extreme DPR and survivability. Operating as DM required up-leveling published encounters, building custom battlemaps, and adding extra threats to maintain intense combat stakes:
* **Krageer's Double-Fail with *Danger Sense* (Act 1):** At the [[Temple of Abbathor]], right after unlocking *Danger Sense* at Level 2 (granting Advantage on Dexterity saves against traps), Krageer pried at the trapped statue gem and rolled **under 15 on both d20s** against the DC 15 save!
* **Custom Roll20 Map for [[Yargath]]'s Patrol (Act 1):** En route to the [[Circle of Thunder]], Peter built a custom Roll20 wilderness battlemap where the party and centaur [[Xanth]] ambushed [[Yargath]] and ten Talosian orcs.
* **Rooftop Ice Mephits with [[Cryovain]] (Act 1 Finale):** To elevate the final *Dragon of Icespire Peak* rooftop boss fight against a Level 6 party armed with a *Dragon Slayer* Longsword, Peter added a pack of **Ice Mephits** alongside [[Cryovain]], while Tvyordiy landed clutch *Stunning Strikes* on the dragon.
* **High-Tier Planar Reinforcements (Act 4):** In the *Divine Contention* finale, Peter reinforced [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] with an Air Elemental Myrmidon and a Nalfeshnee demon.

### 🍕 Table Running Gags & Homages
* **DonJon's Cajun Accent:** Voiced by Peter with an exaggerated Louisiana Cajun drawl, improvising endless monologues about wild youth escapades and grievances concerning his ex-wives.
* **The Wayside Inn Fiery Meat Pies & Tattoos:** Krageer's romance with half-orc head cook [[Cooragh]] Struckt was an ongoing comedy highlight driven by his obsession with her alarmingly well-spiced stews and special fiery pork pies. Whenever asked about the curved dagger tattoos beneath her ears, Cooragh would deadpan that she got them purely to spite her human mother from Luskan as youthful folly.
* **[[Ultima Exodus]] Greataxe:** Krageer's signature axe was named after *Ultima III: Exodus*, a shared inside joke between Peter and Gary as lifelong *Ultima* fans.
* **"Shadow's Run" Retro Homage:** Interlude 1 was named *"Shadow's Run"* by Gary as a deliberate nod to *[[Shadowrun]]*, the classic PC game Peter and Gary played together long ago.
* **Salvatore Drow Lore & The Twin Ships:** Peter's deep familiarity with R.A. Salvatore's 39 *Legend of Drizzt* audiobooks inspired the portrayal of Jarlaxle Baenre's Bregan D'aerthe privateer ships *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle and Icingdeath]]*. Rhynok pierced the illusory glamour cloaking Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s drow crew, forging a friendly surface alliance that culminated in Deloz boarding the dreadnaught alongside the Order.
* **Krageer as the Unlikely "Party Face":** Despite playing a Goliath barbarian with an abysmal Charisma of 6 (-2 penalty), Gary frequently volunteered Krageer to speak for the party during tense diplomatic negotiations. Krageer succeeded a surprising number of times against impossible odds—most famously when he swayed Lord Protector [[Dagult Neverember]] into sending full Neverwinter aid to Leilon after every other party member had failed.
* **The "Sacrificial Lamb" Hoard Breach:** During the Deeping Cave dungeon crawl in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, the party tackled the poison gas chamber trapped behind a *Wall of Force* by sending Krageer in first as the "sacrificial lamb" to soak the initial poison damage. Once Krageer scrambled out and was healed, Peter realized Tvyordiy's Level 10 Monk feature *Purity of Body* made him completely immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition, allowing Tvyordiy to casually stroll through the deadly fumes without taking a single point of damage to secure the dragon's entire hoard.
* **Krageer's Naval Archery Duel:** During the fleet battle against [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships, Krageer decided that if Rhynok could snipe, he could too—setting aside his legendary greataxe *Ultima Exodus* to enthusiastically trade arrow volleys across the waves using his own longbow.
* **The "Rope Trick" Extradimensional Sniper Pocket:** One of Gary's favorite tactical optimization tricks for Rhynok was casting *Rope Trick*, climbing up, pulling the rope into the extradimensional space, and raining down poisoned arrows from complete cover with absolute impunity.
* **Krageer's Only Unflinching Pain (Psychic Damage):** As a Bear Totem Barbarian, Krageer resisted all physical, elemental, necrotic, and radiant damage while raging. The Star Spawn Manglers at Thalivar's Beacon introduced the party to pure psychic damage—the single agony Krageer's barbarian constitution could not flinch off, leading to much roleplay grumbling about "brain pain."
* **Rhynok's Snake Venom Milking (Downtime):** During campaign downtime intervals, Gary would send Rhynok out into the wilderness specifically to hunt and milk giant poisonous snakes, ensuring his Poisoner feat was continuously supplied with lethal fresh toxins for every battle.
