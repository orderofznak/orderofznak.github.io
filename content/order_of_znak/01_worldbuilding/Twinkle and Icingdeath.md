# ⛵ Twinkle and Icingdeath (Vessels)

> [!NOTE]
> **Owner & Fleet Patron:** [[Jarlaxle Baenre]] (Bregan D'aerthe)  
> **Naming Origin:** Named in homage to **Drizzt Do'Urden**'s legendary twin scimitars, *Twinkle* and *Icingdeath*.  
> **Captains:**  
> • **[[Deloz Montiv|Captain Deloz Montiv]]** — Commanding the *Twinkle* (the smaller vessel)

## 🛡️ Vessel Overview & Illusory Cloaking
The *Twinkle* and *Icingdeath* are a pair of swift privateer vessels operating in the Sea of Swords on behalf of the mercenary syndicate **Bregan D'aerthe**. Owned by the enigmatic drow mercenary leader **Jarlaxle Baenre**, the ships are crewed entirely by drow mariners.

To navigate the surface waters of the Sword Coast, trade freely, and enter ports without inciting panic or naval conflict with surface settlements, both vessels employ powerful enchantments:
* **The Enchanted Figureheads:** Each ship possesses a figurehead imbued with powerful illusion magic that projects a continuous glamour over the vessel, causing all drow crew members to appear as ordinary human sailors.
* **Captain's Disguise:** Captain Deloz personally reinforced this subterfuge with a *Hat of Disguise*, presenting himself as a human merchant captain.

## ⚔️ Campaign Encounters & The Order of Znak

### Rhynok's Piercing Senses & The Friendly Reveal
When [[The Order of Znak]] first encountered Captain Deloz aboard the *Twinkle*, the disguise held against ordinary scrutiny. However, [[Rhynok]]'s acute ranger perception and elven heritage allowed him to discern subtle biological and physical movements among the crew, recognizing that the mariners were elves rather than humans. 

When the illusory facade was dropped and the crew was revealed to be drow, the revelation caused no hostility or alarm. The Order of Znak welcomed them warmly, forging an enduring naval friendship with Captain Deloz and his crew.

### Diplomatic Mission to Lord Neverember
During the adventure arc involving the twin ships, the heroes journeyed to Neverwinter to petition Lord Protector [[Dagult Neverember]] for desperately needed military resources and naval reinforcements to defend [[Leilon]]. After each of the party's higher-charisma members attempted in turn to persuade the stubborn ruler, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]—wielding a Charisma score of only 6—surprised everyone at the table by delivering the winning argument that swayed Neverember to grant his full aid.

### The Storm Dreadnaught Assault on Fheralai Stormsworn
When the heroes needed to locate and eliminate the Talos cult leader High Priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** amidst hurricane-swept waters, they called upon Captain Deloz and the *Twinkle*. 

Deloz piloted the vessel through violent tempest conditions, successfully tracking down the Talosite fleet. Upon reaching the enemy flagship, Captain Deloz and his crew joined [[The Order of Znak]] in direct combat, boarding and scouring the ironclad dreadnaught deck by deck until the priestess was defeated.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Salvatore Lore Homage
* **Drizzt Do'Urden Legendarium:** As a dedicated reader who listened to all 39 of R.A. Salvatore's *The Legend of Drizzt* audiobooks (narrated by Victor Bevine), Peter used the appearance of *Twinkle*, *Icingdeath*, and Jarlaxle's Bregan D'aerthe privateers in *Divine Contention* as a thematic showcase to bring rich drow lore and Salvatore homages directly into the campaign.
