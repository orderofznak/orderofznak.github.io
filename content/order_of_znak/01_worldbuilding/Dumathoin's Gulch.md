# ⛰️ Dumathoin's Gulch

## 📜 In-Universe Lore & History
* **The Sacred Canyon:** A narrow, jagged ravine carved into the crags of the Sword Mountains south of Neverwinter. The gulch is named in reverence to **Dumathoin**, the dwarven god of mining, buried secrets, and guardian of the dead.
* **Ancient Shrines:** The gulch features weathered dwarven stone steps and a sacred moonstone altar dedicated to the Silent Keeper, protected by the lingering dwarven spirit guardian **[[Sister Silvergranite]]** (an Eidolon inhabiting a massive moonstone statue).
* **Strategic Transit:** Situated along a perilous highland pass connecting the coastal lowlands of Leilon with the hidden gnomish mountain enclave of **[[Gnomengarde]]**.

## ⚙️ Properties & Hazards
* **Choke-Point Terrain:** Steep scree slopes and claustrophobic cliff walls limit mobility and provide elevated perches for ambushes.
* **Cult of Myrkul Incursions:** During the regional conflict across the Sword Coast, the gulch became infested with wandering undead legions, sword wraiths, and an elite **Boneclaw** loyal to Myrkul, god of death.
* **Feral Wereboar Territory:** The scrub brush and ravine foothills surrounding the gulch serve as hunting grounds for packs of ferocious wereboars drawn to the violence of the mountains.

## 🗺️ Campaign Significance & Tactical Narrative

### Act 4 (*Divine Contention*) Opening Quest
Following the terrifying possession of [[Claugiyliamatar]] by [[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]] in Deeping Cave—the final adventure of *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*—the campaign switched directly into *Divine Contention*. With an ancient green dragon now an active undead terror over the Sword Coast, **[[The Order of Znak]]** hastened immediately to Dumathoin's Gulch on an urgent mission to rendezvous with Gnomengarde inventors ([[Fibblestib Pinebark]], [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]], [[Bindlemint Mincemower]]) and secure specialized anti-siege ordnance: the **Gnomengarde Grenade**.

### The Wereboar Ambush & Infection of [[Tvyordiy Znak]]
Approaching the canyon, the party was ambushed by a pack of savage wereboars springing from the highland brush:
* During the ferocious close-quarters brawl, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] was **knocked prone and bitten** by a wereboar, contracting the curse of **Wereboar Lycanthropy**.
* Rather than curing the affliction, Peter and the party embraced the physiological transformation. The curse permanently boosted Tvyordiy's physical Strength to 17 (+3), granted him hybrid-form tusks in combat, and transformed the agile Aarakocra monk into an unyielding, concussive "Hard Sign" (Ъ) shock trooper.

### The Sword Wraiths & Rhynok's "Rope Trick" Sniper Pocket
As undead sword wraiths joined the battle in the tight confines of the gulch, [[Rhynok]] deployed one of his favorite signature combat maneuvers:
* Rhynok cast ***Rope Trick***, climbed into the invisible extradimensional space, and pulled the rope up behind him.
* From the total cover of his extradimensional pocket, Rhynok rained down deadly longbow sniper fire upon the sword wraiths and hostile forces with absolute impunity.

### Diplomacy with [[Sister Silvergranite]]
With the initial ambushers routed, the party advanced to the dwarven moonstone altar where **[[Sister Silvergranite]]** manifested from her sacred statue. Rhynok stepped forward to negotiate, calmly and convincingly persuading the ancient dwarven eidolon that the Order had come to protect the gnomes and wield the weapon in defense of Leilon. Won over by Rhynok's diplomacy, Sister Silvergranite released the **Gnomengarde grenade** into the party's hands.

### The Climax: Slaying the Boneclaw
The battle was not over. Before the Order could safely extract the ordnance and the gnomes, a terrifying **Boneclaw** and an allied sword wraith emerged from the canyon shadows. The party rallied, bringing down the horrific undead monstrosity in a hard-fought battle before marching out of the gulch victorious.

## 👥 Associated Characters & Factions
* **[[Sister Silvergranite]]:** Dwarven Eidolon shrine guardian persuaded by Rhynok to release the grenade.
* **[[Rhynok]]:** Sniped from his *Rope Trick* pocket and negotiated the release of the Gnomengarde grenade.
* **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] (Ъ):** Knocked prone and bitten by a wereboar, permanently gaining lycanthropic strength and tusks.
* **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]:** Provided frontline meat shield control during the wereboar and boneclaw engagements.
* **Gnomengarde Inventors:** [[Fibblestib Pinebark]], [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]], and [[Bindlemint Mincemower]].
* **Cult of Myrkul:** Undead boneclaw and sword wraith forces menacing the pass.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Act 3 to Act 4 Bridge:** At the table, Dumathoin's Gulch served as the immediate, seamless transition from the climax of *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* into *Divine Contention*, establishing the rising stakes of the draconic and undead invasion of Leilon.
* **The "Rope Trick" Sniper:** Rhynok's use of *Rope Trick* to establish an untouchable aerial sniper platform was a celebrated tactical staple of Gary's character optimization across the campaign.
