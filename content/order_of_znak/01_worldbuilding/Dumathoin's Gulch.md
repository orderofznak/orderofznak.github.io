# ⛰️ Dumathoin's Gulch

## 📜 In-Universe Lore & History
* **The Sacred Canyon:** A narrow, jagged ravine carved into the crags of the Sword Mountains south of Neverwinter. The gulch is named in reverence to **Dumathoin**, the dwarven god of mining, buried secrets, and guardian of the dead.
* **Ancient Shrines:** The gulch features weathered dwarven stone steps and a sacred moonstone altar dedicated to the Silent Keeper, protected by lingering dwarven spirit guardians (eidolons).
* **Strategic Transit:** Situated along a perilous highland pass connecting the coastal lowlands of Leilon with the hidden gnomish mountain enclave of **Gnomengarde**.

## ⚙️ Properties & Hazards
* **Choke-Point Terrain:** Steep scree slopes and claustrophobic cliff walls limit mobility and provide elevated perches for ambushes.
* **Cult of Myrkul Incursions:** During the regional conflict across the Sword Coast, the gulch became infested with wandering undead legions and elite shock forces loyal to Myrkul, god of death.
* **Feral Wereboar Territory:** The scrub brush and ravine foothills surrounding the gulch serve as hunting grounds for packs of ferocious wereboars drawn to the violence of the mountains.

## 🗺️ Campaign Significance & Key Events
* **Act 4 (*Divine Contention*) Expedition:** Following the defense of Leilon, **The Order of Znak** traveled into Dumathoin's Gulch on an urgent mission to rendezvous with gnomish inventors from Gnomengarde (including Fibblestib and Bindlemint) and recover specialized anti-siege ordnance—the **Gnomengarde Grenade**.
* **The Wereboar Ambush:** Approaching the canyon, the party was ambushed by a pack of savage wereboars springing from the highland brush.
* **The Infection of [[Tvyordiy Znak]]:** In the ferocious melee combat that ensued, Aarakocra Monk [[Tvyordiy Znak]] engaged the beasts toe-to-toe. During the brutal brawl, Tvyordiy was bitten and gored by their tusks, contracting the curse of **Wereboar Lycanthropy**. 
* **Embracing the Curse:** Rather than curing the affliction, the party and Tvyordiy embraced the physiological transformation. The curse permanently boosted Tvyordiy's physical Strength to 17 (+3), granted him hybrid-form tusks in combat, and transformed the agile monk into an unstoppable, concussive "Hard Sign" shock trooper.

## 👥 Associated Characters & Factions
* **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] (Ъ):** Contracted wereboar lycanthropy during the battle outside the gulch.
* **[[The Order of Znak Complete Campaign Chronicle]]:** Act 4 key milestone in the *Divine Contention* saga.
* **Gnomengarde Inventors:** Gnomish delegates dispatched through the gulch to deliver defensive siege weapons to Leilon.
* **Cult of Myrkul:** Undead factions menacing the Sword Mountains pass.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Official D&D 5e Adventure Context:** In *Divine Contention* (the climax of the *Beyond the Dragon of Icespire Peak* expansion trilogy), the *"Dumathoin's Gulch"* quest is tuned for 11th-level parties. It features an encounter with panicked gnomes, a surprise wereboar attack carrying the DC 12 Constitution curse save, and an undead confrontation with a boneclaw and sword wraith platoon inside the canyon.
* **Tabletop Improv & Mechanical Synergy:** In standard D&D 5e play, parties often rush to cast *Remove Curse* immediately after lycanthropy infection. At Peter and Gary's table, Peter elected to retain the wereboar curse. The resulting combination of an Aarakocra Drunken Master Monk possessing 17 Strength, Crusher forced movement, unarmored flight, and ferocious wereboar hybrid tusks became an iconic, beloved table legend that perfectly matched Tvyordiy's "Hard Sign" (Ъ) unyielding persona.
