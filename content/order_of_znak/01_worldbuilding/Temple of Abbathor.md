# ⛏️ Temple of Abbathor (Dwarven Excavation)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** Ancient stone ruins tucked into an isolated canyon within the foothills of the **Sword Mountains**, east of [[Leilon]] and south of [[Phandalin]].
* **Dedication:** Originally constructed centuries ago as a secret temple honoring **Abbathor**, the dwarven god of greed and avarice. The temple was subsequently buried under tons of rubble and forgotten following an solar eclipse and earthquake.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Excavation Survey & Ochre Jellies (Level 2):** Arriving at the Dwarven Excavation at **Level 2**, [[The Order of Znak]] met shield dwarf prospectors **[[Dazlyn Grayshard]]** and **[[Norbus Ironrune]]**, who promised their pair of *Sending Stones* if the party slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** lurking inside the temple halls.
* **Secret Doors, Rubble Excavation & Krageer's *Danger Sense* Double-Fail (DC 15):** After slaying the Ochre Jellies, the party discovered the temple's secret doors and passages and spent hours excavating the collapsed rubble tunnel leading to the innermost chamber housing the **Statue of Abbathor**. Tempted by the glowing gem in the statue, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** pried at it and triggered the exploding gem trap. Despite having just unlocked ***Danger Sense*** at Level 2 (granting Advantage on Dexterity saves against visible traps), **both of Krageer's d20 rolls came up under the DC 15**, and the Goliath took the full blast!
* **Failed Persuasion & The Campaign-Long *Sending Stones*:** Returning to Dazlyn and Norbus, the party failed to persuade the dwarves to part with additional rewards, but claimed the promised pair of ***Sending Stones***—which the Order used continuously for tactical communication across the remainder of the entire campaign.
* **Orc Ambush on Exit & Level 3 Milestone:** Upon exiting the canyon, the party was ambushed by a pack of marauding **Orcs** utilizing their *Aggressive* trait to close the gap. However, with **Orcs** as **[[Rhynok]]**'s Favored Enemy and a high Initiative roll (backed by his longbow and *Hail of Thorns*), the orcs were no match and were slaughtered. Completing the quest advanced the party to **Level 3**, locking in their iconic subclasses (Bear Totem for Krageer, Gloom Stalker for Rhynok, Way of the Drunken Master for [[Tvyordiy Znak]], and Life Domain for [[Myagkiy Znak]]).
