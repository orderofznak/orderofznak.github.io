# ⛏️ Temple of Abbathor (Dwarven Excavation)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** Ancient stone ruins tucked into an isolated canyon within the foothills of the **Sword Mountains**, east of [[Leilon]] and south of [[Phandalin]].
* **Dedication:** Originally constructed centuries ago as a secret temple honoring **Abbathor**, the dwarven god of greed and avarice. The temple was subsequently buried under tons of rubble and forgotten following an solar eclipse and earthquake.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Excavation Survey & Ochre Jellies:** Arriving at the Dwarven Excavation, [[The Order of Znak]] met shield dwarf prospectors **[[Dazlyn Grayshard]]** and **[[Norbus Ironrune]]**, who promised their pair of *Sending Stones* if the party slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** lurking inside the temple halls.
* **Secret Doors, Rubble Excavation & The Exploding Gem of Abbathor:** After slaying the Ochre Jellies, the party discovered the temple's secret doors and passages and spent hours excavating the collapsed rubble tunnel leading to the innermost chamber housing the **Statue of Abbathor**. Tempted by the glowing gem in the statue, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** pried at it and triggered the exploding gem trap. Even though the Goliath's newly honed barbarian instincts warned him of the imminent danger the instant the stone shifted, he failed to twist clear in time and took the full brunt of the fiery blast!
* **Unmoved Prospectors & The Campaign-Long *Sending Stones*:** Returning to Dazlyn and Norbus, the party's appeals failed to persuade the stubborn dwarves to part with additional rewards, though the champions claimed the promised pair of ***Sending Stones***—which the Order used continuously for long-range communication across the remainder of the entire campaign.
* **Orc Ambush on Exit & Newfound Callings:** Upon exiting the canyon, the party was ambushed by a pack of marauding **Orcs** charging aggressively to close the gap. However, with **[[Rhynok]]**'s deep hatred of orcs and lightning-quick reflexes (backed by his longbow and *Hail of Thorns*), the raiders were cut down before they could overrun the line. Hardened by the trials of the excavation, each champion stepped into their signature calling—Krageer embracing the primal resilience of the Bear Totem, Rhynok becoming an unseen Gloom Stalker of the dark wilds, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] mastering the unpredictable sway of the Drunken Master, and [[Myagkiy Znak]] deepening his devotion as a healer of the Life Domain.
