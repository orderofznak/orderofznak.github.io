# ⛏️ Temple of Abbathor (Dwarven Excavation)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** Ancient stone ruins tucked into an isolated canyon within the foothills of the **Sword Mountains**, east of Leilon and south of Phandalin.
* **Dedication:** Originally constructed centuries ago as a secret temple honoring **Abbathor**, the dwarven god of greed and avarice. The temple was subsequently buried under tons of rubble and forgotten following an earthquake.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Excavation Survey:** In Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*), dwarf prospectors **[[Dazlyn Grayshard]]** and **[[Norbus Ironrune]]** began unearthing the ruins in search of ancient relics and gold deposits.
* **Ochre Jelly Infestation:** Excavation crews disturbed sleeping colonies of corrosive **ochre jellies** and gelatinous horrors lurking within the temple halls, pinning the miners outside.
* **The Order's Rescue:** Dispatched from Phandalin by Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]], [[The Order of Znak]] arrived, cleared the subterranean chambers of oozes, and secured the site. In gratitude, Dazlyn and Norbus gifted the party their pair of paired *sending stones*.
