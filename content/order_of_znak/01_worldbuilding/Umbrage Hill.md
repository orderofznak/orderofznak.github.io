# 🌿 Umbrage Hill

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A solitary stone windmill situated atop a grassy knoll along the High Road, south of Phandalin.
* **Apothecary Haven:** Serves as the home and laboratory of midwife and herbalist **[[Adabra Gwynn]]**, priestess of Chauntea, who cultivates healing herbs and prepares restorative draughts for frontier settlers.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **The Manticore Assault (Act 1 — *Dragon of Icespire Peak*):** Driven south from the mountains by white dragon [[Cryovain]], a hungry manticore besieged the windmill, battering against the heavy oak doors.
* **Rescue by the Order:** Dispatched by Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]] to escort Adabra back to safety, [[The Order of Znak]] arrived in the nick of time, defeated the manticore, and secured the frontier apothecary.
