# 🌿 Umbrage Hill

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Location:** A solitary stone windmill situated atop a grassy knoll along the High Road, south of [[Phandalin]].
* **Apothecary Haven:** Serves as the home and laboratory of midwife and herbalist **[[Adabra Gwynn]]**, priestess of Chauntea, who cultivates healing herbs and prepares restorative draughts for frontier settlers.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Quest
* **Quest 1 — Driving Off the Manticore (Level 1 &rarr; Level 2):** Dispatched from [[Phandalin]] at **Level 1** by Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]] to urge [[Adabra Gwynn]] to return to safety, [[The Order of Znak]] arrived to find a hungry **Manticore** (driven south from the mountains by white dragon [[Cryovain]]) battering at the windmill. The party fought the beast and **drove it off** (as it fled rather than staying to be killed).
* **Failed Persuasion & Potion of Healing:** Afterward, the party failed to convince the stubborn Adabra to abandon her windmill and return to Phandalin, though she rewarded them with her scripted **Potion of Healing** and a signed note confirming her safety for Harbin. Completing this first quest advanced the Order to **Level 2** (unlocking [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s *Danger Sense* & *Reckless Attack* and [[Rhynok]]'s Ranger spellcasting with *Goodberry* and *Hail of Thorns*).
* **World Event — [[Cryovain]] vs. The Manticore Pair:** Later in Act 1 (prior to the party's expedition to the [[Woodland Manse]]), the original Manticore returned to Umbrage Hill **accompanied by its mate**—only to cross paths with **[[Cryovain]]** himself! Rather than a player encounter, a monster battle erupted at Umbrage Hill between Cryovain and the two manticores, with the white dragon ultimately defeating them both.
