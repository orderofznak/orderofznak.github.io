# 🛡️ Sergeant Hazz Yorrum

## 🛡️ Overview & Personality
* **Identity:** Slovenly male Damaran human sergeant in charge of the Neverwinter garrison soldiers deployed to protect settlers during the initial founding and reconstruction of [[Leilon]].
* **Personality & Demeanor:** As lazy and corrupt as he is cowardly. He prioritizes avoiding personal physical danger, eating rations, and collecting his Lords' Alliance stipend over executing competent frontier security.
* **Equipment & Sigils:** Wears ill-maintained soldier's armor and carries a heavy shield decorated with the prominent sigil of **Neverwinter**, projecting official authority while delivering none of its protection.

---

## 🏛️ Dereliction of Duty & Deference to Gallio Elibro
* **Passing the Buck:** Rather than coordinating aggressive patrols or addressing local banditry, Yorrum systematically avoids making tough administrative or tactical calls.
* **Deference to the Wizard:** For nearly every meaningful civic and military matter, Yorrum defers entirely to the town wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** in the House of Thalivar, preferring to hide behind the mage's authority rather than take responsibility for the settlement's defense.

---

## ⚔️ Dynamic with The Order of Znak
* **The True Protectors:** When real dangers besieged Leilon—such as the kraken cult raid in *Storm Lord's Wrath*, talosian storm cultists, or dragon incursions—Yorrum's garrison retreated behind barricades or ducked for cover.
* **The Order's Ascendance:** His sheer incompetence created the vacuum that allowed [[The Order of Znak]] to step up as the beloved, true champions of Leilon, leaving Yorrum relieved that someone else was doing the dangerous fighting.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Official Adventure Context:** Sourced directly from *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Sergeant Yorrum represents the classic tabletop trope of the useless, bureaucratic guard captain whose cowardice highlights the heroism of the player characters.
