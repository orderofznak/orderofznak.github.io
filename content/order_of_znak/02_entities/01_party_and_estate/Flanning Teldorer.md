# 🔨 Flanning Teldorer

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A venerable, master-smith and high cleric of the **Forge Domain** dedicated to **[[Gond]]** (the Wonderbringer, Lord of All Smiths).
* **Sanctuary & Forge:** Maintains a secluded, consecrated mountain forge high in **the Crags** northeast of Neverwinter, channeling geothermal vents and sacred rites to craft masterwork weapons and divine reliquaries.
* **Master & Mentor:** Teacher and mentor to gifted young prodigy **[[Dayne Beltran]]**, guiding the boy's natural smithing brilliance into devout holy service to Gond.
* **The Sacred Relic:** Master of Ippen's holy forge and keeper of the **[[Right Hand of Gond]]** (The Hammer of Gond), an ancient, consecrated smithing artifact capable of forging monumental implements of war.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **The Stolen Relic:** In June 2022, Flanning's mountain forge was infiltrated and desecrated by shadowy forces who stole the *Right Hand of Gond*. Desperate to recover his faith's most sacred implement, Flanning sent an urgent plea to [[The Order of Znak]], prompting [[Tvyordiy Znak]] to fly north alone into the Crags to meet him.
* **Dispatching Dayne:** Flanning dispatched his apprentice Dayne Beltran to guide the Order into Neverwinter and track the thieves through the Starmetal Hills and Neverwinter Wood.
* **Gond's Divine Boons:** When the Order returned from Mount Hotenow with the recovered *Right Hand of Gond* after Krageer's double-enlarged victory over Fire Giant Queen [[Kevlaan the Merciless]], Flanning was overcome with reverence. Channeling Gond's direct divine grace, Flanning:
  * Identified the mysterious enchanted rings and amulet recovered from Mount Hotenow.
  * Repaired, honed, and consecrated all of the Order's weapons and armor.
  * Approved Dayne's gift of consecrated **+2 Plate Armor** to Life Cleric **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** (elevating him to 24 AC).
  * Bestowed **Four Divine Feats** upon the party: granting [[Tvyordiy Znak]] ***Crusher***, [[Myagkiy Znak]] ***Resilient (Constitution)***, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] ***Lucky***, and [[Rhynok]] ***Poisoner***.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Introduction:** Introduced by Gary in June 2022 as the quest-giver and patron of the homebrew adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*.
* **Discord Canon Note:** On July 8, 2022, following the volcano climax, Peter logged Flanning's divine rewards: *"Flanning: Give everyone a feat, give everyone +2 levels on an item not exceeding +3, can find magic items, repair items, and will ID the rings and amulet"*, establishing the major power jump heading into *Divine Contention*.
