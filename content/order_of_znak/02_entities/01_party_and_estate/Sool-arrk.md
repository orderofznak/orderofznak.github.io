# 🦅 Sool-arrk (Scholar-Scribe & Disciple)

* **Identity & Heritage:** Thoughtful young eagle Aarakocra from the Boundless Whorl, kin to matriarch Soolin. Disciple of [[Tvyordiy Znak]].
* **Role & Discipline:** The scholar-scribe of the disciples, transcribing the philosophical aphorisms of the Hard Sign and recording the monastic katas at [[Aufox Manor]].
* **Mission:** Preparing to return to the Plane of Air to help establish monastic archives and libraries for future Aarakocra Open Hand dojos.
