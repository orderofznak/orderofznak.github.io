# 🦅 Wing-Captain Deekek

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Veteran male Aarakocra sentinel of the Boundless Whorl, elder brother of [[Tvyordiy Znak]] and [[Klee'errk]]. Commander of the Roost Sentinels guarding the planar bastions of the Elemental Plane of Air.
* **Appearance:** Imposing, broad-shouldered eagle-folk with rich chestnut-and-cream plumage, an eagle beak notched from dozens of aerial dogfights, and keen golden eyes. Wears reinforced aerial harness and wields a heavy, two-handed notched sky-glaive.
* **The Fall of the Lower Perch (1484 DR):** At three winters fledged, Deekek witnessed their parents Harek and Vaelen defend the lower roost against the Talos cultists. Obeying their final command, he helped guide the surviving crèche into the deep vaults. For seventeen years, he carried the sorrow of believing their bloodline had been severed.
* **The Brother's Return (1501 DR):** Instantly recognized his father Harek's likeness in Tvyordiy when his younger brother arrived on the cloudstone terrace bearing the ancestral *Tessera of the Western Gale* (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 4]]).
* **Flight Instructor to [[Yeri]]:** Taught Yeri the intricacies of planar thermals and vertical "chimney chutes," guiding her early flights with her transfigured dragonfly wings.
* **The Defense of the Nursery & Singularity:** Led the defense of the crèche during the siege of the Boundless Whorl, later joining Klee'errk on the stone redoubt to hold Tvyordiy back from diving into the collapsing singularity. Stays behind on the Plane of Air to lead the rebuilding of the sentinel corps.

## 📊 Combat & Mechanics
* **Class & Role:** Level 13 Champion Fighter (Aerial Heavy Vanguard).
* **Weapons:** Notched Heavy Sky-Glaive (+2 Glaive), javelins of lightning.
