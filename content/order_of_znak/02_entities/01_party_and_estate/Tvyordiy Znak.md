# 🦅 Tvyordiy Znak (Ъ)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Aarakocra Planar Aging & Lifespan Reconciliation (5e vs. 5.5e / MotM):**
  - **Natural Elemental Lifespan:** In the pure Elemental Plane of Air, Aarakocra live natural **century-long lifespans (100+ years)**.
  - **The Lowlands Accelerated Aging Curse:** On Toril, an ambient atmospheric corruption tied to **Talos the Destroyer** accelerates avian metabolic decay by **3.33×**, compressing 100 biological years into roughly 30 calendar years.
  - **Tvyordiy's Timeline & Timeless Body:**
    - Born in the Boundless Whorl in **1484 DR**; evacuated as an egg to Toril during a Talosite raid.
    - Met Myagkiy in **1488 DR at age 4** (physically adult due to accelerated lowlands maturation).
    - Arrived on the Plane of Air in **1501 DR at age 17** (having burned ~56 biological years, physically appearing like a seasoned master in his mid-50s; kin initially mistook him for his father Harek's ghost).
    - In **1503 DR at age 18**, Tvyordiy defeats the Avatar of Talos, shattering the atmospheric curse on Toril and harmonizing Aarakocra biology to the natural 1:1 rate across both planes.
    - Attaining **Timeless Body (Monk 15)** at age 18, Tvyordiy stops suffering the physical frailty of aging. With ~44 natural biological years remaining, he lives out a total lifespan of **~62–65 calendar years**, giving him over **40+ years of vibrant life, mentoring, and adventure** following the Bridge Campaign!

* **Personality & Temperament:** Stoic, pragmatic, and remarkably unflappable. Possesses a surprisingly resonant, gravel-deep baritone voice that contrasts sharply with his avian Aarakocra heritage.
* **Background & Urchin Origins:** Urchin. Orphaned in early fledglinghood, Tvyordiy drifted down the Sword Coast until settling in the grime and shadows of Waterdeep's Dock Ward. He carried only a single physical heirloom from his lost parents: a feather-light metallic coin inscribed with an ancient elemental whorl in **Auran**. Surviving on fish scrapings and back-alley grit, he honed an instinctive, unyielding stubbornness.
* **The Fellowship of the Twin Signs (Знаки):** In 1488 DR, after a four-year-old Tvyordiy (possessing the rapidly matured physical frame of a young adult due to the lowlands' accelerated aging curse) broke an oar-handle over the jaw of a dock syndicate boss in Fishgut Alley, he encountered hermit [[Myagkiy Znak]]. Sensed a mysterious rhythmic "pulse" resonating between his own chest, the hermit, and a tattered cloth inscribed with the glyphs **Ъ** (The Hard Sign) and **Ь** (The Soft Sign). The two outcasts adopted each other as brothers and took the shared surname **Znak**, vowing to bring balance to a cruel world.
* **Table Nicknames:** Famously dubbed **"Wereboar-diy Znak"** (or *"Wearboar-diy Znak"*) by Gary following the wereboar encounter in *Divine Contention*, an enduring running joke at the table.
* **Battlefield Role:** High-velocity aerial shock trooper—combining natural winged flight, the swaying redirection of the Drunken Master, rapid *Qiang* spear and staff combinations, and nerve-locking *Stunning Strikes* that can halt even a full-grown dragon in its tracks.
* **The Wereboar's Mark:** Contracted wereboar lycanthropy late in the campaign during the expedition into **[[Dumathoin's Gulch]]**—embracing the curse's feral brawn and hybrid tusks to become an even more concussive, unyielding frontline striker.

## 📜 In-Universe Chronicle & Key Deeds

### 🏔️ [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
* **The *Qiang* Spear & The Drunken Master:** Arriving in [[Phandalin]] carrying a slender Eastern-style ***Qiang* spear**, Tvyordiy fought beside his new companions to drive off the manticore at [[Umbrage Hill]] and clear the Ochre Jellies and orc ambush at the [[Temple of Abbathor]]—stepping into the swaying, unpredictable martial discipline of the **Way of the Drunken Master**.
* **The "Flight" Rescue at the [[Woodland Manse]]:** During the party's harrowing first expedition to the [[Woodland Manse]], the companions were overwhelmed in the Gulthias Tree courtyard by [[Grannoc]], swarms of blights, wild boars, and anchorite *Lightning Bolts*. As the party broke into a fighting withdrawal, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] fell unconscious twice—dropping a second time at the edge of the clearing under a parting volley of needle blight spines. Swooping back from his **flight** (*in both the avian and tactical senses of the word!*), Tvyordiy scooped up the pin-cushioned Goliath and carried him away to safety.
* **Heisting the Gold Bell at the [[Shrine of Savras]]:** After fighting through the ankhegs of the [[Loggers' Camp]], the harpies and undead keeper [[Moesko]] at the [[Tower of Storms]], and the horde of orcs and ogres at the [[Shrine of Savras]] alongside [[DonJon Raskin]], Tvyordiy and Krageer scaled the ruined belfry tower and pried loose the shrine's **solid gold bell**, which the party sold for **2,500 gp**.
* **Silvering the *Qiang* & Cleansing [[Axeholm]]:** When his wooden-and-steel *Qiang* failed to wound [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s immune wererats at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] (where Rhynok and Myagkiy ran off the surviving wererats as Zeleen escaped), Tvyordiy had his ***Qiang* spear silvered** in [[Phandalin]] alongside Krageer's greataxe. Rappelling down the chimney of [[Axeholm]] under Myagkiy's *Guidance*, Tvyordiy unleashed a *Flurry of Blows* in the surprise ambush against the giant spiders and helped purge the fortress of ghouls, the banshee [[Vyldara]], and the undead Castellan.
* **The [[Circle of Thunder]] & [[Woodland Manse]] Vengeance:** Riding into Neverwinter Wood with centaur ally **[[Xanth]]** after clearing [[Dragon Barrow]], Tvyordiy unleashed rapid multi-strike combinations and nerve-locking *Stunning Strikes* to help slaughter [[Yargath]]'s ten-orc patrol, fell **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, ten twig blights, and two anchorites ([[Flenz]] and [[Narux]]) at the [[Circle of Thunder]], and wipe out [[Grannoc]], his anchorite cohort, twelve twig blights, five vine blights, twelve stirges, and eight needle blights at the [[Woodland Manse]].
* **Stunning [[Cryovain]] Atop [[Icespire Hold]]:** After fighting mountain hobgoblins and the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]] with his **Silvered *Qiang***, Tvyordiy ascended to the icy roof of [[Icespire Hold]]. Darting through freezing wind and four Ice Mephits, **Tvyordiy landed a series of clutch *Stunning Strikes* directly on [[Cryovain]]**—locking the white dragon in place on the roof so [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] could unload with his *Dragon Slayer* Longsword and [[Rhynok]] could finish the wyrm off!

### ⛈️ [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
* **Wayside Defense & Leilon Quayside:** Intercepted undead besiegers at [[The Wayside Inn]] alongside [[Martisha Vinetalker]]'s caravan, and shielded fleeing children in [[Leilon]] when the Drowned Cult launched their water-steed raid.
* **The House of Thalivar & Near-Fatal Mangler Ambush:** Investigated Gallio Elvers's nocturnal possessions in the ruined spire. In the shadow-cloaked upper chambers, Tvyordiy took the brunt of a lethal multi-claw ambush from two Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers**, dropping unconscious before **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** rushed to his side and stabilized him with ***Spare the Dying***.
* **Mere of Dead Men & Lizardfolk Alliance:** Fought side-by-side with the marshland lizardfolk against invading rot trolls to help forge the peace pact between Leilon and sovereign chieftain [[Grixxugroaz]].
* **Mutiny on the *Iceflow* & Swashbuckler Performance:** When Captain [[Kristoffen]]'s crew mutinied off the coast, Tvyordiy diffused the bloodshed through an impromptu martial display and performance, backing down the cutthroat swashbucklers in tandem with Krageer's intimidation and Myagkiy's calming magic.
* **The Gale of Thunder Cliffs:** During the treacherous rowboat approach to [[Thunder Cliffs]], Tvyordiy braved the hurricane-force coastal winds to dive upon the giant sea crab upending their skiff. Scaling the cliff face, the party lifted [[Sovendahl Erkinze]]'s curse, executed the traitor Kristoffen, defeated wizard [[Gadrille]], and claimed the ***Wand of Magic Missile*** from the Talosite coastal vault.

### 🐲 [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
* **The Renault Commission & Owlbear Road Ambush:** Entrusted by Waterdhavian carriage driver Liefold with an attuned ***Messenger Stone*** to coordinate extraction for Daniel Renault's kidnapped son **Heracio**. When a pair of hungry owlbears ambushed the coach fifty-five miles north of Neverwinter, Tvyordiy vaulted from the carriage steps—driving his Eastern *Qiang* spear into the lead beast, landing a natural 20 critical unarmed strike, and discharging 3rd-level volleys from his ***Wand of Magic Missile***.
* **The Woodland Infiltration & The *Vicious Quarterstaff*:** Flared his enchanted ***Cloak of Billowing*** in a dramatic flourish to steady his companions' nerves approaching the cult's hidden forest estate. Descending into the lightless lower levels, Tvyordiy searched behind a masonry bookcase in Room 1 to uncover a locked iron chest containing an exquisitely balanced, rune-carved weapon: the ***Vicious Quarterstaff***. Testing its lethal thrum on critical strikes, Tvyordiy permanently set aside his starting *Qiang* spear, adopting the staff as his signature melee weapon throughout the rest of the campaign.
* **The Dragon Door & Shadow Dragon Showdown:** Recovered the ancient ritual stones (Blood, Nightshade, Moon, Shadow) to open the sealed basalt Dragon Door. In the freezing subterranean lair, Tvyordiy danced beneath the colossal **Shadow Dragon**'s tail swipes, scoring multiple concussive hits and critical strikes against its wing joints with the *Vicious Quarterstaff* to break its aerial mobility—freeing young Heracio and claiming the enchanted ***Sash of the Patient Master*** (allowing him to regenerate **1d4 + 1 Ki points** once per day) from the draconic hoard before activating the *Messenger Stone* for carriage pickup.

### 🐲 [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]
* **Defending [[Leilon Point]] with the *Vicious Quarterstaff*:** Vaulted across the coastal bluffs of Leilon Point, wielding his signature *Vicious Quarterstaff* and rapid *Flurry of Blows* to break sahuagin waves and resisting the High Priestess's *Hold Person* spell with a 20 Constitution save.
* **Striding Through Claugiyliamatar's Poisoned Hoard:** In Deeping Cave, after Ebondeath possessed [[Claugiyliamatar]] and the party discovered the dragon's treasure hoard trapped behind a *Wall of Force* and choked with lethal green dragon poison gas, Krageer charged in first as the "sacrificial lamb" to test the trap. Once Krageer retreated, Tvyordiy centered his monastic breath—his body and spirit completely impervious to poison via **Purity of Body**—and strolled calmly through the deadly green fumes without taking a scratch, extracting the dragon's entire hoard unharmed!
* **Level 11 Milestone:** Following the dragon's possession and the party's debrief with the Leilon council, Tvyordiy achieved **Level 11**, gaining *Drunkard's Luck*.

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Avian Scout to the Crags:** When forge cleric [[Flanning Teldorer]] sent an urgent call regarding the stolen *Right Hand of Gond*, Tvyordiy used his aarakocra flight to fly north alone into the Crags—meeting Flanning, assessing the crisis, and escorting Flanning's apprentice [[Dayne Beltran]] to rendezvous with the Order in Neverwinter.
* **Urchin Empathy for Young [[Yasmina]]:** In Neverwinter Wood, the Order stormed the lair of Night Hag [[Balheelsa]] and liberated young [[Yasmina]]. Tvyordiy formed an immediate, deeply empathetic bond with the rescued girl—seeing his own impoverished urchin upbringing reflected in her eyes and vowing to protect her as she was brought to Leilon as a ward of the Order.
* **Disarming Mt. Hotenow & Stunning Queen Kevlaan:** In the volcanic tunnels of Mount Hotenow, Tvyordiy and Krageer combined acrobatics and brawn under Myagkiy's divine guidance to bypass lethal engineering traps and unlock a trapped chest (4,000 gp, 4 Potions of Greater Healing). In the magma caldera showdown, as a "Double-Enlarged" Krageer grappled Fire Giant Queen [[Kevlaan the Merciless]], Tvyordiy delivered a textbook flurry of *Stunning Strikes*, locking the fire giant monarch in place until Krageer crushed her into the obsidian floor.

### ⚡ [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
* **The Wereboar Curse of [[Dumathoin's Gulch]]:** Ambushed by five feral wereboars at the entrance to [[Dumathoin's Gulch]], Tvyordiy was knocked prone and bitten—contracting **wereboar lycanthropy**. Rather than seeking magical cleansing, the stoic monk embraced the curse's feral brawn, hybrid tusks, and boosted 17 Strength, harmonizing the lycanthropic power with his Drunken Master martial arts across the remainder of the campaign.
* **Thalivar's Beacon & High Seas Boarding:** Leaped across planar rifts atop the House of Thalivar to pummel Star Spawn Manglers. Aboard *Twinkle*, boarded Ship Rethnor's warship *Throatcutter* through gale-force winds, subduing enemy crewmen with non-lethal martial arts.
* **The Siege of Leilon:**
  - Held the South Gate threshold alongside Krageer and Myagkiy's *Spirit Guardians*, battering ghasts with rapid quarterstaff strikes.
  - Sprinted into the Town Square to strike at Claugiyliamatar's hind legs, rolling nimble Dexterity checks to evade necrotic breath and tail sweeps before pursuing the dragon to the tower roof.
* **Possession & Destruction of Ebondeath:**
  - *The Octagonal Vault Cliffhanger:* In the Crypt of First Royals in Uthtower, the spectral spirit of ancient dracolich **[[Ebondeath]]** ambushed the party and successfully possessed Tvyordiy's body!
  - *Restrained in the Adamantine Net:* Infused with dracolich malice, Tvyordiy's body battered against his companions (`25, 28 to hit, Flurry of Blows`) until Rhynok and Krageer snared him in an Adamantine Net.
  - *The Exorcism & Critical Vengeance:* Dispossessed when Myagkiy blasted Ebondeath with a 34-damage *Guiding Bolt*, Tvyordiy collapsed to the flagstones with only **17 HP remaining**. Rising in righteous fury, Tvyordiy swung his *Vicious Quarterstaff +2*, scoring a monumental **31 critical hit** (`19 bludgeoning`) followed by a **29 critical Unarmed Strike** to shatter the dracolich's spirit forever!
* **Claiming the Circlet of Blasting & Aerial Duel:**
  - Attuned to the **[[Circlet of Blasting]]** recovered from Ebondeath's ancient 14,000 gp hoard.
  - *Stillness of Mind vs. Kata Ironhide:* When Kata Ironhide unleashed her *Dreadful Presence* from atop the Roc, Tvyordiy failed his save (`rolled an 8`), but immediately activated ***Stillness of Mind*** to center his ki and clear the fear!
  - Fired blazing rays from his new *Circlet of Blasting* (`24, 22 to hit`) into the Roc, contributing to the 44-damage barrage that routed the flyer.
* **Dreadnaught Climax:** Boarded the flagship dreadnaught, using *Patient Defense* and *Stunning Strike* against Air Elemental Myrmidons and planar horrors before escaping via Myagkiy's *Word of Recall*.
* **Level 13 Milestone:** Attained **Level 13** Way of the Drunken Master Monk, gaining ***Tongue of the Sun and Moon*** (understanding all spoken languages and being understood by any creature that understands a language). Honored with a stone statue in Leilon's town square.

### 🦅 [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Campaign: "The Breath of the Boundless Sky"]]
* **Departure from Leilon (1500 DR):** Following five winters of rebuilding [[Leilon]] and instructing the young wards at [[Aufox Manor]], Tvyordiy and [[Myagkiy Znak]] heeded divergent callings atop the manor parapets. Recalling the dying Auran whisper of the storm myrmidons on the Sea of Swords, Tvyordiy entrusted the estate keys to majordomo [[Leopold]] and parted with a proverb for young [[Burnice Znak]].
* **The Evermoor Way & Troll Ambush:** Marching disguised as an elderly human monk via his *Hat of Disguise*, Tvyordiy neutralized an ambush of four river trolls along the Evermoor Way without bloodshed—using his *Vicious Quarterstaff +2*, *Patient Defense*, and *Tongue of the Sun and Moon* to offer sage proverbs and spare their lives.
* **The High Forest & The Grandfather Tree:** Escorted through ancient Eaerlann by wood elf path-wardens, Tvyordiy visited the sacred ancestral mound of the Tree Ghost Uthgardt. Beneath the colossal roots of the [[Grandfather Tree]], blind shaman [[Elder Kaya]] and huntress [[Athela Orcbane]] diagnosed the lingering necrotic chill of [[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]] and his contracted wereboar curse. Through night-long meditation beside the sacred longfire, Tvyordiy harmonized his avian discipline with the feral beast within.
* **Ascent of Mount Nimbus & Meeting [[Yeri]]:** Scurrying along the freezing precipices of Mount Bardun in the Star Mounts, Tvyordiy encountered the eccentric Kenku sorcerer **[[Yeri]]**. Trading wits, tea, and philosophical proverbs, Tvyordiy shared his *Potion of Diminution* to navigate a sheer crevasse. Scaling to the hurricane summit of Mount Nimbus, Tvyordiy was struck by primordial planar lightning—purging Ebondeath's frost forever and awakening **Diamond Soul (Monk 14)** with a fly speed of **75 ft**, while Yeri transfigured her kenku curse into four iridescent **Dragonfly Wings** (Level 14 Divine Soul Sorcerer).
* **The Elemental Plane of Air & Family Reunion (1501 DR):** Passing through the planar portal into the boundless sapphire skies of the Plane of Air, Tvyordiy followed his father's silver heirloom (*Tessera of the Western Gale*) to the **Aerie of the Boundless Whorl**. There, after seventeen years of separation, he was reunited with his elder brother **[[Deekek|Wing-Captain Deekek]]** and surviving clutch-twin sister **[[Klee'errk]]** (*Aial Sun-Catcher*), who had long believed him lost to the lowlands of Toril.
* **The Courtyard Challenge & The Unbending Talons:** Challenged by the arrogant vanguard fledglings—led by peregrine recruit **[[Kree-uk]]**—who sneered at grounded martial arts, Tvyordiy deflected an 80-mph aerial dive with tranquil mastery. Deeply humbled, Kree-uk and thirty sky-sentinels knelt to learn the grounded discipline of the Hard Sign, mastering the concussive ki shockwaves of *Stunning Strike*.
* **The Siege of the Boundless Whorl & Singularity Climax (1502 DR):** When the corrupted storm armada of the [[Anchorites of Talos]] laid siege to the Boundless Whorl, Tvyordiy, Klee'errk, Deekek, and Yeri mounted a desperate defense. In the eye of the planar tempest, Tvyordiy unleashed five consecutive *Stunning Strikes* upon the colossal **Avatar of Talos**, enabling Klee'errk to deliver the killing spear-thrust. As the corrupted rift collapsed into an imploding singularity, Yeri channeled a final *Telekinetic Shove* to blast Tvyordiy onto the redoubt gallery before being pulled through the anomaly.
* **Attaining Timeless Body (Monk 15) & Return to Toril:** Meditating in the high roosts of the Aerie, Tvyordiy unlocked the pinnacle monastic milestone of **Timeless Body (Level 15)**. Joined by his sister Klee'errk and four devoted disciples—[[Kree-uk]], [[Qeerik]], [[Arak-tai]], and [[Sool-arrk]]—Master Tvyordiy returned through the summit gate of Mount Nimbus, noting the crisp mountain air tasted clean, light, and vibrant. Arriving back in Leilon in 1502 DR, they established the open-air courtyard dojo at [[Aufox Manor]], drilling the grown wards in balance and the Way of the Hard Sign until departing in 1505 DR to reunite with his companions at the Wayside Inn.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Player & Dual-PC Dynamics:** Played by Peter as a dual-PC / DMPC alongside Gary's frontline builds.
* **Interlude Level Freeze Policy:** Peter intentionally froze level ups during the homebrew Interludes (*"Shadow's Run"* and *"Keeping the Faith"*) to keep party progression strictly tethered to the published trilogy tiers and prevent out-leveling the adventure module math.
* **Linguistic Origin (Твёрдый знак / Ъ):** Named after the Russian Cyrillic "Hard Sign" (Ъ) inspired by conversations at work with coworker Grigory. The symbol represents firmness, hardness, and unyielding impact—directly reflecting Tvyordiy's stoic temperament, wereboar constitution, and concussive martial strikes.
* **Oriental Adventures Homage (*Qiang*):** Flavoring Tvyordiy's starting spear as a ***Qiang*** was Peter's deliberate nod to classic 1e/3e *Oriental Adventures* monk weaponry.
* **Table Roleplay:** Peter voiced Tvyordiy with a deadpan, baritone gravel that became an enduring comedy point whenever interacting with excitable NPCs.
* **Gary's Table Nickname ("Wereboar-diy Znak"):** Following the infamous wereboar encounter outside Dumathoin's Gulch in *Divine Contention*, Gary affectionately and permanently nicknamed him **"Wereboar-diy Znak"** (or *"Wearboar-diy"*)—an enduring table joke that stuck with the character whenever feral brawn, tusks, or monk agility were discussed.
* **The Wereboar Lycanthropy Ruling:** While most parties immediately cast *Remove Curse* after the [[Dumathoin's Gulch]] wereboar ambush in *Divine Contention*, Peter and Gary leaned into the mechanical and roleplay chaos of an Aarakocra Drunken Master Monk who gained 17 Strength and hybrid wereboar tusks, perfectly complementing the unyielding "Hard Sign" (Ъ) concept.
