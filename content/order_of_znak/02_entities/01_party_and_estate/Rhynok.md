# 🏹 Rhynok

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Silent, watchful, and lethal. Moves through shadowed forest canopies, fetid marshlands, and subterranean caverns without leaving a footprint or a sound.
* **Upbringing & Sworn Faith:** Raised by his **Uncle Christos** in the vast, untamed swamps of the Forgotten Realms—learning the deadly arts of stealth, silent tracking, swamp navigation, and survival from an early age. Holds deep reverence and high regard for the nature deities **Silvanus** (the Oak Father) and **Mielikki** (Lady of the Forest).
* **Alignment:** **Neutral Evil** (ruthlessly pragmatic, operating by a cold, self-interested code of survival and lethal efficiency, while fiercely loyal to his comrades in the Order).
* **Character Feature — *Heart of Darkness*:**
  > *"Those who look into your eyes can see that you have faced unimaginable horror and that you are no stranger to darkness. Though they might fear you, commoners will extend you every courtesy and do their utmost to help you. Unless you have shown yourself to be a danger to them, they will even take up arms to fight alongside you, should you find yourself facing an enemy alone."*
* **Background:** Criminal / Smuggler. Master of disguises, covert waterways, and concealed pathways before placing his deadeye marksmanship at the service of [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Wilderness Mastery & Sworn Quarry:** Born to the tangled boughs of forest and swamp, harboring a lifelong, merciless enmity toward **Orcs** and the **Undead**, and harvesting deadly serpent venoms during quiet days between campaigns.

## 📜 In-Universe Chronicle & Key Deeds

### 🏔️ [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
* **Awakening the Wilds at [[Umbrage Hill]] & [[Temple of Abbathor]]:** After helping drive off the manticore besieging [[Adabra Gwynn]]'s windmill at [[Umbrage Hill]], Rhynok began weaving primal woodland magic into his craft—conjuring nourishing *Goodberries* and turning his arrows into bursts of piercing thorns (*Hail of Thorns*). Exiting the [[Temple of Abbathor]] with the dwarves' *Sending Stones*, Rhynok's lightning reflexes and sworn hatred of orc-kind cut down an ambushing pack of marauding orcs before they could close the canyon gap, and he embraced the shadows of the wilds as an unseen **Gloom Stalker**.
* **Crossbow Ambush in [[Gnomengarde]] & The Outhouse Strike at [[Butterskull Ranch]]:** Took a surprise bolt from manic gnome inventor [[Facktoré]]'s autoloading crossbow contraption upon entering [[Gnomengarde]] before helping slay the barrel-room mimic, and later spearheaded the stealth assault at [[Butterskull Ranch]]—dropping the sentry at the ranch outhouse and creeping up to peek in the farmhouse window before helping defeat twelve marauding orcs to rescue [[Alfonse Kalazorn|"Big Al" Kalazorn]] and [[Petunia (cow)|Petunia]].
* **[[Loggers' Camp]], [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] & The Shaved One-Eyed Goat of Gruumsh:** Guided the party's ox-drawn supply wagon through Neverwinter Wood—spotting a shapeshifted anchorite boar fleeing along the trail—and helped slay the burrowing ankhegs at [[Tibor Wester]]'s camp. Bringing a bottle of wine to [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]], Rhynok helped **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** drive off [[Cryovain]], and as the party departed heading southeast toward the [[Woodland Manse]], Falcon rode out on [[Baatorius]] to offer his ***Boots of Elvenkind***—which everyone immediately agreed had Rhynok's name all over them! Approaching the [[Woodland Manse]] with a **shaved, one-eyed goat** tattooed by [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Stoneface]] with an Orcish prophecy of **Gruumsh**'s wrath, the party set the goat loose to terrify a large contingent of the stationed orcs into fleeing before Rhynok opened the assault from ambush.
* **The Awakened Crab's Gift at the [[Tower of Storms]] & [[Shrine of Savras]]:** After befriending the awakened giant crab on the beach, slaying the cliffside harpies and undead anchorite [[Moesko]], destroying Moesko's beating heart atop the beacon, and returning the conch shell to banshee [[Miraal]], Rhynok received an enchanted **+1 Longbow** retrieved by the crab from the wreck of the *Star-Crossed Lover*! Wielding his new bow alongside [[DonJon Raskin]] at the [[Shrine of Savras]], Rhynok helped fell the occupying horde of orcs and ogres and laid hands upon the altar to behold the vision of [[Cryovain]] atop [[Icespire Hold]] before the party sold the shrine's solid gold bell for 2,500 gp.
* **Carrying the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Wererat Battle:** When [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] discovered their mundane weapons could not scratch [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s lycanthrope pack, Rhynok's enchanted **+1 Longbow** and [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s radiant magic carried the entire battle—running off the surviving wererats as Zeleen escaped!
* **[[Axeholm]], [[Circle of Thunder]], & The *Boots of Elvenkind*:** Silvered a shortsword in [[Phandalin]], rappelled down the chimney of [[Axeholm]] to pick off ceiling-climbing giant spiders with six arrows and banish [[Vyldara]] the banshee, and helped slay the Invisible Stalker at [[Dragon Barrow]] before enlisting centaur ally **[[Xanth]]**. Together they rained lethal arrows into [[Yargath]]'s ten-orc patrol, felled [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar, ten twig blights, and two anchorites at the [[Circle of Thunder]], and marched back to purge the [[Woodland Manse]] of twelve twig blights, five vine blights, two anchorites ([[Grannoc]]), twelve stirges, and eight needle blights. Returning victorious to [[Phandalin]] where the evacuated [[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]] had taken shelter after Gorthok ransacked his lodge, Rhynok claimed his prized ***Boots of Elvenkind*** (which later inspired him to hunt down a matching ***Cloak of Elvenkind***).
* **Slaying [[Cryovain]] at [[Icespire Hold]]:** Cut down mountain **Hobgoblins** in close quarters with his **Silvered Shortsword**, helped subdue the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]], and drove deadeye arrows from his **+1 Longbow** into a stunned **[[Cryovain]]** atop the frozen roof of [[Icespire Hold]].

### ⛈️ [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
* **Pioneering Combat Netting & Foiling Backstabs:** In the wilderness outside Phandalin, Rhynok used weighted hunting nets to round up panicked Giant Goats during a wyvern scare, launching his signature net-fighting mastery. In Phandalin, he netted Master Thief [[Velleen Firecrow]] on round one, completely denying her sneak attack backstabs before tracking her down with an upcast *Hunter's Mark* to recover the secret crystalline lenses (*Eyes of Minute Seeing*).
* **The Extradimensional Sniper & Swamp Mastery:** Deployed ***Rope Trick*** in the House of Thalivar to snipe Star-Spawn Manglers from safety, and guided the party through the Mere of Dead Men without a single misstep using his swamp Favored Terrain. In the sea approach to Thunder Cliffs, used his newly won ***Ring of Water Walking*** and a sea-air *Rope Trick* to execute the Giant Crab, lifted merchant [[Sovendahl Erkinze]]'s curse to claim his prized ***Dagger of Venom***, and supported the downfall of Captain [[Kristoffen]] and [[Gadrille]].

### 🐲 [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
* **High Road Owlbear Ambush & 15-Mile Woodland Navigation:** When Daniel Renault's private coach was ambushed fifty-five miles north of Neverwinter, Rhynok opened fire with his *+1 Longbow*, rolling a 23 on initiative and landing multiple opening-round sneak attacks (scoring a 31-to-hit critical hit) to fell the flanking predators. Leaving retainers Liefold and Joffrey with the coach, Rhynok leveraged his Gloom Stalker tracking expertise to guide the Order across fifteen grueling miles of dense, uncharted old-growth forest directly to the cult's hidden redoubt.
* **Silent Infiltration & Subterranean Net Warfare:** Sniped perimeter sentries silently from the shadows with his *+1 Longbow*, allowing the party to breach the upper estate without raising general alarms. Descending into the lightless subterranean tunnels, Rhynok helped discover the hidden masonry compartment in Room 1, deployed his weighted hunting net to immobilize cult skirmishers, and burned four hit dice staunched during grueling tunnel clashes while the party gathered the four ritual stones.
* **Dragon Door Vanguard & Slaying the Shadow Dragon:** When the basalt Dragon Door unsealed, Rhynok drove venomous fire into Fanatic F's inner vanguard. In the lightless cavern of the **Shadow Dragon**, Rhynok leveraged Gloom Stalker invisibility along the stalactites, raining arrows from his *+1 Longbow* before dropping into melee with his ***Dagger of Venom***—landing a natural 20 critical strike (10 piercing + 16 poison damage) that crippled the wyrm alongside Krageer's *Dragon Slayer* blade. After securing chained child **Heracio Renault**, Rhynok claimed a shirt of **Adamantine Chain Mail** from the dragon's hoard to bring back to Phandalin/Leilon to be custom-reforged into his dream crowd-control weapon: the **Adamantine Net**.

### 🐲 [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]] & [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Deeping Cave Acid Relay & Hunt for [[Viantha Cruelhex]]:** Loaned his *Ring of Water Walking* to [[Myagkiy Znak]] to ferry the party safely across the boiling acid pools of [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s lair, ambushed and slew undead warlock [[Viantha Cruelhex]] in Kryptgarden Forest, and supported the volcano showdown against the **Fire Giant Queen** in *"Keeping the Faith"*.

### ⚡ [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
* **The Extradimensional Sniper of [[Dumathoin's Gulch]]:** When spectral sword wraiths swarmed the canyon, Rhynok cast ***Rope Trick***, climbed into the unseen pocket dimension above the battlefield, pulled up the rope, and rained poisoned arrows down with total cover and impunity. Inside the dwarven shrine, he persuaded eidolon **[[Sister Silvergranite]]** to yield the volatile **Gnomengarde grenade** before spearheading the destruction of a lethal **Boneclaw**.
* **Piercing the Glamour of *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath]]* & Naval Marksman:** Aboard Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s caravel *Twinkle*, Rhynok's keen elven senses pierced the illusory human disguise projected by the ship's figurehead—uncovering their friendly Bregan D'aerthe drow allies. On the return voyage from Neverwinter, he picked off Invisible Stalkers and enemy siege crews aboard [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships, and delivered lethal suppressing fire aboard the Talosite flagship dreadnaught against **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Optimization Strategy:** Played by Gary. Gary built Rhynok to push 5e's ranged opening-round Nova damage to its mathematical ceiling, combining Gloom Stalker's *Dread Ambusher*, Assassin's *Assassinate*, *Sharpshooter*, and *Poisoner*.
* **Interlude Level Freeze Policy:** Peter intentionally froze level ups during the homebrew Interludes (*"Shadow's Run"* and *"Keeping the Faith"*) to keep party progression strictly tethered to the published trilogy tiers and prevent out-leveling the adventure module math.
* **The "Rope Trick" Pocket Sniper:** One of Gary's signature tactical plays was having Rhynok cast *Rope Trick*, climb into the invisible extradimensional space, pull the rope up, and snipe from complete cover.
* **Downtime Snake Venom Milking:** Between quests, Gary regularly sent Rhynok out into the swamps and foothills to hunt and milk **giant poisonous snakes**, keeping his *Poisoner* vials stocked with fresh natural toxins.
