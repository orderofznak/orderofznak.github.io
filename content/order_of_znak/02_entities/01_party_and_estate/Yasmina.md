# 🔮 Yasmina

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Origin & Rescue:** Rescued as a frightened young girl from the isolated woodland hovel of sinister Night Hag **[[Balheelsa]]** in Neverwinter Wood by [[The Order of Znak]] during [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]] (July 4–5, 2022).
* **Eerie Hag Lineage & Soul Lore:** Revealed to be the fatherless captive daughter of Balheelsa. During her imprisonment, Yasmina bore witness to horrific occult rites—learning that Balheelsa trafficked and sold stolen mortal souls into the Lower Planes to fuel the cosmic war between fiends and devils (the Blood War), and often **imprisoned screaming human spirits inside the hollow heads of porcelain dolls**.
* **Special Bond with Tvyordiy Znak:** Upon her liberation, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] stepped forward to comfort her. Recognizing his own impoverished, street-urchin childhood reflected in Yasmina's terrified eyes, the stoic Aarakocra monk formed a deeply protective, lifelong bond with the girl, ensuring she would never face hunger or neglect again.
* **Ward of Aufox Manor & Young Portrait Artist:** Welcomed into the Order's estate in [[Leilon]] under the watchful shelter of [[Myagkiy Znak]] and majordomo **[[Leopold]]** alongside fellow wards **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]**. During the 1-year interim leading into *Divine Contention*, Yasmina participated in daily combat and wilderness lessons alongside the Order and local lizardfolk allies. Shortly after her rescue, young Yasmina canonically drew and painted expressive portraits of her four saviors—immortalizing **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** as tokens of profound child-like gratitude.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Introduction:** Introduced by Gary during his DM adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*, serving as a central rescue objective of the Night Hag dungeon crawl alongside the recovery of the *Right Hand of Gond* and [[Dayne Beltran]]'s +2 Plate Armor.
* **Tvyordiy's Urchin Upbringing:** In Discord table chat on July 4, 2022, Peter noted: *"He saw himself in his urchin upbringing in Yasmina"*, establishing the emotional connection between Tvyordiy and the rescued orphan.
* **In-Universe Portraits:** The four stylized portraits of the founding Order champions (`Krageer_Portrait.png`, `Rhynock_Portrait.png`, `TZnak_Portrait.png`, and `MZnak_Portrait.png`) were shared in Discord by Gary on July 22, 2022 with the note: *"Portraits of The Order of Z'nak sent as a token of appreciation done by Yasmina"*, establishing them as canonical in-universe artifacts.
* **Bridge to Barovia:** Designed as a living narrative bridge between the two campaigns—growing from a traumatized child ward in *The Order of Znak* into an adventurer ready for Ravenloft in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
