# 🔮 Yasmina

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Origin & Rescue:** Rescued as a young girl from the clutches of a sinister Night Hag in the Sword Coast wilderness by [[The Order of Znak]] during [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]].
* **Eerie Hag Lineage:** Despite her liberation, prolonged confinement under the hag left her with an indelible witch-like aura, granting unusual fey-fiendish resilience and intuitive spellcasting.
* **Ward of the Order & Young Portrait Artist:** Raised under the watchful eye and spiritual shelter of [[Myagkiy Znak]] and the [[Leilon]] headquarters alongside [[Smithwell and Burnice]]. Shortly after her rescue, young Yasmina canonically drew and painted expressive portraits of her four saviors—[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], [[Rhynok]], [[Tvyordiy Znak]], and [[Myagkiy Znak]]—preserving an enduring child's-eye tribute to the founding champions of the Order.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (*The Acolytes of Znak*)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Cleric / Fiend Warlock
* **Heritage Traits:** Custom hag-touched lineage traits blending divine protection with dark occult patron boons.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Introduction:** Introduced by Gary during his DM adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*, serving as a central rescue objective of the Night Hag dungeon crawl alongside the recovery of [[Dayne Beltran]]'s +2 Plate Armor.
* **In-Universe Portraits:** The four stylized portraits of the founding Order champions (`Krageer_Portrait.png`, `Rhynock_Portrait.png`, `TZnak_Portrait.png`, and `MZnak_Portrait.png`) are canonically attributed in-universe to young Yasmina's artwork created shortly after she was welcomed into the Leilon estate.
* **Bridge to Barovia:** Designed as a narrative bridge between the two campaigns—growing from a rescued ward in *The Order of Znak* into an adventurer ready for Ravenloft in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
