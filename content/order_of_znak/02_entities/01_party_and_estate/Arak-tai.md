# 🦅 Arak-tai (Harrier Powerhouse & Disciple)

* **Identity & Heritage:** Broad-shouldered, powerful male Aarakocra of the northern harrier lineage from the Boundless Whorl. Disciple of [[Tvyordiy Znak]].
* **Martial Focus:** Specializes in crushing unarmed strikes and stunning blows (*Open Hand Technique*), channeling heavy kinetic force with grounded discipline.
* **Role at Aufox Manor:** Traveled to Toril to train under Tvyordiy at [[Aufox Manor]] in [[Leilon]], sparring with [[Burnice Znak]], [[Yasmina]], and fellow disciples.
