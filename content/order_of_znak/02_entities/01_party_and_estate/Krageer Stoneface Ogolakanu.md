# 🛡️ Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Personality & Demeanor:** A towering, athletic Goliath warrior with a hard, worn countenance. Known as "Stoneface" among the members of the Ogolakanu Clan due to his stoic lack of emotion and minimal social skills. Fearless, immovable, and relentlessly loyal to his companions, measuring worth by martial prowess, honor in battle, and the quality of campfire food.
* **Background:** Soldier (Ogolakanu Clan). Forged through rigorous mountain discipline and battlefield skirmishes before finding his true brothers-in-arms in [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Battlefield Role:** The unstoppable frontline juggernaut of the company—charging headfirst into the fiercest melees, shrugging off elemental and physical punishment through the primal spirit of the Bear Totem, and cleaving foes in two with sweeping two-handed blows and *Great Weapon Master* strikes.
* **Tattoos & Markings:**
  * **Left Ribcage ("Fallen"):** Inked with the word *"Fallen"* in Giant, below which are etched **81 X's and 1 Lightning Bolt**, keeping a grim, unyielding tally of fallen comrades and storm-shattered foes.
  * **Right Ribcage ("Qolzaz" & The Rat):** Inked with a small tattoo of what appears to be a dead rat, alongside a crowned bird and the name **"[[Matriarch Qolzaz|Qolzaz]]"**—commemorating the lizardfolk queen slain during the Mere of Dead Men alliance and celebrated in the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***.
  * **Right Shoulder, Back, Arm & Leg Tribal Sleeve:** An intricate, expansive tribal design cascading down his right shoulder, across the right side of his back, and extending all the way down his right arm and right leg.

## 📜 In-Universe Chronicle & Key Deeds

### 🏔️ [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
* **The Gem of Greed at the [[Temple of Abbathor]]:** After helping drive off the manticore at [[Umbrage Hill]] and slaying the corrosive Ochre Jellies in the Dwarven Excavation, Krageer excavated the rubble tunnel to the inner Statue of Abbathor. Tempted by its glowing jewel, he pried at the gem and triggered its fiery explosive trap—taking the full brunt of the blast even as his newly honed danger instincts warned him of the peril. Emerging from the canyon to slaughter an orc ambush, Krageer bonded with the primal spirit of the **Bear Totem**.
* **[[Gnomengarde]] & [[Butterskull Ranch]]:** Roughed up gnome sentries [[Pog]] and [[Ulla]] in the tense aftermath of [[Facktoré]]'s crossbow ambush before helping slay the **Mimic in the barrel room**, and ambushed the twelve orc raiders at [[Butterskull Ranch]] to rescue [[Alfonse Kalazorn|"Big Al" Kalazorn]] and [[Petunia (cow)|Petunia]].
* **The Tattooed One-Eyed Goat of Gruumsh & Double Down at the [[Woodland Manse]]:** After staying at [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] (bringing a bottle of wine for room and board), the party marched toward the [[Woodland Manse]] with a **shaved, one-eyed goat** in tow. Before reaching the clearing, **Stoneface tattooed a prophetic warning in Orcish directly onto the goat's hide**: *"Take this goat as an offering of sustenance. For if you do not take it and flee from this mansion and cease your worship of false and weak gods then my wrath will come upon thee. My blood spear is unforgiving and though I only have one eye... it sees all. — He Who Watches, He Who Never Sleeps, The One Eyed God, Gruumsh."* Setting the goat loose terrified a large contingent of the stationed orcs into fleeing, though in the ensuing courtyard clash Krageer fell unconscious **twice**—dropped a second time at the edge of the trees by needle blight spines before **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** swooped back from his **flight** and hauled the pin-cushioned Goliath to safety. To honor the bravery Tvyordiy showed in saving his life, Krageer got a tattoo on his right ribcage of a bird wearing a crown. 
* **The 2,500 gp Gold Bell of the [[Shrine of Savras]]:** Fought shoulder-to-shoulder with [[DonJon Raskin]] against a massive warband of orcs and ogres at the [[Shrine of Savras]], beheld the altar's divinatory vision of [[Cryovain]] atop [[Icespire Hold]], and scaled the crumbling belfry tower with [[Tvyordiy Znak]] to pry loose the shrine's **solid gold bell**, which the party sold for **2,500 gp**.
* **The Wererat Lesson, Silvered Steel & Cleansing [[Axeholm]]:** When his mundane iron greataxe proved powerless to wound [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s lycanthrope pack at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] (where Rhynok and Myagkiy ran off the surviving wererats as Zeleen escaped), Krageer marched back to [[Phandalin]] and had his **Greataxe coated in silver**, vowing never again to stand helpless before an enemy. Rappelling down the chimney of [[Axeholm]], he raged through a surprise ambush on the giant spiders, carved through ghouls and the moon elf banshee [[Vyldara]], and claimed the crimson-eyed ***Dread Helm*** from the Castellan's hidden fireplace vault before putting down the undead Castellan ghoul.
* **The *Dragon Slayer* Blade & The Storm of Neverwinter Wood:** Delving into [[Dragon Barrow]] to overcome a Will-o'-Wisp and the Invisible Stalker, Krageer claimed Lady Tanamere Alagondar's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** and enlisted centaur ally **[[Xanth]]**. Together they slaughtered [[Yargath]]'s ten-orc patrol, crushed **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, ten twig blights, and the two anchorites ([[Flenz]] and [[Narux]]) at the [[Circle of Thunder]] to satisfy Xanth, and marched back to the [[Woodland Manse]] to wipe out [[Grannoc]], his anchorite cohort, twelve twig blights, five vine blights, twelve stirges, and eight needle blights.
* **Slaying [[Cryovain]] Atop [[Icespire Hold]]:** After cutting through mountain hobgoblins and the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]], Krageer ascended to the frozen roof of [[Icespire Hold]]. When [[Tvyordiy Znak]] locked [[Cryovain]] in place with a series of stunning blows amidst four cackling Ice Mephits, Krageer roared into a rage and unleashed devastating salvos with his ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, felling the white dragon!

### ⛈️ [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
* **The Wayside Ambush & Leilon Quayside Defense:** Cleaved through the undead horde beseiging [[The Wayside Inn]], standing vanguard alongside [[Martisha Vinetalker]]'s caravan. In [[Leilon]], helped corral panicked town children away from the marshes before facing the water-steed raid of the Drowned Cult, trading blows with water elementals and berserkers.
* **The Phandalin Ogre Ambush & Veleen Hunt:** Stood watch over [[Tanor]]'s draft wagons along the foothills, chopping through seven marauding ogres targeting the goat herds. In Phandalin, chased the fleeing master thief [[Veleen]] into [[Rhynok]]'s net snare.
* **Shattering the Cult at the Wayside Inn:** Returned to the storm-battered Wayside Inn, braving swirling air elemental myrmidons and clearing the courtyard. Breached the cellar, withstood the lightning discharges of the animate **Statue of Talos**, and shattered the stone idol with crushing greataxe blows while freeing Martisha.
* **Mutiny on the *Iceflow* & Assault on Thunder Cliffs:** Intimidated Captain [[Kristoffen]]'s mutinous crew into dropping their cutlasses alongside Myagkiy's calming magic. At [[Thunder Cliffs]], braved the violent gale winds to smash the giant sea crab threatening the rowboat, executed the traitorous half-dragon Kristoffen, and slaughtered the dual hounds **Tooth and Claw** to dismantle the Talosite coastal redoubt.
* **Mere of Dead Men Alliance & The Fallen Matriarch:** Clashed with the marshland lizardfolk and slew [[Matriarch Qolzaz]] (later commemorated with a ribcage tattoo and the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***), before forging an enduring alliance with lizardfolk sovereign [[Grixxugroaz]] by driving off invading rot trolls.
* **Hearth & Romance with [[Cooragh]]:** Courted half-orc head cook [[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]] at the Wayside Inn, won over by her iron will and alarmingly spicy pork pies.

### 🐲 [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
* **High Road Owlbear Ambush & Estate Breach:** When Daniel Renault's private carriage was ambushed fifty-five miles north of Neverwinter, Krageer bounded into the flanking brush with his greataxe to butcher the attacking owlbears. At the cult's hidden woodland compound, he shattered heavy timber barricades on the first floor to corner sentries before alarms could spread through the estate.
* **Tunnel Warfare & The Basalt Dragon Door:** Descended into the lightless subterranean depths beneath the estate, burning through four hit dice staunched during fierce clashes with shadowy horrors and cultist skirmishers while helping uncover the four ancient ritual stones. When the circular basalt Dragon Door was unlocked, Krageer roared into the inner sanctum, carving through Fanatic F's vanguard defenders.
* **Slaying the Shadow Dragon with the *Dragon Slayer* Blade:** In the lightless abyssal cavern, Krageer went into a full Bear Totem rage and drew Lady Alagondar's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** claimed from Dragon Barrow. Bounding onto the wyrm's back, Krageer brought the blade down in two-handed overhead chops, landing a devastating natural 20 critical strike that seared through the beast's necrotic defenses until the Shadow Dragon shuddered and collapsed into ash—freeing kidnapped child **Heracio Renault**.

### 🐲 [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]
* **The Salt Caves of [[Leilon Point]]:** Infiltrated the subterranean Salt Caves; accidentally detonated the *Glyph of Warding* in S1; heaved aside the heavy boulder in S3 with an Advantage Athletics check to uncover the hidden cache; and in S4 cast *Speak with Animals* on the Giant Skeletal Shark (discovering mindless undead bones have nothing to say) before executing a 28 Athletics charge and landing Adamantine Greataxe crits.
* **The Ghost Ship (*The Relentless*):** Infiltrated the beached Talosite warship with group stealth; survived lightning strikes on deck; battled skeletal minotaurs and First Mate [[Gol Badwind]] below decks (landing brutal 27-to-hit greataxe crits); chopped animated hammocks to shreds in the crew quarters; and claimed the ***Alchemy Jug*** from Gol's quarters.
* **The Iniarv's Tower Roof Collapse & Ogre Bottleneck:** Battled zombies, chimera riders, and rampart snipers at Iniarv's Tower. On the decaying upper floor, stepped onto weakened masonry and collapsed through the ceiling into a zombie nest below—rolling **double 11s** on his Dex save despite *Danger Sense*! The falling debris crushed half the zombies instantly, and Krageer held the main tower doorway bottleneck to cut down wounded Ogre Zombies one by one.
* **The Bronze Shrine & Deeping Cave Acid Charge:** Cleaved orc ambushers along the sea cliffs with a 28-crit greataxe blow. Later at Deeping Cave, grew impatient waiting for the party's *Water Walk* relay and charged straight through the boiling caustic acid pools in a Bear Totem rage. Helped slay [[Viantha Cruelhex]] and the Sword Wraiths, and acted as the "sacrificial lamb" triggering the poison gas vault trap before [[Tvyordiy Znak]] looted the hoard.
* **Level 11 Milestone:** Advanced to **Level 11** at the conclusion of Act 3 alongside his brothers-in-arms.

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Confronting Graag the Werebear:** Clashed with [[Graag]] the Werebear in Neverwinter, absorbing the lycanthrope's ferocious claws and teeth with Bear Totem endurance while hewing down the monster's cultist henchmen.
* **Shrugging Off the Chain Devil's Unnerving Mask:** In the Starmetal Hills cave, stood firm when a fiendish Chain Devil attempted to paralyze him with its shifting, nightmarish *Unnerving Mask*—gritting his teeth and breaking the devil's vanguard.
* **Mount Hotenow Traps & The Ring of Animal Influence:** Helped Tvyordiy disarm engineered mechanical traps and a trapped chest in Mount Hotenow, claiming **four Potions of Greater Healing** and an enchanted ***Ring of Animal Influence*** (+ additional magical effect).
* **The "Double-Enlarge" Titan Grapple vs. Queen Kevlaan:** Inside the volcanic caldera, quaffed [[Puck Caribdas|Puck's]] ***Potion of Growth*** (`Medium` &rarr; `Large`) while [[Myagkiy Znak]] cast ***Enlarge*** (`Large` &rarr; `Huge`). Swelling to true **Huge** titan size, Krageer engaged Fire Giant Queen [[Kevlaan the Merciless]] in an epic, bare-handed wrestling grapple at the edge of the lava—pinning her until Tvyordiy stunned her and Krageer smashed her into the obsidian floor!
* **Gond's Blessing — The *Lucky* Feat:** Reclaiming the *Right Hand of Gond*, Krageer was blessed by Forge Cleric [[Flanning Teldorer]] with Gond's divine favor, gaining the permanent ***Lucky*** feat to turn future critical blunders into miraculous survival.
* **Puck's Growth Potion Endorsements:** Following his volcano triumph, offered Puck two hilarious dad-joke endorsement quotes for his potion labels (*"I am a huge fan of Puck's Potion of Growth"* / *"I didn't care for the taste at first, but I grew to like it"*).

### ⚡ [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
* **Psychic Agony at Thalivar's Beacon:** Battled Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers** atop the House of Thalivar to claim **[[The Ruinstone]]**, enduring the one form of pain his Bear Totem rage could not dull—mind-rending psychic damage ("brain pain").
* **The Unlikely Envoy & High Seas Archer:** Sailing to Neverwinter aboard Captain [[Deloz Montiv]]'s *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath]]*, Krageer stepped forward when every other appeal failed and personally persuaded **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** to commit his full military aid to Leilon. On the return voyage against [[Galas Windrage]]'s warships (*Throatcutter* and *Thunderstrike*), Krageer set aside his axe to gleefully trade longbow arrow volleys across the waves.
* **Dreadnaught Climax:** Wielding his legendary greataxe ***[[Ultima Exodus]]***, Krageer stormed the Talosite flagship dreadnaught beneath the tempest apparition of [[Talos]], helping vanquish **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an Air Elemental Myrmidon, and a Nalfeshnee demon.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Optimization Strategy:** Gary min-maxed Krageer for maximum DPR and survivability, blending Bear Totem universal damage resistance with *Reckless Attack*, *Great Weapon Master* (-5/+10), and Champion Fighter's 19–20 critical threat range.
* **The *Danger Sense* Double-Fail (DC 15):** At Level 2 inside the [[Temple of Abbathor]], right after unlocking *Danger Sense*, Krageer pried at the trapped gem in the Statue of Abbathor—rolling with Advantage on his DC 15 Dexterity save, yet **rolling under 15 on both d20s**!
* **The Iniarv's Tower Roof Collapse (Double 11s):** In a hilarious recurrence of Danger Sense irony, during the exploration of Iniarv's Tower in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, Krageer stepped onto a rotting stone roof. When the ceiling caved in, Gary rolled his DC 12 Dexterity save with advantage and produced **double 11s** (`11 11`), plunging into a zombie horde below—though the crashing masonry crushed half the zombies to paste on impact!
* **Interlude Level Freeze Policy:** Peter intentionally froze level ups during the homebrew Interludes (*"Shadow's Run"* and *"Keeping the Faith"*). Even though the party completed substantial boss battles (such as the Shadow Dragon and Fire Giant Queen), milestone advancement was strictly tethered to the published trilogy acts so the party did not over-level the official adventure encounters.
* **Fighter Multiclass Timing:** Analysis of the combat logs confirms Krageer fought as a pure Barbarian through the end of *Storm Lord's Wrath* (relying on Bear Totem resistance and *Great Weapon Master*). His Champion Fighter dip (*Second Wind*, *Great Weapon Fighting* rerolls, and *Action Surge*) debuted during *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*.
* **The "Rule of Cool" Double-Enlarge (`Medium` &rarr; `Large` &rarr; `Huge`):** During the volcano climax of *"Keeping the Faith"*, a single *Enlarge* effect would only increase a Medium Goliath to Large against the **Huge** Fire Giant Queen. Invoking the Rule of Cool, Peter and Gary stacked **[[Puck Caribdas]]'s *Potion of Growth*** with **[[Myagkiy Znak]]'s *Enlarge* spell**, allowing Krageer to reach true **Huge** size and wrestle the Fire Giant monarch as an equal!
* **Puck's *Potion of Growth* Label Endorsements:** Because punny dad-jokes were right up Krageer's alley, Peter suggested at the table that Krageer give [[Puck Caribdas]] two official endorsement quotes to print on his potion bottle labels:
  * *"I am a huge fan of Puck's Potion of Growth." — Krageer*
  * *"I didn't care for the taste at first, but I grew to like it." — Also Krageer*
* **Inside Joke — *Ultima Exodus*:** The signature greataxe was named after the classic RPG *Ultima III: Exodus*, an intentional inside joke between Gary and Peter as lifelong fans of the *Ultima* franchise.
* **Running Table Gag — Wayside Inn Meat Pies:** Krageer's courtship of [[Cooragh]] was an improvised table running gag—his affection for her was driven almost entirely by his obsession with her alarmingly spicy tavern pork pies.
* **The Accidental "Party Face":** Despite having an abysmal Charisma score of 6 (-2 modifier), Gary frequently volunteered Krageer to act as the party's diplomat. Defying all statistical odds, Krageer rolled remarkably high on Persuasion checks and succeeded a surprising number of times—most notably swaying [[Dagult Neverember]] in Neverwinter.
