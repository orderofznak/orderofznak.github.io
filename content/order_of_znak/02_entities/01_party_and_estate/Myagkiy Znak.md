# 🕊️ Myagkiy Znak (Ь)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Core Philosophy:** Dedicated to the preservation and defense of life at all costs, standing as the compassionate moral center and spiritual anchor of [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Background:** Hermit. Years of solitary contemplation and prayer gave way to a sacred calling to heal the wounded and shield the Sword Coast from elemental and necromantic cults.
* **Battlefield Role:** A fearless heavy battle priest who wades directly into the teeth of the melee clad in gleaming plate and shield—anchoring the frontline beside [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] while calling down swirling hosts of radiant *Spirit Guardians* and restorative blessings.

## 📜 In-Universe Chronicle & Key Deeds

### 🏔️ [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
* **Frontline Healer of the Frontier:** Arriving in [[Phandalin]] already ordained in the **Life Domain**, Myagkiy sustained the company through their early trials at [[Umbrage Hill]], the [[Temple of Abbathor]], [[Gnomengarde]], [[Butterskull Ranch]], the desperate fighting retreat from the [[Woodland Manse]], and the ankheg nests of the [[Loggers' Camp]].
* **Sanctifying the [[Tower of Storms]]:** Inside the storm-lashed lighthouse of the [[Tower of Storms]], Myagkiy recognized the desecrated altar and iconography of **[[Talos]]**, god of storms, before helping slay the undead anchorite [[Moesko]], destroy his beating heart atop the beacon tower, and lay the banshee [[Miraal]] to rest.
* **Carrying the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Wererat Battle:** After fighting alongside [[DonJon Raskin]] against the orcs and ogres at the [[Shrine of Savras]] (and selling the shrine's solid gold bell for 2,500 gp), the Order confronted [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s wererat gang at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]. When neither Krageer nor Tvyordiy could wound the lycanthropes with mundane steel, Myagkiy stepped forward and carried the melee—melting the wererat pack with swirling ***Spirit Guardians***, his floating ***Spiritual Weapon***, and searing columns of ***Sacred Flame*** alongside [[Rhynok]]'s enchanted bow to run off the surviving wererats as Zeleen escaped!
* **Prayerful *Guidance* & The Secret Vault of [[Axeholm]]:** At the sealed dwarven fortress of [[Axeholm]], Myagkiy invoked prayerful ***Guidance*** over each companion so all four rappelled down the mountain chimney without a single slip. After summoning his *Spiritual Weapon* in the surprise ambush against the giant spiders, helping purge the lower halls of ghouls, and banishing the moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**, Myagkiy's sharp eyes spotted a hidden iron vault concealed inside the fireplace of the Castellan's Bedchamber—claiming and attuning the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power*** before the party slew the undead Castellan ghoul.
* **The Storm of the [[Circle of Thunder]], The *+1 Shield* & [[Woodland Manse]] Cleanup:** Marching into Neverwinter Wood with centaur ally **[[Xanth]]**, Myagkiy unleashed his radiant ***Spirit Guardians*** to shred [[Yargath]]'s ten-orc patrol and the ten twig blights during the epic hilltop clash against **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, [[Flenz]], and [[Narux]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]]. Claiming the enchanted ***+1 Shield*** from the anchorite caves as the party's sole shield-bearer, Myagkiy marched back to the [[Woodland Manse]] and maintained his ***Spirit Guardians*** through a fierce five-round courtyard battle against twelve twig blights, five entangling vine blights, and two anchorites ([[Grannoc]]), before helping clear twelve stirges and eight needle blights.
* **Blessing the Rooftop Clash Against [[Cryovain]]:** Atop the frozen battlements of [[Icespire Hold]], Myagkiy invoked ***Bless*** upon his brothers-in-arms and commanded his floating ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat down the **four cackling Ice Mephits**, clearing the way for Tvyordiy, Krageer, and Rhynok to stun and slay the white dragon **[[Cryovain]]**.

### ⛈️ [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]
* **Wayside Deliverance & The Leilon Inundation:** Cast *Turn Undead* and healed stranded commoners spiked to the palisade gates during the siege of [[The Wayside Inn]]. In [[Leilon]], helped shepherd terrified town children to safety before unleashing radiant magic against the Drowned Cult's kraken priest and water elementals.
* **Adoptive Father & Spiritual Hearth of [[Leilon]]:** Served as the compassionate heart of the party upon arriving in Leilon, rescuing orphaned ten-year-olds **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** from the marsh fringes after defeating kraken priest [[Nixoxious]], adopting them into the Znak family at Aufox Manor under majordomo [[Leopold]], and mentoring young [[Burnice Znak|Burnice]] in sacred devotion.
* **Fellowship with [[Merrygold Brightshine]]:** Forged a close spiritual bond with halfling priestess [[Merrygold Brightshine]] at Leilon's Shrine of Lathander—sharing dawn prayers, feeding [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]'s town laborers, and establishing the church as the town's spiritual anchor.
* **The Phandalin Ogre Defense & Argus Turn Undead:** Guarded the goat wagons against seven marauding ogres, and in [[Phandalin]] turned the undead vampire spawn [[Argus]] while [[Rhynok]] pinned the master thief [[Veleen]].
* **The House of Thalivar & The Sacred *Ceremony*:** Consecrated the haunted ruins of the House of Thalivar. When Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers** ambushed and critically dropped [[Tvyordiy Znak]], Myagkiy cast ***Spare the Dying*** to pull his brother back from death's brink. Following a long rest, he prepared and cast the sacred ***Ceremony*** ritual to lay Thalivar's wandering ghost to eternal rest.
* **Cellar Execution of Teega:** Re-entering the captured Wayside Inn, Myagkiy weathered the cellar's glyph of warding and channeled a devastating upcast ***Inflict Wounds*** directly into cult leader [[Teega]], incinerating her with necrotic justice while Krageer shattered the Idol of Talos to liberate [[Martisha Vinetalker]].
* **The Permanent Twin *Banishment* & The Cliffs of Thunder:** Aboard the *Iceflow*, cast ***Calm Emotions*** to pacify mutinous deckhands, and when confronted by Captain [[Kristoffen]], cast ***Banishment*** on both of the traitor's summoned Air Elementals—maintaining unbreakable concentration for the full ten minutes to permanently cast them back to the Elemental Plane of Air. Scaling [[Thunder Cliffs]], cured [[Sovendahl Erkinze]] of his lycanthropic curse and claimed the ***Ring of Warmth*** from the Talosite cave vault.

### 🐲 [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
* **The High Road Owlbear Ambush & Estate Infiltration:** When owlbears attacked Daniel Renault's private carriage en route to the cult compound, Myagkiy invoked a 3rd-level ***Spiritual Weapon*** and swung his mace with *Divine Strike* to protect the horses and retainers. During the 15-mile woodland approach, he cast ritual ***Detect Magic*** and applied prayerful Insight and Medicine checks to secure the approach.
* **Purging the Subterranean Complex:** Supported the party's push through the lightless lower chambers with restorative blessings and prayers, helping uncover the four ritual stones needed to unseal the basalt Dragon Door and obliterate Fanatic F's inner cult vanguard.
* **Radiant Bastion vs. The Shadow Dragon & *Raise Dead*:** In the lightless abyssal cavern, Myagkiy anchored the party by upcasting a 4th-level ***Spirit Guardians***—manifesting a blinding vortex of radiant angels that burned away the supernatural gloom and seared the **Shadow Dragon** whenever it swooped low. Closing into melee when the wyrm landed, Myagkiy channeled two devastating 3rd-level ***Inflict Wounds*** directly into its chest, delivering 31 and 25 necrotic damage to break the dragon's resolve. Following the beast's defeat and the liberation of young **Heracio Renault**, Myagkiy channeled a sacred diamond to cast ***Raise Dead***, restoring life to a fallen member/victim, and claimed an enchanted ***+1 Light Hammer*** and the wondrous ***Cape of the Mountebank*** (*Dimension Door* in a cloud of brimstone smoke) from the dragon's hoard.

### 🐲 [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]]
* **The Deeping Cave Acid Ferry:** In [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s lair, Myagkiy cast *Water Walk* and personally ferried [[Rhynok]]'s *Ring of Water Walking* back and forth across the boiling subterranean acid pools to guide each companion safely across—and poured out restorative healing after Krageer impulsively charged straight through the acid.
* **Level 11 Milestone:** Following the tragic possession of Claugiyliamatar by the death dragon Ebondeath and the Order's report to the Leilon council, Myagkiy and his companions officially achieved **Level 11**!

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Dimension Door Chain-Escape in the Starmetal Hills:** Operating at **Level 11** alongside Level 13 ally [[Dayne Beltran]], Myagkiy accompanied the expedition to recover the stolen *Right Hand of Gond*. When ambushed in the Starmetal Hills cavern by deadly **Chain Devils** (Kytons) whose animated barbed chains coiled to bind and crush him, Myagkiy activated his ***Cape of the Mountebank***—vanishing in a cloud of brimstone smoke via ***Dimension Door*** to slip the grapple, redeploy across the cavern, and cast ***Spirit Guardians*** and mass healing to turn the tide.
* **Legend Lore on Barovia:** Unfolding the cultist parchment reading *"Barovia, Barovia, Barovia... I call upon the fiends of Barovia..."*, Myagkiy cast ***Legend Lore*** during deep meditation—discovering the ancient demiplane of dread and seeding the first knowledge of Ravenloft for the Order.
* **Dayne's Plate & Double-Enlarging Krageer in the Volcano:** In Neverwinter Wood, Myagkiy helped slay Night Hag [[Balheelsa]] and rescue young [[Yasmina]]. Grateful for the Order's courage, [[Dayne Beltran]] and master smith [[Flanning Teldorer]] rewarded Myagkiy with Dayne's consecrated **+2 Plate Armor** (boosting Myagkiy to **24 AC**). In the Mount Hotenow caldera climax, after [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] quaffed a ***Potion of Growth*** from [[Puck Caribdas]] (`Medium` &rarr; `Large`), Myagkiy cast ***Enlarge*** on top of it (`Large` &rarr; `Huge`)—allowing Krageer to wrestle Fire Giant Queen [[Kevlaan the Merciless]] eye-to-eye in the lava!

### ⚡ [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
* **The Siege of Leilon:**
  - *The South Gate Meatgrinder:* Cast ***Spirit Guardians*** at the gate threshold, creating a 15-foot zone of swirling radiant spirits that shredded waves of ghasts and greater zombies as Krageer and Tvyordiy held the line.
  - *Guiding Bolt & Frontline Healing:* Supported the frontline in the Town Square and atop the House of Thalivar with critical *Cure Wounds* and radiant offensive spells, forcing Ebondeath to sever his link to Claugiyliamatar.
* **The Exorcism in the Dragon's Mausoleum:**
  - Weathered Ebondeath's terrifying **28 Wisdom Save** in the Crypt of First Royals.
  - Stepped into point-blank range of the possessed Tvyordiy and channeled a searing 4th-level ***Guiding Bolt*** (`26 to hit, 34 radiant damage`) straight into the spirit's spectral core, violently ripping the dracolich out of his brother's body.
  - Immediately healed Tvyordiy with *Cure Wounds*, allowing the monk to rise from 17 HP and destroy the ghost dragon.
* **Naval Scrying & The Tempest Miracle:**
  - Cast multiple 5th-level ***Scrying*** rituals (`DC 17 Wisdom Save`) from Leilon to pierce storm wards and track the coordinates of the Talosite dreadnaught.
  - *The High-Seas Lifeline:* When *Twinkle* was obliterated by an oceanic tornado, hurling everyone into twenty-foot waves, Myagkiy cast ***Beacon of Hope*** followed by a 4th-level ***Mass Healing Word***. Amplified to absolute maximum (17 HP to each survivor), the divine magic stabilized Captain Deloz, the officers, and 5 drow sailors, saving them from drowning.
* **The Dreadnaught Climax & Word of Recall:**
  - Boarded the flagship dreadnaught, providing radiant offense and vital healing against five Wraiths, four Air Elemental Myrmidons, and demon lord Ub-Vhagga the Nalfeshnee.
  - *The Final Miracle:* As Talos's divine lightning tore the dreadnaught apart and dragged it into the ocean vortex, Myagkiy cast ***Word of Recall***, teleporting the party, Captain Deloz, and the 5 surviving drow safely onto the consecrated stones of Leilon's sanctuary!
* **Level 13 Milestone:** Attained **Level 13** Life Domain Cleric, gaining access to 7th-level spells (such as *Conjure Celestial* and *Divine Word*). Honored with a stone statue in Leilon's town square and establishing the town's premier temple academy.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Player & Dual-PC Dynamics:** Played by Peter as a dual-PC / DMPC alongside Gary's martial champions. While DMing, Peter intentionally kept Myagkiy in a supportive anchor role so Gary's characters held the narrative decision spotlight.
* **Interlude Level Freeze Policy:** Peter intentionally froze level ups during the homebrew Interludes (*"Shadow's Run"* and *"Keeping the Faith"*) to keep party progression strictly tethered to the published trilogy tiers and prevent out-leveling the adventure module math.
* **Linguistic Origin (Мягкий знак / Ь):** Named after the Russian Cyrillic "Soft Sign" (Ь) inspired by conversations at work with coworker Grigory. The symbol represents softening, soothing, and modifying harshness—directly reflecting Myagkiy's role as a compassionate healer who tempers the party's ferocious frontline.
* **Character Sheet Symbolism:** Peter proudly incorporated the Russian Cyrillic symbol **(Ь)** into Myagkiy's official D&D Beyond character sheet title.
