# 🕊️ Myagkiy Znak (Ь)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Core Philosophy:** Dedicated to the preservation and defense of life at all costs, standing as the compassionate moral center and spiritual anchor of [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Background:** Hermit. Solitary contemplation gave way to a call to heal and defend the Sword Coast from elemental primordial cults.
* **Party Role:** Tempered the ferocious, berserker instincts of frontline comrades [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]].

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics & Stats
* **Race:** Variant Human
* **Class:** Level 13 **Life Domain** Cleric (Single class — Life Domain from Level 1)
* **Background:** Hermit
* **ASIs & Feats:** **+1 Constitution / +1 Wisdom ASI** (Level 4), **Observant**, **Resilient (Constitution)**
* **Ability Scores:** STR 19 (+4) | DEX 8 (-1) | CON 19 (+4) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 20 (+5) | CHA 12 (+1) *(STR boosted by Gauntlets of Ogre Power)*
* **Armor Class (AC):** **24 AC** (Dayne's +2 Plate Armor & +2 Shield)

## ⚔️ Combat Role & Tactics
* **Fearless Heavy Battle Cleric:** Wades fearlessly into the thick of frontline melee combat clad in 24 AC impenetrable plate, controlling choke points, mitigating status conditions, and delivering Life Domain burst healing.
* **Crowd-Shredding Signature Spells (*Spirit Guardians* & *Spiritual Weapon*):** Myagkiy's go-to bread-and-butter spells in battle were ***Spiritual Weapon*** (bonus action floating force strike) and ***Spirit Guardians*** (3rd-level swirling radiant aura unlocked at Level 5), which he used to **deadly effect in crowds**—melting wererats at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], [[Yargath]]'s 10-orc patrol, and the twig/vine blights at the [[Circle of Thunder]]!
* **Support Synergy:** Coordinates tactical spell placement (*Bless*, *Guidance*, *Enlarge*) with frontline bruisers, turning martials into titans via high-impact buffs.

## 🛡️ Equipment & Artifacts
* **Armor & Defense:**
  * **Dayne's Plate Armor:** +2 Plate Armor (recovered from a Night Hag and rewarded by [[Dayne Beltran]]).
  * **Shields:** Started with a mundane **Shield** in [[Phandalin]], claimed the ***+1 Shield*** from the anchorites' cave at the **[[Circle of Thunder]]** (as the party's sole shield-bearer), and later upgraded to a **+2 Shield**.
* **Weapons:** Started at Level 1 in [[Phandalin]] with a mundane **Mace**, later upgrading to **Bonecounter** (signature mace) and a Light Hammer.
* **Magic Items & Accessories:**
  * ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power* (sets Strength to 19):** Discovered by Myagkiy's own keen **Perception check** inside the secret fireplace vault of the Castellan's Bedchamber in **[[Axeholm]]**!
  * ***Sending Stones* (Shared Party Relic):** Acquired from [[Dazlyn Grayshard]] and [[Norbus Ironrune]] at the [[Temple of Abbathor]] (Level 2) and used throughout the rest of the campaign.
  * **Lantern of Revealing** (reveals invisible foes)
  * **Cape of the Mountebank** (emergency *Dimension Door* teleportation)
  * **Ring of Warmth** (cold resistance)

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Milestones
* **Deeping Cave Acid Pool Ferry & Krageer Healing:** In [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s lair (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*), Myagkiy cast *Water Walk* and personally ferried [[Rhynok]]'s *Ring of Water Walking* back and forth across the cavern's caustic acid pools to bring each party member across safely. When Krageer impulsively charged through the acid in a rage, Myagkiy poured out life-domain healing to restore the barbarian afterwards.
* **Dormant Volcano Climax:** Cast *Enlarge* on **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, enabling Krageer to wrestle the **Fire Giant Queen** in molten grapple combat inside a dormant volcano during *"Keeping the Faith"*.
* **Rescue & Adoption of the Leilon Wards:** Served as the moral compass of the party during the Kraken Priest raid on Leilon (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), rescuing orphaned children **[[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]]** from the marshlands after their fisher parents were sacrificed. Formally adopted them into the Order, mentoring young Burnice in spiritual devotion and partnering with majordomo [[Leopold]] to provide a secure, loving home at the Leilon estate.
* **Fellowship with Merrygold Brightshine:** In Leilon, Myagkiy forged a close spiritual bond with [[Merrygold Brightshine]], the halfling priest of Lathander. The two shared communion at the dawn shrine services, coordinated care and nourishment for town laborers alongside [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]'s workforce, and sought the Morninglord's radiant blessings prior to hazardous frontier excursions.
* **Slaying [[Cryovain]] & The Ice Mephits at [[Icespire Hold]] (*Dragon of Icespire Peak* Finale):** During the rooftop boss fight against [[Cryovain]] and Peter's added **Ice Mephits**, Myagkiy cast ***Bless*** across the party and summoned his ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat down the Ice Mephits while [[Tvyordiy Znak]] stunned the dragon and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Rhynok]] finished the wyrm off.
* **[[Circle of Thunder]] & The *+1 Shield* (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*):** Unleashed ***Spirit Guardians*** against [[Yargath]]'s 10-orc patrol and during the epic clash against [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar, [[Flenz]], [[Narux]], and the twig/vine blights at the [[Circle of Thunder]]—claiming the ***+1 Shield*** afterward as the party's only shield-holder.
* **[[Axeholm]] Chimney *Guidance* & Secret Vault Discovery (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*):** Cast ***Guidance*** so every party member succeeded on their Athletics checks climbing down the [[Axeholm]] chimney, helped destroy the ghouls and [[Vyldara]] the banshee, and spotted the secret fireplace vault in the Castellan's Bedchamber with his **Perception check** to claim the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power***.
* **Carrying the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Wererat Fight & [[Tower of Storms]]:** Recognized the desecrated statue of **Talos** at the [[Tower of Storms]] before helping slay [[Moesko]] and destroy his heart; and at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]—when Krageer and Tvyordiy couldn't hurt the immune wererats—carried the battle alongside [[Rhynok]] using ***Spirit Guardians***, ***Spiritual Weapon***, and ***Sacred Flame***!

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player & Dual-PC Dynamics:** Played by Peter as a dual-PC / DMPC alongside Gary's martial champions.
* **Linguistic Origin (Мягкий знак / Ь):** Named after the Russian Cyrillic "Soft Sign" (Ь) inspired by conversations at work with coworker Grigory. The symbol represents softening, soothing, and modifying harshness—directly reflecting Myagkiy's role as a compassionate healer who tempers the party's ferocious frontline.
* **Character Sheet Symbolism:** Peter proudly incorporated the Russian Cyrillic symbol **(Ь)** into Myagkiy's official D&D Beyond character sheet title.
