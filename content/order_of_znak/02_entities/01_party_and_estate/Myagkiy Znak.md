# 🕊️ Myagkiy Znak (Ь)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Core Philosophy:** Dedicated to the preservation and defense of life at all costs, standing as the compassionate moral center and spiritual anchor of [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Background:** Hermit. Years of solitary contemplation and prayer gave way to a sacred calling to heal the wounded and shield the Sword Coast from elemental and necromantic cults.
* **Battlefield Role:** A fearless heavy battle priest who wades directly into the teeth of the melee clad in gleaming plate and shield—anchoring the frontline beside [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] while calling down swirling hosts of radiant *Spirit Guardians* and restorative blessings.

---

## 📜 In-Universe Chronicle & Key Deeds

### 🏔️ [[01 - Act 1 - Dragon of Icespire Peak|Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak]]
* **Frontline Healer of the Frontier:** Arriving in [[Phandalin]] already ordained in the **Life Domain**, Myagkiy sustained the company through their early trials at [[Umbrage Hill]], the [[Temple of Abbathor]], [[Gnomengarde]], [[Butterskull Ranch]], the desperate fighting retreat from the [[Woodland Manse]], and the ankheg nests of the [[Loggers' Camp]].
* **Sanctifying the [[Tower of Storms]]:** Inside the storm-lashed lighthouse of the [[Tower of Storms]], Myagkiy recognized the desecrated altar and iconography of **[[Talos]]**, god of storms, before helping slay the undead anchorite [[Moesko]], destroy his beating heart atop the beacon tower, and lay the banshee [[Miraal]] to rest.
* **Carrying the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Wererat Battle:** After fighting alongside [[DonJon Raskin]] against the orcs and ogres at the [[Shrine of Savras]], the Order confronted [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s wererat gang at [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]. When neither Krageer nor Tvyordiy could wound the lycanthropes with mundane steel, Myagkiy stepped forward and carried the melee—melting the wererat pack with swirling ***Spirit Guardians***, his floating ***Spiritual Weapon***, and searing columns of ***Sacred Flame*** alongside [[Rhynok]]'s enchanted bow!
* **Prayerful *Guidance* & The Secret Vault of [[Axeholm]]:** At the sealed dwarven fortress of [[Axeholm]], Myagkiy invoked prayerful ***Guidance*** over each companion so all four rappelled down the mountain chimney without a single slip. After helping purge the lower halls of ghouls and banishing the moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**, Myagkiy's sharp eyes spotted a hidden iron vault concealed inside the fireplace of the Castellan's Bedchamber—claiming the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power*** before the party slew the undead Castellan ghoul.
* **The Storm of the [[Circle of Thunder]] & The *+1 Shield*:** Marching into Neverwinter Wood with centaur ally **[[Xanth]]**, Myagkiy unleashed his radiant ***Spirit Guardians*** to shred [[Yargath]]'s ten-orc patrol and the swarms of twig and vine blights during the epic hilltop clash against **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, [[Flenz]], and [[Narux]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]]. In the aftermath, as the party's sole shield-bearer, Myagkiy claimed the enchanted ***+1 Shield*** from the anchorite caves before helping purge the [[Woodland Manse]].
* **Blessing the Rooftop Clash Against [[Cryovain]]:** Atop the frozen battlements of [[Icespire Hold]], Myagkiy invoked ***Bless*** upon his brothers-in-arms and commanded his floating ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat down the cackling **Ice Mephits**, clearing the way for Tvyordiy, Krageer, and Rhynok to stun and slay the white dragon **[[Cryovain]]**.

### ⛈️ [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]] & [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Interlude 1: "Shadow's Run"]]
* **Adoptive Father & Spiritual Hearth of [[Leilon]]:** Served as the compassionate heart of the party during [[Nixoxious]]'s Kraken Priest raid on Leilon, rescuing orphaned ten-year-olds **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** from the marshes, adopting them into the Znak family under majordomo [[Leopold]], and mentoring young [[Burnice Znak|Burnice]] in sacred devotion.
* **Fellowship with [[Merrygold Brightshine]]:** Forged a close spiritual bond with halfling priestess [[Merrygold Brightshine]] at Leilon's Shrine of Lathander—sharing dawn prayers, feeding [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]'s town laborers, and anchoring the company with radiant light against the **Shadow Dragon** in *"Shadow's Run"*.

### 🐲 [[04 - Act 3 - Sleeping Dragon's Wake|Act 3: Sleeping Dragon's Wake]] & [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **The Deeping Cave Acid Ferry:** In [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s lair, Myagkiy cast *Water Walk* and personally ferried [[Rhynok]]'s *Ring of Water Walking* back and forth across the boiling subterranean acid pools to guide each companion safely across—and poured out restorative healing after Krageer impulsively charged straight through the acid.
* **Dayne's Plate & Enlarging Krageer in the Volcano:** In *"Keeping the Faith"*, Myagkiy helped rescue young **[[Yasmina]]** from a predatory Night Hag and recovered the stolen **+2 Plate Armor** of Forge Cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**, who bestowed the sacred armor upon Myagkiy. In the dormant volcano climax, Myagkiy cast ***Enlarge*** on [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], empowering the Goliath to wrestle the **Fire Giant Queen** in the lava!

### ⚡ [[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]
* **The Bastion of Leilon & The Dreadnaught Deck:** Clad in 24 AC enchanted plate and shield, Myagkiy stood unyielding against the Star Spawn Manglers atop the House of Thalivar, weathered [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s siege of Leilon, and boarded the hurricane-lashed Talosite flagship dreadnaught aboard *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]* to vanquish **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an Air Elemental Myrmidon, and a Nalfeshnee demon.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **Player & Dual-PC Dynamics:** Played by Peter as a dual-PC / DMPC alongside Gary's martial champions. While DMing, Peter intentionally kept Myagkiy in a supportive anchor role so Gary's characters held the narrative decision spotlight.
* **Linguistic Origin (Мягкий знак / Ь):** Named after the Russian Cyrillic "Soft Sign" (Ь) inspired by conversations at work with coworker Grigory. The symbol represents softening, soothing, and modifying harshness—directly reflecting Myagkiy's role as a compassionate healer who tempers the party's ferocious frontline.
* **Character Sheet Symbolism:** Peter proudly incorporated the Russian Cyrillic symbol **(Ь)** into Myagkiy's official D&D Beyond character sheet title.
