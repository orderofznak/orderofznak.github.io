# 🦅 Kree-uk (Disciple of the Hard Sign)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Young male Aarakocra of the peregrine lineage from the Boundless Whorl on the Elemental Plane of Air. Disciple of [[Tvyordiy Znak]].
* **The Courtyard Challenge ("Black Belt of the Mud"):** Initially an arrogant young vanguard recruit who sneered at the slow, grounded monastic katas of Tvyordiy as "dock-laborers rolling beer casks in the mud." Challenged Tvyordiy to an unrestricted 80-mph aerial dive; Tvyordiy calmly parried his glaive and swept his talons, leaving Kree-uk flat on the marble without inflicting a scratch. Deeply humbled, Kree-uk pledged himself to learn the discipline of the Hard Sign (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 4]]).
* **The Battle of the Boundless Whorl:** Fought on the vanguard shelves against the Avatar of Talos, suffering severe wounds while holding the flank. On the inner redoubt gallery, he stood guard beside Deekek and Klee'errk when the rift collapsed.
* **Journey to Toril & The Aufox Manor Dojo (1502–1505 DR):** Led three fellow planar disciples—**[[Qeerik]]**, **[[Arak-tai]]**, and **[[Sool-arrk]]**—in petitioning Tvyordiy to journey down to the mortal realm. At [[Aufox Manor]] in [[Leilon]], he trains daily in the mud alongside [[Burnice Znak]], [[Smithwell Znak|Smithwell]], and [[Yasmina]], dreaming of returning one day as a master to open dojos of the Hard Sign across the winds of the Plane of Air.

## 📊 Combat & Mechanics
* **Class & Role:** Level 7 Way of the Open Hand Monk.
* **Weapons:** Unarmed strikes, practice quarterstaff, sky-glaive.
* **Features:** Open Hand Technique, Stunning Strike, Deflect Missiles, Evasion.
* **Fly Speed:** 50 ft.
