# 🪽 Klee'errk (Aial Sun-Catcher)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Female Aarakocra of the Boundless Whorl, the surviving clutch-twin sister of [[Tvyordiy Znak]] and younger sister of [[Deekek|Wing-Captain Deekek]]. Known among the aerial sentinels by her honor-title, *Aial Sun-Catcher*.
* **Separation & The Lower Perch Tragedy (1484 DR):** When Talosite storm cultists attacked the ancestral roost in 1484 DR, her parents Harek and Vaelen held the Lower Perch to allow the crèche to flee. Tvyordiy’s egg was evacuated to Toril, while Klee'errk and Deekek retreated into the upper vaults. For seventeen years, she believed her twin brother had perished.
* **The Reunion (1501 DR):** Reunited with Tvyordiy when he returned to the Plane of Air carrying their father’s silver coin, the *Tessera of the Western Gale* (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 4]]).
* **The Defense of the Boundless Whorl (1502 DR):** Fought beside Tvyordiy and [[Yeri]] against the Avatar of Talos, plunging her feather-spear into the herald's skull-helm to deliver the final blow. Held Tvyordiy back with Deekek when the singularity imploded.
* **Migration to Toril & The Aufox Manor Dojo (1502–1505 DR):** Refusing to let her brother live alone in the lowlands, she accompanied Tvyordiy and four disciples to Toril. At [[Aufox Manor]] in [[Leilon]], she co-presides over the gravel courtyard training, teaching balance and aerial forms to [[Burnice Znak]], [[Smithwell Znak|Smithwell]], [[Yasmina]], and the planar disciples.

* **Maneuvers:** Sweeping Attack, Trip Attack, Distracting Strike, Evasive Footwork.
