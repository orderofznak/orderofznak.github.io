# 🦅 Qeerik (Kestrel Scout & Disciple)

* **Identity & Heritage:** Swift, young male Aarakocra of the kestrel lineage from the Boundless Whorl on the Elemental Plane of Air. Disciple of [[Tvyordiy Znak]].
* **Martial Focus:** Specializes in high-agility missile deflection (*Deflect Missiles*), evasion, and swift counter-attacks.
* **Role at Aufox Manor:** Traveled to Toril alongside [[Klee'errk]], [[Kree-uk]], [[Arak-tai]], and [[Sool-arrk]] to train under Tvyordiy at [[Aufox Manor]] in [[Leilon]], preparing to one day open dojos on the Plane of Air.
