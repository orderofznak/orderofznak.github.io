# 🎩 Leopold

## 🛡️ Overview & Identity
* **Identity:** Human majordomo and butler of [[The Order of Znak]]'s fortified headquarters in [[Leilon]].
* **Role & Demeanor:** Dignified, highly organized, and unflappable. Leopold represents the steady domestic anchor of the Order, ensuring that the chaotic heroes always return from deadly wilderness campaigns to hot baths, clean linens, organized armories, and a well-defended home.

---

## 🏰 Headquarters Operations & Logistics
* **Running Day-to-Day Operations:** While Peter and Gary piloted the champions across the Sword Coast fighting dragon cults and planar fiends, Leopold ran every facet of the manor's daily life.
* **Provisions & Trade Manifests:** Coordinated directly with **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]** at the Wayside Inn to receive regular shipments of fresh livestock and vegetables in exchange for manufactured town supplies, keeping the manor's cellars fully stocked.
* **Structural Maintenance:** Worked alongside **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]**'s construction crews to oversee stone repairs, gate reinforcements, and training yard upkeep.

---

## 👦👧 Guardian of the Young Wards & Acolytes
* **Raising the Next Generation:** Leopold's greatest campaign responsibility was serving as the daily guardian and parental figure for the Order's adopted wards:
  * Young fisher orphans **[[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]]** (rescued from the kraken cult in *Storm Lord's Wrath*).
  * Young **[[Yasmina]]** (rescued from the Night Hag in *"Keeping the Faith"*).
* **Care for the Wards & Envoys:** Watched over young wards **[[Smithwell and Burnice|Smithwell and Burnice]]**, young **[[Yasmina]]**, and welcomed lizardfolk exchange allies **[[Graxxoamo]]** and **[[Shriss]]** at the manor.
