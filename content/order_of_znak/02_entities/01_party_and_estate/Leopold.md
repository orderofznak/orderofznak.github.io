# 🎩 Leopold

## 🛡️ Overview & Identity
* **Identity:** Human majordomo, trusted driver, and permanent steward of [[The Order of Znak]]'s fortified headquarters at **Aufox Manor** in [[Leilon]].
* **Role & Demeanor:** Dignified, highly organized, and unflappable. Leopold represents the steady domestic and intelligence anchor of the Order, ensuring that the chaotic heroes always return from deadly wilderness campaigns to hot baths, clean linens, organized armories, and a well-defended home.

---

## 🏰 Headquarters Operations & The 1-Year Interim
* **Permanent Stewardship of Aufox Manor:** Following the events of [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]] (July 2022), Leopold was brought into the Order of Znak's service permanently—not just as an elite driver and carriage master, but as the primary overseer of day-to-day operations across Aufox Manor in [[Leilon]].
* **Overseeing the "Orphans of Znak":** Leopold took direct charge of cultivating the blooming and adolescent talents of the Order's young adopted wards:
  * Rescued fisher twins **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** (adopted following *Storm Lord's Wrath*).
  * Rescued daughter **[[Yasmina]]** (liberated from Night Hag [[Balheelsa]] during *Keeping the Faith*).
* **Cross-Training & Lizardfolk Alliance:** Under Leopold's watchful eye during the one-year transition into *Divine Contention*, all the young wards began daily cross-training lessons with the Order champions, joined by visiting marshland lizardfolk allies from the Mere of Dead Men who imparted crucial lessons regarding wetland survival, local flora, dangerous fauna, and swimming mechanics.
* **Regional Intelligence Network:** Leopold cultivated an extensive network of informants and wagon drivers along the High Road. At the close of the one-year interim, it was Leopold who first broke urgent intelligence to the Order: warning them that an immense undead host had emerged under the banner of Myrkul's death-priest **[[Ularan Mortus]]**, and that Talosite war-priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** had returned to lead the storm cult's renewed assault on Leilon!
* **Provisions & Trade Manifests:** Coordinated directly with **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]** at the Wayside Inn to receive regular shipments of fresh livestock and vegetables in exchange for manufactured town supplies, keeping the manor's cellars fully stocked.
* **Structural Maintenance:** Worked alongside **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]**'s construction crews to oversee stone repairs, gate reinforcements, and training yard upkeep.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **The "Liefold" Candidate Lineage:** In Gary's August 2020 homebrew adventure ***[[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Shadow's Run]]***, the loyal carriage driver and retainers of Waterdhavian noble Daniel Renault who hired the Order were named **Liefold** and **Joffrey**. Given the phonetics and coachman background, Peter and Gary noted at the table that Liefold subsequently transitioned from Renault's service to become the Order's permanent majordomo **Leopold** at Aufox Manor in Leilon.
* **Canonical 1-Year Bridge Lore:** In Discord notes from July 22, 2022, Peter recorded: *"Leopold was brought into the Order of Znaks service permanently, not just as a driver but overseer of the day to day operations of Aufox Manor as well as overseeing the blooming and adolescent talents of the Orphans of Znak... Leopold has heard word through his network that an undead army has emerged granted to the cult of Myrkuls leader Ularan Mortus"*, cementing Leopold as both estate manager and early warning scout for Act 4.
