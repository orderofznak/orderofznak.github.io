# 🪶 Yeri (Ы)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Female Kenku hailing from the dreary demiplane of the **Shadowfell**, born under the ancient racial curse of flightlessness, yet possessing an innate, burning divine spark of raw magic.
* **The Calling of the Third Sign (Ы) & The Shadow's Run Incursion (1493 DR):** In Russian linguistic phonology and the mystical doctrine that binds the Znak champions, **Yeri (Ы)** represents the neutral, guttural sound that bridges the unyielding hardness of **The Hard Sign (Ъ)** and the softening mercy of **The Soft Sign (Ь)**. In August 1493 DR, when [[The Order of Znak]] purged the shadow-complex beneath Neverwinter Wood in [[03 - Interlude 1 - Shadow's Run|Shadow's Run]], the colossal release of radiant energy and the stamping of the Znak symbol upon the dark shrine thinned the planar veil between Toril and the **Shadowfell**. Drawn by the rhythmic pulse in her chest to a forgotten subterranean ruin bearing the glowing rune **Ы**, Yeri touched the glyph and pierced the veil into the mortal world—emerging seven years prior into the material plane.
* **The Obsession with Flight & Mount Nimbus:** While she mastered the 3rd-level spell *Fly*, reveling in soaring across the clouds, its ten-minute limitation was an agonizing tease. Her life's obsession became breaking the kenku curse and discovering true, permanent flight—driving her toward the legendary **Star Mounts** of the High Forest. Atop Mount Nimbus in 1500 DR, she absorbed the celestial Eaerlanni starlight crystal, manifesting four iridescent gossamer dragonfly wings that granted permanent flight (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 3]]).
* **Roleplay Traits:**
  - *Personality:* High-minded snob who openly scoffs at crude manners, armed with witty aphorisms and maxims for every occasion.
  - *Ideal:* **People.** Devoted to individuals she respects rather than dogmatic causes.
  - *Bond:* Driven to prove that even a flightless Kenku can master the skies.
  - *Dynamic with [[Tvyordiy Znak]]:* Uses Kenku mimicry to repeat Tvyordiy's proverbs back to him in his own deep baritone. Kept a leather notebook where she recorded his aphorisms alongside her own.
* **The Sacrifice at the Boundless Whorl (1502 DR):** Fought alongside Tvyordiy against the Avatar of Talos on the Plane of Air, shattering its defenses with a 6th-level *Disintegrate*. When the dying titan collapsed into an inescapable singularity, Yeri sacrificed herself, using her final Sorcery Points for a *Telekinetic Shove* to launch Tvyordiy across the void to safety while she was pulled into the storm with a final parting thought: *"...remember."*
