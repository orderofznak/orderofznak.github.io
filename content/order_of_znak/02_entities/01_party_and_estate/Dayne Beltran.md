# 🔨 Dayne Beltran

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A prodigiously talented, devout Forge Domain Cleric dedicated to **[[Gond]]** (the Wonderbringer, Lord of All Smiths), operating as a potent **Level 13** divine caster and master smith.
* **Origins & Blacksmith Heritage:** Born the son of a humble blacksmith. From early childhood, his parents recognized that his supernatural affinity for hammer, forge, and metal was a sacred divine blessing from Gond, prompting them to send young Dayne to the mountain crags northeast of Neverwinter to apprentice under master forge cleric **[[Flanning Teldorer]]**.
* **Alliance with the Order:** Partnered with [[The Order of Znak]] during [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]] (June–July 2022) to track down the thieves who desecrated Flanning's mountain forge and stole the sacred relic **The Right Hand of Gond**.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Rendezvous in Neverwinter:** After [[Tvyordiy Znak]] flew ahead to Flanning's mountain forge in the Crags, Dayne accompanied Tvyordiy south to Neverwinter, uniting with [[Myagkiy Znak]], [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], and [[Rhynok]] to investigate the brutal serial murders committed by [[Graag]] the Werebear and his heart-consuming cultists.
* **The Starmetal Hills & *Zone of Truth*:** Channeling divine guidance, Dayne deduced that the cult's stolen Heartstone was carried east into the Starmetal Hills. In the subterranean cavern, Dayne fought alongside the four Level 11 champions against deadly **Chain Devils** (Kytons), subsequently casting ***Zone of Truth*** to interrogate the broken cult survivors and extract the truth about the Lower Planes soul trades.
* **Uncovering Queen Kevlaan & Surtur's Enmity:** Following the defeat of Night Hag [[Balheelsa]] and the rescue of young [[Yasmina]] in Neverwinter Wood, Dayne recalled vital planar and giant lore: that Fire Giant Queen **[[Kevlaan the Merciless]]** had obtained the stolen Hammer of Gond. He revealed that **Surtur**, the legendary fire giant patron god of smithing, was an ancient divine adversary of Gond, explaining why Kevlaan sought the hammer to forge devastating instruments of war.
* **Mount Hotenow & The Double-Enlarge Triumph:** Accompanied the Order into the volcanic caldera of Mount Hotenow to witness Krageer's legendary "Double-Enlarge" wrestling victory over Queen Kevlaan.
* **The Gift of Consecrated Armor:** Following the recovery and return of the *[[Right Hand of Gond]]* to master [[Flanning Teldorer]], Dayne gratefully bestowed his masterwork, consecrated **+2 Plate Armor** upon Life Cleric **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**—permanently boosting Myagkiy's frontline defenses to an unyielding **24 AC**!

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **DM Campaign Design:** Created and introduced by Gary as the principal companion and patron NPC of his homebrew adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*, bridging the narrative gap between *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* and *Divine Contention*.
* **Party Level Scaling:** In table chat logs on June 30, 2022, Peter noted the encounter math: *"for 4 level 11s and a level 13 or whatever Dayne is"*, establishing Dayne's senior tier status as an elite Level 13 mentor/ally.
* **Frontline Cleric Fellowship:** Gary intentionally tailored Dayne's +2 Plate Armor as a reward to bolster Peter's Life Domain cleric against lethal high-tier melee punishment in the climactic battles of *Divine Contention*.
