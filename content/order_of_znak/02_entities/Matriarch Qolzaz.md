# 🦎 Matriarch Qolzaz

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Tribal Leadership:** Respected matriarch and spiritual leader of the Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk clans encountered during the *Missing Patrol* questline.
* **Fierce Defender:** Fiercely guarded the sovereignty and nesting grounds of her swamp tribe against humanoid incursions along the Sword Coast.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Milestones
* **Clash & Aftermath:** Slain in honorable combat during the party's initial encounter with the tribe.
* **Undead Troll Defense & Mutual Honor:** When a horrific horde of undead trolls subsequently threatened the wetlands, [[The Order of Znak]] stood shoulder-to-shoulder with the surviving clan to repel the invasion.
* **Krageer's Chest Tattoo:** Following Goliath customs of honoring worthy rivals, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** had an elaborate portrait of Matriarch Qolzaz inked across his chest in tribute.
* **Bardic Ballad:** Her valor and the clan's alliance are immortalized in the campaign's epic poem, the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Emergent Narrative:** What started as a standard hostile wilderness encounter naturally evolved through table roleplay into an enduring alliance, directly leading to the adoption of lizardfolk exchange champions **[[Graxxoamo]]** and **[[Shriss]]** in later arcs.
