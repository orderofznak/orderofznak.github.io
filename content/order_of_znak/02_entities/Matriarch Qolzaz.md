# 🦎 Matriarch Qolzaz

## Overview & Role
* **Role:** Matriarch of the lizardfolk tribe encountered during the *Missing Patrol* questline in *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*.
* **Fate:** Initially slain in combat by [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Legacy & Tattoo:** After the Order later allied with the lizardfolk tribe to defeat a massive invasion of undead trolls, the tribe gained immense respect for the party. Following Goliath tradition, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** had a detailed tattoo of Matriarch Qolzaz inked onto his chest out of mutual honor.
* **Bardic Ballad:** Immortalized in ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***.
