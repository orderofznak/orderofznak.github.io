# 🪄 Aubrey Silverspun

## 🛡️ Overview & Shopkeeper Persona
* **The Peculiarities Shop:** Runs **Aubrey's Peculiarities** in [[Leilon]], dealing in antiquities, oddities, curiosities, and minor magic trinkets.
* **Spectral Nature:** Aubrey was likely a ghost the whole time. Only the player characters of [[The Order of Znak]] were able to see and interact with him.

---

## 🐉 Revelation During Divine Contention
* **The Attack of Claugiyliamatar:** During the siege of Leilon in *Divine Contention*, the green dragon **Claugiyliamatar** assaulted the settlement, destroying Aubrey's shop.
* **The Swords of Leilon Portrait:** Following the defeat of Claugiyliamatar, Aubrey's ghostly status was revealed. After the destruction of his shop, an ancient painting depicting the historic **Swords of Leilon** showed Aubrey standing among the figures in the portrait.
