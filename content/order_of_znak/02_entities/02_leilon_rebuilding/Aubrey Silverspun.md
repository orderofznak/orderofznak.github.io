# 🪄 Aubrey Silverspun

## 🛡️ Overview & Shopkeeper Persona
* **The Peculiarities Shop:** Runs **Aubrey's Peculiarities** in [[Leilon]], dealing in antiquities, oddities, curiosities, and minor magic trinkets.
* **Spectral Nature:** Aubrey was likely a ghost the whole time. Only the player characters of [[The Order of Znak]] were able to see and interact with him.

---

## 🐉 Revelation During Divine Contention (The Siege of Leilon)
* **The Crash of Claugiyliamatar:** In *Divine Contention* (Chapter 5), after the Order drove the possessed green dragon Claugiyliamatar from the Town Square, the climactic battle raged across the roof of the House of Thalivar. Defeated and dispossessed of Ebondeath's spirit, the colossal green dragon plummeted from the spire, crashing straight through the timber roof of **Aubrey's Peculiarities** and reducing the shop to splintered rubble.
* **Krageer's Investigation & The Ancient Oil Painting:** In the aftermath of the battle, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] conducted a careful search through the smoking ruins of the shop. Digging through shattered display cases, Krageer unearthed an ancient, miraculously intact oil painting depicting the legendary **Swords of Leilon** from centuries past.
* **The Immortal Guardian Unveiled:** Standing prominently among the painted heroes in the portrait was Aubrey Silverspun himself—wearing the exact same eccentric robes and gentle smile. The discovery confirmed that Aubrey was never a living shopkeeper, but the immortal, spectral guardian of Leilon who had watched over the town across centuries, subtly aiding and equipping [[The Order of Znak]] to fulfill their destiny as the new protectors of the realm.
