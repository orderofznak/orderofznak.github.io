# ⚓ Captain Deloz Montiv

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Drow (Dark elf)
* **Role & Occupation:** Captain of the privateer vessel *Twinkle*
* **Allegiance & Network:** Bregan D'aerthe / Jarlaxle Baenre; Allied with [[The Order of Znak]]
* **Vessel:** *Twinkle* (the smaller twin to *[[Xalti Tezzan|Icingdeath]]*, both owned by Jarlaxle Baenre and named in homage to Drizzt Do'Urden's legendary scimitars)
* **Alignment:** Chaotic neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sea of Swords, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Divine Contention*

## 📜 Lore, Vessels & Disguises

### The Twin Ships of Bregan D'aerthe
Captain Deloz commands the *Twinkle*, a swift sailing caravel operating along the Sword Coast. The ship is the smaller companion to the warship *Icingdeath*, captained by [[Xalti Tezzan]]. Both vessels are owned by the mercenary lord **Jarlaxle Baenre** and were deliberately named after the iconic twin scimitars wielded by **Drizzt Do'Urden**.

### The Illusory Facade
Operating openly on the surface waters of the Sea of Swords without drawing hostile attention from local navies required elaborate deception:
* **The Figurehead Illusion:** The figurehead of the *Twinkle* was enchanted with an illusion that cast a permanent glamour over the ship, making the all-drow crew appear as ordinary human sailors.
* **The Hat of Disguise:** Captain Deloz personally wore a *Hat of Disguise* to project the appearance of a human ship captain when interacting with port authorities and surface folk.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters

### The Reveal to The Order of Znak
When [[The Order of Znak]] first encountered Captain Deloz and the *Twinkle*, [[Rhynok]]'s sharp ranger senses pierced the facade—noticing subtle physiological movements and distinctly elven mannerisms among the crew that betrayed their true dark elven heritage. When the illusion was dropped and Deloz and his crew were revealed as drow, the revelation caused no hostility; the champions welcomed the privateers warmly, establishing a firm mutual trust.

### Diplomatic Summit & Naval Fleet Combat
* **The Audience at Castle Never:** In *Divine Contention* (Chapter 3), Captain Deloz transported [[The Order of Znak]] aboard *Twinkle* to Neverwinter, accompanying them into Castle Never before **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]**. Backed by Lord Neverember, Captain Deloz and Captain Xalti received formal letters of marque, chartering *Twinkle* and *Icingdeath* as privateers of Neverwinter.
* **The Battle of Ship Rethnor:** On the return voyage (Chapter 4), Deloz engaged the reaver fleet in hurricane squalls. Piloting *Twinkle* through twenty-foot swells, Deloz laid his vessel alongside the pirate flagship *Throatcutter*. Following the Order's victory and the interrogation of Captain Galas Windrage, the party scuttled the ordnance and officially gifted the captured *Throatcutter* to Captain Deloz and Bregan D'aerthe as a prize of war.

### The Dreadnaught Climax: Aerial Duels & The Sea of Swords
* **The Levitation Duel Against the Roc:** Sailing into the tempest to hunt down the Talosite dreadnaught (Chapter 7), *Twinkle* was ambushed by a colossal Roc carrying storm blackguard Kata Ironhide. When the beast snatched [[Rhynok]] thirty feet into the air, Captain Deloz activated his innate drow ***Levitate*** (1/day), drifting thirty feet off the rolling deck to cross flashing scimitars with the blackguard and monster in mid-air.
* **Surviving the Catastrophic Tornado:** Deeper in the tempest, *Twinkle* was caught in an oceanic waterspout and torn apart, dealing 42 bludgeoning damage to everyone aboard. While common deckhands perished, Captain Deloz, his senior officers, and **5 lucky drow sailors** survived the plunge. Treading water in giant swells, they were kept alive by Myagkiy's ***Beacon of Hope*** and maximized ***Mass Healing Word***.
* **Boarding the Dreadnaught & The Decisive Killing Blow:** Hauling themselves up the bone-and-metal hull of the Talosite dreadnaught, Deloz and his surviving crew fought shoulder-to-shoulder with the Order against five Wraiths, four Air Elemental Myrmidons, and demon lord Ub-Vhagga the Nalfeshnee. In the final moments of the battle, Captain Deloz leaped forward to deliver the **decisive killing blow** against the last Air Elemental Myrmidon!
* **Salvation via Word of Recall:** As the avatar of Talos tore the sinking dreadnaught apart, Deloz and his 5 surviving sailors were gathered into Myagkiy's golden corona of ***Word of Recall***, teleporting safely to the sanctuary of Leilon. Honored as heroes of the realm, Deloz and Bregan D'aerthe cemented an unbreakable alliance with The Order of Znak.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **R.A. Salvatore Lore Mastery:** Peter is a dedicated fan of R.A. Salvatore's *The Legend of Drizzt* legendarium, having listened to all 39 audiobooks (narrated by Victor Bevine). Bringing Captain Deloz, *Twinkle*, and the Bregan D'aerthe network into the campaign gave Peter the opportunity to dip deeply into his extensive lore knowledge of Jarlaxle Baenre and the famous drow of the Sword Coast.
