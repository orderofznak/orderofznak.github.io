# ⚓ Captain Deloz Montiv

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Drow (Dark elf)
* **Role & Occupation:** Captain of the privateer vessel *Twinkle*
* **Allegiance & Network:** Bregan D'aerthe / Jarlaxle Baenre; Allied with [[The Order of Znak]]
* **Vessel:** *Twinkle* (the smaller twin to *[[Xalti Tezzan|Icingdeath]]*, both owned by Jarlaxle Baenre and named in homage to Drizzt Do'Urden's legendary scimitars)
* **Alignment:** Chaotic neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sea of Swords, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Divine Contention*

## 📜 Lore, Vessels & Disguises

### The Twin Ships of Bregan D'aerthe
Captain Deloz commands the *Twinkle*, a swift sailing caravel operating along the Sword Coast. The ship is the smaller companion to the warship *Icingdeath*, captained by [[Xalti Tezzan]]. Both vessels are owned by the mercenary lord **Jarlaxle Baenre** and were deliberately named after the iconic twin scimitars wielded by **Drizzt Do'Urden**.

### The Illusory Facade
Operating openly on the surface waters of the Sea of Swords without drawing hostile attention from local navies required elaborate deception:
* **The Figurehead Illusion:** The figurehead of the *Twinkle* was enchanted with an illusion that cast a permanent glamour over the ship, making the all-drow crew appear as ordinary human sailors.
* **The Hat of Disguise:** Captain Deloz personally wore a *Hat of Disguise* to project the appearance of a human ship captain when interacting with port authorities and surface folk.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters

### The Reveal to The Order of Znak
When [[The Order of Znak]] first encountered Captain Deloz and the *Twinkle*, [[Rhynok]]'s sharp ranger senses pierced the facade—noticing subtle physiological movements and distinctly elven mannerisms among the crew that betrayed their true dark elven heritage. When the illusion was dropped and Deloz and his crew were revealed as drow, the revelation caused no hostility; the champions welcomed the privateers warmly, establishing a firm mutual trust.

### The Hunt for Fheralai Stormsworn & Dreadnaught Assault
Later in the campaign, [[The Order of Znak]] called upon Captain Deloz to navigate the treacherous hurricane waters of the Sea of Swords to hunt down High Priestess [[Fheralai Stormsworn]]. Deloz piloted *Twinkle* through the storm, tracked the Talosite fleet, and brought the heroes directly alongside the cult's flagship. Deloz did not merely ferry the champions—he joined the battle alongside the Order, fighting on deck as they boarded and scoured the dreadnaught to destroy Fheralai's forces.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **R.A. Salvatore Lore Mastery:** Peter is a dedicated fan of R.A. Salvatore's *The Legend of Drizzt* legendarium, having listened to all 39 audiobooks (narrated by Victor Bevine). Bringing Captain Deloz, *Twinkle*, and the Bregan D'aerthe network into the campaign gave Peter the opportunity to dip deeply into his extensive lore knowledge of Jarlaxle Baenre and the famous drow of the Sword Coast.
