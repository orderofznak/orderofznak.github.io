# 👤 Manda Gutterpunch

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Supply Storekeeper & Villager
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Leilon Point]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Manda Gutterpunch** was a gruff and pragmatic resident who managed the general supply storehouse at [[Leilon Point]], stocking fishing line, salt barrels, iron hooks, and rations for local fishing crews. During the sudden [[Sahuagin]] amphibious assault, Manda was ambushed and killed inside her shop while trying to defend the village supplies.

* **Encounter Status:** Deceased (Body Discovered)
