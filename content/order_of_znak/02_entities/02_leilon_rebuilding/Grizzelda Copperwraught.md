# 🔨 Grizzelda Copperwraught ("The Growler")

## 🛡️ Overview & Identity
* **Identity:** Gruff female dwarf master builder personally commissioned and hired by Lord Protector **Dagult Neverember** of Neverwinter to oversee the physical rebuilding of [[Leilon]].
* **Moniker ("The Growler"):** Behind her back, the townsfolk and laborers refer to her as **"The Growler"** due to her gravelly voice, unyielding scowl, blunt instructions, and absolute zero tolerance for laziness or shoddy craftsmanship.

## 🏗️ Rebuilding Leilon & Work Crew Stewardship
* **Civil Architecture:** Entrusted with the construction of every primary municipal structure, defense rampart, harbor pier, and residential block in the rebuilt town.
* **Master of the Works:** Instructs the teams of masons, carpenters, and laborers directly on the scaffolding, barking precise engineering specifications.
* **Tenday Paymaster:** At the end of each tenday, Grizzelda personally inspects the completed works and pays the builders their hard-earned coin, ensuring fair compensation and meticulous accountability.

---

## 🏰 Constructing The Order of Znak Headquarters
* When [[The Order of Znak]] arrived in Act 2 (*Storm Lord's Wrath*) to establish their permanent base of operations, Grizzelda coordinated directly with the heroes and their newly appointed majordomo **[[Leopold]]**.
* Respected the party's pragmatic architectural demands—reinforced stone battlements, expansive training yards, and a secure armory—ensuring the Order's manor became the strongest fortress in town.

## 👥 Key Relationships & Dynamics
* **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]:** Despite Grizzelda's gruff exterior, she harbors deep respect for the halfling priestess whose morning ministry and meals keep her builders on their feet.
* **[[Hazz Yorrum|Sergeant Hazz Yorrum]]:** Grizzelda holds open contempt for the lazy Neverwinter sergeant, frequently dressing him down for failing to post competent guards around her active construction yards.
* **[[Leopold]]:** Maintains a regular professional working relationship with the Order's majordomo for ongoing manor repairs and stonework upkeep.
