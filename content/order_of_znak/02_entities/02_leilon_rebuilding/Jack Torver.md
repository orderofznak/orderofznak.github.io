# 👤 Jack Torver

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Shopkeeper
* **Primary Region / Home:** Leilon, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Sleeping Dragon's Wake, Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Jack Torver** was the proprietor of the [[Torver's Post]] general store in [[Leilon]] during the late 15 century [[DR]]. He also served on the town council.

### History
Jack arrived in Leilon as the long-ruined town was being rebuilt in the late 15 century DR, and he promptly built its first general store. He briefly served as a member of the town council, becoming its newest member upon the opening of his store. Even by the end of his council tenure, his shop remained the only general store in town. 

During the subsequent [[Battle of Leilon]], he served as a messenger to relay the grim news that berserkers of [[Talos]] were attacking from the north at the same time that zombies were attacking from the west.

### Description
He was a young [[Turami]] man known for his optimism.

### Activities
He was a devout follower of [[Lathander]], and attended sermons each morning at the [[Lathander Shrine (Leilon)|Lathander Shrine]] in town. He was known to parrot the messages of these sermons to his customers.

If he did not carry something in his store, he was happy to direct customers to other vendors who did. Most notably, he would direct adventurers to the nearby [[Tymora Shrine (Leilon)|Tymora Shrine]] if they needed **potions of healing**.
