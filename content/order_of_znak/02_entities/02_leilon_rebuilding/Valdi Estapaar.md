# 👤 Valdi Estapaar

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-elf
* **Role & Occupation:** Fisher
* **Primary Region / Home:** Leilon, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath, Sleeping Dragon's Wake, Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Valdi Estapaar** was the half-elf overseer of the fishing business and a member of the town council of [[Leilon]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Description
Valdi was an elderly half-elven woman.

### Activities
When Leilon was resettled in the late 15 century DR, Valdi was assigned to supervise the fishing business that was meant to sustain the town's construction. The caught fish was used to feed the population, while the surplus was cured and sold in surrounding cities.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Active Ally & Rescued Council Member
* **The Quayside Boneclaw Ambush (The Siege of Leilon — Act 4 Chapter 5):**
  - When the Cult of Myrkul launched a multi-pronged assault on Leilon, a detachment of undead struck the harbor. An enormous, horrifying **Boneclaw** attacked the fishers, pinning Councilor Valdi underneath a heavy overturned rowboat!
  - Hearing the commotion from the South Gate, [[The Order of Znak]] sprinted to the Quayside.
  - While [[Rhynok]] scored deadly critical sniper hits against the monster, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] charged forward, rolling a dominant Strength check to heave the crushing rowboat off Valdi's body.
  - Rather than fleeing, the resilient half-elf councilor drew her veteran's longsword and joined the fray, trading blows alongside Krageer and Tvyordiy to butcher the remaining ghouls and destroy the Boneclaw.
  - In the aftermath, Valdi pointed the Order toward the Town Square just as northern alarm bells began to toll, directing them to confront the dragon Claugiyliamatar.
