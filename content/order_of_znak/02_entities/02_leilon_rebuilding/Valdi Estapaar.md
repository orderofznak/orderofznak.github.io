## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Valdi Estapaar** was the [[half-elf]] overseer of the fishing business and a member of the town council of [[Leilon]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Description
Valdi was an elderly half-elven woman.

### Activities
When Leilon was resettled in the late 15 century DR, Valdi was assigned to supervise the fishing business that was meant to sustain the town's construction. The caught fish was used to feed the population, while the surplus was cured and sold in surrounding cities.
