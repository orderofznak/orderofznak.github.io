# ☀️ Merrygold Brightshine

## 🛡️ Overview & Personality
* **Identity:** Amiable, outgoing young female halfling priest of **Lathander**, the god of light, dawn, vitality, and renewal.
* **Civic Role:** One of the foundational community leaders who helped establish [[Leilon]] following its reclamation, well-regarded by the settlers, fishers, and laborers for her optimism, gentle empathy, and cheer.

## 🌅 The Shrine of Lathander & Dawn Ministry
* **Daily Ministry:** Cares for the **Shrine of Lathander** in the heart of Leilon, tending the altar and providing warm pastoral counsel to any weary traveler, settler, or soldier who stops to pray before its small stone altar.
* **Dawn Services:** Every morning at the break of dawn, Merrygold conducts outdoor sunrise services, greeting the new day with choral hymns of light, renewal, and hope.

---

## 🏛️ Leilon's First Temple & Care for the Workers
* **First Temple Rights:** Merrygold and the Church of Lathander were granted the official charter to construct the first permanent temple in rebuilt Leilon. Her church paid handsomely into the municipal treasury for the honor.
* **Laborer Hospitality:** Outside of their standard priestly devotions, Merrygold and her shrine acolytes personally assist in caring for the construction crews working under [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]. They prepare hot meals, distribute clean water, and dress injuries outside of normal work shifts to ensure no laborer is neglected.

---

## 👥 Key Alliances & Dynamics
* **[[Myagkiy Znak]]:** Both serve as Life Domain clerics in Leilon, conferring on healing and divine assistance.
* **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]:** While Grizzelda oversees the town construction crews, Merrygold provides pastoral care, ensuring workers receive nourishment and support.
