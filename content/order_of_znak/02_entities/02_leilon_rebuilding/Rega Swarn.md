# 👤 Rega Swarn

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Cult Commander & Chimera Rider
* **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Mere of Dead Men]] / [[Iniarv's Tower]]
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Rega Swarn** was a formidable cult leader affiliated with the Cult of the Dragon and the necromantic forces garrisoning [[Iniarv's Tower]]. Commanding the scouts positioned along the ancient ramparts, Rega was the bonded handler of the three-headed Chimera **[[Ashbreath]]**, using the flying monstrosity to dominate the surrounding skies of the Mere of Dead Men.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The Ramparts Aerial Showdown (October 1 – 10, 2020):** After [[The Order of Znak]] executed her traitorous scouts in the courtyard and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] scaled the battlements to engage her archers, Rega Swarn launched into the fray mounted atop Ashbreath.
* **Fire and Brimstone:** Rega commanded Ashbreath's aerial strikes, raining down a 15-foot cone of *Fire Breath* across the stone courtyard and ramparts.
* **The Slaying:** Despite battering Tvyordiy down to 0 HP on the walls, [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s divine healing restored the monk while an upcast 5th-level ***Spirit Guardians*** flooded the ruins with blinding radiant fire, disintegrating Ashbreath and incinerating Rega Swarn.
