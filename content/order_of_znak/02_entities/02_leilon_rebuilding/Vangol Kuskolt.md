# 👤 Vangol Kuskolt

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Dwarf
* **Role & Occupation:** Priest
* **Primary Region / Home:** Leilon, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Vangol Kuskolt** was a [[dwarven]] [[priest]] of [[Tyr]] active in the [[Sword Coast North]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Description
He walked heavily on an [[iron]] [[Prosthetic|peg leg]].

### Personality
Vangol was fiery yet fair.

### Activities
Vangol assumed the duties of presiding over the [[Tyr Shrine (Leilon)|Tyr Shrine]] when it was repaired and rebuilt along with the town of [[Leilon]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. He soon became the newfounded community's judge, presiding over and adjudicating legal disputes on the steps of his shrine. When he judged a criminal guilty of an awful crime, he would heat his peg leg in a fire and use it to brand the guilty party before driving them out of town.

Shortly after his arrival, he became one of Leilon's five town councilors.
