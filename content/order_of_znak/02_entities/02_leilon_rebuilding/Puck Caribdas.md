# 👤 Puck Caribdas

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Elf
* **Role & Occupation:** Acolyte of [[Tymora]] & Town Potion Seller / Apothecary
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Leilon]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]], *Divine Contention*

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Puck Caribdas** was an elven acolyte of [[Tymora]] and seller of potions in the rebuilding town of [[Leilon]] as of the late 15th century DR. Physically delicate and brimming with nervous energy, Puck brewed and peddled healing draughts and transmutation elixirs to adventurers and town defenders along the High Road.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met & Patronized (*Allied Leilon Potion Merchant*)
* **Puck's *Potion of Growth* & The Volcano Titan Grapple:** Prior to (or during) **[[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]**, Goliath barbarian **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** purchased a ***Potion of Growth*** from Puck's apothecary stall in [[Leilon]]. Deep inside the dormant volcano during the interlude's boss battle against the **Huge Fire Giant Queen**, a single enlargement effect would have only increased the Medium Goliath to Large. Invoking the **Rule of Cool ("Double-Enlarge")**, Krageer quaffed Puck's *Potion of Growth* (`Medium` &rarr; `Large`) **and** **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** cast ***Enlarge*** on him (`Large` &rarr; `Huge`)—stacking both effects so Krageer swelled to full **Huge** titan stature and wrestled the Fire Giant Queen bare-handed beside the molten lava!

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Table Trivia
* **The "Rule of Cool" Double-Enlarge:** Under strict 5e RAW, identical magical effects don't stack and a single *Enlarge* spell or *Potion of Growth* only increases a Medium character one size category to **Large**. Because the **Fire Giant Queen** was **Huge**, Peter ruled via the **Rule of Cool** that drinking Puck's *Potion of Growth* **and** receiving Myagkiy's *Enlarge* spell stacked ("Double-Enlarge"), boosting Krageer two size categories (`Medium` &rarr; `Large` &rarr; `Huge`) for an unforgettable kaiju-scale wrestling match in the volcano.
* **Table Pun Craft:** Peter pitched both endorsement puns at the table as classic Krageer-style humor, turning Puck Caribdas's humble Leilon potion stall into an enduring campaign meme.
