# 🐴 Kaelen Greenbottle

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Cheery, enterprising Lightfoot Halfling caravan master, teamster, and merchant operating out of [[Leilon]].
* **Personality:** Gregarious, fond of road-songs, whistling tavern jigs, and haggling with a broad grin. Possesses an uncanny knack for securing rare mountain provisions and fine spirits.
* **The High Road Trader:** During the reconstruction of Leilon and the peace secured by [[The Order of Znak]], Kaelen established trade caravans running between Leilon, Triboar, and Yartar. In 1500 DR, before [[Tvyordiy Znak]] departed for the Star Mounts, Kaelen provided him with dried provisions, trail rations, and a flask of potent dwarven spirits (which later saved Tvyordiy and [[Yeri]] from freezing atop Mount Nimbus).
* **The Return to Leilon (1502 DR):** In the autumn of 1502 DR, Kaelen was unsaddling his pack-pony beside the town square fountain as Tvyordiy marched into town with his sister [[Klee'errk]] and the planar disciples, directing teamsters unloading dried trout, wheel-cheese, and amber apple brandy for the booming markets.

## 📊 Combat & Mechanics
* **Class & Role:** Level 6 Scout Rogue / Merchant.
* **Proficiencies:** Persuasion, Deception, Animal Handling, Insight, Land Vehicles.
