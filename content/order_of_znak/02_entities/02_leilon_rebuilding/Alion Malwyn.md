# 👤 Alion Malwyn

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Innkeeper
* **Primary Region / Home:** Leilon, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Sleeping Dragon's Wake, Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Alion Malwyn** was the proprietor of **[[The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn]]** in [[Leilon]] as of the late 15th century DR.

### Description
They were a nonbinary Illuskan human.

### Personality
Alion was known for being kind and for being eager to share stories. They collected tales and rumors from travelers and happily relayed them to the inn's guests each night.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (September 1, 2020)
* **The Bronze Dragon Rumor (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** Following [[The Order of Znak]]'s successful defense of [[Leilon Point]] from sahuagin invaders, the party stopped into **[[The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn]]** on the morning of September 1, 2020. Alion served the companions and shared the defining rumor that steered the party toward their next major expedition:
  > *“Did you hear about the bronze dragon? Apparently, a few travelers from Neverwinter saw one fly north along the coast with a dolphin in its claws. They say those metallic dragons are good. I hope they’re right.”*
  This tip directly inspired the Order's subsequent journey north along the cliffs to discover the **Bronze Shrine** and parley with ancient bronze dragon **[[Lhammaruntosz]]**.
