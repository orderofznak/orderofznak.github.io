# 👤 Nava Wavecrash

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Priest of Umberlee
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Leilon Point]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Nava Wavecrash** was a resident priest who tended the small coastal Shrine of Umberlee (the Bitch Queen) at [[Leilon Point]]. Nava performed daily offerings of salt, fish, and rum to placate the tempestuous sea goddess on behalf of local fishers. When the [[Sahuagin]] raided the promontory, Nava was murdered at her roadside shrine by the invaders.
