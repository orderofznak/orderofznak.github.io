# 🥧 Cooragh

## Overview & Role
* **Role:** Half-orc proprietor of the Wayside Inn.
* **Specialty:** Famous across the region for her legendary, delicious meat pies.
* **Romance:** Romantically entangled with **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, who frequented the Wayside Inn during [[The Order of Znak]]'s campaigns in Leilon.
