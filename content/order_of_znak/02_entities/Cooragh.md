# 🥧 Cooragh

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** Half-orc proprietor and head cook of the historic Wayside Inn, situated strategically along the High Road between Waterdeep and Neverwinter near Leilon.
* **Tavern Specialty:** Celebrated across the Sword Coast for her hearty, savory meat pies, drawing weary travelers, merchants, and sellswords from miles away.
* **Romance with Krageer:** Developed a deep romantic bond with Goliath champion **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, making the Wayside Inn the favorite resting stop and unofficial sanctuary of [[The Order of Znak]].

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Running Table Romance Gag:** The romance between Krageer and Cooragh began as a spontaneous roleplay interaction during *Storm Lord's Wrath*. Gary humorously established that Krageer's ferocious warrior instincts were completely disarmed by Cooragh's meat pies, making their courtship one of the campaign's fondest recurring comedic subplots.
