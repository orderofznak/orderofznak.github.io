# 🛡️ Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu

## Character Overview & Stats
* **Player:** [[Gary Forsyth]]
* **Race:** Goliath
* **Class:** Level 13 Multiclass — Level 9 Barbarian (Totem Warrior) / Level 4 Fighter (Champion Archetype)
* **Background:** Soldier
* **Ability Scores:** STR 20 (+5) | DEX 13 (+1) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 12 (+1) | CHA 6 (-2)
* **Armor Class (AC):** 18 (wearing +2 Scale Mail Armor)
* **Feats:** Great Weapon Master (GWM)

---

## ⚔️ Weapons & Legendary Gear
* **Signature Weapon — *Ultima Exodus*:** A two-handed **+2 Greataxe** forged from silvered and adamantine metal. Named after classic *Ultima III: Exodus* (shared inside joke between Gary and User as *Ultima* fans).
* **Armor:** +2 Scale Mail Armor.
* **Utility & Magic Items:**
  * **Alchemy Jug:** Provides free drinks at camp and taverns!
  * **Adamantine Shackles:** Used for capturing and securing high-value foes alive.
* **Chest Tattoo:** Inked on his chest depicting **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]** (lizardfolk matriarch slain in battle, tattooed out of mutual respect after allied victory against undead trolls).

---

## 📖 Combat Role & Story Highlights
* **Combat Role:** Frontline damage-dealing meat shield. Wields *Ultima Exodus* with GWM to soak massive damage while delivering high DPR.
* **Wayside Inn Romance:** Romantically entangled with **[[Cooragh]]** over her legendary meat pies.
* **Dormant Volcano Boss Battle:** Enlarged by **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**'s *Enlarge* spell, allowing Krageer to wrestle a **Fire Giant Queen** in epic grapple combat inside a dormant volcano!
* **Woodland Manse Escape:** Pincushioned by needle blights during an early underleveled retreat, carried off the map by **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**.
