# 👤 Adabra Gwynn

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Humanoid
* **Role & Occupation:** Midwife, apothecary
* **Primary Region / Home:** Umbrage Hill, Phandalin, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Adabra Gwynn** was an acolyte of [[Chauntea]] who lived in a stone windmill on [[Umbrage Hill]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Activities
She served as the local midwife and apothecary for the nearby village of [[Phandalin]]. She used the millstone of the windmill in which she lived to pulverize the ingredients for her medicines. She instead made her home outside of town in the old windmill on Umbrage Hill, using the first floor as her apothecary shop, the second floor as her own living space, and reserving the third floor for guests. 

When the white dragon [[Cryovain]] began terrorizing the region, the people of Phandalin feared for Adabra's safety, and Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]] offered to pay anyone willing to brave the short journey to check on her and urge her to return to town. He was somewhat right to be worried as Adabra's home was attacked by a manticore, which had been driven from from its mountaintop nest in the [[Sword Mountains]] by the dragon.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Rescued Ally & Apothecary)
* **Party Notes:** Rescued by [[The Order of Znak]] during their very first frontier quest at [[Umbrage Hill]]. Rather than parleying with the hungry **manticore** besieging her stone windmill, the party **fought and drove the beast off**. Although the champions could not convince the stubborn acolyte to abandon her windmill for [[Phandalin]], a grateful Adabra gifted them a *Potion of Healing* and signed a letter confirming her safety for [[Harbin Wester]]. *(Later, prior to the [[Woodland Manse]], the manticore and its mate returned to Umbrage Hill and were slain by [[Cryovain]].)*
