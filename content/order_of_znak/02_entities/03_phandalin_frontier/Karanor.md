* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Kryptgarden Forest]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Karanor** was an animated, spellcasting Flameskull created by **[[Ularan Mortus]]** and the high necromancers of Myrkul. Bound by unholy magic and wreathed in flickering emerald flames, Karanor was dispatched at the head of a pack of ghouls to breach the outskirts of [[Kryptgarden Forest]] and assault the outer perimeter of [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s lair.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Temporarily Destroyed
* **The Overland Ambush (November 13, 2020):** While marching toward Deeping Cave on the world map, [[The Order of Znak]] was ambushed by Karanor and his ghoul vanguard.
* **The Slaying:** The Order made swift work of the undead raiders—[[Rhynok]]'s archery and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s greataxe hacking apart the ghouls while [[Myagkiy Znak]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] smashed the floating skull to bone cinders.
* **The Undead Trait:** Lacking holy water or dispel magic on the scattered fragments, Karanor's unholy nature meant the flameskull would reform and rejuvenate an hour later, leaving the party to push quickly into the northern caves.
