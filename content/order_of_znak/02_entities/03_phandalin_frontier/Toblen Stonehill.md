# 👤 Toblen Stonehill

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Innkeeper
* **Primary Region / Home:** Phandalin, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit, Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Toblen Stonehill** was the proprietor of the [[Stonehill Inn]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
Toblen and his family were originally from [[Triboar]].

By [[1496 DR]], Pip was old enough to help run the business. That year, Harbin Wester began seeking ways to leverage Toblen's debts to seize control of the inn.

### Description
Toblen was a young man who was quite short and very friendly.
