## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Breltora Red-Eye** was a [[half-orc]] stonemason in [[Leilon]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
**Circa** [[1491 DR]], Private Jessup's commander, Sergeant [[Hazz Yorrum]], sent him and three other soldiers to patrol the [[High Road]] along the [[Mere of Dead Men]]. When the patrol didn't return after already being two days late, Breltora began to worry. However, Yorrum did nothing to investigate the disappearance of the soldiers, so Breltora contacted a group of [[adventurers]]. Breltora and the adventurers pressed Yorrum into showing them the patrol route on a map. The adventurers then rescued the missing soldiers, who had been captured by a [[lizardfolk]] tribe.
