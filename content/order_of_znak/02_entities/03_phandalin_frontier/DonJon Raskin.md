# 🤠 DonJon Raskin

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Role in Phandalin:** Overlord and overseer of the [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] in the foothills of the Sword Mountains, appointed by the [[Phandalin]] Townmaster.
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Gregarious, boastful, and surprisingly resilient. A veteran adventurer turned mine supervisor with an unending repertoire of tall tales.
* **Party Standing:** Recognized as the absolute favorite NPC of [[The Order of Znak]], who frequently checked in on him long after clearing the mine.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Milestones
* **Fierce Battle at the [[Shrine of Savras]]:** Hired in [[Phandalin]] to escort DonJon to the troubled [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], [[The Order of Znak]] first detoured with him to the [[Shrine of Savras]]. Fighting shoulder-to-shoulder with the boisterous troubleshooter, the party clashed with a massive warband of occupying **Orcs** and **Ogres** in a fierce courtyard battle, slaying the entire horde before receiving Savras's altar vision of [[Cryovain]] and prying loose the shrine's solid gold bell.
* **[[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Liberation:** Arriving at the mine with DonJon in tow, the Order saw straight through wererat boss [[Zeleen Varnaster|Zeleen Varnaster's]] transparent pose as mine overseer. In the ensuing battle, when [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak|Tvyordiy]] discovered their mundane weapons could not scratch the lycanthropes' cursed hides, [[Rhynok]] (with his enchanted *+1 longbow*) and [[Myagkiy Znak|Myagkiy]] (wielding *Spirit Guardians*, *Spiritual Weapon*, and *Sacred Flame*) carried the entire fight—liberating the mine for DonJon.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Table Portrayal & Voice:** Voiced by Peter with an exaggerated, hilarious Louisiana Cajun accent that immediately endeared him to Gary.
* **Running Table Gags:** Peter constantly improvised colorful monologues during interactions, featuring absurdly detailed grievances about DonJon's multiple ex-wives and exaggerated brags about his mythical youth exploits.
