# 👤 Erlum

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human

* **Primary Region / Home:** Leilon, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Erlum** was a [[Neverwinter|Neverwintan]] soldier stationed in the [[Leilon]] garrison in the [[High Tower of Thalivar]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
When Leilon was resettled in the late 15 century DR, [[Dagult Neverember]] sent some of Neverwinter's soldiers to defend the settlers during the town's reconstruction. Erlum was among these soldiers, serving under Sergeant [[Hazz Yorrum]]'s command. The High Tower of Thalivar acted as a garrison for the soldiers. However, it turned out that the wizard tower was haunted by the ghost of [[Thalivar]]. The wizard's spirit drove away Erlum, Yorrum and two other soldiers ([[Gori]] and [[Koz]]). However, the town mage [[Gallio Elibro]] refused to leave. The soldiers had recently noticed Gallio's odd behavior but they were unaware that Thalivar's ghost took possession of the mage every night. A group of adventurers took care of the ghost.
