# 👤 Elmar Barthen

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Shopkeeper
* **Primary Region / Home:** Phandalin, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit, Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Elmina Barthen** was the proprietor of [[Barthen's Provisions]], the largest trading post in [[Phandalin]], in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
In [[1491 DR]], Barthen and other townsfolk of Phandalin were constantly harassed by the [[Redbrands]], which made local trade more difficult. She was willing to share information with anybody who wished to sort the Redbrands out.

### Personality
Barthen had a kindly manner.

Barthen had a good business relationship with Phandalin's Townmaster, [[Harbin Wester]], and helped Harbin and his half-brother, [[Tibor Wester]], to circumvent [[Neverwinter]] taxes by transporting supplies to the latter's [[Tibor Wester's loggers' camp|logging business]].
