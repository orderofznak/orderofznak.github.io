# 👤 Dazlyn Grayshard

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Shield dwarf
* **Role & Occupation:** Prospector
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Dazlyn Grayshard** was a shield dwarf prospector in the late 15<sup>th</sup> century [[DR]]. Together with her business partner, [[Norbus Ironrune]], she discovered the ruins of the [[temple of Abbathor]] in the [[Sword Mountains]].

### History
When prospecting for gold in the Sword Mountains southwest of [[Phandalin]], Dazlyn and Norbus came across the ruins of a temple of [[Abbathor]] and the nearby dwarven settlement that had been buried by an landslide a long time ago. In the following months, the two dwarves excavated the ruins until they encountered the ochre jellies that had infested the temple.

When a group of adventurers came to the excavation site and warned Dazlyn and Norbus of the young white dragon [[Cryovain]] who had settled in the area, the dwarves hired the adventurers to clear the temple of the [[Ooze|oozes]]. As payment, they offered their sending stones.

### Description
Dazlyn was sincere and enjoyed joking around with Norbus.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Employer & Allied Prospector)
* **Party Notes:** Shield dwarf prospector excavating the [[Temple of Abbathor]] alongside her partner [[Norbus Ironrune]]. Arriving after [[Umbrage Hill]], [[The Order of Znak]] slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** inside the temple, discovered its secret doors, and excavated the rubble-choked tunnel to the inner Statue of Abbathor (where [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] triggered the exploding gem trap). Although the party could not persuade Dazlyn and Norbus to part with extra coin, the prospectors gifted them their promised pair of ***Sending Stones*** before the Order slaughtered an orc ambush at the canyon exit.
