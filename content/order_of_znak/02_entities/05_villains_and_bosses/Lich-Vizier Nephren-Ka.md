# 💀 Lich-Vizier Nephren-Ka

## 🛡️ Overview & Dark Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Ancient Netherese archlich and high ritualist of fallen **Anauria** who survived the fall of the floating enclaves millennia ago by binding his life essence to an extraplanar phylactery.
* **Role in Myrkul's Resurgence:** Re-awakened to serve the diminished **[[Myrkul|Lord of Bones]]**, Nephren-Ka established the sunken black glass pyramid of **Aul-Khem** as a primary mortal harvesting nexus. He oversaw the construction of the **Blood Chrysalis**—a calcified sacrificial font designed to extract living mortal souls from kidnapped Bedine nomads and caravan travelers to feed Myrkul's planar engines.
* **Appearance:** A desiccated skeletal arch-mage hovering aloft in a vertical column of dark levitation energy above the blood font. Draped in rotted gold-and-purple ceremonial linen, his hollow eye sockets burn with malignant violet witchfire, wielding withered claw-hands that weave primordial high-magic.
* **The Confrontation at Aul-Khem (1502 DR):**
  * When [[Myagkiy Znak]] breached the subterranean sanctum, Nephren-Ka directed [[Death Knight Vaelen]] and four mummy attendants to crush the cleric.
  * Caught in Myagkiy's *Flame Strike*, Nephren-Ka retaliated ruthlessly, casting an upcast 6th-level *Blight* and a descending 60-foot *Cone of Cold* directly into the sanctuary.
  * In Round 3, when Myagkiy shattered the Blood Chrysalis with *Bonecounter*, the active conduit violently backlashed into Nephren-Ka for 30 force damage, knocking him prone onto the glass floor.
  * Nephren-Ka cast *Power Word Stun* to freeze Myagkiy and unleashed legendary *Ray of Frost* beams, bringing the cleric to 8 HP.
  * However, Myagkiy broke the stun through sheer Constitution resilience, invoked *Preserve Life* and *Regenerate*, and directed his *Spiritual Weapon* to batter the lich down to 52 HP.
* **Discorporation & Legacy:** Out of high-circle spells and facing an unbroken champion of Life, Nephren-Ka triggered emergency contingency magic to discorporate his physical form, fleeing south-southwest across Anauria to his hidden phylactery vault. In his haste, he left behind ***The Astrolabe of Nephren-Ka***—a soul-compass that later guided Myagkiy to the true planar roots of the conspiracy.
