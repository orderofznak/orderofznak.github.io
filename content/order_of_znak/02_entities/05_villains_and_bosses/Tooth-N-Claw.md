# 🐺 Tooth and Claw (Tooth-N-Claw)

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Hell hounds (Pair of fiendish hounds)
* **Role & Occupation:** Guardian fiends of Gadrille the Reef-Reaver
* **Alignment:** Lawful evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Thunder Cliffs, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
In printed adventure canon, **Tooth-N-Claw** was the fiendish pet of [[Gadrille]] the Reef-Reaver, granted by the god [[Talos]] in recognition of her fanatical devotion to maritime destruction.

### Campaign Adaptation: The Twin Hell Hounds
In *The Order of Znak* campaign, rather than a single entity, the guardian was adapted into **two savage hell hounds: Tooth and Claw**. Together they formed Gadrille's inner sanctum vanguard within the Thunder Cliff Caves, breathing plumes of hellfire and protecting their evoker mistress.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain in Battle (Act 2: *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Chapter 7: "Assault on Thunder Cliffs")
* **The Sanctum Defense:** Flanking Gadrille during [[The Order of Znak]]'s breach of the inner cavern, Tooth and Claw unleashed coordinated fiery breath attacks across the chamber. [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] surged directly into the flame cones, engaging the fiendish beasts in brutal melee. Krageer's greataxe and Tvyordiy's concussive strikes battered the hounds down, slaying both beasts before turning to finish off Gadrille.
