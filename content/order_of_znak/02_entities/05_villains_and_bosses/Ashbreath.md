# 👤 Ashbreath

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Monstrosity (Three-Headed Chimera)
* **Role & Occupation:** Bonded Mount & Aerial Terror
* **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Mere of Dead Men]] / [[Iniarv's Tower]]
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Ashbreath** was a ferocious three-headed chimera possessing the heads of a red dragon, a ravenous lion, and a horned goat, complete with leathery draconic wings. Bonded to cult commander **[[Rega Swarn]]**, Ashbreath roosted atop the ruined parapets of [[Iniarv's Tower]], serving as an aerial apex predator guarding the cult's swamp operations.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The Ramparts Aerial Showdown (October 1 – 10, 2020):** When [[The Order of Znak]] assaulted the ruined tower, Ashbreath surged from the heights carrying Rega Swarn into battle.
* **Fire Breath Attack:** Ashbreath unleashed a 15-foot cone of searing *Fire Breath* across the stone courtyard and battlements, scorching the champions and forcing rapid Dexterity saves (Tvyordiy 23, Krageer 7/10).
* **The Radiant Demise:** After Ashbreath and the archers brought Tvyordiy down to 0 HP on the battlements, [[Myagkiy Znak]] revived the monk and unleashed a devastating 5th-level ***Spirit Guardians***. Swirling angelic radiance engulfed the monstrosity, incinerating its wings and crashing Ashbreath into the ruins.
