# 💀 Ularan Mortus

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** High Priest of Myrkul / Overarching BBEG
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath, Sleeping Dragon's Wake, Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
Ularan Mortus was the dreaded High Priest of Myrkul (Lord of Bones) and the overarching mastermind and primary antagonist of the Leilon expansion trilogy.

* **Strings in the Shadows:** Operating from the sunken, mist-shrouded ruins of Iniarv's Tower deep within the Mere of Dead Men, Ularan commanded the Cult of Myrkul across the Sword Coast North, deploying death cultists and undead spies (such as Argus Skeel in Phandalin).
* **The Ebondeath Pact:** Formed an unholy covenant with the disembodied spirit of the ancient black dracolich Chardansearavitriol (Ebondeath), seeking to anchor the legendary wyrm's soul into a physical draconic vessel and raise an invincible legion of the dead.
* **The Siege of Leilon & The Ruinstone:** In the climax of the Divine Contention arc, Ularan marched an undead host upon Leilon to seize the planar Ruinstone from the House of Thalivar, clashing simultaneously with the town garrison and rival Talos cultists.
* **Climactic Defeat:** Confronted during the battle of Leilon by **The Order of Znak**, who severed his necrotic connection to Ebondeath, disrupted his unholy rituals, and destroyed the necromancer for good.
