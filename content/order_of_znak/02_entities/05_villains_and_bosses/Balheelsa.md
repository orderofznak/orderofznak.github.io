# 🪆 Balheelsa (Night Hag)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A malevolent, ancient **Night Hag** who operated from a secluded, rotting hovel deep in the dark boughs of **Neverwinter Wood**.
* **Soul Trafficker of the Lower Planes:** Actively participated in the interplanar soul trade, harvesting mortal souls across the Sword Coast and selling them to fiends and devils to fuel the eternal **Blood War** in the Lower Planes.
* **The Soul Dolls:** Known for a gruesome, perverse magical practice: whenever she harvested innocent mortal souls, she would either ferry them to the Abyss/Hells or **trap their screaming spirits inside the hollow heads of porcelain dolls** lining the shelves of her hut.
* **Mother & Captor of Yasmina:** Kept a young girl named **[[Yasmina]]** in terrifying confinement, claiming to be her mother while subjecting the child to occult nightmares.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Middleman of Gond's Relic:** After the theft of the *[[Right Hand of Gond]]* from [[Flanning Teldorer]]'s forge and the serial killings by [[Graag]] in Neverwinter, the trail led to Balheelsa. Interrogated cultists revealed that Balheelsa had acquired the sacred hammer and brokered a deal to trade it to Fire Giant Queen **[[Kevlaan the Merciless]]** in Mount Hotenow.
* **The Hut Ambush & Defeat (July 4, 2022):** [[The Order of Znak]] stormed Balheelsa's woodland lair. Anticipating her ethereal escape, the champions struck with lightning speed—cleaving through her illusory defenses and striking her down before she could slip into the Ethereal Plane.
* **Liberation of Yasmina & Hoard:** Slaying Balheelsa allowed the Order to rescue terrified young [[Yasmina]], who revealed the hag's soul-doll horrors. Searching the lair, the party liberated a locked tin coffer holding **2,500 gp** and **5,000 sp**, raw ivory, ruby dust, and dark ritual components.
