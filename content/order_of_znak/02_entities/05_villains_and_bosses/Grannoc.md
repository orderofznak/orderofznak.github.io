# 👤 Grannoc

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-orc
* **Role & Occupation:** Anchorite of Talos
* **Alignment:** Neutral evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Woodland Manse, Neverwinter Wood, northwest Faerûn
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Grannoc** was an [[anchorite of Talos]] who grew and nurtured a [[Gulthias tree]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
During the late 15 century DR, Grannoc was a member of the anchorites of Talos who venerated their god's servant, [[Gorthok|Gorthok the Thunder Boar]], at the Circle of Thunder.

At some point not long before the arrival of the white dragon [[Cryovain]] in the [[Sword Coast North|region]], Grannoc travelled to the ruins of the [[Woodland Manse]] not far from the Circle of Thunder, and planted a Gulthias tree at the bottom of the well. and several were sent back to the Circle of Thunder to bolster the site's defenses. Meanwhile, three more anchorites joined Grannoc at the Woodland Manse to oversee the raising of pumpkins and boars for the organization's food supply and rituals. A contingent took up residence in the Woodland Manse under Grannoc's supervision, and alongside his blights, they used the ruin as a staging ground to launch attacks on [[Neverwinter]] hunters at [[Falcon's hunting lodge]]. Grannoc himself remained at the Manse, and performed profane rituals involving the consumption of possum entrails to beseech Talos for aid in destroying the hunters.

### Description
Grannoc was a half-orc who dressed in hide armor and was a devout follower of [[Talos]], the god of storms. He was known to perform eerie dances while invoking his god, and to become enraged if anyone dared to interrupt him.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Woodland Manse Boss)
* **Party Notes:**
  * **First Encounter — The Level 3 Retreat:** When [[The Order of Znak]] first scouted the [[Woodland Manse]] at **Level 3** after discovering the dead wizard and his map to the [[Tower of Storms]], Grannoc's ritual in the central courtyard unleashed a massive swarm of twig, needle, and vine blights alongside his fellow [[Anchorites of Talos]]. Outnumbered by the zerging horde, the Level 3 party executed a fighting withdrawal into the forest.
  * **Second Encounter — The Level 6 Vengeance:** Immediately after slaying [[Gorthok]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]], the **Level 6** party marched back to the [[Woodland Manse]] accompanied by centaur ally [[Xanth]]. Armed with Extra Attacks, [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s *Spirit Guardians*, [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s *Stunning Strikes*, [[Rhynok]]'s *Sharpshooter* volleys, and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]'s silvered greataxe, the Order annihilated Grannoc, his anchorite lieutenants, the blight hordes, and a flock of stirges.
