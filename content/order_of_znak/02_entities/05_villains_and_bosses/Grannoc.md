# 👤 Grannoc

* **Species / Heritage:** Half-orc

* **Alignment:** Neutral evil

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Grannoc** was an [[anchorite of Talos]] who grew and nurtured a [[Gulthias tree]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
During the late 15 century DR, Grannoc was a member of the anchorites of Talos who venerated their god's servant, [[Gorthok|Gorthok the Thunder Boar]], at the Circle of Thunder.

At some point not long before the arrival of the [[white dragon]] [[Cryovain]] in the [[Sword Coast North|region]], Grannoc travelled to the ruins of the [[Woodland Manse]] not far from the Circle of Thunder, and planted a Gulthias tree at the bottom of the well. and several were sent back to the Circle of Thunder to bolster the site's defenses. Meanwhile, three more anchorites joined Grannoc at the Woodland Manse to oversee the raising of [[pumpkin]]s and boars for the organization's food supply and [[ritual]]s. A contingent took up residence in the Woodland Manse under Grannoc's supervision, and alongside his blights, they used the ruin as a staging ground to launch attacks on [[Neverwinter]] hunters at [[Falcon's hunting lodge]]. Grannoc himself remained at the Manse, and performed profane rituals involving the consumption of [[possum]] entrails to beseech Talos for aid in destroying the hunters.

### Description
Grannoc was a [[half-orc]] who dressed in [[hide armor]] and was a devout follower of [[Talos]], the god of storms. He was known to perform eerie dances while invoking his god, and to become enraged if anyone dared to interrupt him.
