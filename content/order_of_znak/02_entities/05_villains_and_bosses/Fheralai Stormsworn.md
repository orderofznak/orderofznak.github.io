# ⚡ Fheralai Stormsworn

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-Orc
* **Role & Occupation:** High Priestess of Talos (God of Storms) / Supreme Commander of the Storm Flotilla
* **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** The Sea of Swords / Thunder Cliffs / The Dreadnaught
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, *Divine Contention*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Fheralai Stormsworn** was a zealous Half-Orc High Priestess of **Talos the Destroyer** and the supreme field commander of the storm cultist fleet threatening the Sword Coast North. Commanding anchorites, reavers, storm elementals, and reanimated horrors, Fheralai sought to drown Leilon in an eternal hurricane and bring glory to her destructive deity.

## ⚔️ Campaign Encounters with The Order of Znak

### 1. Act 3: The Captain's Cabin Showdown on *The Relentless* (Sept 18, 2020)
* In *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, [[The Order of Znak]] assaulted the beached ghost ship *The Relentless* near the Tower of Storms.
* Breaching the captain's quarters, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] lunged across the cabin with his *Vicious Quarterstaff*, landing a natural 20 critical unarmed strike (`29 to hit`) and spending ki to successfully **stun** Fheralai, shutting down her somatic spellcasting.
* [[Myagkiy Znak]] stepped forward with a 3rd-level ***Guiding Bolt*** (`21 radiant damage`), and [[Rhynok]] unleashed an assassin sniper volley (`25, 27, 27 to hit`). Battered down to **34 HP**, stunned, and with her crew slaughtered, Fheralai fled into the coastal mists to rebuild her fleet.

### 2. Act 4: The Dreadnaught Climax, The Grapple & Talos's Divine Smite (Nov 20, 2022)
* In *Divine Contention* (Chapter 7), the Order tracked Fheralai aboard her massive, bone-and-metal flagship dreadnaught in the heart of an unnatural hurricane.
* **The Cabin Breakdown:** Kicking open the doors to her quarters, the companions found a broken, trembling champion:
  > *Having failed her mission, the champion begs her deity’s forgiveness. Fheralai has no fear of death but is terrified of failing Talos: if the characters enter her cabin, she chooses to drunkenly unload the details of her plight rather than fight them. Did she not honor her god with chaos and ruin? Were her conquests not glorious?*
* **Krageer's Grapple:** Recognizing her instability and treachery, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] charged forward, rolling a dominant Athletics check and **grappling Fheralai** (`failed save`), pinning her arms and dragging her out onto the storm-swept deck before the burning mainmast brazier.
* **The Smite of Talos:** 
  > *Bolts of lightning lash from the heavens, sizzling on the surface of the sea. Through the crackling electricity, a giant figure emerges from the churning water: a muscular man wearing half-plate armor and a stitched leather eyepatch.*
  > *“You have failed your lord!” he cries in a voice that shakes the heavens.*
  - Fheralai collapsed to her knees, sobbing and screaming into the storm, begging for mercy and recounting the blood shed in his name.
  - But Talos felt only contempt for failure: with a single deafening lightning bolt from his divine fingertips, **Talos smote Fheralai dead on the spot**!

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **DM Encounter Tuning & Min-Max Countermeasures:** Because Gary built Krageer and Rhynok for extreme burst martial damage, DM Peter recognized that a lone priestess would be overwhelmed in a single round. To maintain climactic stakes following Fheralai's divine execution, Peter summoned **Four Air Elemental Myrmidons** and the towering demon lord **Ub-Vhagga the Nalfeshnee** as Talos's personal retribution against the party.
