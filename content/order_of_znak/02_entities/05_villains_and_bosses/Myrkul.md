# 💀 Myrkul (The Lord of Bones)

## 🛡️ Overview & Dark History
* **Divine Portfolio:** Death, Decay, Corruption, Skeletons, Undeath.
* **Symbol:** White human skull crowned with horns or surrounded by grasping skeletal hands.
* **Historical Decline:** Formerly the Greater Deity of the Dead alongside the Dead Three (Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul). Slain during the Time of Troubles (1358 DR) at Boareskyr Bridge by the mortal avatar Midnight (Mystra), his portfolio was eventually claimed by Cyric and subsequently [[Kelemvor]].
* **The Lingering Hunger:** Reduced across the centuries to a desiccated quasi-deity, Myrkul survived through lingering cult cells, ancient Netherese lich cabals, and necrotic relics such as the *Crown of Horns*.
* **The Leilon & Anauria Conspiracy (1492–1504 DR):**
  * Sown through cult disciples such as [[Ularan Mortus]] and [[Emberlost]], Myrkul sought to claim the legendary *Ruinstone* to power a permanent trans-planar tear.
  * In the desert ruins of Anauria, ancient Netherese servants led by [[Lich-Vizier Nephren-Ka]] and [[Death Knight Vaelen]] built the terrestrial feeder conduits at Aul-Khem and throughout the desert.
  * In the deep planar void, Myrkul established the **Negative Energy Siphon**—an obsidian Void-Pillar anchored between the Fugue Plane and the Negative Energy Plane guarded by a Nightwalker to siphon 10,000 ascending souls and restore his former godhood.
* **The Shattering & Final Fall (1504–1505 DR):**
  * In 1504 DR, [[Myagkiy Znak]] shattered the Cenotaph of the Black Star in the Negative Energy Plane, instantly liberating the trapped souls and starving Myrkul of his harvested divine sustenance.
  * Reeling and weakened, Myrkul retreated to the **Sunken Bone Citadel of Oinos in Hades**, where his physical manifestation—the **[[Avatar of Myrkul]]**—was confronted, bested in a 7-round duel, and obliterated by Myagkiy's 6th-level *Guiding Bolt*, crushing his cult networks across Faerûn for an age.
