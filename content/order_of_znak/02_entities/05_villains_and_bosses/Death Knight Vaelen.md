# ⚔️ Death Knight Vaelen

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity & History:** Ancient Netherese martial champion of **Anauria** who fell into dark pacts with the cult of **[[Myrkul]]** during the collapse of Netheril, rising as an undead death knight bound to preserve the empire's sunken secrets.
* **Master of the Guard at Aul-Khem:** Served as the martial warlord within the black glass pyramid of **Aul-Khem**, commanding skeletal nightmares, mummies, and death-priests under the direction of [[Lich-Vizier Nephren-Ka]].
* **The Ritual Defense (1502 DR):**
  * When [[Myagkiy Znak]] entered the chrysalis sanctum, Vaelen engaged in mounted combat upon his burning skeletal nightmare, hurling a devastating *Hellfire Orb*.
  * When Myagkiy invoked *Destroy Undead* and shattered the blood chrysalis with *Bonecounter*, Vaelen was turned and driven in panic from the sanctum.
  * Abandoning the pyramid, Vaelen retreated across the dunes, leaving behind his personal war-journal: ***The Obsidian Ledger of the Grave-Sworn***.
* **The Obsidian Ledger:** Vaelen's recovered ledger contained comprehensive logistic records of four centuries of Myrkulite cells, hidden bone cisterns, and embalming vaults across the Anauroch desert, enabling Myagkiy and the Bedine outriders of Clan Bait Mahra to systematically hunt down and cleanse his entire desert network.
