# 👤 Gadrille

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-elf
* **Role & Occupation:** The Reef-Reaver
* **Primary Region / Home:** Thunder Cliffs, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Gadrille the Reef-Reaver** was a lieutenant of the [[Cult of Talos]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. She was in charge of the cult's operations in the [[Thunder Cliff Caves]].

### History
**Circa** [[1491 DR]], Gadrille's second-in-command, Sovendahl Erkinze, disapproved of some of her more reckless actions and secretly met with the cult's leader, Fheralai Stormsworn, urging her to put a stop to it. Gadrille complied with Fheralai's request but prayed to Talos to punish Sovendahl. The god answered her prayers and cursed Sovendahl.

A little later, the Thunder Cliff Caves were attacked from sea by a group of adventurers. They defeated Gadrille and her Talos cultists, putting an end to her operation.

### Personality
Gadrille was a despotic and immensely committed servant of [[Talos]].

### Activities
Gadrille and her subordinates enticed unwary ships near to the caverns and destroyed them on the magical reefs. They kidnapped the crew, either sacrificing them to Talos or selling them as slaves. The debris was dragged into the caves and anything of value was salvaged and sold. The cult of Talos used the wealth gained from this heinous endeavor to finance agents along the [[Sword Coast]] and the [[High Road]] who tried to build shrines that would ultimately give the cult control of the region.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain in Battle (Act 2: *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Chapter 7: "Assault on Thunder Cliffs")
* **The Sanctum Confrontation:** In the inner cavern of Thunder Cliffs, Gadrille confronted [[The Order of Znak]] alongside her two hell hound guardians, **Tooth and Claw**. The evoker unleashed *Bigby's Hand*, freezing rays, and lightning bolts to repel the intruders. However, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] broke through the flaming jaws of the hounds while [[Rhynok]] targeted the wizard with deadly arrow volleys. With both hounds slain, the Order overwhelmed Gadrille's arcane defenses and executed her. In her sanctum cache, the heroes claimed 880 gp, a *Potion of Superior Healing*, the ***Ring of Warmth*** (attuned by [[Myagkiy Znak]]), and the ***Wand of Magic Missile*** (claimed by Tvyordiy).
