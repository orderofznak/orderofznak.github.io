# 👑 Kevlaan the Merciless (Fire Giant Queen)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** The ruthless, towering **Queen of the Fire Giants** who established her molten seat of power deep within the volcanic caldera of **Mount Hotenow**.
* **Devotee of Surtur:** A fanatical worshiper of **Surtur**, the legendary fire giant patron god of smithing, warfare, and elemental fire. Surtur stands as an ancient divine adversary to **[[Gond]]**, the Lord of All Smiths.
* **The Stolen Relic:** Acquired the sacred relic **[[Right Hand of Gond|The Right Hand of Gond]]** (The Hammer of Gond) through dark brokers, intending to harness its masterwork divine power to forge catastrophic implements of war and lead a massive giant-and-orc army to crush Neverwinter.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Volcanic Caldera Confrontation (July 8, 2022):** The Order traced the stolen hammer through Mount Hotenow's engineered trapped tunnels, confronting Queen Kevlaan upon a stone dais surrounded by roaring rivers of molten lava.
* **The "Double-Enlarge" Titan Grapple:** Standing over 18 feet tall, Kevlaan expected to easily swat aside the mortal heroes. Instead, she was met by **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, who quaffed [[Puck Caribdas|Puck's]] ***Potion of Growth*** while [[Myagkiy Znak]] cast ***Enlarge*** (`Medium` &rarr; `Large` &rarr; **Huge**)! Swelling to equal titan size, Krageer engaged Kevlaan in a bare-handed wrestling grapple at the edge of the magma, locking her in an unbreakable choke.
* **Defeat & Plunder:** While Kevlaan was pinned, [[Tvyordiy Znak]] delivered a flurry of *Stunning Strikes* with his *Vicious Quarterstaff*, locking her in place while [[Rhynok]] unleashed a storm of arrows and Krageer delivered the reckless finishing hews.
* **Spoils:** Slaying Kevlaan reclaimed the *Right Hand of Gond*, broke the fire giant command structure, and yielded an enchanted ring and amulet. Without her leadership, the ensuing monstrous attack on Neverwinter crumbled into chaos and was routed with minimal casualties.

M'Znak maintained the parties health... In a bold move an enlarged Krageer grappled the Queen of the Fire Giants. From there T'Znak was able to stun Kevlaan the Merciless. Rhynok buried several arrows from afar and Krageer was able to finish her off in reckless fashion."*
