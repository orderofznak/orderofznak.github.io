# 👤 Daggermaw

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Shark
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sea of Swords
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Daggermaw** was a large, old, and notably aggressive hunter shark in the [[Sea of Swords]] along the [[Sword Coast North]] as of the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
In the late 15 century DR, the lighthouse at the [[Tower of Storms]] was hijacked by an [[anchorite of Talos]] and used to lure ships to their doom on the nearby rocks. These wrecks became the favorite hunting ground for Daggermaw, along with two other hunter sharks who were relatively tame by comparison.

### Personality
Daggermaw was mean and always hungry. She would attack any potential prey she saw without warning, and it was said that she enjoyed the act of killing.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Tower of Storms Reef Threat)
* **Party Notes:** Ferocious hunter shark prowling the sunken shipwrecks below the [[Tower of Storms]] while guarding the skeletal remains of the banshee [[Miraal]]'s corpse. At **Level 3**, after slaying [[Moesko]] in the lighthouse, [[The Order of Znak]] braved the freezing surf, dispatched the reef threat, and recovered Miraal's jeweled spellbook to earn the **+1 Longbow** for [[Rhynok]] as well as looting the five shipwrecks.
