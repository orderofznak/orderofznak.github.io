# 🐻 Graag (The Werebear)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A savage, shape-shifting barbarian assassin and cult leader who operated as a lethal **Werebear**.
* **Unnatural Mountain Predator:** Unlike standard benevolent or solitary werebears, Graag was corrupted by dark planar fiends—serving as an extraplanar herald sent to terrorize Faerûn and harvest mortal hearts.
* **The Heart-Consuming Murderer:** Spearheaded a synchronized serial slaughter across the city of **Neverwinter**, tearing open the chests of his victims and **consuming their hearts** in dark planar rites.

---

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **The Neverwinter Murders (June 18–19, 2022):** Over the course of several nights, Graag and his cultist henchmen butchered victims across Neverwinter:
  * Slain noblewoman at Sleeping Dragon Bridge.
  * Nine Oghma monks massacred inside the House of Knowledge (leaving only [[Brother Christoph]] alive).
  * Four city watch sentries at the South East Gate.
  * Two tinkerers at Dannar's Mechanical Marvels.
  * Six elite Grey Cloak investigators who attempted to track the killer.
* **Confrontation at Castle Never (June 23, 2022):** [[Rhynok]] identified the killer's heavy paw prints as belonging to an unnatural mountain bear. Tracking the killers through the alleys toward Castle Never, [[The Order of Znak]] intercepted Graag and two henchmen. Before the clash, Graag sneered:
  > *"Your world is ours! We were summoned here as a gift from our gods!"*
* **Defeat:** Swelling into a monstrous half-beast with savage claws and fangs, Graag attacked the party, but was overwhelmed by Krageer's Bear Totem rage, Myagkiy's radiant magic, Rhynok's sniper arrows, and Tvyordiy's martial arts. Searching his lair, the Order confirmed all hearts had been consumed, leading the investigation toward the Starmetal Hills.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Introduction:** Designed by Gary as the introductory boss encounter of *"Keeping the Faith"*, establishing a grim, investigative tone before pivoting to extraplanar fiends.
* **Discord Canon Note:** On June 23, 2022, Gary recorded: *"Graag the large werebear stated 'Your world is ours. We were summoned here as a gift from our gods' before the battle. No sign of the missing hearts... perhaps they were being consumed."*
