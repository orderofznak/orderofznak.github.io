# 💀 Avatar of Myrkul (Weakened Manifestation)

![[myagkiy_bridge_ch05_avatar_myrkul_guiding_bolt_climax.jpg]]
*Figure: The Shattering of the Lord of Bones — Myagkiy Znak unleashing an upcast 6th-level Guiding Bolt through the chest of the Weakened Avatar of Myrkul in the Primordial Titan Skull sanctum of Hades.*

## 🛡️ Overview & Stat Profile
* **Identity:** The physical, mortal-vessel manifestation of **[[Myrkul]]** residing in the subterranean Primordial Titan Skull sanctum of the **Sunken Bone Citadel of Oinos in Hades**.
* **Diminished State:** Following the destruction of his Negative Energy Void-Anchor at the Cenotaph of the Black Star in 1504 DR, the death god was severed from his harvested soul power, reducing his manifestation to an unstable, weakened quasi-deity (Hit Points: 210 HP; AC: 18, rising to 20 under *Unholy Shroud*).
* **Appearance:** A towering, 12-foot skeletal demigod draped in decaying black funerary shrouds, crowned by a massive, ram-horned bone skull helm. Wields the massive, jagged **Scythe of the Reaper** (10-ft reach, dealing brutal slashing and necrotic damage) and glares with a lone witchfire eye that projects a terrifying *Withering Gaze*.
* **Combat Traits & Abilities:**
  * *Scythe of the Reaper:* Multiattack dealing heavy melee damage and threatening necrotic rot.
  * *Bone Eruption:* Blasts razor-sharp osseous spikes across the chamber floor (DC 18 Dex save).
  * *Finger of Death:* High-circle execution magic (DC 18 Con save).
  * *Unholy Shroud:* Dark magical aura raising Armor Class to 20.
  * *Legendary Resistances (3/Day):* Shakes off lethal magical saves.
  * *Withering Gaze (Legendary Action):* Necrotic gaze attack dealing 3d8 necrotic damage.

## ⚔️ The Final Duel in Hades (1505 DR)
Chronicled in [[07c - Bridge - Myagkiy - The Shepherd of the Quiet Grave|Chapter 5 of Myagkiy's Bridge Chronicle]]:
* **Round 1–2:** Myagkiy strode into melee enveloped in 8th-level ***Holy Aura*** (disadvantage on all Avatar attacks), coated his flanged mace ***Bonecounter*** with Natron Salt Unguents (+3d6 radiant, suppressing necrotic regeneration), and summoned a 5th-level *Spiritual Weapon* (twin-sign warhammer of **Ь** and **Ъ**), taking the Avatar from 210 to 143 HP.
* **Round 3–4:** Myagkiy burned the Avatar's Legendary Resistance and sheared its right ram horn with *Bonecounter* and *Spiritual Weapon*, battering the god down to 51 HP.
* **Round 5–6 (The Tactical Teleport & Withdrawal):** Sensing lethal danger, Myagkiy activated his ***Cape of the Mountebank*** to cast ***Dimension Door*** across the chamber, critically saving against *Finger of Death*. Withdrawing an additional 15 feet down the corridor (45 ft total gap) and drinking a maximized ***Potion of Superior Healing*** (restoring 40 HP), the advancing Avatar lunged 30 feet to the archway—**stranded 15 feet away**, unable to reach with its 10-foot scythe!
* **Round 7 (The Final Decree):** Myagkiy raised both gauntlets and unleashed an upcast 6th-level ***Guiding Bolt*** (`28 to hit, 44 radiant damage`), impaling the Lord of Bones dead-center through its chest. The Avatar detonated inward into harmless grey ash and splintered bone, extinguishing the dark animating nexus across Hades and collapsing his Death Knights into dust.
