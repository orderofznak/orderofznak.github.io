## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Gorthok the Thunder Boar** was a primal spirit in service of the god [[Talos]]. It preferred to take the form of a massive giant boar.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Circle of Thunder Boss)
* **Party Notes:** Primordial spirit of thunder and lightning in the form of an elephant-sized wild boar, summoned by the [[Anchorites of Talos]] [[Flenz]] and [[Narux]] atop the [[Circle of Thunder]]. At **Level 5–6**, after ambushing [[Yargath]] and ten orcs on a custom battlemap en route to the standing stones, [[The Order of Znak]] and their centaur ally [[Xanth]] clashed with Gorthok, Flenz, Narux, and waves of twig and constricting vine blights amidst booming thunder and *Lightning Bolts*. Despite Gorthok's thundering hooves and lightning-wreathed tusks, the combined might of Xanth's charges, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]'s silvered greataxe, [[Rhynok]]'s *Sharpshooter* bow, [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s martial arts, and [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s *Spirit Guardians* brought the Thunder Boar crashing down.
