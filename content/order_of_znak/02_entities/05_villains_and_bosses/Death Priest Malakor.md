# 💀 Death Priest Malakor

![[myagkiy_bridge_ch01_weeping_stone_crossing_ambush.jpg]]
*Figure: The Ambush at Weeping Stone Crossing — Myagkiy Znak wading through the shallows with glowing Soft Sign shield and flanged mace Bonecounter as Death Priest Malakor recoils by the weeping menhir.*

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** Senior death priest and field commander of surviving Myrkulite zealots operating along the Delimbiyr Vale and the western approaches to the Greypeak Mountains.
* **The Weeping Stone Ambush (Autumn 1500 DR):**
  * Concealed behind an ancient, weeping menhir at the ford of Weeping Stone Crossing, Malakor orchestrated a lethal trap targeting [[Myagkiy Znak]] with four submerged crypt ghouls and two archer wights concealed in pine branches.
  * Myagkiy detected the trap in advance, cast *Guardian of Faith*, and summoned a Large Celestial Avenger angel, reversing the ambush completely.
  * In Round 1, Myagkiy cast *Sacred Flame* on Malakor, scorching his iron skull mask, shattering his bone amulets, and setting his robes ablaze for 22 radiant damage.
  * Surrounded by divine radiance, Malakor’s wights and ghouls were obliterated in the river shallows.
* **The Interrogation & Cartographic Clue:**
  * Disarmed and pinned to the menhir, Malakor stammered under interrogation, revealing that **[[Death Knight Vaelen]]** had unsealed the black glass pyramid of **Aul-Khem** in southern Anauroch to feed captured caravans into a blood chrysalis.
  * Surrendered a bloodstained parchment map of the Greypeak passes, confirming the trail east into the desert.
