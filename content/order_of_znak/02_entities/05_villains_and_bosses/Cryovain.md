* **Species / Heritage:** White dragon
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Primary Region / Home:** Icespire Hold, Sword Mountains
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Cryovain** was a white dragon enslaved by frost giants sometime after the [[War of the Silver Marches]].

Cryovain was chained to the "[[Krigvind]]", the jarl's colossal warship. During battles, Storvald ordered Cryovain to provide air support.

After escaping the frost giants, Cryovain flew south to avoid other more powerful dragons. He killed the orc war chief [[Kra]] and drove his tribe of orcs out of [[Icespire Hold]] on [[Icespire Peak]], taking the fortress as his new lair. Cryovain terrorized the surrounding areas&mdash;including the settlements of [[Phandalin]], [[Gnomengarde]], [[Leilon]], and [[Neverwinter]]&mdash;and the displaced orcs wreaked havoc in the area as well. Cryovain also drove a manticore from its mountaintop nest, which then attacked [[Adabra Gwynn|Adabra Gwynn's]] home on [[Umbrage Hill]].

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Act 1 Final Boss — Icespire Hold Rooftop)
* **Party Notes:**
  * **Frontier Reign of Terror & Death of [[Turkos Doomheart]]:** Cryovain's arrival atop [[Icespire Hold]] drove the manticore to attack [[Adabra Gwynn]] at [[Umbrage Hill]], displaced the mountain orc tribe into [[Butterskull Ranch]], the [[Shrine of Savras]], and the alliance with the [[Anchorites of Talos]], and kept the region in perpetual dread—strafing [[Phandalin]] early in the campaign (killing **[[Turkos Doomheart]]**), clashing briefly with [[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]] and the party at [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]], and prowling the [[Loggers' Camp]], [[Axeholm]], the **Starmetal Hills**, Conyberry, [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], [[Gnomengarde]], and the [[Temple of Abbathor|Dwarven Excavation]].
  * **The Rooftop Showdown at Icespire Hold:** After fighting a hobgoblin warband in the foothills, cutting down the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]] inside the gatehouse and fortress halls, and clearing the stirge-infested keep with their newly silvered weapons, [[The Order of Znak]] ascended to the icy roof of [[Icespire Hold]], where Cryovain laired alongside **4 Ice Mephits**. While [[Myagkiy Znak]] maintained *Bless* on the party and used his *Spiritual Weapon* to swat down the 4 Ice Mephits, the champions dodged blasts of freezing breath. [[Tvyordiy Znak]] delivered a series of clutch **Stunning Strikes** that locked Cryovain in place on the roof, allowing [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] to unleash devastating rages with Lady Tanamere Alagondar's ***Dragon Slayer* longsword** while [[Rhynok]] hammered the beast with pinpoint accuracy from his *+1 longbow*. Slaying Cryovain spread the heroes' renown to [[Dagult Neverember]] in [[Neverwinter]] and the Lords of [[Waterdeep]].
