# 🐉 Hrimgarra (The Pale Widow)

![[krageer-saga-dragon.jpg]]
*The Haul-Down — Krageer astride Hrimgarra's neck in Kuvimeno, the Glass Hall, as Cooragh strikes*

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Adult White Dragon
* **Aliases & Titles:** The Pale Widow, The Dragon of the Glass Hall
* **Primary Lair:** **Kuvimeno** (The Glass Hall), an ice cavern at the head of a blue glacial gorge in the northern heights of the Spine of the World.
* **Family & Lineage:** Elder clutch-sibling of **[[Cryovain]]**, the young white dragon who terrorized the Sword Mountains and was slain atop [[Icespire Hold]] by [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Distinguishing Marks:** Vast white scales the color of bone in a snowfield, dull crystalline blue eyes, and an ancient, jagged scar running the length of her left flank.

## 📜 Canonical History & Tragic Backstory

### ⛓️ The Flight from Jarl Storvald's Chains
Decades prior to the Phandalin frontier crisis, Hrimgarra was hunting three valleys away when the colossal ice-longships of frost giant Jarl Storvald descended upon her clutch's nursery. Overcome with primal terror, she fled into the freezing clouds rather than fight, abandoning her youngest clutch-sibling, Cryovain, to the giants' iron chains.

For years she flew south across thousands of miles of frozen peaks, haunted by the psychic agony and distant screams of her enslaved kin. When Cryovain eventually broke his chains and seized [[Icespire Hold]], Hrimgarra was too paralyzed by guilt and shame to answer his roars across the Sword Coast. 

### ❄️ The Search for Cryovain's Slayers
When travelers' songs reached the frost giant steads carrying tales of Cryovain's death at the hands of lowland heroes, Hrimgarra learned of the Goliath Barbarian wearing a red-eyed [[Axeholm|Dread Helm]], wielding Lady Tanamere Alagondar's ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, and bearing the distinct tribal sleeve tattoo of Clan Ogolakanu. 

Seeking an end to decades of self-loathing, Hrimgarra flew to the northern peaks of the Spine of the World. Her presence generated an unnatural, creeping frost-fog that chilled the high pastures, inadvertently driving the rival goliath clans off their grazing lands and forcing them into conflict in the warm valleys below. Her goal was neither conquest nor cruelty: she came solely to face the one who had ended her brother—either to answer his death in blood, or to pay for her cowardice with her own life.

## ⚔️ The Duel in the Glass Hall (Late Spring, Year 4)

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]], Krageer marched into Kuvimeno alongside his bride-to-be, [[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]]:

* **The Pact of Honor:** Hrimgarra agreed to an honorable ground duel under Kavaki's sky (*"On the ice, on the ground, one to one. I will not take the sky. Let no one say I was not fair"*). Cooragh stepped forward to make it "two for one."
* **The Broken Vow & The Haul-Down:** After suffering ferocious damage from Krageer's *Dragon Slayer* blade and Cooragh's greatsword, the dragon panicked under heavy assault and took flight forty feet into the cavern dome. Enraged by her betrayal and seeing Cooragh dropped to 1 HP by a stooping dive, Krageer triggered **Action Surge** to leap onto Hrimgarra's neck, executing an unprecedented **"haul-down" feat of strength** (contested Athletics at disadvantage: `19` vs `18`) to drag the Huge wyrm crashing to the ice.
* **Keeping the Vow:** Once grounded, Hrimgarra honored the terms. Despite having legendary actions available, she refused to flee or take flight again. 
* **The Death by Request:** Brought down to 13 hit points, the dying wyrm laid her head upon the ice and asked Krageer for an honorable end:
  > *"I broke my word. I took the sky... I am tired of breaking my word. You killed my youngest. I came to answer it. I have killed shepherds in your mountains, and my fog drove your clans onto each other's grazing until they bled. That is not an answer. That is more shame... Then let me have an end that is not a fleeing. Ask nothing of the hoard. Take it. Make it right."*
* **The Execution:** Krageer struck once, clean, granting her the honorable death she sought.

## 💎 The Peace-Hoard & Legacy

* **The Pamolivu:** In accordance with Hrimgarra's dying plea, neither Krageer nor Cooragh claimed a single coin of her treasure. The entire hoard was converted into the **Pamolivu** (Peace-Hoard), held in common by the newly united Four Fires (*Mevolukanipa*), paying blood-prices for lost herds, rebuilding toppled cairns, and cementing peace among the clans.
* **The Coin-Witness & The Lament:** The oldest Ogolakanu skywatchers set coins in the ice surrounding her body, honoring the mountain custom that an expiated debt deserves a witness. A goliath lamentor sang over her corpse—the first time in the history of the Spine of the World that a dragon was mourned with goliath funerary honors.
* **The Pale Widow's Eye:** Krageer permanently commemorated her on his right shoulder with a tattoo of a **white dragon's eye ringed in river stones, with a small pie crust curled at its edge**.
