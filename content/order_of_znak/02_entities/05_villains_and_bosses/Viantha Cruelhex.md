# 👤 Viantha Cruelhex

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Sea hag / Warlock
* **Role & Occupation:** Cultist operative & fugitive
* **Alignment:** Chaotic evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Kryptgarden Forest, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Viantha Cruelhex** was a sinister hag warlock operating within the shadowed canopy of [[Kryptgarden Forest]]. Her presence and insidious magical activities drew the ire of the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** ("Old Gnawbone"), who ruled the region from [[Deeping Cave]].

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The Hunt in Kryptgarden Woods (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** When [[The Order of Znak]] entered Claugiyliamatar's lair seeking safe passage and intelligence, the green dragon tasked the champions with tracking down and eliminating Viantha. 
* **Swift Execution:** The party hunted Viantha through the dense undergrowth of Kryptgarden Forest and easily dispatched her in combat. 
* **The Corpse & Aftermath:** The Order hauled Viantha's corpse back to Deeping Cave to present as proof to Claugiyliamatar. However, delivering her body unwittingly triggered the dark ritual wherein [[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]]'s dracolich spirit possessed the green dragon.
