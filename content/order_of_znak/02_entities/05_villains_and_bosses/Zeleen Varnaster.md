# 👤 Zeleen Varnaster

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human wererat
* **Role & Occupation:** Bandit
* **Alignment:** Lawful evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Zeleen Varnaster** was the leader of the wererat bandit group known as the [[Whiskered Gang]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Personality
Zeleen was ruthless and deceitful. She was more than willing to fight and to kill for what she wanted, and to lie to anyone she couldn't fight or kill. they regarded her as easily replaceable. Zeleen soon led her gang to the [[Mountain's Toe]] gold mine, where they killed most of the miners, claimed the mine as their new home, and Zeleen began attempting to pass herself off as the mine overseer to any visitors who came snooping around. This put her on a collision course with [[Don-Jon Raskin]], the new overseer being sent by the mine's owners in [[Neverwinter]] around the same time.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Routed & Escaped ([[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] Boss)
* **Party Notes:** Leader of the "Whiskered Gang" wererat clan that seized [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]. When [[The Order of Znak]] arrived escorting new overseer [[DonJon Raskin]], Zeleen attempted to pass herself off as the legitimate mine supervisor, but the champions were not fooled for a moment. In the ensuing battle, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] and [[Tvyordiy Znak|Tvyordiy]] discovered to their dismay that their unsilvered, mundane weapons could not wound the immune lycanthropes. [[Rhynok]] (firing his enchanted *+1 longbow*) and [[Myagkiy Znak|Myagkiy]] (channeling *Spirit Guardians*, *Spiritual Weapon*, and *Sacred Flame*) carried the entire fight—routing the Whiskered Gang and running off the surviving wererats as Zeleen escaped from the mine, and inspiring Krageer, Tvyordiy, and Rhynok to silver their melee weapons in [[Phandalin]] afterward.
