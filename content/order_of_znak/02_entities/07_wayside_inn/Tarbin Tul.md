* **Primary Region / Home:** High Road & Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Tarbin Tul** was a wandering bard known across the taverns and coaching inns along the [[High Road]] for his vast repertoire of ballads, frontier gossip, and local tunes. In *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Tarbin was present at the [[Wayside Inn]] when it was assaulted by the undead forces of [[Ularan Mortus]]. 

Later, while traveling along the coast during *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, Tarbin was abducted by the cultists of Talos aboard *The Relentless* under [[Fheralai Stormsworn]]. Bound below decks, his captive presence unwittingly served as the focal point anchoring the ghost ship's spectral soul to the mortal shore.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Rescued Twice by The Order of Znak
* **The Ghost Ship Rescue (September 18, 2020):** Following the defeat of High Priestess Fheralai and First Mate [[Gol Badwind]], [[The Order of Znak]] found Tarbin Tul bound and guarded in the ship's holding area. 
* **Severing the Spectral Anchor:** The champions unchained the battered bard and communed with the ship's lingering Death Knight soul. As the Order escorted Tarbin off the ship onto the rocky shoals, the dark curse anchoring the vessel was broken, allowing *The Relentless* to slip off the rocks and sail silently out into the fog of the Sea of Swords.
* **Return to Leilon:** Tarbin accompanied the Order back to Leilon, singing ballads in praise of the four champions at [[The Umber Hulk's Shell Inn]].
