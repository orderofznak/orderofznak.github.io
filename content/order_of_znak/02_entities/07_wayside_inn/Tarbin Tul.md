## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Tarbin Tul** was a wandering [[bard]] on the [[Sword Coast North]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
Tarbin Tul performed along the [[High Road]] and always looked for new tunes to add to his enormous collection. **Circa** [[1491 DR]], while staying at the [[Wayside Inn]] one night, he accidentally witnessed an [[undead]] attack. The undead had been created by the cult of [[Myrkul]] led by [[Ularan Mortus]]. A group of [[adventurer]]s was able to defend the inn against the attack. Eventually, the same group of adventurers came to the Tarbin's rescue and freed him from his captivity.
