# 🕵️ Lars Drumbuckle

## 🛡️ Overview & Persona
* **Allegiance:** The Lords of Waterdeep (Espionage & Mercantile Intelligence)
* **Role:** Covert field handler and commercial operative.
* **Associate:** Partnered with fellow operative **[[Orson Longfellow]]**.

---

## 📜 Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **The High Road Rendezvous (Act 2 — *Storm Lord's Wrath*):**
  - Alongside [[Orson Longfellow]], Lars Drumbuckle was stationed at **[[The Wayside Inn]]** to observe merchant movements and coordinate covert operations.
  - Following the party's victory over the undead siege on June 21, 2020, Lars and Orson met privately with the four champions at a quiet corner table.
  - Lars debriefed the party, explaining that uncovering Neverwinter's true intentions behind the southern mining industry and the Leilon reconstruction while securing trade pacts would build a powerful alliance with the **Lords of Waterdeep**. This covert commission served as the primary narrative catalyst propelling the Order into [[Leilon]].
