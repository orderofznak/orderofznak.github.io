# 🥧 Cooragh Struckt

## 🛡️ Overview & Personality
* **Identity:** Half-orc head cook of the historic Wayside Inn, situated strategically along the High Road between Waterdeep and Neverwinter near Leilon.
* **Friendship with Martisha:** Longtime close friend and confidante of innkeeper **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]**. While Martisha runs the books, room lodgings, and trade contracts, Cooragh rules the kitchen and pantry with fiery independence.

## 🍲 Culinary Legend: Alarmingly Spicy Stews & Pork Pies
* **Fierce Flavors:** Cooragh is celebrated across the Sword Coast for her extraordinarily tasty, and *alarmingly well-spiced*, stews and savory meat pies.
* **The Special Pork Pie:** Whenever grateful travelers or adventurers render service to the inn, Cooragh thanks them by slicing off a generous portion of her signature **special pork pie**. The pie is intensely spicy—blistering hot to most unseasoned tongues—but considered an addictive delicacy by anyone with the iron constitution to stomach the heat.

---

## 🗡️ Physical Appearance & Luskan Neck Tattoos
* **Defiant Tattoos:** Etched onto her neck beneath each ear are distinct tattoos of twin **curved daggers**.
* **Youthful Folly:** When asked about them, Cooragh chuckles openly and admits she got the ink simply because she knew it would infuriate her human mother from Luskan. She brushes it off with a grin as a "foolish youthful folly."

---

## 💘 Romance with Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu
* **An Iron Stomach for Love:** During Act 2 (*Storm Lord's Wrath*), Goliath barbarian **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** encountered Cooragh's spicy pies and was completely captivated. 
* While most soft-skinned travelers choked on the blistering spice, Krageer reveled in the heat, declaring Cooragh's pork pies the greatest food in the realm.
* An ongoing comedic romance developed between the towering barbarian and the half-orc chef, making the Wayside Inn a regular stop and favored resting sanctuary for [[The Order of Znak]].

![[Krageer-and-cooragh.jpg]]
*Early Romance: Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu and Cooragh Struckt at the Wayside Inn (Act 2)*

* **The Bridge Campaign Era (10+ Years Later):** Over a decade after the conclusion of *Divine Contention*, as the original adventuring days of The Order of Znak were drawing to a close and their adopted wards prepared to carry the torch into Ravenloft, the bond between Krageer and Cooragh had deepened into effortless, weathered familiarity. In the warmth of the hearth and kitchen, with pie crust rolled and flour on her fingers, Cooragh affectionately boops the stoic Goliath's nose—a touching testament to a love forged over ten years of peace, shared laughter, and alarmingly spicy meat pies.
