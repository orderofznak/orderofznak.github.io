# 🍺 Wyndle

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A beloved, seasoned regular patron and wilderness guide at the **Iron Flagon Inn**, located along the road northeast toward Mount Hotenow.
* **Guide to the Volcano:** Possesses deep, intimate knowledge of the rugged terrain, subterranean fissures, and volcanic corridors of the volcanic mountain **Mount Hotenow**.

---

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Aiding the Order (July 6, 2022):** After [[The Order of Znak]] slew Night Hag [[Balheelsa]] and rescued young [[Yasmina]] from Neverwinter Wood, they regrouped safely at the Iron Flagon Inn to ask around about the whereabouts of Fire Giant Queen **[[Kevlaan the Merciless]]**.
* **Directions to Kevlaan's Lair:** Wyndle recognized the danger immediately and stepped forward to offer crucial navigational aid—providing precise directions through the treacherous lava tubes and engineering hazards leading into the belly of Mount Hotenow.
* **The Hotenow Expedition:** Guided by Wyndle's directions, the party safely located the volcanic entrance and penetrated Kevlaan's caldera, setting the stage for Krageer's legendary "Double-Enlarge" wrestling victory!
