# 🕵️ Orson Longfellow

## 🛡️ Overview & Persona
* **Allegiance:** The Lords of Waterdeep (Espionage & Southern Trade Division)
* **Role:** Confidential handler, surveyor, and covert intelligence contact along the High Road.
* **Associate:** Partnered with fellow operative **[[Lars Drumbuckle]]**.

---

## 📜 Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **The High Road Rendezvous (Act 2 — *Storm Lord's Wrath*):**
  - Following their heroic defense of Phandalin and the slaying of Cryovain, [[The Order of Znak]] received confidential instructions from the Lords of Waterdeep to rendezvous at **[[The Wayside Inn]]** on June 13 & 21, 2020.
  - Orson Longfellow, alongside [[Lars Drumbuckle]], operated under cover in the taproom. Following the repelling of the undead siege, they met privately with the Order to debrief.
  - Orson explained that the Lords of Waterdeep were deeply concerned about Neverwinter's aggressive southern expansion into [[Leilon]] to monopolize the High Road mining trade. Orson and Lars officially engaged the Order to travel to Leilon, establish themselves as town champions, monitor Neverwintan motives, and broker mercantile treaties favorable to Waterdeep.
