# 🔨 Dayne Beltran

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A devout, master-smith Cleric of the Forge Domain dedicated to crafting divine armor and consecrating sacred armaments.
* **Alliance with the Order:** Encountered along the Sword Coast and became an enduring friend and patron of [[The Order of Znak]].

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests
* **The Stolen Masterwork Armor:** Commissioned [[The Order of Znak]] to track down and recover his prized ceremonial plate armor, which had been stolen by malevolent forces during the events of *"Keeping the Faith"*.
* **The Night Hag Liberation:** The quest led the Order into conflict with a predatory Night Hag; upon slaying the hag, the party reclaimed the armor and liberated the imprisoned young ward **[[Yasmina]]**.
* **Reward — Dayne's Plate Armor:** In profound gratitude, Dayne gifted his consecrated **+2 Plate Armor** to battle cleric **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, permanently boosting Myagkiy's defenses to an impenetrable **24 AC**.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **DM Campaign Design:** Created and introduced by Gary as the principal patron NPC of his homebrew adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*, seamlessly interwoven between *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* and *Divine Contention*.
* **Defensive Benchmark:** Gary intentionally tailored Dayne's +2 Plate Armor as a reward to help Peter's Life Domain cleric withstand lethal frontline punishment in late-tier encounters.
