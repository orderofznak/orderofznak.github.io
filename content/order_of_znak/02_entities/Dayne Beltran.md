# 🔨 Dayne Beltran

## Overview & Role
* **Role:** Forge Domain Cleric NPC featured in Gary's 2nd DM homebrew adventure, *"Keeping the Faith"*.
* **Quest:** Commissioned [[The Order of Znak]] to recover his stolen armor.
* **Reward / Gear Artifact:** **Dayne's Plate Armor** (+2 Plate Armor), which became the signature armor worn by **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** (raising his AC to 24).
* **Night Hag Encounter:** During the quest, the Order defeated a Night Hag, liberating young **[[Yasmina]]**.
