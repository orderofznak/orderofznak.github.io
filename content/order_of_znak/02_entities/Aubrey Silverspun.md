# 🪄 Aubrey Silverspun

## 🛡️ Overview & Shopkeeper Persona
* **The Peculiarities Shop:** The eccentric, fastidious proprietor of **Aubrey's Peculiarities**, a quirky establishment in reconstructed [[Leilon]] packed with dusty curio shelves, oddities, antiquities, and minor magic trinkets.
* **Mannerisms:** Flamboyant, warm, and deeply eccentric. He speaks in elaborate prose and loves bartering strange artifacts, regaling visitors with obscure historical anecdotes of the Sword Coast.

---

## 👻 The Spectral Mystery: Visible Only to the Champions
Throughout *Storm Lord's Wrath*, *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*, and *Divine Contention*, [[The Order of Znak]] regularly visited Aubrey to purchase spell components, identify curious loot, and banter with the odd merchant. 

Yet unknown to the party, **Aubrey Silverspun was a ghost the entire time**:
* Ordinary Leilon settlers never saw Aubrey; to them, the building was simply an abandoned stone structure or a quiet, shuttered curio store where odd trinkets mysteriously traded hands.
* Due to an ancient spiritual anchor, **only true champions bound to the destiny and defense of Leilon could perceive and converse with him**. The Order assumed he was merely a reclusive, strange living merchant.

---

## 🐉 The Claugiyliamatar Climax & The Swords of Leilon Portrait
During the climactic siege of Leilon in *Divine Contention*, the ancient green dragon **Claugiyliamatar** ("Old Gnawbone") assaulted the settlement in a tempest of corrosive breath and crushing devastation. In the raging battle, Aubrey's Peculiarities was struck directly and collapsed into burning rubble.

Following the defeat of the dragon and the preservation of the town:
* The Order rushed to the smoking ruins of the shop to search for their eccentric merchant friend, finding no living body or remains among the ashes.
* Sifting through the debris, the heroes uncovered a miraculously preserved, gilded oil painting dating back centuries: a portrait of the legendary **Swords of Leilon**, the mythic martial champions who defended the ancient settlement before its destruction centuries ago.
* Gazing at the painted heroes, the champions were stunned to recognize **Aubrey Silverspun standing proudly among the ancient Swords of Leilon**, smiling back at them from the canvas!
* Aubrey had never been a living shopkeeper in the present era—he was an ancient heroic spirit who lingered across the ages, keeping vigil over Leilon until worthy successors arose to inherit the defense of the coast.

---

## 👥 Key Relationships & Dynamics
* **[[The Order of Znak]]:** His favored patrons. He subtly tested, guided, and equipped the champions, recognizing them as the true inheritors of the Swords of Leilon.
* **[[Leopold]]:** The Order's majordomo, who occasionally sent runners to the shop with lists of household curios, mystified by the party's descriptions of its flamboyant owner.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Milestone:** The post-battle revelation of Aubrey's portrait was one of the most unforgettable, heartwarming narrative moments in Peter and Gary's campaign, seamlessly bridging the published module lore with deep emotional resonance.
