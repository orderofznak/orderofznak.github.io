# 🔮 Yasmina

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Origin & Rescue:** Rescued as a young girl from the clutches of a sinister Night Hag in the Sword Coast wilderness by [[The Order of Znak]].
* **Eerie Hag Lineage:** Despite her liberation, prolonged confinement under the hag left her with an indelible witch-like aura, granting unusual fey-fiendish resilience and intuitive spellcasting.
* **Ward of the Order:** Raised under the watchful eye and spiritual shelter of [[Myagkiy Znak]] and the Leilon headquarters.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (*The Acolytes of Znak*)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Cleric / Fiend Warlock
* **Heritage Traits:** Custom hag-touched lineage traits blending divine protection with dark occult patron boons.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign Introduction:** Introduced by Gary during his DM adventure *"Keeping the Faith"*, serving as the emotional core of the Night Hag dungeon crawl alongside the recovery of [[Dayne Beltran]]'s +2 Plate Armor.
* **Bridge to Barovia:** Designed as a living narrative bridge between the two campaigns—growing from a vulnerable rescued orphan in *The Order of Znak* into a gothic-themed hero ready for Ravenloft in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
