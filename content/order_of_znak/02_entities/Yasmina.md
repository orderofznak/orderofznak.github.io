# 🔮 Yasmina

## Profile & Background
* **Origin:** Rescued as a young girl by [[The Order of Znak]] after they defeated a Night Hag during [[Gary Forsyth]]'s *"Keeping the Faith"* DM adventure.
* **Lineage & Dark Aura:** Despite being liberated, she carries a dark, witch/hag-influenced aura and homebrew lineage mechanics.
* **Adult Character Build (*The Acolytes of Znak*):** Appears as a Level 3 Cleric / Fiend Warlock multiclass in the upcoming D&D 2024 *Curse of Strahd* campaign.
