# 🦎 Grixxugroaz

## 🛡️ Overview & Sovereign Authority
* **Identity:** Chieftain and supreme ruler of the lizardfolk tribe inhabiting the desolate wetlands of the **Mere of Dead Men**.
* **Supreme Leadership:** Governed the tribe with absolute executive authority. Grixxugroaz dictated territorial borders, established tribal policy, judged disputes, and decided all military and diplomatic actions.

---

## ⚖️ Cold Logic & Tribal Survival
* **Pragmatic Rule:** Governed according to ancient lizardfolk principles of cold, unyielding survival logic. In a swamp teeming with black dragons, will-o'-wisps, and undead horrors, sentimentality was a fatal weakness; every decision was measured strictly by whether it enhanced tribal survival and resource strength.
* **The Missing Patrol & Encampment Clash:** When the Order tracked missing Leilon soldiers to the tribe's snail-wagon encampment in the Mere of Dead Men, the heroes infiltrated under Rhynok's *Pass Without Trace*. During the fierce initial battle, champion [[Hissain]] and the swamp render [[Slosh]] were slain alongside battle-matron **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]**, and Private Vesper and the captive soldiers were freed. Grixxugroaz assessed the outcome not with blind vengeance, but with analytical respect for [[The Order of Znak]]'s lethal martial efficiency.

---

## 🤝 The Fateful Alliance & The Exchange Treaty
* **The Rot Troll Shield Wall:** When a horrific horde of plague-ridden rot trolls ambushed the swamp shortly thereafter, the Order stood side-by-side with Grixxugroaz's surviving warriors in a desperate joint defense.
* **Ratifying the Truce:** Impressed by the heroes' ferocity and honor—particularly Goliath barbarian [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]'s decision to tattoo a crowned bird and the name Qolzaz across his ribcage (celebrated in the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***)—Grixxugroaz personally decreed an enduring peace between the Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk and the settlers of [[Leilon]].
* **The Exchange Envoys:** To cement the alliance and study the tactics of the soft-skinned champions, Grixxugroaz selected two promising tribal youths to travel north to Leilon as official exchange emissaries:
  * **[[Graxxoamo]] ("Graxx"):** The tribe's athletic competitor and hunter (apprenticed under [[Tvyordiy Znak]] and [[Rhynok]]).
  * **[[Shriss]]:** The tribe's intellectual sage and herbalist (apprenticed under [[Myagkiy Znak]]).
