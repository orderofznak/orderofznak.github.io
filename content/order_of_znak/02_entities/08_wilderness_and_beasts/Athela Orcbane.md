# 🏹 Athela Orcbane

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Fierce Uthgardt human barbarian-ranger of the **Tree Ghost** tribe, daughter of chieftain Boorvald Orcbane of the Grandfather Tree.
* **Weapon of Legend:** Wields an ancient, finely carved elven *Oathbow* recovered from the ruins of ancient Eaerlann.
* **First Meeting with Tvyordiy (1500 DR):** Intercepted [[Tvyordiy Znak]] in the outer fern groves of the Grandfather Tree alongside [[Elder Kaya]]. Initially cautious of his strange blend of eagle and wild boar scents, she lowered her bow upon witnessing his deep respect for the forest spirits and the sacred grove (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 2]]).
* **The Visit to Leilon (1502 DR):** Following the pacification of the northern roads, Athela traveled west along the High Road to the bustling harbor town of [[Leilon]]. Outside [[Puck Caribdas|Puck's Apothecary Shop]], she crossed paths with Tvyordiy upon his return from the Plane of Air, proudly sporting a new leather bandolier laden with red-glass healing potions bought from the flustered gnome, offering Tvyordiy a warrior's salute of mutual respect.

## 📊 Combat & Mechanics
* **Class & Role:** Level 9 Hunter Ranger.
* **Weapons:** Elven *Oathbow*, hunting daggers.
* **Features:** Colossus Slayer, Archery Fighting Style, Land's Stride.
