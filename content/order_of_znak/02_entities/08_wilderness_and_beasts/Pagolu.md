# 🪨 Pagolu "the Ram-Breaker"

![[krageer-saga-parley.jpg]]
*Krageer and Pagolu the Ram-Breaker on the old flat stone above the Mamaluko camp*

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Massive Goliath warrior, chieftain of Clan Mamaluko, and eventual supreme war-leader of the Four Fires (*Mevolukanipa*).
* **Origin of Moniker:** Earned the title "Ram-Breaker" by physically shoving a fully grown, rutting mountain ram clean off a rocky ridge during a ferocious game of King of the Hill (elders claimed Kavaki the Ram-Lord found the feat deeply amusing).
* **Personality & Philosophy:** Boisterous, martial, and intensely honorable. Rejects delicate political maneuvering in favor of raw physical strength and battlefield integrity: *"If the Ogolakanu wished to speak for the clans, Krageer would have to prove he could carry them."*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **The Arm-Wrestle Contest on the Flat Stone:** Refusing to hear diplomatic speeches, Pagolu challenged Krageer to a best-of-three arm-wrestling contest on the ancient flat stone above the Mamaluko camp:
  * *Terms:* No tricks, no tools, no weapons (*"Rage is not a trick; it is a goliath"*).
  * *Round 1:* Krageer won (`24` vs. `19`).
  * *Round 2:* Pagolu hammered down a Natural 20 (`29` vs. `22`), slamming Krageer's knuckles into the boulder hard enough to chip the granite.
  * *Round 3:* Dead tie at `25`.
  * *The Tie-Break:* Krageer tapped into his Bear Totem Rage for Strength advantage, rolling an effective `27` against Pagolu's `23` and slowly forcing the Ram-Breaker's forearm flat to the stone.
  * *The Handshake:* Pagolu burst into cracked, delighted laughter (*"You are the first to ever make me work"* / *"You are second"*), pledging his clan to the Four Fires before the next sunrise.
* **War-Leader of the Four Fires:** Appointed military commander of the united alliance by Chieftain [[Menuka]] following the War of Milagove. Trained and drilled forty goliath archers to cover the retreat during the hunt for [[Hrimgarra]].
* **The Wedding & The Pork Pie Blood-Oath:** Attended the three-day wedding feast of Krageer and [[Cooragh]]. After consuming enormous quantities of Cooragh's spicy tavern pork pies, Pagolu wept openly into his plate on the third night and swore a solemn blood-oath never to raise a weapon against her.
