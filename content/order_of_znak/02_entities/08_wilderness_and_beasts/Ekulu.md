# 🧶 Ekulu "Warm-Lap"

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Matriarchal elder and revered Tent-Mother of Clan Ogolakanu.
* **Role & Sacred Office:** Arbiter of mountain social customs, marriage inspections, elder counsel, and camp domestic order. Known for an earthy sense of humor, keen judgment of character, and zero patience for pretension.

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **The Marriage Inspection (*Kolipemu*):** Prior to the sacred wedding rite on the mountain shoulder, Ekulu performed the official ancestral examination of bride [[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]] to judge whether the half-orc outsider was fit for the daily burdens of mountain life. Ekulu pronounced her fully prepared:
  > *"She has been ready since the day she was born."*
* **The Three Questions:** Along with the tribal elders, Ekulu posed the three sacred questions to Cooragh (*What do you bring? What do you earn? What will you carry?*), roaring with laughter when Cooragh answered in one breath: *"A skillet. My name. Anything he can't."*
* **The "Half-Orc Bush":** When Cooragh asked if Kavaki kept a bush of gems for half-orcs on the peak (referencing the Goliath creation myth), Ekulu smiled warmly and replied: *"No. You will have to grow your own."*
* **Campfire Culinary Debates:** Spent the three-day wedding feast in lively, good-humored culinary arguments with Cooragh around the central fire, vigorously debating the correct ratio of lard to flour for mountain pastry dough.
