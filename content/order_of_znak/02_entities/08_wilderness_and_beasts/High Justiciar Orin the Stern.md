# ⚖️ High Justiciar Orin the Stern

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity & Office:** High Justiciar and Chief Magistrate of the **City of Judgment**, presiding beneath the towering **Crystal Spire** on the **Fugue Plane** under the direct authority of **[[Kelemvor]]**.
* **Appearance:** An austere, commanding figure clad in ceremonial alabaster-and-silver robes, whose piercing grey eyes reflect centuries of unclouded impartiality, flanked by winged Celestial Arbiters bearing balance-scales and greatswords of starlight.
* **The Trial of the Siphon (1503 DR):**
  * Interrogated [[Myagkiy Znak]] after the cleric severed the Astral soul-pipeline feeding into the Fugue Plane.
  * Deliberated upon Myagkiy's defensive actions, declaring that healing and defending the living against entropic predation is never a violation of the natural order: *"Undeath is the theft... You have defended the sheep from the wolves. The Judge takes no offense at the doctor who binds the wound."*
* **The Extraction from Hades & Repatriation (1505 DR):**
  * When Myagkiy cast *Word of Recall* to escape the causeway ambush at 1 HP, Orin oversaw his restorative healing upon the sanctuary dais.
  * Channeling Kelemvor's divine authority, Orin answered Myagkiy's *Divine Intervention* by opening the silver planar portals that permitted the living Host of the Delivered to cross into Hades.
  * Following the Avatar's destruction, Orin personally opened the gates of the City of Judgment, welcoming the fallen desert riders and mountaineers to their eternal reward.
