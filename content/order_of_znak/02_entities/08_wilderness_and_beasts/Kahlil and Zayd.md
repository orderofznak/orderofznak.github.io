# 🐪 Kahlil and Zayd

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** Brothers and elite young dune-scouts of **Clan Bait Mahra**, sons of the elder matriarch Fatima whose sight was restored by [[Myagkiy Znak]].
* **Role in the Odyssey (1501–1505 DR):**
  * Assigned by **[[Sheikh Tariq ibn-Rashid]]** to guide Myagkiy across the treacherous salt flats and shifting dunes of Anauroch to the sunken black glass pyramid of **Aul-Khem**.
  * Armed with long composite bows, light scimitars, and mounted on swift desert dromedaries, they provided vital survival knowledge, tracked water cisterns, and secured the perimeter during the assault on Aul-Khem.
  * Wept with joy and embraced their three rescued kin (Marwan, Tariq's nephew, and their cousin) following Myagkiy's miraculous 42-second triple *Revivify*.
* **The Desert Crusade & The Causeway of Hades:**
  * Rode at the vanguard of the 50-rider Bedine cohort throughout the 4-month winter campaign, helping Myagkiy locate and purge hidden Myrkulite cells detailed in Vaelen's *Obsidian Ledger*.
  * Answered the divine summons of the *Host of the Delivered*, crossing the planar veil with fifty Bedine dune-riders to fight on the causeway outside the Sunken Bone Citadel in Hades.
  * Raised their lances in victory beside Myagkiy upon the death god's destruction, and later served as the honorary escort riding with Myagkiy to the foothills of the Greypeak Mountains on his journey home to Leilon.
