# 🦊 Kenuvi "Slow-Fox"

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Cunning male Goliath chieftain and master tracker of Clan Ivonakega—the oldest and fiercest traditional rivals of Clan Ogolakanu.
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Patient, perceptive, quiet, and deeply suspicious. Prefers prolonged silence and observation over hasty action, earning the title "Slow-Fox" by wearing down opponents through relentless endurance and watchfulness.

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **The Drink-and-Tell Parley:** When Krageer approached the Ivonakega camp to propose an alliance against [[Hrimgarra]], Kenuvi initiated the mountain ritual of *Drink-and-Tell*—passing a mead flagon back and forth, trading one swallow and one tale in turn.
  * Kenuvi spun three long, winding narratives to bait Krageer into revealing political motives.
  * Krageer took a swallow and said only: *"There was a dragon. I killed it."*
  * Krageer then sat in complete silence across the fire for **three full days** without adding a single word. Kenuvi watched him unblinkingly the entire time, hunting for a falsehood or hidden angle (Insight `11` vs. Krageer's Persuasion `15`).
  * On the fourth morning, finding no cracks in the stone, Kenuvi surrendered his ancient grievance and pledged Ivonakega hunters to [[Menuka|Menuka's]] line.
* **The Ivonakega Warband Schism:** When two renegade Ivonakega warbands broke the pact and triggered the raid at [[Milagove]] (Cinder Ford), Kenuvi initially proposed shunning (*volok-thea*). Following Krageer's decisive victory and peaceful offering of water to the survivors at the ford, Kenuvi reasserted complete control over his clan, binding all Ivonakega to the Four Fires.
* **Tracking the Pale Widow:** Kenuvi's veteran trackers discovered the frozen, dead-white trail left by Hrimgarra's creeping frost-fog, successfully locating the entrance to the Glass Hall at Kuvimeno.
