---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Lhammaruntosz**, known across the maritime realms as **"The Claws of the Coast,"** was an ancient and famous bronze dragon renowned for protecting merchant shipping, policing the coastal skies, and establishing sea patrols along the Sword Coast North. Her sanctum and lair, **[[The Bronze Shrine]]**, was carved into the sea cliffs north of [[Leilon]], guarded by her sworn mortal order, the **Scaly Eye**.

In the late 15th century DR, an insidious abyssal parasite—an **Alkilith** demon of Juiblex—infested the temple architecture, subtly feeding on the dragon's mind and afflicting the bronze matriarch with bouts of terrifying paranoia and creeping madness.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Cleansed, Allied & Patron
* **The Audience & Persuasion (November 5, 2020):** Guided by tavern rumors in Leilon, [[The Order of Znak]] approached her shrine. Confronted by the thrashing, paranoid dragon, [[Rhynok]] (18 Persuasion) and [[Myagkiy Znak]] (21 Persuasion) calmed her rage, assuring her they were not plunderers and asking to venerate Bahamut.
* **The Greater Restoration:** Recognizing the supernatural nature of her affliction with a 17 Religion check, Myagkiy cast ***Greater Restoration*** (consuming 100 gp of diamond dust). The sudden surge of cleansing magic severed the demonic curse, temporarily knocking Lhammaruntosz unconscious for 5 minutes.
* **The Alkilith Banishment:** While the dragon slumbered, the exposed Alkilith demon ambushed the party. Myagkiy promptly cast ***Banishment***; the demon critically failed its Charisma save with a `0`, expelling it back into the Abyss.
* **The Prophecy & The Scimitar of Speed:** Awakening with a clear mind, Lhammaruntosz thanked the Order and permitted them to receive prophetic visions at the Altar of Bahamut. Warning them of her ancient green rival **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** ("Old Gnawbone") in **Deeping Cave**, she rewarded the heroes from her hoard, where Rhynok selected the ***Scimitar of Speed***.
