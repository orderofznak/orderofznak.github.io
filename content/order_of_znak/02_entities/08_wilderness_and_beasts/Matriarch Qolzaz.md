# 🦎 Matriarch Qolzaz

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Tribal Leadership:** Respected battle-matriarch and shamanic spiritual leader of the Mere of Dead Men lizardfolk clans, governing the marshland tribe alongside sovereign chieftain **[[Grixxugroaz]]**.
* **Fierce Defender:** Fiercely guarded the sovereignty and nesting grounds of her swamp tribe against humanoid incursions along the High Road and Sword Coast during the *Missing Patrol* questline.

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Milestones
* **Clash & Aftermath:** Slain in honorable combat during the party's initial high-tension encounter with the tribe.
* **Undead Troll Defense & Mutual Honor:** When a horrific horde of invading undead trolls subsequently threatened the wetlands, [[The Order of Znak]] stood shoulder-to-shoulder with Chieftain [[Grixxugroaz]] and the surviving clan to repel the invasion.
* **The Peace Accord:** Convinced by the Order's valor and respect for Qolzaz, Chieftain Grixxugroaz ratified a historic peace treaty between the lizardfolk tribe and the town of [[Leilon]].
* **Krageer's Ribcage Tattoo:** Following Goliath warrior customs of honoring worthy rivals, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** had a crowned bird, a small rat, and the name **"Qolzaz"** tattooed onto his right ribcage in solemn tribute.
* **Bardic Ballad:** Her valor and the clan's alliance are immortalized in the campaign's epic poem, the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***.
