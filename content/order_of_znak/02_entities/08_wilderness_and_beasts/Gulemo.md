# 🎒 Gulemo "Longpack"

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Veteran Goliath fur-trader and long-range high-pass scout of Clan Mamaluko.
* **Origin of Moniker:** Earned the name "Longpack" for his ability to solo-trek massive ironwood frame rucksacks across hundreds of miles of blizzard-swept glacier passes, trading furs and smoked meats directly to the frost giant steadings and dwarven outposts of the Spine of the World.
* **Personality:** Weather-hardened, sharp-eared, observant, and fluent in Giant and dwarven trade jargons.

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **Trading with the Frost Giants:** At the end of the third winter, Gulemo descended from the upper glacial passes into the Four Fires' council camp at [[Pelukanemo]] with urgent, unwelcome intelligence.
* **The Dragon's Inquiry:** While trading furs at the gates of the frost giants' colossal halls, giant jarls informed Gulemo that an Adult White Dragon—[[Hrimgarra]], the "Pale Widow"—had been methodically asking about a specific Goliath Barbarian.
  * The giants knew of the slayer of [[Cryovain]] from lowland songs carried north by Waterdhavian and Neverwinter merchants.
  * They recognized Krageer from his glowing crimson-eyed [[Axeholm|Dread Helm]], the Lady Tanamere ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, and especially the intricate Ogolakanu tribal sleeve cascading down his right arm.
  * Because the Ogolakanu had traded with the frost giants at the Warm Hollow's gates for generations, the giants easily identified which winter valleys the clan inhabited.
* **Sounding the Alarm:** Gulemo's intelligence alerted the council that the spreading frost-fog was not an arbitrary storm, but the herald of a dragon seeking personal vengeance and closure. This gave Krageer, [[Linaku]], and [[Pagolu]] the critical window needed to prepare defenses and mobilize scouts before the confrontation at Kuvimeno.
