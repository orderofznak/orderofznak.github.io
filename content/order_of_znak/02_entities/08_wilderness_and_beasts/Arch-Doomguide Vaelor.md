# ⚖️ Arch-Doomguide Vaelor

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity & Office:** Arch-Doomguide of the church of **[[Kelemvor]]**, serving within the sacred precincts of the **Crystal Spire** in the City of Judgment upon the **Fugue Plane**.
* **Role in the Planar War:** Master of the planar armory and tactical intelligence for Kelemvorite incursions against undeath. 
* **Alliance with Myagkiy Znak (1503–1505 DR):**
  * Parleyed with [[Myagkiy Znak]] following the cleric's severance of the Astral soul-pipeline on the Ashen Plains.
  * Recognized Myagkiy's ordainment under the Life Domain and the sacred balance of the Soft Sign (**Ь**), validating that the preservation of mortal souls aligned with Kelemvor's mandates.
  * Bestowed upon Myagkiy the **Blessing of Soul-Shielding** and unlocked the planar armory to present the **Tuning Fork of Tarnished Silver & Ashen Lead**, attuned to the resonant frequency of the **Gray Waste of Hades**.
  * Following the final reckoning in Hades, coordinated the silver-robed Doomguides in gathering and honoring the departed spirits of the Host of the Delivered at the base of the Crystal Spire.
