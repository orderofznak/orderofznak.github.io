# 🌿 Elder Kaya (Tree Ghost Shaman)

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Ancient human elder and blind spirit-shaman of the **Tree Ghost Uthgardt** tribe dwelling beneath the sacred **[[Grandfather Tree]]** in the High Forest.
* **Appearance:** Withered, sun-browned skin lined with decades of ancestral wisdom; milky, sightless eyes turned upward to commune with forest spirits; graying braids woven with animal teeth and raven feathers. Wields a blackened wooden totem staff crowned with deer antlers and rawhide fetishes, and keeps a heavy soapstone bowl of smoldering wild white sage, cedar, and ghost-lichen.
* **Diagnosing the Twin Afflictions (1500 DR):** When [[Tvyordiy Znak]] arrived at the Grandfather Tree accompanied by dryads, Kaya smelled both eagle feathers and wild boar blood. Resting her withered palm against his chest over the longfire, she perceived both his contracted wereboar lycanthropy and the freezing necrotic frost of **[[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]]** (chronicled in [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Bridge Chronicle: Chapter 2]]).
* **Shamanic Philosophy of the Beast:** Taught Tvyordiy that the beast within was not a disease to be starved with fear, but a brother to be given a seat at the hearth: *"Be master of mind rather than mastered by mind."* Her teachings became the spiritual foundation that allowed Tvyordiy to master the hybrid wereboar form with Zen focus during his battle against the Avatar of Talos.
* **Gift of Bear-Grease:** Gifted Tvyordiy a bone vial of rendered winter cave-bear grease blended with ghost-lichen to insulate his joints against the freezing alpine winds of Mount Nimbus.

## 📊 Combat & Mechanics
* **Class & Role:** Level 11 Circle of the Land (Forest) Druid / Shaman.
* **Features:** Natural Recovery, Land's Stride, Nature's Ward; blindsight 30 ft (via spirit sense).
* **Key Spells:** *Commune with Nature, Divination, Lesser Restoration, Protection from Energy, Wind Wall.*
