## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Gustaf Stellern**, nicknamed **Falcon the Hunter**, was the owner of a [[Falcon's hunting lodge|hunting lodge]] in [[Neverwinter Wood]] during the late 15th century [[DR]]. He catered to nobles from [[Neverwinter]] who were looking for a break from city life.

### Personality
Falcon showed no fear and appeared confident and casual even in difficult situations. He preferred living on the periphery of civilization to overcrowded city life. Falcon was a wine connoisseur and treated other people fairly and patiently. However, he had a deep hatred for orcs. He detested them so much that he hung the heads of killed orcs as trophies in his hunting lodge.

Following the appearance of the white dragon [[Cryovain]] in the region, Falcon noticed an alliance of orcs from the south and a half-orc cult of [[Talos]] that gathered in the [[Woodland Manse]] not far from his hunting lodge. He hired some adventurers to make a preemptive strike against them. In retaliation, a band of twenty orcs attacked Falcon's hunting lodge. The [[anchorites of Talos]] also conjured [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar to aid the attack.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Allied Ranger & Quest Patron)
* **Party Notes:**
  * **Hospitality at Falcon's Hunting Lodge:** Known across Neverwinter Wood as **Falcon the Hunter**, Gustaf welcomed [[The Order of Znak]] to [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] after they rescued [[Tibor Wester]] at the [[Loggers' Camp]], fighting off [[Cryovain]] alongside the arriving heroes. Impressed by [[Rhynok]]'s Gruumsh blood-note ruse during the party's earlier foray to the [[Woodland Manse]], Falcon offered his **Boots of Elvenkind** as a reward for striking against the woodland orcs and [[Anchorites of Talos]].
  * **The Ransacked Lodge & Reunion in Phandalin:** Immediately after the party departed his lodge following their first failed expedition into the [[Woodland Manse]], [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar and a band of orcs ransacked [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]], forcing Falcon and his household to evacuate to [[Phandalin]]. After clearing [[Dragon Barrow]] and enlisting [[Xanth]], the party met Falcon in [[Phandalin]], learned of the lodge's destruction, and received his promised **Boots of Elvenkind** (claimed by [[Rhynok]])—before stopping over at the ruined lodge on their way into Neverwinter Wood to crush [[Gorthok]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]] and purge the [[Woodland Manse]].
