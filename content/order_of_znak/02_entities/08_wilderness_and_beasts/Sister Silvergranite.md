# 🗿 Sister Silvergranite

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Dwarf (Eidolon)
* **Role & Occupation:** Sacred Shrine Guardian / Priestess of Dumathoin
* **Form & Vessel:** A spectral dwarven spirit capable of inhabiting and animating a colossal **sacred moonstone statue** dedicated to Dumathoin.
* **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Dumathoin's Gulch]], Sword Mountains, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Divine Contention*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
Centuries ago, when the ancient dwarven mines of the Sword Mountains were abandoned, the deeply devout dwarven priestess **Sister Silvergranite** refused to leave. She insisted on remaining behind as a hermit to protect the buried dwarven tombs and guard the sacred shrines of **Dumathoin** (the dwarven god of mining, buried secrets, and the dead).

Upon her mortal death, Dumathoin blessed her unyielding loyalty, transforming her into an **Eidolon**—a sacred disembodied spirit bound to guard the holy ground. Whenever intruders or defilers threaten the canyon, Sister Silvergranite manifests through the moonstone statue, testing all who enter.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Allied via Persuasion)
* **Dumathoin's Gulch Rendezvous (*Divine Contention* Opening Quest):** When [[The Order of Znak]] traveled into Dumathoin's Gulch to rendezvous with the gnomish inventors ([[Fibblestib Pinebark]], [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]], [[Bindlemint Mincemower]]), Sister Silvergranite emerged to confront the travelers and assess their intentions.
* **Rhynok's Persuasion & Handover of the Grenade:** Sensing the sacred power of the shrine, [[Rhynok]] stepped forward to parley. Through calm and convincing diplomacy, Rhynok persuaded Sister Silvergranite that the Order had come to protect the gnomes and defend Leilon from the possessed dragon [[Claugiyliamatar]]. Satisfied of their honor, Sister Silvergranite released the volatile **Gnomengarde grenade** into the party's custody.
* **The Final Undead Defense:** Immediately following the peaceful negotiation, the Order stood ground to purge the canyon of the remaining undead threats—a sword wraith and a lethal **Boneclaw**—securing the holy site before their departure.
