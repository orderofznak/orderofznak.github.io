# 🏔️ Chieftain Menuka "Windfist"

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Goliath Chieftain of Clan Ogolakanu; maternal aunt of [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] (whom she raised following the loss of his mother).
* **Appearance & Demeanor:** Weathered, gray-haired goliath warrior with an unyielding jaw, piercing gray eyes, and one arm bound in a linen sling from two brutal winters of inter-clan skirmishes. Known for stoic pragmatism, zero tolerance for fluff, and dry mountain humor.
* **Ancestral Seat:** **Pelukanemo** (The Warm Hollow), a sheltered volcanic valley in the Spine of the World where geothermal vents keep the snow melted and goliaths winter comfortably in their skin.

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **The Return of "Kageleo":** Greeted Krageer upon his return to the Spine of the World using his ancestral Gol-Kaa birth name, **Kageleo**. Inspecting his extensive lowlands tattoos and the lightning bolt tally of the Fallen along his ribs, she dryly noted:
  > *"You have written all over yourself. The skywatchers will say you tried to rewrite your fate."*
  > *"I did," said Krageer. "It was a bad fate."*
* **Embracing Cooragh:** Readily welcomed half-orc cook [[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]], acknowledging her courage and practical mountain grit (*"She learns quickly"*).
* **The Task Captain Protocol:** Operating under ancient Ogolakanu unwritten law—where chieftains hold authority solely by proving daily fitness—Menuka assigned daily responsibilities each morning. She named Krageer **Task Captain of Walking Between Fires** (diplomatic parleys) and later **Task Captain of the Line** at the War of Milagove.
* **The Four Fires (*Mevolukanipa*):** When Krageer successfully parleyed with the rival Mamaluko, Thekupala, and Ivonakega clans, Menuka christened the united coalition **Mevolukanipa** (The Four Fires)—refusing to call it a tribe because *"tribes are for people who have stopped arguing."*
* **Offering the Chieftainship:** Following the victory at Cinder Ford, Menuka formally offered Krageer the leadership of the Ogolakanu. When Krageer declined (*"I am the Order's. I would leave you one day"*), she respected his honesty and appointed Pagolu war-leader and Linaku council speaker.
* **The Binding of Stones (*Kolipemu*):** Presided over the sacred marriage rite between Krageer and Cooragh atop the ancestral mountain shoulder, giving Krageer's full ceremonial name to the winds (*"Kageleo, called Stoneface, of the Ogolakanu"*) and formally bestowing upon Cooragh her goliath honorific name, **Thaluu** ("she who holds the fire").
