# 🏔️ Linaku "Flint-Ear"

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Identity & Heritage:** Female Goliath chieftain of Clan Thekupala; chief administrator and "Voice of the Council" for the Four Fires (*Mevolukanipa*).
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Stern, uncompromising, sharp-eyed, and intensely pragmatic. Skeptical of flowery lowland rhetoric and unearned authority.
* **Ancestral Role:** Keeper of agreements, pasturage schedules, and clan borders in the high country of the Spine of the World.

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Bridge Saga

Chronicled in [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|The Stone and the Skillet]]:

* **The Open-Hand Parley (*Mevalinuko*):** Held out the longest against joining the Ogolakanu alliance. When Krageer arrived at her fire unarmed with [[Cooragh]], Linaku listened with crossed arms to his blunt, unvarnished offer of shared grazing and joint defense against the white dragon:
  > *"That was not a speech."*  
  > *"No," said Krageer. "It was a fact."*  
  Krageer rolled a natural-19 Persuasion (17 total with his -2 Charisma penalty), earning her respect through sheer unyielding honesty.
* **The Spicy Pie Defeat:** Concurrently, Cooragh pitched her mobile kitchen in the center of the Thekupala camp, serving batches of her fiercest pork pies. Thekupala warriors and elders were reduced to streaming tears and drinking gallons of snowmelt—refusing to admit defeat, they granted Cooragh and Krageer permanent sanctuary.
* **Voice of the Council:** Appointed chief magistrate and speaker of the Four Fires council by [[Menuka]]. Linaku managed and defended the rotating pasturage and valley grazing schedules entirely in her head, guarding them with ferocious bureaucratic tenacity (*"Nobody drew a blade on a neighbor. Krageer called this the hardest fight of his life, and Pagolu said that was only because it had no end"*).
* **Mapping the Dragon's Lair:** Led the scouts who surveyed the icy gorges of the Spine of the World and drafted the approach routes into [[Hrimgarra|Hrimgarra's]] cavern at Kuvimeno.
