# 👤 Xanth

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Centaur
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Neutral good
* **Primary Region / Home:** Neverwinter Wood, northwest Faerûn
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Xanth** was a centaur who made his home in the southern reaches of [[Neverwinter Wood]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
When the white dragon [[Cryovain]] arrived in the [[Sword Mountains]] in the late 15 century DR, he drove the orcs from [[Icespire Hold]], and many sought refuge with the [[anchorites of Talos]] at the [[Circle of Thunder]] in Neverwinter Wood. The newly arrived orcs drove Xanth from his home, and he fled west toward the [[High Road]] to seek adventurers who would help him to strike back at the orcs. He ended up sheltering in the hills near the [[Dragon Barrow]], where he observed that the location was haunted by will-o'-wisps and decided to avoid the area.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Allied Centaur Combat Companion)
* **Party Notes:** Noble centaur druid and former companion of Lady Tanamere Alagondar who guided [[The Order of Znak]] to the [[Dragon Barrow]]. Although the party's first **Level 4** foray into the barrow against the will-o'-wisps and Invisible Stalker proved premature, they returned after clearing [[Axeholm]] at **Level 5**, finished the barrow trials to claim the ***Dragon Slayer* longsword** for [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]], and enlisted Xanth to ride to war with them. Fighting side-by-side with the Order, Xanth trampled [[Yargath]] and ten orcs on a custom wilderness battlemap, charged into the lightning-swept summit battle at the [[Circle of Thunder]] against [[Flenz]], [[Narux]], and [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar, and helped purge the [[Woodland Manse]] of [[Grannoc]] and his blight legions.
