# 🔮 Gallio Elibro

## 🛡️ Overview & Personality
* **Identity:** Short-tempered male Rashemi wizard appointed by Neverwinter to oversee the reclamation and magical study of the **House of Thalivar** in [[Leilon]].
* **Physical Appearance:** Wears ink-stained wizard's robes, has tousled curly black hair, and features fingertips permanently stained with purple ink from endless scribing.
* **Exhaustion & Temperament:** Relentless arcane research has pushed him to the brink of physical and mental exhaustion. His sleep is chronically troubled, making him irritable, demanding, and quick to snap at interruptions.

## 👻 The Dark Secret: Possession by Thalivar
Unknown to Gallio himself, his insomnia and night terrors stem from a horrific arcane curse: **the ghost of Thalivar possesses him each night while he sleeps**. 
* Centuries ago, the eccentric wizard Thalivar constructed the high tower to conduct planar beacon experiments, accidentally trapping extradimensional horrors before dying.
* Thalivar's lingering spirit uses Gallio's body as a nightly vessel to manipulate planar wards and scry across dimensions, leaving the Rashemi mage completely drained and baffled by his deteriorating stamina.

---

## 📜 The Haunting of the Tower & The Quest
* **Furious at Abandonment:** When spectral apparitions and planar phantoms began manifesting throughout the ruin, the reconstruction laborers panicked and abandoned their posts. Gallio was incensed that work had ground to a halt.
* **Hiring [[The Order of Znak]]:** While Gallio has not seen the spirits with his own eyes, he does not doubt his underlings' genuine terror. He beseeches the champions of [[The Order of Znak]] to enter the tower, ascend the levels, and cleanse every spectral presence so his crews can return.
* **Study in Area H3:** Gallio remains stationed in his second-floor study (area H3) amidst stacks of tomes and parchment while the party explores the upper reaches.
* **Lorekeeper of the Tower:** When approached respectfully, Gallio can recount the ancient history, planar dangers, and architectural layout of the House of Thalivar to anyone who enquires.

## 👥 Key Relationships & Dynamics
* **[[Hazz Yorrum|Sergeant Hazz Yorrum]]:** The cowardly Neverwinter garrison sergeant routinely ducks military and administrative responsibility by deferring civic and defense matters directly to Gallio.
* **[[The Order of Znak]]:** Relies on the champions as the only force capable of braving Thalivar's planar horrors and stabilizing the tower.
* **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]:** Cooperates with Leilon's master builder to secure stonecutters and carpenters for the tower's structural reinforcement once the ghosts are exorcised.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Official Adventure Context:** In *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Gallio Elibro serves as the primary questgiver for the *"House of Thalivar"* dungeon crawl. His purple-stained fingers and nightly possession provide an eerie, atmospheric subplot connecting local Leilon politics with high-stakes planar magic.
