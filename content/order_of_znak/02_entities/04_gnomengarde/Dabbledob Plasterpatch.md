# 👤 Dabbledob Plasterpatch

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Rock gnome
* **Role & Occupation:** Inventor
* **Alignment:** Chaotic neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** Gnomengarde, Sword Mountains
* **Adventure Appearances:** Divine Contention

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Dabbledob Plasterpatch** was a rock gnome from [[Gnomengarde]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. She was one of the tiny kingdom's foremost two inventors along with [[Fibblestib Pinebark]].

### History
When Gnomengarde's King Korboz went mad in the late 15 century DR, Dabbledob and Fibblestib made curing him their sole focus, even as the mimic responsible for his condition rampaged through the tiny kingdom. The two inventors quickly reached an impasse as Dabbledob dismissed Fibblestib's idea of inventing a **sanity ray** as "stupid" in favor of inventing a **straitjacket**.

Not long after this incident, the town of [[Leilon]] asked Gnomengarde for aid, specifically for any magical items or weapons that could fend off a suspected attack by the green dragon [[Claugiyliamatar]]. In response, the gnomish inventors created the **Gnomengarde grenade**, a weapon suffused with wild magic, and personally attempted to deliver it to Leilon's agents at a meeting point in [[Dumathoin's Gultch]]. Because of the item's instability, it was transported in two parts: Dabbledob carried a magical key which was used to arm the device, while Fibblestib carried the weapon itself. However, the necromancer [[Ularan Mortus]] sent undead to waylay the pair, who survived thanks to their escort, [[Bindlemint Mincemower]], fleeing to find the adventurers from Leilon.

### Personality
Dabbledob was argumentative, and preferred solutions to problems which addressed symptoms rather than root causes.

Dabbletob was also skilled at creating magic items. In turn, both inventors were highly devoted to their kings, [[Gnerkli]] and [[Korboz]], but could be somewhat indifferent toward the well-being of other members of the kingdom.
