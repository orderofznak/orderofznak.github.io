# 👤 Caramip

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Rock gnome
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Chaotic neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** Gnomengarde, Sword Mountains
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Caramip** was a female rock gnome living in [[Gnomengarde]] in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### Description
Caramip, like most of Gnomengarde's inhabitants, looked up to the master inventors [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]] and [[Fibblestib Pinebark]], and would refer visitors to them rather than answer any questions herself.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Gnomengarde Expedition — Level 2)
* **Party Notes:** Resident of [[Gnomengarde]] encountered during [[The Order of Znak]]'s **Level 2** expedition, directing the party toward chief inventors [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]] to uncover the truth behind the **Mimic** infiltrating the caverns.
