# 🏹 Rhynok

## Character Overview & Stats
* **Player:** Gary
* **Race:** Elf
* **Class:** Level 13 Multiclass — Level 8 Ranger (Gloomstalker) / Level 5 Rogue (Assassin)
* **Fighting Style:** Archery Fighting Style (+2 to ranged attack rolls)
* **Feats:** Sharpshooter, Poisoner (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)
* **Background:** Criminal / Smuggler (Proficient with Disguise Kit & Navigator's Tools)
* **Ability Scores:** STR 16 (+3) | DEX 20 (+5) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 11 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 11 (+0)
* **Armor Class (AC):** 18 (wearing +1 Breastplate Armor & max DEX)
* **Ranger Features:**
  * **Favored Terrain:** Swamp and Forest
  * **Favored Enemy:** Humanoid and Undead

---

## ⚔️ Weapons & Stealth Equipment
* **Ranged Weapon:** +2 Longbow (combined with Sharpshooter & Archery Fighting Style)
* **Melee Weapons:** Dagger of Venom, Scimitar of Speed
* **Stealth & Utility Gear:**
  * **Boots of Elvenkind** & **Cloak of Elvenkind** (Disadvantage on perception checks to spot him, Advantage on Stealth)
  * **Poisoner's Belt:** Quick-applying poisons on weapon attacks
  * **Ring of the Beast Master**
  * **Adamantine Nets:** Non-lethal restraint for capturing enemies alive
* **Armor:** +1 Breastplate Armor

---

## 🎯 Combat Role & Strategy
* **Backline Ranged Sniper:** Rhynok's signature opening strategy was fishing for the ultimate **Gloomstalker Dread Ambusher + Assassin Assassinate + Sneak Attack** auto-critical hit combo on surprised targets, dealing catastrophic first-round damage.
