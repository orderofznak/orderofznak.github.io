# 🕊️ Myagkiy Znak (Ь)

## Character Overview & Stats
* **Player:** Peter (DMPC / Dual PC)
* **Race:** Variant Human
* **Class:** Level 13 Life Domain Cleric (Single class)
* **Background:** Hermit
* **Feats:** Observant, Resilient (Constitution)
* **Philosophy:** Dedicated to preserving life at all costs.
* **Linguistic Origin (Мягкий знак / Ь):** Named after the Russian Cyrillic "Soft Sign" (Ь) inspired by conversations at work with coworker Grigory. The symbol represents softening, soothing, and modifying harshness, embodying his role as a compassionate, life-preserving battle cleric who tempers the party's ferocious frontline.
* **Ability Scores:** STR 19 (+4) | DEX 8 (-1) | CON 19 (+4) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 20 (+5) | CHA 12 (+1) *(STR boosted by Gauntlets of Ogre Power)*
* **Armor Class (AC):** **24 AC** (Dayne's +2 Plate Armor & +2 Shield)

## ⚔️ Combat Role & Story Highlights
* **Fearless Heavy Battle Cleric:** Wades fearlessly into the thick of melee combat clad in Dayne's +2 Plate Armor and +2 Shield, focusing on crowd control, status mitigation, and Life Domain party healing.
* **Dormant Volcano Enlarge Spell:** Cast *Enlarge* on **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, allowing Krageer to wrestle a **Fire Giant Queen** in epic grapple combat inside a dormant volcano during *"Keeping the Faith"*.
* **Leilon Base & Wards:** Spiritual anchor of [[The Order of Znak]], helping adopt orphans **Smithwell** and **Burnice**.
