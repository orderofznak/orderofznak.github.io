# ⚖️ Kelemvor (Lord of the Dead)

## 🛡️ Divine Overview & Theological Doctrine
* **Divine Portfolio:** Death, the Dead, the Natural Transition of Souls, Fair Judgment.
* **Realm:** **The Crystal Spire** rising above the City of Judgment on the **Fugue Plane**.
* **Core Tenet & Philosophy:** Death is a natural, inevitable completion of mortal existence that must be met with dignity, solemn peace, and fair reckoning. Kelemvor strictly opposes **undeath**, soul-trapping, and necrotic desecration, viewing the theft of mortal souls as the highest metaphysical blasphemy.
* **The Fellowship of the Soft Sign (1503–1505 DR):**
  * When [[Myagkiy Znak]] stepped onto the Ashen Plains of the Fugue Plane to investigate the Astral soul-siphon, Kelemvor's senior clergy—led by [[High Justiciar Orin the Stern]] and [[Arch-Doomguide Vaelor]]—parleyed with the Life Cleric.
  * Though Kelemvor operates under the strict neutrality of divine judgment and cannot unilaterally fight mortal wars, the Judge approved Myagkiy's crusade to sever **[[Myrkul]]**'s illegal soul pipelines.
  * Granted Myagkiy the **Blessing of Soul-Shielding** (preserving maximum HP against entropic decay) and access to the planar armory containing the tarnished tuning forks for the Negative Energy Plane and Hades.
  * Following the final battle in Hades, Kelemvor's Celestial Arbiters and Doomguides gathered the fallen souls of the **Host of the Delivered** from the blood-soaked causeway, escorting them to the Crystal Spire to receive honor, peaceful rest, and righteous judgment.
