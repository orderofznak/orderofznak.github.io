# ⚡ Anchorites of Talos

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Lore
* **Identity:** A fanatical circle of nature priests, storm reavers, and half-orc shapechangers dedicated to **Talos**, the chaotic evil god of storms, thunder, and destruction.
* **Beast Shape:** Most anchorites possess the blessing of Talos granting them the supernatural ability to polymorph into ferocious wild boars and summon concussive lightning bolts.
* **Sacred Sanctuaries:** Operated from secret shrines across the Sword Coast, notably the **Circle of Thunder**, the **Woodland Manse**, the **Tower of Storms** near the coast, and their dreadnaught warship.

---

## 👥 Notable Leaders & Champions
* **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]:** High priestess and storm warlord who commanded the cult's military offensive during the Leilon trilogy.
* **[[Grannoc]]:** Fanatical anchorite leader who summoned [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar at the Circle of Thunder in Act 1.
* **[[Moesko]]:** Undead anchorite heart-keeper bound to the lighthouse of the Tower of Storms.
* **Cult Commanders & Reavers:** [[Drethna]], [[Flenz]], [[Gahza]], [[Galas Windrage]], [[Gol Badwind]], [[Narux]], and [[Yargath]].

## ⚔️ Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **First Foray into the [[Woodland Manse]], [[Loggers' Camp]] & [[Tower of Storms]]:** [[The Order of Znak]] first clashed with the cult at the [[Woodland Manse]], where [[Rhynok]] scared off many of the stationed orcs with a Gruumsh blood-note ruse before [[Grannoc]], the Gulthias blights, and anchorite *Lightning Bolts* forced the party into a desperate fighting retreat. After spotting a shapeshifted anchorite boar en route to clear the ankheg-infested [[Loggers' Camp]] and visiting [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] (which the anchorites and [[Gorthok]] ransacked in retaliation shortly after the party departed), the champions traveled to the [[Tower of Storms]] and slew the undead anchorite **[[Moesko]]**, destroying his beating heart atop the beacon tower.
* **The [[Circle of Thunder]] & Vengeance at the [[Woodland Manse]]:** Enlisting the centaur [[Xanth]] at [[Dragon Barrow]], the party intercepted and slaughtered [[Yargath]] and ten Talosian orcs en route to the [[Circle of Thunder]], then defeated [[Flenz]], [[Narux]], and the summoned primordial boar [[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar in a tempestuous summit battle. Immediately afterward, the party marched back to the [[Woodland Manse]] with *Spirit Guardians*, *Stunning Strikes*, and Xanth's hooves to annihilate [[Grannoc]], the remaining anchorites, their blight hordes, and the stirges.
* **The Siege of Leilon ([[06 - Act 4 - Divine Contention|Act 4: Divine Contention]]):** Under High Priestess [[Fheralai Stormsworn]], the cult marched upon rebuilt [[Leilon]] in a multi-front assault against both town defenders and rival Myrkulite death cultists under [[Ularan Mortus]], culminating in the final flagship dreadnaught battle beneath the storm apparition of Talos.
