# 👤 Sajia Hurwi (Saija Hurwi)

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Beastwarden Druid / Member of [[The Gnawbones]]
* **Physical Demeanor:** An eighteen-year-old human youth who shunned humanoid society, far preferring the company, instincts, and loyalty of wild predatory beasts.
* **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Kryptgarden Forest]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Backstory
**Sajia Hurwi** (often referred to as *Saija Hurwi*) was drawn into the ranks of [[The Gnawbones]] after hearing widespread frontier legends of the ancient green dragon [[Claugiyliamatar]]'s primal mastery over nature magic.

### Beast Dominion
Disillusioned by civilized towns and humanoid frailties, Sajia developed a fanatical desire to help wild beasts rise up and conquer Faerûn. In Claugiyliamatar, she saw the ultimate apex predator and natural monarch capable of shattering humanoid dominion across the Sword Coast.

* **Encounter Status:** Met (Bluffed)
* **The Deeping Cave Bluff (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** Stood vigil alongside [[Argina Therrow]], [[Delis Venomcauldron]], and [[Zuri Kimyak]] at the mouth of Deeping Cave when [[The Order of Znak]] emerged with the dragon's hoard.
