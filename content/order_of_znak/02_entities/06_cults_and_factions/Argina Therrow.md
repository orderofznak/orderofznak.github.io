# 👤 Argina Therrow

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Leader of [[The Gnawbones]] / Cult Druid
* **Physical Appearance:** A tall, thin human woman in her late sixties who covers her entire body in vivid green paint in reverent devotion to [[Claugiyliamatar]] ("Old Gnawbone").
* **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Kryptgarden Forest]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Backstory
**Argina Therrow** is the fanatical leader of the Gnawbones, a sinister circle of druids and dragon cultists based in the shadowed depths of [[Kryptgarden Forest]] around [[Deeping Cave]].

### The Dragon's Savior
Argina's unshakeable devotion was forged in her youth. When she was a young girl, vicious bandits attacked her in the wilds. In the midst of the assault, the ancient green dragon [[Claugiyliamatar]] descended and devoured the bandits alive. Rather than fleeing in terror, Argina came to revere Claugiyliamatar as a divine savior of those who offer absolute obedience to her might, dedicating her entire adult life to the dragon's service.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Bluffed)
* **The Deeping Cave Bluff (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** Following the harrowing possession of Claugiyliamatar by [[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]] and the breach of the dragon's toxic hoard, [[The Order of Znak]] was intercepted at the cavern entrance by Argina and her three Gnawbones companions ([[Delis Venomcauldron]], [[Sajia Hurwi|Saija Hurwi]], and [[Zuri Kimyak]]).
* **Outwitted by the Order:** Laden with dragon treasure, the Order chose guile over combat. The party convincingly persuaded Argina that they were trusted agents and allies dispatched on the green dragon's business, bluffing the fanatical druids into letting them depart the Kryptgarden caverns completely unmolested.
