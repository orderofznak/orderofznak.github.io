---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Naval Command
**Galas Windrage** was a fanatical war priest of Talos who commanded the storm god's naval raiders in the Sea of Swords. Dedicated to wreaking destruction and enforcing the storm god's wrath upon coastal shipping lanes, Galas commanded a formidable pirate flotilla:
* ***Throatcutter*:** A heavily armored warship equipped with ballistas and mangonels.
* ***Thunderstrike*:** A swift, reinforced longship equipped with siege weapons.
* **Crew Complement:** Each vessel was crewed by 40 seasoned reavers, berserkers, and cult officers eager for blood and plunder.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The High Seas Ambush (*Divine Contention*):** On the return voyage from Neverwinter to [[Leilon]] aboard *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]* and *Icingdeath*, [[The Order of Znak]] and the privateer crew of Captain [[Deloz Montiv]] and Captain [[Xalti Tezzan]] were ambushed by Galas Windrage's warships.
* **The Artillery & Archery Duel:** Galas ordered both *Throatcutter* and *Thunderstrike* to close distance, unleashing punishing broadsides of ballista bolts and mangonel stones. 
* **Defeat and Slaying:** As [[Rhynok]] picked off officers and deck crews with pinpoint longbow fire and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]] enthusiastically traded arrows from the deck, the Order dismantled the enemy siege crews. In the final boarding assault, Galas Windrage was struck down and his reaver fleet was thoroughly vanquished, allowing the Order to sail safely home to Leilon.
