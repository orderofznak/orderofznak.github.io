# 👤 Zuri Kimyak

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human with ancient green dragon ancestry
* **Role & Occupation:** Draconic Scion / Member of [[The Gnawbones]]
* **Physical Appearance:** A short, fierce woman in her twenties with wild black hair dyed with a vivid streak of bright green.
* **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Kryptgarden Forest]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Backstory
**Zuri Kimyak** joined [[The Gnawbones]] after discovering that green dragon blood coursed through her ancient ancestry. This lineage instilled in her a deep, instinctive kinship with [[Claugiyliamatar]] ("Old Gnawbone").

### Draconic Supremacy
Fuelled by her dragon heritage, Zuri possesses supreme arrogance. Except for true dragons and her devoted companions among the Gnawbones, she views all other mortal races and humanoids as inherently inferior vermin worthy only of cold disdain.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Bluffed)
* **The Deeping Cave Bluff (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** Encountered at the mouth of Deeping Cave alongside [[Argina Therrow]], [[Delis Venomcauldron]], and [[Sajia Hurwi|Saija Hurwi]] as [[The Order of Znak]] escaped with the dragon's hoard.
* **Swayed by Guile:** Despite her habitual contempt for outsiders, Zuri was duped alongside her fellow cultists by the party's authoritative bluff that they were allied emissaries of Claugiyliamatar, enabling the Order to depart peacefully.
