# 👤 Auntie Unk

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Sea hag
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Chaotic evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Thunder Cliffs
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Auntie Unk** was a [[sea hag]] in the [[Thunder Cliff Caves]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. She and her sisters [[Nana Pocktuss]] and [[Granny Muyuk]] formed a sea hag coven.

### History
Auntie Unk spent a few years in the [[Mere of Dead Men]]. She adducted travelers off the [[High Road]] and questioned them about news of [[Waterdeep]] or [[Neverwinter]] before eating them. Unk was the last sea hag to join the coven at the Thunder Cliffs.

**Circa** [[1491 DR]], the Thunder Cliff Caves were attacked by a group of [[adventurers]]. Their main objective was to defeat the Talos cultists but they also confronted Unk and her sisters.

### Personality
Unk loved gossip and was bored with the fanatical [[Talos]] cultists she was allied with.

### Activities
Unk and her sisters ate the edible body parts of drowned sailors and animated the inedible body parts as [[skull flier]]s to protect their lair.
