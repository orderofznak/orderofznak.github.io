---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Gahza** was a fanatical Sahuagin High Priestess devoted to the bloodthirsty shark-god **Sekolah**. In late 1491 DR, Gahza led an amphibious raiding force out of the Sea of Swords to overrun the fishing hamlet of [[Leilon Point]]. Slaying the local fishers, Gahza established a forward operational temple within the subterranean **Salt Caves**, stationing four-armed blademasters in the barracks (S2) and preparing dark blood sacrifices.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The S1 Ambush (August 28, 2020):** When [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] inadvertently detonated the hidden *Glyph of Warding* in the entrance cavern (S1), the thunderous explosion alerted Gahza and her elite bodyguards in S2.
* **Combat in S1:** Gahza surged into S1 with her four-armed blademasters wielding *Wavecutter Blades*. She commanded the assault, attempting to suppress the champions with prayers to Sekolah.
* **The Slaying:** [[The Order of Znak]] broke her offensive line—[[Rhynok]] immobilizing her vanguard with the Adamantine Net, [[Myagkiy Znak]] deploying radiant *Spirit Guardians*, and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] and Krageer shattering her guard. Gahza was cut down in the cavern spray.
