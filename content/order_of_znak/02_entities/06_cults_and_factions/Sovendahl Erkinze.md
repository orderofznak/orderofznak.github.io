# 👤 Sovendahl Erkinze

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Drow
* **Role & Occupation:** Cultist
* **Primary Region / Home:** Thunder Cliffs, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Storm Lord's Wrath

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Sovendahl Erkinze** was a drow cultist of [[Talos]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. He was the second-in-command to [[Gadrille]], who was in charge of the cult's operations in the [[Thunder Cliff Caves]].

### History
**Circa** [[1491 DR]] Sovendahl disapproved of some of Gadrille's more reckless actions, judging them too dangerous and likely to attract unwanted attention. He secretly met with Fheralai, urging her to put a stop to it. Gadrille complied with Fheralai's request but prayed to Talos to punish Sovendahl. The god answered her prayers and cursed Sovendahl.

While Sovendahl suffered from his curse and delegated his responsibilities to his bodyguards, the Thunder Cliff Caves were attacked by a group of adventurers. Sovendahl fought well against the attackers, but when offered the opportunity, he was willing to leave the cult in order to receive a cure for his curse.

### Personality
Sovendahl acted cautiously and calculated, in contrast to his reckless superior. He was loyal to the [[Cult of Talos]], but not to the point of placing the cult above his self-preservation.

### Activities
Sovendahl was responsible for managing the sorting, transportation, and sale of stolen commodities seized from destroyed ships by the cult.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Cured & Spared (Act 2: *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Chapter 7: "Assault on Thunder Cliffs")
* **Curse Removal & The Dagger of Venom:** Having slipped into the Thunder Cliffs redoubt without alerting the exterior guards, [[The Order of Znak]] discovered the tormented drow suffering from Talos's divine affliction. Prizing self-preservation above fanatical devotion, Sovendahl surrendered in exchange for salvation. [[Myagkiy Znak]] cured his curse with divine magic; in return, Sovendahl yielded intelligence on Gadrille's inner chambers and handed over a ***Dagger of Venom*** (claimed by [[Rhynok]]). Convinced that their lives were forfeit if they remained, Sovendahl and his contingent of scouts and spies evacuated the cliffs peacefully.
