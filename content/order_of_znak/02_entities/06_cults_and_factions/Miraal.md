# 👤 Miraal

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Banshee<br/> Sea elf
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Chaotic evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Tower of Storms, Sea of Swords
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Miraal** was an aquatic elf spellcaster who was slain and transformed into a banshee in the late 15 century [[DR]].

### History
Miraal was present at the lighthouse on the [[Sword Coast North]] that would come to be known as the [[Tower of Storms]] when she was attacked and killed in a nearby cave by an [[anchorite of Talos]] named [[Moesko]]. The anchorite took Miraal's precious spellcasting focus and left her undead form to guard the cave, which happened to be the only land route leading to the lighthouse. Moesko then claimed the tower, transforming it into a temple to [[Talos]], and used his magic to bewitch the beacon and lure ships to their doom on the rocks below the tower. Meanwhile, Miraal remained bound to the site where she had been killed, and raged at the anchorite but could not reach the tower to enact revenge. During this time, a giant crab whom she had **awakened** on a nearby beach began seeking someone who could help to release her spirit.

### Description
As a banshee, Miraal was undead and appeared as a spectral, once-beautiful elven woman with gills and a mess of tangled hair framing her twisted face. Her hands and feet were webbed, and her ghostly tattered clothes floated around her as if she was underwater.

### Activities
In life, Miraal was known to **awaken** sea creatures and earn their loyalty. In death, she was bound to the place in which she was murdered, a tunnel-like sea cave, and was unable to travel more than  from either entrance. While she was as hateful as any banshee, her hatred was mostly directed toward her murderer, [[Moesko]]. She remained rational enough to attempt to bargain with adventurers, offering them safe passage in exchange for their promise to kill Moesko and return her conch shell spellcasting focus. However, she would relentlessly attempt to kill any who refused this offer.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met & Laid to Rest ([[Tower of Storms]])
* **Party Notes:** Sea elf spellcaster turned **banshee** haunting the tidal sea cave at the base of the [[Tower of Storms]]. After befriending the **awakened giant crab** on the shingle beach outside, [[The Order of Znak]] entered the cave and parleyed with Miraal, agreeing to slay her murderer **[[Moesko]]** and recover her stolen **opalescent conch shell spellcasting focus**. Once the champions fought past the cliffside harpies, slew Moesko in the lighthouse, destroyed his disembodied beating heart atop the beacon tower, and returned the opalescent conch shell to Miraal, her spirit departed in peace—and the grateful awakened crab retrieved an enchanted **+1 Longbow** from the sunken offshore shipwreck for **[[Rhynok]]**.
