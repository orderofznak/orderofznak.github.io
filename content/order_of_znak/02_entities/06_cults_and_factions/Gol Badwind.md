# 👤 Gol Badwind

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-Orc
* **Role & Occupation:** First Mate of *The Relentless* / Anchorite of Talos
* **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** The Sea of Swords / Beached Warship near Tower of Storms
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Gol Badwind** was the ruthless First Mate serving aboard *The Relentless*, a heavily armed Talosite dreadnaught under High Priestess [[Fheralai Stormsworn]]. A seasoned raider devoted to the Storm Lord Talos, Badwind was stationed in the vessel's lower decks, overseeing four skeletal minotaurs and guarding the vessel's cargo holds.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain
* **The Lower Decks Melee (September 15, 2020):** When [[The Order of Znak]] dropped through the 20-foot cargo shaft into the 3rd lower deck, they were ambushed by four **Minotaur Skeletons**. Mid-fight, Gol Badwind rolled initiative (12) and opened fire from the shadowed gangways with his shortbow.
* **The Stunning Lockdown:** [[Tvyordiy Znak]] closed into melee with his *Vicious Quarterstaff*, hitting Gol with repeated strikes and expending ki to trigger **Stunning Strike**. Gol rolled Constitution saves (`7` fail, `22` pass), leaving the warlord stunned and defenseless.
* **The Execution:** [[Myagkiy Znak]] blasted Gol with a 2nd-level *Guiding Bolt* (`19 to-hit`, 12 radiant), lighting him up for [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], who landed brutal Adamantine Greataxe crits (`27 to-hit`, 26 damage; `27 to-hit`, 14 damage). Tvyordiy finished him with a natural 20 critical quarterstaff strike (`29 to-hit`, 18 damage).
* **The Alchemy Jug:** Searching Gol Badwind's private cabin following his demise, Krageer discovered and claimed the ***Alchemy Jug***.
