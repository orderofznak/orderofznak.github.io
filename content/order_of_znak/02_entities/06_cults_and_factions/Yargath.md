# 👤 Yargath

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Half-orc
* **Role & Occupation:** Anchorite of Talos
* **Alignment:** Neutral evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Circle of Thunder, Neverwinter Wood, northwest Faerûn
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Yargath** was a member of the [[anchorites of Talos]] in the late 15 century [[DR]]. She was one of the caretakers of the [[Circle of Thunder]] in [[Neverwinter Wood]], a site dedicated to the worship of Talos and the veneration of his servant, [[Gorthok|Gorthok the Thunderboar]].

### Description
Yargath was a half-orc, but her appearance left some doubt as to her exact heritage at first glance. Her fingernails were long and sharp, appearing as claws. She kept few furnishings or possessions, save for a **potion of greater healing** that she took with her when out on patrol in the nearby woods. The anchorites put these orcs to work harassing local hunters or patrolling the area around the Circle, and Yargath was known to lead such orc patrols.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Wilderness Clash en route to Circle of Thunder)
* **Party Notes:** Half-orc [[Anchorites of Talos|Anchorite of Talos]] warband commander. As [[The Order of Znak]] and their centaur companion [[Xanth]] marched from [[Phandalin]] and the ransacked ruins of [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] toward the [[Circle of Thunder]], they intercepted Yargath and her patrol of **ten Talosian orcs** along the forest trail. With [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s *Spirit Guardians*, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]'s silvered greataxe, [[Rhynok]]'s deadly longbow, [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s *Stunning Strikes*, and Xanth's charging hooves, the party slaughtered Yargath and all ten orcs before pressing on to slay [[Gorthok]].
