# 🗡️ Redbrands (Redbrand Ruffians)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A notorious gang of human brigands, extortionists, and ruthless highwaymen who terrorized the frontier town of **Phandalin**, wearing scarlet cloaks as their menacing calling card.
* **Lair:** Operated from the cellars and catacombs beneath ruined **Tresendar Manor** on the eastern edge of Phandalin, frequenting the local taphouse **[[Sleeping Giant]]**.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Role in Phandalin
* **Town Extortion:** Intimidated local shopkeepers including [[Linene Graywind]] at Lionshield Coster and miner representative [[Halia Thornton]], until broken and driven out by visiting heroes.

* **Official Adventure Modules:** *Lost Mine of Phandelver*, *Dragon of Icespire Peak*
