# ⚓ Captain Xalti Tezzan

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Drow (Dark elf)
* **Role & Occupation:** Captain of the privateer warship *Icingdeath*
* **Allegiance & Network:** Bregan D'aerthe / Jarlaxle Baenre
* **Vessel:** *Icingdeath* (the larger companion warship to *[[Deloz Montiv|Twinkle]]*, both owned by Jarlaxle Baenre and named in homage to Drizzt Do'Urden's legendary twin scimitars)
* **Alignment:** Chaotic neutral
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sea of Swords, Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Divine Contention*

---

## 📜 Lore & Vessels

### The Warship *Icingdeath*
Captain Xalti commands the *Icingdeath*, a formidable warship sailing the Sea of Swords. Operating as part of **Jarlaxle Baenre**'s privateer syndicate (Bregan D'aerthe), *Icingdeath* works in tandem with its smaller companion caravel, the *Twinkle*, commanded by Captain [[Deloz Montiv]].

### The Illusory Figurehead
To sail surface waters along the Sword Coast and dock at human settlements without inciting anti-drow panic or engaging Waterdhavian patrols, the *Icingdeath*'s figurehead is enchanted with permanent illusory magic that projects a glamour making the dark elven crew appear as human mariners.

## ⚔️ Campaign Context & Table Ties
* **Bregan D'aerthe Surface Operations:** Operating under the orders of Jarlaxle Baenre during the Leilon rebuilding and the coastal conflicts of *Divine Contention*.
* **Companion Vessel:** Paired with Captain Deloz Montiv's *Twinkle*, which directly allied with [[The Order of Znak]] and ferried the heroes into the storm to destroy High Priestess [[Fheralai Stormsworn]].
