# 👤 Runa Vokdottir

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Neutral evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Runa Vokdottir** was a member of the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]], a group of reckless adventurers who made their living sometimes as sellswords and sometimes as brigands.

### Description
Runa was a muscular woman. She had black hair and cold blue eyes.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Icespire Hold)
* **Party Notes:** Shieldmaiden of the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]] stationed at [[Icespire Hold]]. Slain alongside [[Brakkis Elspaar]], [[Dobin Noreth]], and [[Jabarl]] at **Level 6** when [[The Order of Znak]] purged the fortress en route to their rooftop battle against [[Cryovain]].
