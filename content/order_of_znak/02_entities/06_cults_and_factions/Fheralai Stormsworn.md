---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Fheralai Stormsworn** was a zealous High Priestess of **Talos the Destroyer** and the supreme commander of *The Relentless*, a massive war vessel grounded upon the rocks near the Tower of Storms. Wielding storm magic and commanding anchorites, cultists, and reanimated horrors, Fheralai captured traveling bard **[[Tarbin Tul]]** and bound the vessel through dark necromantic pacts to unleash tidal tempests upon the Sword Coast North.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Defeated / Fate Sealed
* **The Captain's Cabin Showdown (September 18, 2020):** Following their purge of the upper decks, minotaur skeletons, and First Mate [[Gol Badwind]], [[The Order of Znak]] breached Fheralai's personal sanctum.
* **Tvyordiy's Stunning Blow:** Tvyordiy lunged across the cabin with his *Vicious Quarterstaff*, striking twice (`25` and `22`) before landing a natural 20 critical unarmed strike (`29 to-hit`). Spending ki on his second strike, Tvyordiy successfully **stunned** Fheralai, robbing the high priestess of her somatic spellcasting.
* **The Focused Barrage:** 
  * [[Myagkiy Znak]] stepped forward with an upcast 3rd-level ***Guiding Bolt*** (`22 to-hit`, 21 radiant damage), scouring her storm wards with holy brilliance.
  * [[Rhynok]] unleashed a deadly sniper volley with his +1 Longbow and Sneak Attack (`25`, `27`, `27`), driving arrows through her defenses.
* **Defeat:** Battered down to **34 HP**, stunned, and surrounded with her crew slaughtered, Fheralai was completely overwhelmed and broken, sealing her fate as the Order liberated the ship.
