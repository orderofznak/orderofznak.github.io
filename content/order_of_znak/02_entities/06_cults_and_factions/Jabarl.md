# 👤 Jabarl

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Neutral evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Jabarl the Orc-Biter** was a member of the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]], a group of reckless adventurers who made their living sometimes as sellswords and sometimes as brigands.

### Personality
Jabarl was a rugged, ill-tempered thug who intimidated people to get what he wanted. If that didn't work, he resorted to brute force. Jabarl liked to take care of horses, however, which could calm his violent nature.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Slain (Icespire Hold)
* **Party Notes:** Ruthless veteran of the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]] encountered inside [[Icespire Hold]]. Slain at **Level 6** by [[The Order of Znak]]—fighting with [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu|Krageer]]'s silvered greataxe, [[Tvyordiy Znak|Tvyordiy]]'s silvered *Qiang*, and [[Rhynok]]'s silvered short sword—before the party ascended to the roof to face [[Cryovain]].
