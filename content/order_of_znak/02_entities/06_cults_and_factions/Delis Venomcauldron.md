# 👤 Delis Venomcauldron

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Cult Alchemist & Toxicologist / Member of [[The Gnawbones]]
* **Physical Appearance:** A middle-aged human woman with a completely shaved head and severe facial scarring from volatile alchemical acid burns.
* **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** [[Kryptgarden Forest]], Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** *Sleeping Dragon's Wake*

---

## 📜 Canonical Role & Backstory
**Delis Venomcauldron** serves as the chief alchemist and poisoner of [[The Gnawbones]], brewing caustic mixtures, sleep draughts, and virulent venoms inspired by the toxic breath of [[Claugiyliamatar]].

### Flight from Civilization
Delis abandoned humanoid society after murdering her cruel, abusive husband with a meticulously crafted lethal poison. Fleeing into the primordial gloom of [[Kryptgarden Forest]], she found sanctuary among the dragon worshippers. Delis possesses a wicked, macabre sense of humor and an obsessive fascination with deadly poisons, venoms, and chemical reagents.

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Encounter Status:** Met (Bluffed)
* **The Deeping Cave Bluff (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake*):** Encountered at the mouth of Deeping Cave alongside [[Argina Therrow]], [[Sajia Hurwi|Saija Hurwi]], and [[Zuri Kimyak]] as [[The Order of Znak]] emerged laden with Claugiyliamatar's hoard.
* **Guile over Combat:** Delis and her cultist companions were convinced by the party's fast-talking bluff that the Order were authorized dragon allies, permitting the champions to exit Kryptgarden Forest unhindered.
