# 🐉 Cult of the Dragon

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A notorious, secretive religious organization operating across Faerûn dedicated to the veneration and creation of immortal dracoliches (undead dragons).
* **The Mere of Dead Men Connection:** Centered historically around the ancient black wyrm **[[Chardansearavitriol]] ("Ebondeath")**, who ruled the Mere of Dead Men and transformed into a dracolich under the cult's rites before his physical body crumbled into dust.

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **Unholy Alliance with Myrkul:** In *Sleeping Dragon's Wake* and *Divine Contention*, splinter elements and ancient rites of the Cult of the Dragon intersected with the death cult of **[[Ularan Mortus]]** (High Priest of Myrkul).
* **The Vessel of Ebondeath:** The cult's ancient necromantic rituals were repurposed by Ularan's followers to anchor Ebondeath's disembodied soul into the body of the green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]**, threatening to unleash an undead cataclysm upon the Sword Coast until thwarted by [[The Order of Znak]].
