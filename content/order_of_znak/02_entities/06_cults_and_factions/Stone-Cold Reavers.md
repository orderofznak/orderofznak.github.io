# ⚔️ Stone-Cold Reavers

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A band of ruthless, self-serving mercenaries, grave-robbers, and opportunists operating along the Sword Coast and frozen peaks of the Sword Mountains.
* **The Icespire Hold Heist:** During Act 1 (*Dragon of Icespire Peak*), the Reavers tracked white dragon [[Cryovain]] to his mountain redoubt at Icespire Hold, lurking in the fortress sub-levels hoping to plunder the dragon's hoard once someone else did the heavy fighting.

---

## 👥 Roster & Mercenary Members
* **Frontline Fighters:** [[Brakkis Elspaar]], [[Dobin Noreth]], [[Jabarl]], and shieldmaiden [[Runa Vokdottir]].
* **Allied Rogue:** [[Syleen Wintermoon]].

---

## ⚔️ Campaign Interactions & Party Encounters
* **Confrontation at Icespire Hold:** Encountered by [[The Order of Znak]] within the cold stone chambers of Icespire Hold. Faced with the Order's overwhelming martial resolve, the Reavers were subdued and forced to abandon their opportunistic plunder.
