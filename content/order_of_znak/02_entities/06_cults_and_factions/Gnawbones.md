# 🌿 The Gnawbones

## 🛡️ Overview & Cult Lore
* **Identity:** A sinister woodland cult and druidic circle operating in the shadowed canopy of [[Kryptgarden Forest]], fanatically devoted to the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** ("Old Gnawbone").
* **Sanctuary & Domain:** The cult maintains vigilance around [[Deeping Cave]] and throughout Kryptgarden Forest, venerating the green dragon as an untouchable natural godhead, divine savior, and apex predator.
* **Ritual & Practice:** The Gnawbones practice dark nature rites, venom cultivation, and draconic idolatry, seeking to preserve the dragon's territory and dismantle civilized incursions along the Sword Coast.

---

## 👥 Inner Circle & Key Champions
The inner circle of The Gnawbones is led by four distinct evil human druids:
* **[[Argina Therrow]]:** Cult Leader and high priestess. A tall, thin woman in her late sixties entirely coated in green body paint; saved from bandits by Claugiyliamatar in childhood, she leads the circle with fanatical devotion.
* **[[Delis Venomcauldron]]:** Master Alchemist and poisoner. A middle-aged woman with a shaved head and alchemical burn scars who fled civilization after murdering her cruel husband; obsessed with toxic concoctions.
* **[[Sajia Hurwi|Sajia (Saija) Hurwi]]:** Feral Beastwarden. An 18-year-old youth who prefers beasts to humans, dreaming of wild animals conquering Faerûn under Claugiyliamatar's nature magic.
* **[[Zuri Kimyak]]:** Draconic Scion. A short woman in her twenties with green-streaked black hair, possessing green dragon ancestry and regarding all non-dragons with haughty disdain.

## ⚔️ Campaign Role in *The Order of Znak*
* **The Deeping Cave Bluff (*Sleeping Dragon's Wake* Finale):** As [[The Order of Znak]] emerged from Deeping Cave carrying Claugiyliamatar's looted hoard following the dragon's possession by [[Chardansearavitriol|Ebondeath]], they were confronted by the four Gnawbones druids. 
* Rather than battling the exhausted champions, the four druids were successfully bluffed by the Order into believing the heroes were trusted emissaries of the dragon. Convinced of the party's false authority, the Gnawbones allowed the Order to depart Kryptgarden Forest unmolested.
