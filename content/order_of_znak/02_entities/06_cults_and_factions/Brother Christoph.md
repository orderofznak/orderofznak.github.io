# 📖 Brother Christoph

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Identity:** A devout scholar-monk of the **House of Knowledge** in **Neverwinter**, dedicated to **Oghma**, god of knowledge and wisdom.
* **The Massacre Witness:** The sole survivor and primary eyewitness of the gruesome midnight massacre inside the temple of Oghma during the serial killing spree orchestrated by [[Graag]] the Werebear in June 2022.

---

## 📜 Key Story Beats & Quests

### 🔥 [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]
* **Surviving the House of Knowledge Slaughter:** When [[Graag]] and his cultist killers crept into the House of Knowledge under cover of darkness, they slaughtered nine scholar-monks in cold blood and tore out their hearts. Christoph survived by hiding in the library stacks, witnessing the atrocity in paralyzed terror.
* **Interrogation by the Order:** When [[The Order of Znak]] arrived in Neverwinter alongside apprentice forge cleric [[Dayne Beltran]] to investigate the murders, they interrogated Brother Christoph. Despite his severe shock, Christoph provided the crucial breakthrough testimony:
  * Describing the killers as men who stepped out of the shadows looking normal, before the leader shape-shifted into a horrifying "half-man, half-beast" with lethal claws and fangs.
  * Corroborating that the victims' hearts were ripped out and taken.
  * Guiding [[Rhynok]] to analyze the muddy clawed paw prints, confirming the beast was an unnatural mountainous bear.
