# 👤 Brakkis Elspaar

## 🛡️ Overview & Canon Profile
* **Species / Heritage:** Human
* **Role & Occupation:** Adventurer / Resident
* **Alignment:** Neutral evil
* **Primary Region / Home:** Sword Coast North
* **Adventure Appearances:** Dungeons & Dragons Essentials Kit

## 📜 Canonical Role & Lore
**Brakkis Elspaar** was a member of the [[Stone-Cold Reavers]], a group of reckless adventurers who made their living sometimes as sellswords and sometimes as brigands.

### History
The group tried to steal the presumed treasure hoard of the young [[white dragon]] [[Cryovain]] from its lair, [[Icespire Hold]]. However, Cryovain hadn't amassed any treasure yet.

### Description
Brakkis was out of shape and had lost the benefits of youth.

### Personality
He took advantage of any opportunities presented to him, resorting to thievery when honest work was unavailable. He was a follower rather than a leader. This suited him because all he really wanted to think and talk about were [[dog]]s and what he would do when he retired.
