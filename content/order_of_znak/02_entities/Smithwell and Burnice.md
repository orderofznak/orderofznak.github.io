# 👦👧 Young Smithwell & Burnice (The Leilon Wards)

## 🛡️ Overview & Lore
* **Origins in Leilon:** Two 10-year-old village children and inseparable best friends living in the coastal fishing settlement of Leilon on the Sword Coast during *Storm Lord's Wrath*. Smithwell (boy) and Burnice (girl) were both children of local fishers.
* **The Kraken Cult Assault:** Caught in the crossfire when the tiefling Kraken Priest **Nixoxious** and her cultist berserkers stormed the town fishery (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*). The cultists captured the townsfolk and sacrificed their fisher parents in dark rites to summon destructive water elementals and water weirds. In the terror and confusion of the assault, the two children fled into the surrounding marshlands and went missing.
* **Rescue by The Order of Znak:** Discovered and rescued from the marshes by the champions of [[The Order of Znak]], who slew the cultists and disrupted the summoning ritual. 
* **Adoption into the Order:** Heartbroken to discover that both sets of parents had perished in the kraken sacrifices, the heroes refused to leave the children to fate. The Order formally adopted both orphans as their personal wards, bestowing upon them the honorary surname **Znak** and bringing them to live at the party's newly established Leilon headquarters as adoptive brother and sister.
* **Childhood at the Headquarters:**
  * **Smithwell:** Captivated by the booming laughter, colossal physical presence, and epic tavern tales of Goliath barbarian [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]. Smithwell spent his boyhood imitating Krageer's battle roars and soaking in campfire skaldic songs.
  * **Burnice:** Drawn to the quiet serenity, profound spiritual devotion, and compassionate healing arts of Life Domain cleric [[Myagkiy Znak]]. Burnice watched Myagkiy bind the wounds of the afflicted, fostering an unyielding conviction to defend the innocent against cosmic evil.

## 📊 Legacy & Second-Generation Evolution (*The Acolytes of Znak*)
As adults, the two wards took up the adventuring mantle to lead the next generation into the mists of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*):
* **[[Smithwell Znak]]:** Level 3 Multiclass — Bard / Barbarian (frontline skald combining Krageer's barbarian rage with skaldic storytelling, played by Gary).
* **[[Burnice Znak]]:** Level 3 Multiclass — Paladin / Hexblade Warlock (devoted holy defender combining Myagkiy's divine vows with shadowy patron bladecraft, played by Peter).

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Official D&D 5e Adventure Context:** In the Wizards of the Coast adventure *Storm Lord's Wrath* (the middle tier of the *Beyond the Dragon of Icespire Peak* trilogy), Smithwell and Burnice appear in the introductory encounter *"A Normal Day in Leilon"*. The module describes them as 10-year-old NPC children whose parents are fishers targeted by the kraken priest Nixoxious.
* **Table Emergence & Parallel to Yasmina:** At many tables, rescued children fade into the background once the quest XP is awarded. At Peter and Gary's table, the champions formed deep emotional attachments to their rescuees. Much like [[Yasmina]] was adopted after her liberation from a Night Hag in *"Keeping the Faith"*, Smithwell and Burnice became beloved fixtures of the campaign hearth—ultimately graduating from module NPC cast members to full player characters for the second-generation campaign.
