## 📜 Overview of the Third Act
As the storm clouds of **[[Talos]]** darkened over the Sea of Swords, a second ancient terror rose from the mire: **[[Ularan Mortus]]**, High Priest of **Myrkul**, and his necromantic **Cult of the Dragon** allies sought an ancient vessel for the spirit of the dracolich **[[Chardansearavitriol]]** ("Ebondeath"). Caught between rival apocalyptic cults, **[[The Order of Znak]]** journeyed beneath the boughs of **Kryptgarden Forest** into the treacherous **Deeping Cave**—lair of the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** ("Old Gnawbone").

---

## ⚔️ The Chronicles of Deeping Cave & Kryptgarden Forest

### 1. Infiltration of Deeping Cave
Venturing deep into the primordial canopy of Kryptgarden Forest to seek an audience with [[Claugiyliamatar]], the Order bypassed the main approaches and infiltrated Deeping Cave via its perilous northern cavern entrance—hacking through grasping **Assassin Vines** and fending off ambushes from ethereal-jaunting **Phase Spiders**.

### 2. The Caustic Acid Relay & Krageer's Boiling Charge
Deep within the caverns, the party's advance was barred by wide, bubbling subterranean pools of caustic acid. Devising an ingenious relay across the hazard, **[[Rhynok]]** offered his *Ring of Water Walking* while **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** invoked the *Water Walk* spell—ferrying the enchanted ring back and forth across the fuming surface so each companion could step safely across.

Growing impatient with the methodical ferrying operation, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** let out a roar, fly into a Bear Totem rage, and charged straight through the boiling acid pools on foot—emerging smoking and blistered on the far bank, where an exasperated Myagkiy poured out restorative prayers to mend the Goliath's burns.

### 3. Audience with [[Claugiyliamatar]] & The Hunt for [[Viantha Cruelhex]]
Entering the emerald-lit inner sanctum of **[[Claugiyliamatar]]**, the four champions stood before the ancient green wyrm. Rather than striking immediately, Old Gnawbone demanded a tribute of blood service: the elimination of **[[Viantha Cruelhex]]**, an insidious **Deathlock Mastermind** (undead warlock) defiling her forest domain.

Tracking the undead warlock to a foul-smelling clearing in Kryptgarden Forest echoed by guttural chanting, the Order ambushed Viantha just as she brandished a bone wand to call forth shrieking **Specters**. With overwhelming speed and radiant fury, the champions cut down the specters, destroyed Viantha Cruelhex, and hauled her corpse back to Deeping Cave as proof of the deed.

### 4. Ebondeath's Possession & The Sword Wraith Ambush
When the Order returned to the inner cavern with Viantha's remains, the confrontation spiraled into catastrophe. Before the champions' very eyes, the incorporeal, necrotic spirit of the ancient dracolich **[[Chardansearavitriol]]** (Ebondeath) lunged from the shadows and **possessed [[Claugiyliamatar]]**! As the massive green dragon writhed and roared under the dracolich's foul dominion, ancient **Sword Wraiths** materialized throughout the cavern tunnels, forcing the Order to fight through ranks of spectral undead warriors.

### 5. The Poisoned Hoard & Monastic Purity
Discovering Claugiyliamatar's glittering treasure hoard sealed behind an invisible *Wall of Force* and choked with lethal green dragon poison gas, the party turned to audacious frontline tactics. **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** stepped up first as the "sacrificial lamb," plunging into the toxic fumes to trigger and absorb the initial brunt of the trap before scrambling back out for healing.

Once Krageer was clear, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** centered his breath and drew upon his supreme monastic mastery of body and spirit—a state of internal purity that rendered him utterly impervious to poison. Striding calmly into the swirling green miasma without taking a scratch, the Aarakocra monk methodically gathered and extracted the dragon's entire treasure hoard.

### 6. Bluffing [[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]] Circle
Laden with draconic plunder as they made their exit toward the surface of Deeping Cave, the four champions came face-to-face with the fanatical inner circle of **[[Gnawbones|The Gnawbones]]**—cult leader **[[Argina Therrow]]**, venom alchemist **[[Delis Venomcauldron]]**, beastwarden **[[Sajia Hurwi|Saija Hurwi]]**, and draconic scion **[[Zuri Kimyak]]**. Rather than igniting another grueling cavern battle, the Order coolly bluffed the druids and cultists into believing they were acting as the authorized emissaries of Claugiyliamatar herself—strolling out of Kryptgarden Forest with the dragon's treasure completely unmolested.
