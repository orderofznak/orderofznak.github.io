## 📜 Overview of the Bridge Era
Following the defeat of [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] on the Sea of Swords and the restoration of peace to [[Leilon]], the four founding champions of **[[The Order of Znak]]**—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**—spent five winters rebuilding the town and raising their adopted wards at [[Aufox Manor]].

In 1500 DR, with the town secure and the young wards coming of age, each champion departed Leilon on staggered journeys to fulfill personal callings, family legacies, and planar destinies. These solo and paired adventures form the **Bridge Campaign**—the narrative connective tissue between their Sword Coast heroics and the day their grown protégés (**[[The Acolytes of Znak Campaign Plan|The Acolytes of Znak]]**: [[Burnice Znak]], [[Smithwell Znak]], [[Graxxoamo]], [[Shriss]], and [[Yasmina]]) step into the creeping mists of Barovia.

### 🪨 [[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|Chapter 7a: The Stone and the Skillet]]
* **Protagonists:** [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] and **[[Cooragh]]** (*Thaluu*).
* **Setting:** The Spine of the World & The High Ogolakanu Country (1500–1504 DR).
* **Narrative Arc:** Krageer returns to the cold northern peaks of his ancestors alongside Cooragh. Facing four fractured, warring Goliath clans, Krageer uses both unyielding strength and the warm hospitality of Cooragh's fires to forge unity. In the Glass Hall, Krageer duels and slays the colossal white dragon **Hrimgarra the Pale Widow** (elder clutch-sister to Cryovain), concluding with the sacred **Binding of Stones** wedding ceremony.
* **Milestone Progression:** Krageer reaches **Level 15** (Barbarian 9 / Fighter 6), taking +2 Constitution (18 CON) and reaching **164 HP**.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chronicle:** **[[07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet|07a - Bridge - Krageer - The Stone and the Skillet]]**

### 🦅 [[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|Chapter 7b: The Breath of the Boundless Sky]]
* **Protagonists:** **Master [[Tvyordiy Znak]]** and **[[Yeri]]**.
* **Setting:** Leilon, Evermoor Way, Grandfather Tree, Mount Nimbus, and The Elemental Plane of Air (1500–1505 DR).
* **Narrative Arc:** Answering the dying Auran whisper of the storm myrmidons on the Sea of Swords, Tvyordiy leaves Aufox Manor to seek his planar heritage. After pacifying river trolls on the Evermoor Way and harmonizing his inner wereboar curse beneath the [[Grandfather Tree]] with [[Elder Kaya]], Tvyordiy ascends Mount Nimbus with the eccentric Kenku sorcerer **[[Yeri]]**. Struck by primordial lightning, Tvyordiy unlocks **Diamond Soul (Monk 14)** while Yeri manifests **Dragonfly Wings**. Crossing into the Elemental Plane of Air, Tvyordiy reunites with his elder brother **[[Deekek|Wing-Captain Deekek]]** and twin sister **[[Klee'errk]]** at the **Aerie of the Boundless Whorl**, trains the Unbending Talons (including disciple **[[Kree-uk]]**), and shatters the colossal **Avatar of Talos**. After Yeri's sacrifice into the singularity, Tvyordiy attains **Timeless Body (Monk 15)** and returns to Toril with four planar disciples to establish the courtyard dojo at [[Aufox Manor]].
* **Milestone Progression:** Tvyordiy reaches **Level 15 Monk** (*Way of the Drunken Master*), gaining ***Diamond Soul*** and ***Timeless Body*** (108 HP, 15 Ki, 75 ft fly speed). Yeri reaches **Level 14 Divine Soul Sorcerer** with permanent dragonfly wings.
* 👉 **Read the Full Chronicle:** **[[07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky|07b - Bridge - Tvyordiy - The Breath of the Boundless Sky]]**

## 🍻 The 1505 DR Reunion
In autumn 1505 DR, ten winters after parting ways, a call sent over the Sending Stones draws the four champions back together at **[[The Wayside Inn]]** on the High Road—setting the stage for the next era of legend.
