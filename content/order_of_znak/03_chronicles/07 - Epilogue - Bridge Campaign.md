## 📜 Overview of the Bridge Chronicle
Following the defeat of [[Fheralai Stormsworn]] on the Sea of Swords and the restoration of peace to [[Leilon]], an upcoming final chapter awaits the four original champions of **[[The Order of Znak]]**—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**—serving as the narrative bridge between their Sword Coast triumphs and the day their grown wards and lizardfolk protégés (**[[The Acolytes of Znak Campaign Plan|The Acolytes of Znak]]**: [[Burnice Znak]], [[Smithwell Znak]], [[Graxxoamo]], [[Shriss]], and [[Yasmina]]) step into the creeping mists of Barovia.
