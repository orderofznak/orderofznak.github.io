## 📜 Overview of the Second Act
Following the slaying of [[Cryovain]] atop [[Icespire Hold]], the renown of **[[The Order of Znak]]** drew a formal commission to travel south along the High Road to the coastal settlement of **[[Leilon]]**, then undergoing an ambitious reconstruction funded by **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** of Neverwinter. What began as a frontier security charter swiftly evolved into a war against the resurgent tempest cult of **[[Talos]]**, the founding of the Order's permanent fortified estate, the adoption of two orphaned wards, and an enduring blood-and-honor alliance with the lizardfolk clans of the Mere of Dead Men.

## ⚔️ The Chronicles of Leilon

### 1. Defense of [[Leilon]] & The Kraken Priest Raid (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*)
The Order's arrival in [[Leilon]] was greeted not with fanfare, but with the roar of crashing surf and screams from the muddy docks. The tiefling Kraken Priest **[[Nixoxious]]** and her fanatical cultist berserkers had stormed the town fishery from the sea, seizing local fishers as blood sacrifices to summon rampaging **Water Elementals** and **Water Weirds**. Amidst the chaos and rising tide, two ten-year-old village children and inseparable best friends—**Smithwell** (boy) and **Burnice** (girl)—vanished into the surrounding salt marshes.

### 2. Rescue & Adoption of the Znak Wards
Charging into the flooded fishery and marsh flats, the four champions shattered Nixoxious's dark summoning ritual, cut down the elemental horrors, and tracked **[[Smithwell and Burnice]]** deep into the reeds, rescuing both children unharmed. Tragedy lingered in the aftermath, however: both children's fisher parents had perished on the cultists' sacrificial altars. Refusing to leave the orphans to the mercy of the frontier, the four champions formally adopted Smithwell and Burnice into the company as brother and sister, bestowing upon them the surname **Znak** and raising them as the cherished wards of their new household.

### 3. Establishing the Order's Estate under Majordomo [[Leopold]]
With their growing responsibilities in [[Leilon]], the Order commissioned the construction of a fortified stone manor and compound to serve as their permanent headquarters. Structural engineering and masonry were coordinated alongside Lord Neverember's gruff dwarf master builder **[[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]** ("The Growler").

To oversee the household and care for [[Smithwell and Burnice]] while the champions rode out on perilous campaigns, the Order appointed the unflappable human majordomo **[[Leopold]]**. With quiet dignity and ironclad organization, Leopold managed the estate's supply manifests, armory upkeep, defensive patrols, and the daily academic and moral education of young Smithwell and Burnice.

### 4. Civic Life, The Shrine of Lathander & The Mystery of [[Aubrey Silverspun]]
As Leilon rose from the mud around the mysterious ruins of the **House of Thalivar**, the Order wove itself into the fabric of the town:
* **The Shrine of Lathander:** [[Myagkiy Znak]] formed a warm spiritual fellowship with halfling priestess **[[Merrygold Brightshine]]**, joining her dawn prayers to the Morninglord and helping feed [[Grizzelda Copperwraught]]'s exhausted laborers.
* **The Town Council & Tower:** Official garrison sergeant **[[Hazz Yorrum]]** proved lazy, venal, and cowardly, deferring nearly every civic crisis to the short-tempered Rashemi wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]**—who spent his days deciphering the arcane records of the rebuilt wizard's tower, unaware that the restless ghost of **[[Thalivar]]** was subtly possessing him each night.
* **Aubrey's Peculiarities:** Between expeditions, the four champions frequently browsed the cluttered aisles of **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]**'s curio shop, bargaining with the eccentric, old-fashioned merchant without realizing that no one else in town ever seemed to see him.

### 5. Hearth & Romance at [[The Wayside Inn]]
Patrolling the High Road south of Leilon, the Order forged a vital trade and mutual-defense pact with **[[Martisha Vinetalker]]**, the half-elf proprietor and former bandit captain of **[[The Wayside Inn]]**. Inside the bustling tavern kitchen, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** was immediately smitten by Martisha's fiercely independent half-orc head cook, **[[Cooragh|Cooragh Struckt]]**—a budding romance fueled in no small part by the Goliath's boundless devotion to her alarmingly spicy stews and signature fiery pork pies.

### 6. The Mere of Dead Men Truce & The [[Ballad of Qolzaz]]
When a Leilon guard patrol vanished along the misty borders of the **Mere of Dead Men**, the Order tracked the missing soldiers into the territory of a fiercely territorial lizardfolk clan. In the initial clash, the champions fought and slew the clan's formidable war leader, **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]**.

Before the feud could spiral into a war of extermination, a horde of necrotic **Rot Trolls** erupted from the swamp to devour lizardfolk and humans alike. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with the surviving marsh warriors, the Order helped annihilate the undead troll incursion. Moved by the outsiders' ferocity and honor, tribal sovereign **[[Grixxugroaz]]** ratified an enduring peace and alliance treaty with Leilon. In solemn tribute to their fallen adversary, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** had the likeness of **[[Matriarch Qolzaz]]** tattooed across his broad chest—a deed immortalized in verse as the ***[[Ballad of Qolzaz]]***—and Chieftain Grixxugroaz dispatched two young lizardfolk exchange warriors, **[[Graxxoamo]]** and **[[Shriss]]**, to live and train at the Order's estate in Leilon.
