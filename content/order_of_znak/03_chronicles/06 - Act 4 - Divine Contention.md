## 📜 Overview of the Final Act
As the twin armies of the storm high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** and the death-priest **[[Ularan Mortus]]** converged upon **[[Leilon]]**, **[[The Order of Znak]]** raced from the haunted canyons of the Sword Mountains to the high seas of the Sword Coast—defending their home from draconic and undead siege before sailing straight into the eye of a divine hurricane to board the flagship dreadnaught of **[[Talos]]**.

---

## ⚔️ The Final Campaign

### 1. The Perils of [[Dumathoin's Gulch]] & The Wereboar's Bite
Hastening into the Sword Mountains to recover a cache of Gnomengarde anti-siege munitions needed to defend [[Leilon]] against the Ebondeath-possessed green dragon, the Order walked into a ferocious ambush of feral **Wereboars** at the mouth of **[[Dumathoin's Gulch]]**. In the savage close-quarters melee, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** was knocked from his feet and bitten by a wereboar—contracting **wereboar lycanthropy**. Rather than shunning the affliction, the stoic Aarakocra monk harnessed the curse's feral brawn and hybrid tusks as an extension of his unyielding martial discipline.

Deeper in the canyon, when spectral **Sword Wraiths** swarmed the gulch, **[[Rhynok]]** cast ***Rope Trick***, climbed into the unseen extradimensional pocket above the battlefield, pulled up the rope, and rained poisoned arrows down from complete cover. Entering the dwarven shrine, Rhynok persuaded the sacred eidolon **[[Sister Silvergranite]]** to entrust them with the volatile **Gnomengarde grenade**, before the companions joined forces to destroy a lingering sword wraith and a terrifying undead **Boneclaw**.

### 2. Thalivar's Beacon & [[The Ruinstone]]
Riding hard back to [[Leilon]], the Order rushed straight to the top of the **House of Thalivar**. Months after the Order had put Thalivar's original haunting to rest in *Storm Lord's Wrath*, wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** had successfully rebuilt the planar beacon atop the spire—yet in secret, Gallio had been obsessively hunting for the mythical Netherese artifact known as **[[The Ruinstone]]**. The beacon's renewed activation overcharged dimensional rifts, unleashing a fresh outbreak of Far Realm **Star Spawn Manglers** that plagued the upper tower. The party was summoned not to investigate a ghost, but to help Gallio contain and control the rampaging aberrations before they overran Leilon.

In the frantic struggle atop the tower, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** discovered once more that the aberrations' mind-rending psychic claws were the one agony his Bear Totem rage could not dull—yet the Goliath gritted his teeth through the "brain pain" and cleaved the horrors apart alongside his brothers-in-arms. Securing **[[The Ruinstone]]** from the dimensional breach, the Order watched as a completely shell-shocked, terrified **[[Gallio Elibro]]** realized the catastrophic scope of his folly, resigned his post on the spot, and packed his bags for Neverwinter.

### 3. Voyage Aboard *[[Twinkle and Icingdeath]]* & The High Seas Fleet Battle
To secure regular army reinforcements before the cult armies closed the ring around Leilon, the Order embarked aboard the twin caravels ***[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]*** and ***Icingdeath***, commanded by the dashing Captain **[[Deloz Montiv]]**. Along the voyage, [[Rhynok]]'s sharp elven senses pierced the illusory human glamour projected by *Twinkle*'s enchanted figurehead—revealing that Deloz and his privateers were actually surface-allied **drow of Bregan D'aerthe** sailing in the service of Jarlaxle Baenre.

In the court of Neverwinter, when every other appeal to **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** faltered, the gruff, blunt-spoken **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** stepped forward as the unlikely diplomat of the company—winning over the Lord Protector and securing his pledge of full military aid for Leilon!

On the return voyage south, the ships first repelled an aerial boarding ambush of **Invisible Stalkers**, and then sighted the black sails of Talosite reaver **[[Galas Windrage]]**, commanding the warship *Throatcutter* and longship *Thunderstrike*. As the vessels traded roaring ballista and mangonel broadsides across the whitecaps, Rhynok sniped enemy officers from the rigging while Krageer—deciding two could play at the archer's game—set aside his greataxe and gleefully traded longbow volleys across the waves until the reaver fleet was sent to the bottom of the sea.

### 4. The Siege of [[Leilon]] & The Portrait of [[Aubrey Silverspun]]
When the combined fury of the cults broke against [[Leilon]]'s palisades, the possessed green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** descended upon the town in a cloud of chlorine gas, smashing **Aubrey's Peculiarities** into splinters. After the Order drove the wyrm from the skies and broke the siege, the four champions searched the rubble of their favorite curio shop—discovering that none of the townsfolk knew who "Aubrey" was, and uncovering an ancient, dust-covered oil painting of the historic **Swords of Leilon** that bore the unmistakable smiling face of **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]**, the spectral hero who had watched over them all along.

### 5. Into the Hurricane — The Dreadnaught Climax Against [[Fheralai Stormsworn]]
To end the tempest threat at its source, the Order called once more upon Captain **[[Deloz Montiv]]** and ***[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]***, steering straight into a howling, unnatural hurricane to hunt down the Talosite flagship dreadnaught.

![[talos.jpg]]
*Talos, God of Storms — The Apparition Above the Flagship Dreadnaught*

Beneath a colossal, lightning-wreathed apparition of the storm god **[[Talos]]** towering in the thunderheads above the sea, the four champions and Captain Deloz boarded the massive dreadnaught. Sweeping the storm-swept decks and lower holds in a climactic clash of steel and spell, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] (wielding his legendary greataxe *[[Ultima Exodus]]*), [[Rhynok]], [[Myagkiy Znak]], and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] vanquished high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an **Air Elemental Myrmidon**, and a summoned **Nalfeshnee demon**—shattering the storm fleet and cementing the legend of **[[The Order of Znak]]** across the Sword Coast forever.
