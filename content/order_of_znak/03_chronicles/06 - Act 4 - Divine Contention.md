## 📜 Overview of the Final Act
Commencing **one full year after the volcanic trials of [[05 - Interlude 2 - Keeping the Faith|Interlude 2: "Keeping the Faith"]]**, the relative peace of the Sword Coast is shattered as the twin cults of storm and death return with devastating fury. During the year of downtime, **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** returned from his political hiatus to heavily finance the fortification and rebuilding of [[Leilon]], while **[[The Order of Znak]]** operated at **Level 11**—fortified by Flanning Teldorer's divine feats, Myagkiy's 24 AC +2 Plate Armor, and **[[Rhynok]]** finally donning his newly delivered ***Cloak of Elvenkind***.

As the twin armies of the storm high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** and the death-priest **[[Ularan Mortus]]** converged upon **[[Leilon]]**, **[[The Order of Znak]]** raced from the haunted canyons of the Sword Mountains to the high seas of the Sword Coast—defending their home from draconic and undead siege before sailing straight into the eye of a divine hurricane to board the flagship dreadnaught of **[[Talos]]**.

## ⚔️ The Final Campaign

### 1. The Perils of [[Dumathoin's Gulch]] & The Wereboar's Bite

#### The Trail of Senseless Slaughter & The Chipmunk Witness (July 24, 2022)
Setting out into the crags of the Sword Mountains toward **[[Dumathoin's Gulch]]** to retrieve a cache of experimental Gnomengarde anti-siege munitions needed to defend [[Leilon]], the Order came upon a gruesome scene within five miles of the defile. The tranquil mountain woods were shattered by evidence of indiscriminate carnage: forest beasts—some as large as deer and others as small as mountain hares—lay butchered across the needle-strewn floor. 

**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** inspected the carcasses (rolling a **20 Investigation** check), observing that every animal had been cleanly cut down by sharp swords. None were eaten, harvested, or skinned; they had been slaughtered brutally and senselessly without cause. Scanning the canopy with razor-sharp elven senses, **[[Rhynok]]** spotted a lone survivor (rolling a **28 Perception** check): a terrified chipmunk trembling upon a high branch above the gore. Extending a morsel of travel rations, Rhynok cast ***Animal Friendship*** (DC 13 Wisdom save). Soothed by the ranger's magic, the chipmunk chattered frantically, revealing that several skeletal creatures wielding gleaming blades had butchered the animals before marching directly toward the gulch.

#### Bindlemint's Warning & The Wereboar Ambush (July 24, 2022)
Moments later, the party heard a sudden, thrashing commotion in the woods as something hurried toward them. Gnome inventor **Bindlemint Mincemower** burst through the thick brambles. Her clothes were torn, her face was bloodied by cuts, and she was terrified to the point of exhaustion. She had only enough energy to gasp a single panicked word—*"Beasts!"*—before collapsing unconscious at their boots.

Crashing through the pines right behind her came five snarling **Wereboars**, hunting the fleeing gnome. Rolling high initiative (`Tvyordiy 25, Rhynok 25, Myagkiy 18, Krageer 16`), Rhynok marked a target with *Hunter's Mark* and unleashed a deadly *Dread Ambusher* longbow barrage (`30 to hit`), while Krageer charged into the fray with his great axe. In the furious close-quarters melee, a hulking wereboar slammed its tusks directly into **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** (`21 attack roll`). Knocked prone (failing his DC 12 Strength save with a `5`) and failing his Constitution saving throw (`10`), the Aarakocra monk was infected with **wereboar lycanthropy**. Rather than despairing or seeking immediate magical cleansing, the stoic Way of the Drunken Master monk resolved to harmonize the beast's feral brawn and hybrid tusks with his monastic ki discipline.

Once the lycanthropes were slain, [[Myagkiy Znak]] tended to Bindlemint with a 2nd-level *Cure Wounds* (`13 HP`). Her injuries were mostly superficial cuts and exhaustion. Once revived, she explained that she had been racing toward Leilon when the wereboars caught her scent. She carried urgent, dire news: Dumathoin's Gulch had been ambushed earlier that morning by horrific creatures that *"stank of death."* Bindlemint suspected the monsters were hunting for the experimental **Gnomengarde grenade**, but she hadn't lingered to ascertain their full intent—fleeing in sheer terror and presuming her two gnomish colleagues were already dead.

#### Descent into Dumathoin's Gulch: Fibblestip & Dabbledop (July 24–26, 2022)
Leaving Bindlemint safely behind the lines, the companions advanced into the rocky gorge:
> *A fast-moving stream crashes down the gulch. Tiers of rock ascend some seventy feet to the clifftop above, and two stone pillars, fifty feet above the gulch floor, are connected by a stone arch that spans the middle of the gulch. A crumpled gnome figure rests in the center of the arch.*
>
> *Near the rear of the gulch, a large glowing purple moonstone, carved into the form of a dwarf, stands on a rocky ledge. Glowing purple runes, etched into the ground and walls of the gulch, encircle the moonstone. Another gnomish figure rests, unmoving, with his back against the moonstone. Bones, bits of armor, and weapons are scattered around him.*

The two figures were none other than their old eccentric inventor friends from Gnomengarde: **Fibblestip** lay unconscious fifty feet overhead on the high stone arch, while **Dabbledop** was paralyzed with terror against the carved moonstone dwarf at the rear ledge.

#### The Specters of the Defile & The Extradimensional Perch (July 26, 2022)
As the Order moved to reach the tinkerers, the chill of undeath gripped the canyon. A vanguard of spectral warriors—a **Sword Wraith Commander** and several **Sword Wraith Warriors**—manifested from the mist, drawing phantom greatswords. Bolstered by supernatural *Turning Defiance*, the wraiths held firm against Myagkiy's divine rebuke.

Seeing the choke point below, [[Rhynok]] cast ***Rope Trick***, anchoring a length of rope into an invisible extradimensional pocket twenty feet above the canyon floor. Climbing up and hauling the rope inside, Rhynok gained total immune cover—peering through the unseen window to rain deadly poisoned shafts into the wraith ranks with complete impunity. Down on the rocks, Krageer cleaved through spectral plate with devastating great axe strikes (`31 crit`), while Tvyordiy cracked ghostly forms with his *Vicious Quarterstaff +2*. 

Suddenly, the dwarven shrine reacted: the massive **Sacred Statue** of Dumathoin awakened, animated by the sacred eidolon of **[[Sister Silvergranite]]** (`Init 11`). Its towering stone fists pulverized the undead with colossal slams (`42 to 44 bludgeoning damage`).

#### The Boneclaw Ambush, The 6th-Level Grace, & Favora's Platoon (July 27, 2022)
With the initial wraiths crushed, Myagkiy recited ancient dwarven rites (rolling a **23 Religion** check) while Rhynok called out with respectful diplomacy (rolling a **20 Persuasion** check). Recognizing the Order's reverence for Dumathoin and their desperate quest to defend Leilon, Sister Silvergranite held her massive fists and agreed to yield the gnomish ordnance.

Before the covenant could be settled, the shadows at the canyon's edge twisted violently as a terrifying, nine-foot-tall **Boneclaw** ambushed the party (`Init: Boneclaw 19, Eidolon 16`). Lunging with elongated, scythe-like talons (`ATTACK: 27`), it impaled Krageer, dragging the raging Goliath ten feet into its clutches (`27 piercing damage`) and pinning him tight. 

With Krageer enduring agonizing puncture wounds, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** vaulted into the fray, driving his quarterstaff into the Boneclaw's spine with a fight-turning **Stunning Strike**. The blow completely incapacitated the monstrosity, instantly forcing its claws open and ending the grapple!

To keep their frontline juggernaut from collapsing, [[Myagkiy Znak]] unleashed one of his highest divine invocations: a **6th-level *Heal*** spell, channeling a brilliant surge of pure vitality that restored **78 hit points** to Krageer on the spot. Reinvigorated, Krageer swung with reckless abandon alongside Rhynok's sniper fire and Sister Silvergranite's stone fists, smashing the Boneclaw into splinters.

Just as the fiend crumbled to dust, a concealed side passage opened and an armed **platoon of gnomes** came charging out into the gulch, ready to defend their kin! At their head was **Favora Corehoarder**, a brave cleric of Garl Glittergold renowned for putting herself in danger to save others. Though breathless and ready for war, Favora and her platoon stopped in awe, seeing that the Order of Znak had already wiped out every horror in the defile. With great joy, Favora and her squad tended to the unconscious Fibblestip and petrified Dabbledop, safely bundling the relieved inventors together ("gnomes in a box").

#### Munitions Secured & The Looming Shadow (July 28, 2022)
From the grateful Gnomengarde inventors and Sister Silvergranite's vault, the Order secured a game-changing arsenal for the defense of Leilon:
- **The Gnomengarde Grenade:** Entrusted to Rhynok, this volatile prototype was packed with unpredictable wild magic that triggered fire bursts (`28 damage`), thunder waves (`28 damage`), and three simultaneous *Wand of Wonder* surges (*slow* and twin *lightning bolts*) upon detonation.
- **The Dragon Slayer Longsword:** Awarded to Krageer, a magic `+1 longsword` specifically forged to pierce dragon scales (`+3d6` slashing against dragons).
- **Rhynok's Poisoner's Belt:** Fitted with stitched tactical quick-release slots holding four poison vials in accordance with Tasha's combat rules.

Returning to Leilon to refit, the champions stopped by **Aubrey's Peculiarities Shoppe**, purchasing specialized toolkits from the smiling proprietor **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]** (`50 gp`). At the local tavern, innkeeper Alion pulled the companions aside to share chilling rumors:
- Folk had spotted the ancient green dragon **Old Gnawbone** ([Claugiyliamatar](file:///E:/obsidian/02-Hobbies/tabletop/campaigns/03_archived/order_of_znak/03_chronicles/06%20-%20Act%204%20-%20Divine%20Contention.md#L48)) flying low over the lowlands far outside her normal domain in Kryptgarden Forest—a sure omen of disaster.
- Fish and wildlife were dying inside the Mere of Dead Men, poisoned by blighted waters slowly spreading toward Leilon.

Preparing for the coming storm, Rhynok cast *Locate Animals or Plants* and harvested venom from the surrounding marshlands, brewing fresh batches of Giant Snake poison (`3d6`) into his newly donned belt. The war for the Sword Coast was underway.

### 2. Thalivar's Beacon, Ultima Exodus, & [[The Ruinstone]]

#### The Reconstructed Garrison at Leilon (July 31, 2022)
Returning from Dumathoin's Gulch, the Order arrived at the newly transformed centerpiece of Leilon:
> *The House of Thalivar is a tall tower at the heart of the town. Recently rebuilt by soldiers, the structure now serves as a garrison for Neverwinter’s soldiery. Ravens circle the tower’s pointed roof and warm lantern light shines from its windows. At its foot, two arched doorways open into the interior.*

Where once stood a haunted, crumbling ruin during the trials of *Storm Lord's Wrath*, the spire was now bustling with garrisoned troops. Yet deep within the high laboratory, the Order discovered wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** engaged in reckless arcane experimentation. With Leilon facing an imminent siege from the Cult of Talos and the Cult of Myrkul, Gallio explained that he had successfully rebuilt Thalivar's planar beacon. His stated ambition was to tune the beacon to trap extraplanar creatures and magically bind them into town service as defensive guardians—but he needed seasoned champions to journey into the **Border Ethereal** with him to trap more specimens.

#### Striking the Silver Bell & The Astral Spire (July 31, 2022)
Gallio gathered the party around the planar apparatus to cast *Etherealness*:
> *As the silver bell is struck, the world fades away. Grey mist swirls into view and the temperature drops to a soul-chilling cold. Your companions and the tower you just stood in remain visible, but appear translucent and oddly distant. In this strange world, a beam of blue light spills from the planar beacon and sweeps around it in a circle, like a spectral lighthouse. Most mysterious of all, a spiral staircase rises from an archway in the wall that wasn’t present before.*

Gallio immediately cast *Greater Invisibility* upon himself and slipped up the ethereal spiral staircase toward the astral spire. Unknown to the companions, Gallio had not merely sought defensive guardians; he was obsessively pursuing the mythical Netherese artifact known as **[[The Ruinstone]]**, hidden at the pinnacle of the dimensional apparatus!

As sinister shapes began congealing in the mists, catastrophe struck:
> *Gallio screams from the top of the astral spire. A shockwave washes through the mists, and the spectral light of the planar beacon flickers and dies. Looking back into your own world, you see the trapped monsters awaken and break free from their cells!*

#### Battle Across Two Planes & The Christening of Ultima Exodus (July 31 – August 1, 2022)
The violent rupture sheared reality across the planar veil, dividing the Order into a frantic two-front struggle:
- **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** were pinned in the laboratory on the **Material Plane**, surrounded by rampaging aberrations.
- **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** and **[[Rhynok]]** were stranded across the threshold in the **Ethereal Plane**!

Rolling into combat (`Init: Krageer 19, TZ 18, Rhynok 18, Gallio 17, MZ 14`), four horrifying **Star Spawn Manglers** swarmed the laboratory. The multi-limbed horrors unleashed savage *Flurry of Claws* assaults, making six claw attacks per turn that dealt vicious slashing and psychic damage (`27 attack roll, 26 damage`). While Bear Totem rage blunted physical wounds, the psychic rending bypassed Krageer's defenses entirely. Gritting his teeth through the excruciating "brain pain", the Goliath leaned on *Stone's Endurance* (`9`) and *Second Wind* (`10`) to remain upright.

During *Keeping the Faith*, Krageer had forged a massive, custom two-handed greataxe layered in adamantine and silver, previously recorded on his battle sheet simply as *"Krageer's Great Axe"*. In this desperate struggle atop Thalivar's spire, the weapon reached its legendary maturity. Honoring the 1983 classic RPG *Ultima III: Exodus*—a beloved inside joke between Gary and Peter—Krageer officially christened the devastating blade **[[Ultima Exodus]]**! Wielding the molten-inscribed adamantine greataxe, Krageer cleaved through aberration chitin with pulverizing critical force (`31 to hit, 28 slashing`).

Meanwhile, Rhynok trapped manglers with well-placed *Net* throws and slashed with his *Scimitar of Speed* coated in potent DC 14 poison, Tvyordiy hammered joints with his *Vicious Quarterstaff +2* and stunning flurries, and Myagkiy channeled *Mass Healing Word* and high-tier *Cure Wounds* across the planar divide until all four Far Realm horrors were obliterated.

#### The Warning of The Swords of Leilon & Fortifying the Spire (August 1, 2022)
With the beacon powered down and the Order reunited on the Material Plane, the dimensional mists parted:
> *A pair of ghostly figures emerge from the mists: a dark-skinned warrior woman dressed in chainmail, and a red-bearded dwarf carrying a lute. The woman holds up her hand in a sign of peace and speaks in an echoing voice:*
> *"This is what happens to those who meddle with the ruinstone. Leave it here, lest the people of Leilon suffer its wrath once more!"*

The spirits introduced themselves as heroes of the historic adventuring company, **The Swords of Leilon**: the warrior **Augusta Krul** and the dwarven skald **Ogri Ninebeards**. They revealed that centuries ago, the wizard Thalivar had touched the Ruinstone, breaking his beacon and unleashing trapped monstrosities—and the Swords of Leilon had fought and died holding them back. They warned that evil powers had recently learned of the Ruinstone and were actively conspiring to seize it.

Heeding their warning, the Order secured **[[The Ruinstone]]**—a palm-sized, fiery red crystal capable of rewinding actions at the terrifying cost of reality-erasing backlashes. Augusta Krul and Ogri Ninebeards pledged to remain as vigilant spectral guardians in the spire. The garrison was immediately reinforced: town guards were doubled, and allied **Lizardfolk** warriors were stationed within the tower walls to guard the relic.

Thoroughly terrified and disgraced by the catastrophe his vanity had unleashed, **[[Gallio Elibro]]** and the Order of the Many-Starred Cloaks packed their bags, resigned their commission in Leilon, and fled for Neverwinter. From the tower sanctum, Krageer claimed the **Amulet of the Consuming Summoner**, adding a powerful summoning relic to his arsenal as Leilon prepared for the coming war.

### 3. Privateers of Bregan D'aerthe & Audience at Castle Never

#### The Twin Caravels & The Grinning Ranger (August 2–3, 2022)
Needing to rally regular army reinforcements from Neverwinter before the cult armies closed the ring around Leilon, the Order followed tavern rumors from innkeeper Alion regarding two mysterious, opulent pleasure vessels anchored offshore whose crews only ventured out at night:
> *Two magnificent ships are anchored just beyond the marshy shore. Both have long strips of decorative cloth stretched from the tops of their masts down to the railings around their decks. The smaller of the ships is decorated with blue, pink, and purple fabric, and has a massive figurehead shaped like a charging unicorn with a prominent gold horn. A name on the side of this ship reads Twinkle.*
>
> *The larger ship is adorned with white and gold fabric, and its figurehead looks like a lavender-eyed drow leaping forward to attack with twin scimitars. An enormous roaring panther head is attached the aft of this ship. The name on its side reads Icingdeath.*

Boarding the luxurious vessels, the companions discovered remarkable naval enchantments. Near the front of *Icingdeath*'s main deck stood an 8-foot-tall, 4,000-pound stone statue of the legendary drow ranger Drizzt Do'Urden posed with hands on his scimitars and an open, goofy grin. Uttering the command word **`"Artemis"`** animated the statue to move across the deck or fire a crushing, pressurized stream of water from its mouth. Furthermore, a hidden switch on the ship's wheel engaged an aft pump system capable of deploying a wide slick of flammable oil to immolate pursuing vessels.

#### Disguises, Friendly Sparring, & The Poisoned Bolt (August 4, 2022)
The ships were commanded by two starkly contrasting figures:
- **Captain Deloz Montiv (*Twinkle*):** Disguised under an illusory glamour as a quiet, calculating male Rashemi weapon merchant. Observant and thorough, Deloz constantly conducted surprise inspections of his crew and checked in on passengers to ensure they were comfortable—and staying out of trouble.
- **Captain Xalti (*Icingdeath*):** Disguised as an impulsive, confident male Calishite iron merchant who loved showing off his magic and wagering on almost any event.

What began as a friendly exhibition match of unarmed combat between **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** soon became an impromptu sparring duel between Tvyordiy and Captain Deloz (operating with the martial prowess of a Drow Elite Warrior). As fists and parries blurred across *Twinkle*'s deck, Deloz tested the monk with a point-blank hand crossbow bolt coated in sleep-inducing drow poison (`DC 13 Constitution save`). Digging deep into his monastic vitality, Tvyordiy shrugged off the soporific venom with a stout **16 Constitution saving throw**.

Watching the duel from the quarterdeck, **[[Rhynok]]** examined the coated bolt (rolling a **Nature check**). Combining his knowledge of Underdark toxins and elven biology, the Gloom Stalker recognized the unmistakable sheen of drow sleeping venom, instantly seeing through the crew's magical glamours. Confronted with the truth, Deloz and Xalti dropped their human personas, revealing themselves as surface-allied **drow of Bregan D'aerthe**!

The captains admitted their charter: they operated under the direct command of their eccentric patrons in Luskan—**Jarlaxle Baenre** ("One-Eyed Jax") and legendary psionicist **Kimmuriel Oblodra** ("Kim"). Satisfied that the Order were heroes rather than enemies of Bregan D'aerthe, Deloz agreed to ferry the party north to Neverwinter. Meanwhile, the disgraced wizard **[[Gallio Elibro]]** slipped aboard Xalti's *Icingdeath* to flee north toward the Cloaktower.

#### Audience at Castle Never: The Hotenow Debt & Waived Levy (August 4–9, 2022)
Arriving in the jewel of the north, Captain Deloz escorted the Order through the high corridors of Castle Never:
> *You stand in the great marble hall of Castle Never, on a red carpet that leads up to a throne. Atop the chair sits a serious man in shining plate armor and blue cloak with gold trim. Lord Dagult Neverember strokes his short, gray beard as he listens to the pleas of the captains to secure the High Road and send troops to Leilon. When they finish, the lord turns toward you.*
>
> *"Each of you make your case. Why should I send my troops to Leilon? By all accounts it should be able to defend itself now. I’ve already poured many resources into the town and it has yet to pay anything back."*

Under royal protocol, failing to persuade the crown (DC 13 checks) meant Neverember would refuse aid unless the party paid an exorbitant **500 gp protection fee**. 

The opening appeals went disastrously wrong:
- Bristling at Neverember's transactional arrogance, **[[Rhynok]]** lashed out with blistering rhetoric—lambasting the Lord Protector for disappearing on a convenient political hiatus while Queen Helve Grall and her fire giant legions inside Mount Hotenow were **preparing to march upon Neverwinter itself** in *Keeping the Faith*! While accurate, his confrontational delivery grated upon the proud lord (**`Rhynok: 3 Intimidation`**).
- **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** backed Rhynok, loudly reminding the court that the Order had bled inside the magma chambers to slay the **Fire Giant Queen** and save the capital when the crown was absent, but his bombastic tavern swagger fell flat before the noble court (**`Krageer: 0 Performance`**).
- **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** attempted to invoke historical precedent regarding frontier defense pacts, but fumbled the dates (**`Tvyordiy: 1 History`**).

With the court turning icy and the 500 gp levy looming, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** stepped to the fore. Radiating calm, divine authority, the cleric delivered a magnificent sermon on civic duty, the sacred covenant between a lord and his people, and the impending doom facing the Sword Coast (**`Myagkiy: 20 Persuasion`** — **SUCCESS!**).

Moved by Myagkiy's moral clarity, the court softened, granting the companions an inspired opportunity to rally:
- Krageer fixed Neverember with imposing Goliath presence, bluntly warning him that if Leilon falls, Neverwinter's southern gate is blown wide open to the storm and death cults (**`Krageer: 19 Intimidation`** — **SUCCESS!**).
- Tvyordiy recovered his composure, delivering an eloquent and compelling historical allegory of coastal defenses holding against impossible odds (**`Tvyordiy: 18 History`** — **SUCCESS!**).
- Krageer closed the appeal with unforgettable, plain-spoken Goliath sincerity (**`Krageer: 18 Persuasion`** — **SUCCESS!**).

Confronted with the undeniable debt he owed the champions who had saved Neverwinter from the Fire Giant Queen, Lord Neverember dropped all courtly pretenses. He **waived the 500 gp fee entirely**, decreeing that Neverwinter soldiers would patrol and defend the High Road **at no cost to Leilon**!

### 4. High Seas Fleet Combat & The Fall of Ship Rethnor (August 10 – August 24, 2022)

#### The Hurricane Squall & All Hands on Deck (August 10, 2022)
Setting sail south from Neverwinter aboard *Twinkle*, accompanied by *Icingdeath*, the Sea of Swords was suddenly consumed by an unnatural tempest:
> *Lightning and thunder suddenly crack a dark sky as heavy rain pours from above. Cries of, "Hurricane! All hands on deck!" can be heard throughout the vessel. The sailors need all the help they can get.*

To keep the ship afloat amidst churning forty-foot swells, the Order manned critical crisis stations (DC 15 ability checks):
- **Securing Cargo & Rigging:** **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** charged across the pitching deck, rolling a towering **29 Athletics check** (`29 | 29 with advantage`) to single-handedly lash down shifting cargo crates and secure snapping stays before they crushed the crew.
- **Navigating the Churning Seas:** **[[Rhynok]]** took the helm station with navigator's tools. **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** cast *Guidance* (+3), but blinding rain and gale-force spray obscured the stars and landmarks, throwing off the course calculation (`Rhynok: 7 & 9 Intelligence + 3 Guidance = 10-12, failing DC 15`).
- **Steering & Crew Morale:** **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** used his monastic agility to wrestle the bucking ship's wheel through the trough of the waves, while Myagkiy roared impassioned clerical benedictions to steady the panicked sailors.

Having succeeded on more than half the crisis stations, the Order brought *Twinkle* through the squall with minimal damage—absorbing only **22 bludgeoning damage** (halved from the full 44 points)!

#### The Invisible Stalker Assassination Plot (August 10, 2022)
In the squall's swirling aftermath, Talosite reaver lord **[[Galas Windrage]]** dispatched four summoned **Invisible Stalkers** in a cowardly decapitation strike aimed at assassinating Captain Deloz on *Twinkle* and Captain Xalti on *Icingdeath*.

Yet dispatching invisible assassins against drow proved an utter tactical folly:
- Surface-allied drow possess innate ***Faerie Fire***, which immediately limned the unseen elementals in glittering, iridescent violet outlines the moment they set foot upon the deck.
- Aboard *Icingdeath*, Captain Xalti—an accomplished Drow Mage—countered the elementals with sweeping arcane defenses.
- Aboard *Twinkle*, Captain Deloz shouted the alarm as the deck boards groaned under unseen weight (`Init: Stalkers 24, Rhynok 19, Myagkiy 19, TZ 12, Krageer 12`).

Rallying to defend the helm, Rhynok tracked the flickering distortions, slicing through the air elementals with his venom-coated *Scimitar of Speed* (`critical sneak attacks`). Krageer swept the deck with **[[Ultima Exodus]]**, delivering pulverizing cleaves (`31 to hit, 23 damage`) that shredded the stalkers back into harmless sea mist.

#### Ship Rethnor Revealed & "Direct the Siege Weapons!" (August 10, 2022)
As the last elemental fell, the unnatural squall abruptly vanished, revealing the true ambush:
> *The sky becomes clear and sunny as the rain begins to clear, revealing two nearby ships. The smaller vessel, a longship, rows quickly toward you, its crew chanting in prayer. The larger vessel, a warship armed with ballista and catapults, has a large crow painted on its sails. The officers of your vessels cry, "Ship Rethnor! Battle stations!" The ship captain looks at you and says, "Direct the siege weapons!"*

Rolling into naval fleet initiative:
- ***Twinkle:*** `13.06`
- **Warship (*Throatcutter*):** `9.04`
- **Longship (*Thunderstrike*):** `7.06`

#### Siege Engines, Flaming Oil, & The Necklace of Fireballs (August 10–23, 2022)
Operating under naval warfare rules, the 20 drow sailors on each vessel dedicated their full combat actions to steering, aiming, loading, and firing *Twinkle*'s heavy mangonels and ballistas. 

Directing the battery, the Order unleashed devastating tactical maneuvers:
- **Tvyordiy's Fiery Artillery:** Remembering the **Necklace of Fireballs** recovered from the Dragon Barrow during *Dragon of Icespire Peak*, Tvyordiy hurled glowing explosive beads across the water into the densely packed crews of *Thunderstrike* and *Throatcutter*. The soft cultist sailors (11 HP each) were engulfed in rolling detonations, failing their Dexterity saves and burning across the rowing benches.
- **Naval Countermeasures:** Captain Deloz pressed the hidden wheel trigger to release *Twinkle*'s aft pump, igniting a roaring sheet of flaming oil across the wake that scorched pursuing hulls. Meanwhile, the crew spoke the command word **`"Artemis"`**, activating the 8-foot stone Drizzt statue to blast approaching grappling lines with pressurized torrents of water!

#### Closing the Breach: Small-Arms Cross-Deck Fire (August 23–24, 2022)
As the burning hulls closed to boarding range within thirty yards, heavy siege engines could no longer safely depress their firing angles without shattering friendly rigging. Both sides ceased mangonel bombardment and erupted into a ferocious cross-deck exchange of small-arms fire across the churning gap!

The reavers retaliated from their fighting tops with volleys of light crossbow bolts and thrown daggers. From the swaying rigging of *Twinkle*, **[[Rhynok]]** sniped through the drifting gunsmoke with lethal, poison-tipped longbow arrows (`30, 31 to hit, Sneak Attacks`). Down at the gunwales, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** set aside his axe and joyfully traded longbow volleys across the waves (`25, 20, 23 to hit, crits`), bellowing tavern insults while Bregan D'aerthe's privateers answered with disciplined volleys of poisoned hand crossbow bolts.

#### Boarding the Throatcutter & Wrestling the Six Officers (August 24, 2022)
With the longship *Thunderstrike* crippled and dead in the water, *Twinkle* and *Icingdeath* slammed grappling hooks into the gunwales of the flagship warship *Throatcutter*.

Vaulting across the bloodstained gap into close-quarters melee, Krageer and Tvyordiy were confronted by the warship's six senior officers—the **Captain**, **First Mate**, **Bosun**, **Quartermaster**, **Surgeon**, and **Cook** (each fighting with Bandit Captain stat blocks). Entering a thunderous Bear Totem rage, Krageer engaged in brutal close-quarters grappling, seizing officer after officer with overwhelming Goliath brawn (`18, 17 Athletics checks`). Tvyordiy hammered the officers with stunning flurries of blows while Rhynok picked off reinforcing bandits from the shrouds, systematically breaking the reavers' command structure until the ship surrendered!

#### The Interrogation & Fheralai's Plot (August 24, 2022)
With *Thunderstrike* sinking into the whitecaps and *Throatcutter* captured, Krageer dragged the surviving reaver officers before the shattered mast. Towering over the bruised captives with his bloodstained greataxe **[[Ultima Exodus]]**, Krageer roared a fearsome interrogation:
- Rolling an **18 Intimidation check** (`11 | 18 (+2)`), Krageer completely shattered the DC 15 threshold!

Terrified of being cleaved in two and fed to the sharks, the broken officers confessed everything: the naval ambush had been personally commanded by the storm high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** and the **Cult of Talos**. The cult had forged an alliance with pirate fleet lords of Luskan to blockade the sea lanes and isolate Leilon from Neverwinter reinforcements—clearing the path for their combined overland armies to march upon the town for the final siege.

#### Scuttling the Prize & A Gift to Bregan D'aerthe (August 24, 2022)
With the interrogation complete and the reaver officers disarmed in the hold, the companions faced the tactical question of what to do with the battered prize. Rather than burdening their return voyage by towing a heavily damaged Rethnor warship back to Leilon's shallow quayside, the Order graciously turned the captured vessel over to Captain **[[Deloz Montiv]]**, Captain Xalti, and **Bregan D'aerthe** as a gift. 

The drow privateers efficiently stripped the warship of its heavy ballistas, mangonels, and munitions before scuttling the hull into the depths of the Sea of Swords. This gesture of maritime goodwill cemented an ironclad bond between the Order of Znak and Jarlaxle Baenre's mercenary syndicate as trusted sea-brothers!

With the sea lanes secured and the privateers victorious, the Order set sail at full canvas for Leilon to prepare the town for total war.

### 5. The Siege of [[Leilon]] & The Battle of the Three Armies (August 25 – September 10, 2022)

Following the naval triumph over Ship Rethnor, the Order returned to a frontier settlement on high alert. Rather than triggering the climactic siege after only two preparatory quests as suggested in the standard campaign timeline, the party had run the full trilogy gauntlet—securing Gnomengarde's aid at Dumathoin's Gulch, establishing the ethereal beacon atop the Spire of Thalivar, and winning Lord Neverember's naval and military pact at Castle Never. Every scrap of preparation was desperately needed, for two massive invading armies were now converging on Leilon in a catastrophic pincer assault:
- **The Cult of Talos:** Four hundred strong under storm high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, comprised of bloodthirsty berserkers, thunder priests, anchorites, elite blackguards, and half-blue dragon gladiators descending from the northern crags.
- **The Cult of Myrkul:** Eight hundred rotting undead surging out of the southern Mere of Dead Men under dread war priest **[[Ularan Mortus]]**, bolstered by ghasts, ghouls, boneclaws, greater zombies, and their ultimate terror weapon: the ancient green dragon **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** (Old Gnawbone), possessed by the death wraith **[[Ebondeath]]** (the dread "Ebonclaw").
- **Leilon's Defenders:** Roughly two hundred defenders—local commoner militias, Leilon town guards, seasoned veterans, and a stalwart detachment of Neverwinter guards, mounted knights, and scouts secured by the Order's diplomacy at Castle Never.

---

#### Event 1: Dawn of the Dead at the South Gate (August 25–26, 2022)
An hour before dawn amidst torrential downpour, the peace of the frontier was shattered:
> *The night is dark, and torrential rain beats down on the rooftops. An hour before dawn, the bell on the newly erected south gate begins to toll urgently. A cry goes up from the wall as troops dash to man their positions:*
> *"The dead! The dead are coming!"*

Rushing south through the muddy streets, the companions arrived just as the wooden palisade buckled:
> *The din of battle echoes from the palisade as you approach. Through the rain, you see allied troops using spears and bows to fend off a horde of ghouls swarming from the darkness. All along the ramparts, burning torches struggle to provide illumination for your forces. As you watch, these pools of light are extinguished one by one as the dead swarm over the walls!*

##### Holding the Breach (The 5-Round Clock)
Under standard siege conditions, defenders had to survive eight grueling rounds against the endless dead. However, because the Order had secured elite Neverwinter military reinforcements from Lord Neverember, the required survival threshold was reduced to **5 rounds**!
- **The Swarm Mechanics:** Twelve **Ghasts** immediately surged through the muck, with two additional Ghasts clawing over the timber palisade every subsequent round.
- **Defensive Deployments:** Militia commoners and guards manned timber barricades, seasoned Veterans and mounted Knights reinforced the flanks, while sharpshooting Scouts rained arrows down into the bog from the twin gate towers.

##### The Order's Tactical Formation
Plugging the collapsed gateway, the companions formed an unbreakable bulwark:
- **[[Rhynok]]:** Anchored high in the rear sniper line, coating his *Longbow +2* arrows in serpent venom and casting ***Fear*** to rout advancing ghast packs while picking off rotting champions with devastating sneak attacks.
- **[[Myagkiy Znak]]:** Planted firmly in the center forward of the open portal, invoking a radiant, swirling maelstrom of ***Spirit Guardians*** that shredded advancing ghouls and zombies into holy dust the moment they crossed the threshold.
- **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]:** Wading fifteen feet out into the bloody mire before the gate, Krageer entered a thunderous Bear Totem rage, cleaving rotting corpses with massive arcs of **[[Ultima Exodus]]**. Activating his newly claimed ***Amulet of the Consuming Summoner*** (looted from the spire in Chapter 2), Krageer summoned a towering **Minotaur** (`Gore +6, 10 piercing`) that charged into the swamp alongside him, goring and trampling the undead ranks into mulch.
- **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]:** Guarding the inner gate threshold, Tvyordiy's staff flurries snapped ghast spines in mid-leap, leaving piles of broken undead corpses shattered at his talons.

After five harrowing rounds of bone-crunching combat, the vanguard of Myrkul broke against the South Gate!

---

#### Event 2: The Storm Breaks & The Tactical Choice (August 26, 2022)
As the last ghast fell at the gateway, a terrified townsfolk splashed through the mud toward the companions:
> *A man stumbles through the mud, his eyes wide with terror. You recognize him as Jack Torver, the owner of Leilon’s general store.*
> *“Barbarians at the north gate!” he gasps. “The Storm Lord’s berserkers! I was on my way to warn you when I saw the dead wading out of the marsh. They’re attacking the quayside right now!”*

The party faced a critical tactical dilemma:
- **North Gate (Event 3: Against the Lightning):** Talos anchorites and storm berserkers battering the northern gates.
- **The Quayside (Event 4: Back to the Mud):** An amphibious undead force marching silently out of the marsh to butcher the docks.

Knowing the vulnerable civilian fishing fleet and waterfront warehouses were vital to Leilon's survival, the Order made the tactical call: **Sprint for the Quayside!**

---

#### Event 4: Back to the Mud — The Quayside Ambush & Saving Valdi Estapaar (August 26, 2022)
Slogging westward through the driving squall, the companions arrived at the docks to witness a scene of horror:
> *Screams echo across the quayside as zombies emerge from the mire! Defenders dash to repel the undead, but they seem unprepared for the surprise attack. As you get closer, you see a large undead figure with long fingers squatting over an upturned rowboat. A woman squirms in the mud underneath, trapped by the weight of the boat. You recognize her as Valdi Estapaar, the fisherwoman who sits on the town council!*

##### Freeing the Councilor & Crushing the Boneclaw
Ularan Mortus had marched an entire phalanx of waterlogged zombies underwater through the marsh shallows to circumvent the landward palisades:
- **The Rowboat Trap:** A horrific **Boneclaw** had cornered town councilor and veteran fisherwoman **Valdi Estapaar**, pinning her helpless beneath an overturned fishing hull. Freeing her required an action from characters with a combined Strength of **15 or higher** (or **25 or higher** if the Boneclaw was perched on top). Krageer's colossal Goliath brawn simply hefted the timber hull aside with ease.
- **The Waterfront Battle:**
  - Rhynok unloosed precision sniper shots from the piers (`13 + Crit: 7 Piercing, 9 + Crit: 3 Sneak Attack`), punching venom-soaked broadheads through the Boneclaw's ribcage.
  - The Boneclaw struck back with extended *Piercing Claws* (`24 & 15 Piercing`), attempting to drag Krageer across the slick boards.
  - Free from the mud, Councilor Valdi drew her veteran longsword and joined the fray (`20 to hit, 9 slashing`), stabbing the monstrosity alongside the Order.
  - Together, Krageer's greataxe and the party's relentless focus fire shattered the Boneclaw's spine into kindling, while the summoned Minotaur trampled the crawling marsh zombies.

The docks were secured and surviving fishermen rallied around the Order. But in the lull of the water, distant iron bells began tolling frantically from the opposite side of town: **the barbarians of Talos had smashed through the North Gate!**

---

#### Event 6: Rolling Thunder (September 2022)
With the quayside held, fresh runners brought word of catastrophe in the heart of town:
> *Word reaches you that the enemy has breached the town square and the defenders are in dire need of aid. As you prepare to respond, you hear war drums beating from just outside town. Suddenly a bolt of lightning blasts a nearby group of soldiers! The drums reach another crescendo, and a second bolt crackles from the clouds to strike the town’s defenders!*

The companions faced another grueling choice:
1. Hunt down the savage drummers outside town (**Event 8: Thunder Drums**) who were magically calling down lightning strikes onto Leilon's militia.
2. Or charge directly into the town center where civilians and the garrison were being massacred.

With their summoned **Minotaur** still lumbering alongside them, the Order chose to charge directly into the **Town Square**!

---

#### Event 7: Enter the Dragon — The Shadow Wyrm in the Town Square (September 5–10, 2022)
Charging into the central plaza, the companions were plunged into complete pandemonium:
> *Chaos reigns in the town square. On one side, Leilon’s defenders make a valiant stand against the invaders, while on the other, fleeing townsfolk clog the thoroughfare leading out of town. Screams of panic suddenly echo through the crowd. Looking up, you see a huge green dragon swoop out of the storm!*

##### An Unholy Apocalypse
The town square was a slaughterhouse:
- Talosite cultists and dark priests clashed in bloody hand-to-hand combat against Leilon guards and Neverwinter knights, while panicking commoners clogged the muddy streets trying to flee.
- Because the Order had bypassed the war drums outside, jagged lightning bolts repeatedly crashed down from the storm clouds at random, blasting defenders, cultists, and masonry in thunderous collateral explosions.
- Swooping through the thunderheads came **[[Claugiyliamatar]]**, her ancient emerald scales blackened with necrotic veins from the parasitic possession of the ghost dragon **[[Ebondeath]]**.
- Washing over the plaza, the dragon's *Frightful Presence* forced guards and cultists to make desperate Wisdom saves against paralyzing terror.
- Opening her rotting maw, the possessed wyrm unleashed a cataclysmic breath weapon—not the natural poison gas of a green dragon, but a blistering wave of **necrotic chlorine energy** that withered stone walls and dissolved soldiers into smoking ash!

##### Slicing with the Dragon Slayer
Standing firm against the dragon's terror:
- Krageer unsheathed his **Dragon Slayer Longsword** (`+3d6 slashing`), leaping into the dragon's path alongside his roaring Minotaur (`Gore +6, 10 piercing`). Krageer's enchanted blade carved deep bloody furrows into draconic hide.
- Rhynok sniped from the cover of shattered shopfronts (`Longbow +2, 14 Piercing + 10 Sneak Attack`), burying arrows into the beast's wing joints.
- Ebondeath retaliated with punishing tail sweeps (`35 to hit, 16 + 8 bludgeoning`), battering Krageer and the defenders across the flagstones.
- Reeling under the savage bite of the Dragon Slayer sword and the Order's unrelenting focus fire, the possessed dragon roared in agonizing fury, broke off from the plaza, and wheeled north toward the high hill of **Thalivar's Tower**!

Bleeding and coughing from necrotic fumes, the companions huddled in the rubble to down a quick round of **healing potions** before sprinting up the hillside in relentless pursuit.

---

#### Event 9: The Roof of the House of Thalivar & The Swords of Leilon (September 10–24, 2022)
Scaling the steep crag toward the wizard's ancestral mansion:
> *The green dragon squats on the distant roof of the House of Thalivar. As you draw closer, you see a trio of ghostly warriors locked in combat with the beast!*

The companions immediately recognized the glowing phantoms: they were the legendary **[[Swords of Leilon]]**, whose apparitions had aided the party during the beacon crisis in Chapter 2! Having achieved three major battlefield victories (South Gate, Quayside, and Town Square), the Order arrived just in time to witness the spectral heroes striking at full power:
> *One of the warriors, a dark-skinned woman dressed in chainmail, swings her sword at the dragon. The beast howls in pain and for a moment, you see the spectral form of another dragon seethe under its skin. The woman glances down at you as she readies her next attack.*
> *“To arms!” she shouts. “Leilon needs you!”*

Recognizing **Augusta Krul**, the companions vaulted onto the slate roof to stand alongside the ancient champions of Leilon!

##### The Rooftop Climax & Wild Magic Chaos
The final clash atop the slippery roof was a frenzy of legendary steel and wild magic:
- **Necrotic Breath (77 Damage):** Ebondeath opened the rooftop engagement with a point-blank blast of necrotic breath, engulfing the roof in dark flames for a staggering **77 points of necrotic damage**!
- **The Gnomengarde Grenade:** Rhynok whipped out the **Gnomengarde Grenade** recovered from the gnomish inventors in Chapter 1, arming the rune-carved key and hurling it into the dragon's underbelly. The thunderous concussive explosion rocked the mansion's foundation.
- **The Wand of Wonder (Roll 14):** In the desperate heat of melee, Rhynok drew his **Wand of Wonder** and triggered its chaotic magic—rolling a **14**! The unpredictable wand instantly stunned Rhynok until the start of his next turn, but flooded his mind with euphoric psychic feedback that left him utterly convinced something magnificent and godlike had just taken place!
- **Breaking the Threshold:** Ebondeath lashed with heavy tail strikes, forcing the party to chug healing potions mid-swing. But as Rhynok recovered from the wand's stun to loose venomous sneak attack arrows, Krageer drove his **Dragon Slayer Longsword** between the dragon's ribs. With that devastating strike, the companions forced the wyrm below the **50 hit point threshold**, shattering the dark magic anchoring the possession!

---

#### The Fall on Aubrey's Shoppe & The Exorcism of Ebondeath (September 24–25, 2022)
Mortally wounded and convulsing, the gargantuan wyrm lost her footing on the slate roof and crashed down onto the street below:
> *The dragon has crash-landed on Aubrey’s Peculiarities Shoppe, reducing the building to a pile of broken masonry. Bleeding badly and covered with arrows, the dragon struggles to rise to its feet. As you approach it, the specter of another dragon emerges from its body and flies away to the south, fading from sight! As the spirit leaves its body, the dragon slumps exhausted to the ground.*

##### Krageer's Investigation & The Dragon's Parting Words
As the spectral, shadowy phantom of **Ebondeath** ripped free from the green dragon's flesh and flew southward toward the Mere of Dead Men, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** approached the fallen titan with greataxe drawn, rolling **Investigation** on the groaning beast.

Slowly blinking her emerald eyes, **[[Claugiyliamatar]]** (Old Gnawbone) coughed up pools of black blood as her natural green dragon mind returned. Recognizing that the mortal champions before her had severed the unholy parasitic bond that enslaved her, the great wyrm climbed weakly onto her claws. Before spreading her massive, ragged wings and taking flight north toward her lair in the Kryptgarden Forest, she rumbled in a low hiss that she was **indebted to the Order of Znak** for liberating her mind and body. 

Having sparred with treacherous chromatic dragons before, the companions nodded politely while taking the "debt of gratitude" of an ancient green wyrm with a monumental grain of salt!

---

#### Victory Achieved: The Fall of Ularan Mortus & Statues of the Heroes
With the dragon driven off and Ebondeath banished from her flesh, the morale of both invading armies collapsed:
- **The Regular Victory:** Based on the cumulative victory points across the battlefield sectors, the Order achieved a **Regular Victory** for Leilon.
- **The Death of Ularan Mortus:** Because the party had prioritized the Quayside and Town Square over silencing the storm drums, dread war priest **[[Ularan Mortus]]** was chosen as the enemy general slain during the chaotic street fighting. As allied veterans and Neverwinter knights rallied, Ularan Mortus was cut down, and without his unholy necromantic will, the remaining undead hordes disintegrated into inanimate bone and rot.
- **The Heroes of Leilon:**
  > *If the characters survive and save Leilon, they become heroes of the town. The councilors raise statues in the characters’ honor, though they remain fearful of the rising cult activity in the area. In time, Lord Neverember asks the characters to stamp out these threats once and for all by striking at the cults’ headquarters.*

In gratitude for their salvation, the Leilon Town Council commissioned heroic stone statues of **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** to be erected in the rebuilt town square, while awarding the party a purse of **2,500 gp**.

---

#### Aubrey's Peculiarities & The Secret of Aubrey Silverspun
Turning back to the smoking ruins where the dragon had crash-landed, Krageer set about investigating the flattened rubble of their favorite curiosity shop:
- **Heaving the Masonry:** Rolling an **Athletics check**, Krageer's Goliath brawn shoved aside crushed granite lintels and splintered wooden support beams from the flattened shopfront.
- **The Investigation Sweep:** Sifting through the shattered display cases and shattered clockwork curios, Krageer's hands uncovered a miracle: an ancient, gilded oil painting preserved completely intact beneath the debris.

Wiping away centuries of dust, the companions stared in stunned realization at the portrait:
- The canvas depicted the historic **[[Swords of Leilon]]**—the ancient adventuring champions who had defended the town centuries ago.
- Standing proudly in the painting alongside the chainmailed commander **Augusta Krul** and the bearded dwarf **Ogri Ninebeards** was the familiar, smiling face of **[[Aubrey Silverspun]]** himself!
- When the party questioned the surviving townsfolk and councilors, none remembered any living merchant named Aubrey ever leasing the storefront. 

The truth was laid bare: Aubrey Silverspun was never an ordinary shopkeeper. He was an immortal spectral guardian of Leilon—an ancient hero who had walked among them in disguise, stocking magical curios, offering vital counsel, and secretly shepherding the Order of Znak to become the new champions worthy of taking up the mantle of the Swords of Leilon!

---

#### The Final Two Bounties
With the siege broken and the dead buried, Leilon remained gripped by fear. Lord Neverember and the town council realized that so long as the leadership of the two cults survived in their secret redoubts, the town would never be safe. 

Following their triumphant salvation of the settlement, the four companions officially attained **Level 12**—standing at the peak of their mortal power as legendary tier-three heroes of the Sword Coast. Recognizing their unmatched prowess, Lord Neverember issued two monumental bounties of **8,000 gp** each, commissioning the Order of Znak to strike directly at the heart of both hostile powers:
1. Journey into the treacherous Mere of Dead Men to infiltrate the sunken mausoleum and destroy the ghost dragon **[[Ebondeath]]** once and for all (**Chapter 6**).
2. Board ***[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]*** once more to hunt down high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]** aboard her flagship storm dreadnaught on the raging high seas (**Chapter 7**).

### 7. Into the Hurricane — The Dreadnaught Climax Against [[Fheralai Stormsworn]]
To end the tempest threat at its source, the Order called once more upon Captain **[[Deloz Montiv]]** and ***[[Twinkle and Icingdeath|Twinkle]]***, steering straight into a howling, unnatural hurricane to hunt down the Talosite flagship dreadnaught.

![[talos.jpg]]
*Talos, God of Storms — The Apparition Above the Flagship Dreadnaught*

Beneath a colossal, lightning-wreathed apparition of the storm god **[[Talos]]** towering in the thunderheads above the sea, the four champions and Captain Deloz boarded the massive dreadnaught. Sweeping the storm-swept decks and lower holds in a climactic clash of steel and spell, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] (wielding his legendary greataxe *[[Ultima Exodus]]*), [[Rhynok]], [[Myagkiy Znak]], and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] vanquished high priestess **[[Fheralai Stormsworn]]**, an **Air Elemental Myrmidon**, and a summoned **Nalfeshnee demon**—shattering the storm fleet and cementing the legend of **[[The Order of Znak]]** across the Sword Coast forever.
