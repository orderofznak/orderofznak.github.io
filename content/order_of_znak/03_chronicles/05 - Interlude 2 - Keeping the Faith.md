## 📜 Overview of the Second Interlude
Between the fateful possession of [[Claugiyliamatar]] in Deeping Cave and the final siege of [[Leilon]], **[[The Order of Znak]]** answered a desperate plea from allied Forge Domain cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**. Their quest led them from the lair of a child-stealing Night Hag to the molten heart of a dormant volcano for one of the most legendary physical showdowns in the Order's history.

---

## ⚔️ The Trials of Faith & Fire

### 1. The Night Hag's Lair, The Rescue of [[Yasmina]], & Her Portraits of the Order
Tracking down the fiendish thieves who had desecrated [[Dayne Beltran]]'s sanctuary and stolen his sacred suit of enchanted plate armor, the Order infiltrated the lair of a predatory **Night Hag**. Slaying the fiendish crone before she could slip into the Ethereal Plane, the champions rescued a young girl named **[[Yasmina]]** from the hag's clutches—welcoming her under the protective wing of the Order in [[Leilon]] alongside [[Smithwell and Burnice]]—and recovered **Dayne's +2 Plate Armor**, which Dayne gratefully bestowed upon **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** to wear into the coming war.

Shortly after her rescue and arrival at the Leilon headquarters, young **[[Yasmina]]** canonically drew and painted expressive portraits of her four rescuers—immortalizing **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** through the eyes of the grateful child they had saved:

### 2. Titan Grapple in the Volcano — The "Double-Enlarge" & Puck's *Potion of Growth*
The trail of fire and iron culminated deep inside the caldera of a dormant volcano, where the Order confronted a towering, **Huge-sized Fire Giant Queen** amidst rivers of glowing magma. Because a single enlargement effect would only increase a **Medium** Goliath to **Large**, the table invoked the **Rule of Cool ("Double-Enlarge")**: **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** uncorked and quaffed the ***Potion of Growth*** he had purchased in [[Leilon]] from nervous elven potion-seller **[[Puck Caribdas]]** (`Medium` &rarr; `Large`), **and** **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** simultaneously cast ***Enlarge*** upon him (`Large` &rarr; `Huge`)!

Swelling two full size categories to **Huge** titan proportions to match the monarch's stature eye-to-eye, the Goliath barbarian locked arms with the **Fire Giant Queen** in a titanic, bare-handed wrestling match at the edge of the lava flows—pinning and overpowering the ruler of fire while [[Rhynok]], [[Tvyordiy Znak]], and [[Myagkiy Znak]] poured relentless strikes into the fray until the giant queen fell!
