## 📜 Overview of the Second Interlude
Between the fateful possession of [[Claugiyliamatar]] in Deeping Cave and the final siege of [[Leilon]], **[[The Order of Znak]]** answered a desperate plea from allied Forge Domain cleric **[[Dayne Beltran]]**. Their quest led them from the lair of a child-stealing Night Hag to the molten heart of a dormant volcano for one of the most legendary physical showdowns in the Order's history.

---

## ⚔️ The Trials of Faith & Fire

### 1. The Night Hag's Lair & The Rescue of [[Yasmina]]
Tracking down the fiendish thieves who had desecrated [[Dayne Beltran]]'s sanctuary and stolen his sacred suit of enchanted plate armor, the Order infiltrated the lair of a predatory **Night Hag**. Slaying the fiendish crone before she could slip into the Ethereal Plane, the champions rescued a young girl named **[[Yasmina]]** from the hag's clutches—welcoming her under the protective wing of the Order alongside [[Smithwell and Burnice]]—and recovered **Dayne's +2 Plate Armor**, which Dayne gratefully bestowed upon **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** to wear into the coming war.

### 2. Titan Grapple in the Volcano — Krageer vs. The Fire Giant Queen
The trail of fire and iron culminated deep inside the caldera of a dormant volcano, where the Order confronted a towering **Fire Giant Queen** amidst rivers of glowing magma. Rather than yielding the high ground to the giant monarch, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** invoked the transmutation magic of ***Enlarge*** upon **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**!

Swelling to giant-kin proportions to match the monarch's stature, the Goliath barbarian locked arms with the **Fire Giant Queen** in a titanic, bare-handed wrestling match at the edge of the lava flows—pinning and overpowering the ruler of fire while [[Rhynok]], [[Tvyordiy Znak]], and [[Myagkiy Znak]] poured relentless strikes into the fray until the giant queen fell!
