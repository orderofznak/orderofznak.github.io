# 🏔️ Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak

> **Chronicle Navigation:** 🏠 [[The Order of Znak Complete Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle Hub]] | ➡️ **Next:** [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]

## 📜 Overview of the First Act
When the young white dragon **[[Cryovain]]** descended upon the snow-capped spurs of **[[Icespire Hold]]**, his arrival sent a cascade of terror across the Sword Mountains and Neverwinter Wood—driving monstrous predators from the high peaks and displacing ferocious mountain orc tribes into a dark pact with the half-orc **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**. Answering the call for adventurers in the frontier boomtown of **[[Phandalin]]**, four sellswords—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**—forged the brotherhood that would become **[[The Order of Znak]]**.

---

## ⚔️ The Frontier Quests

### 1. Arrival in [[Phandalin]], Frontier Rumors & The Death of [[Turkos Doomheart]]
Arriving in the frontier settlement of [[Phandalin]] as untested sellswords, the four companions toured the town and met its colorful residents: innkeeper [[Toblen Stonehill]] at the [[Stonehill Inn]], provisioner [[Elmar Barthen]] and his clerks [[Ander (Phandalin)|Ander]] and [[Thistle (clerk)|Thistle]], Lionshield Coster master [[Linene Graywind]], Phandalin Miner's Exchange guildmaster [[Halia Thornton]], scholarly elf acolyte [[Garaele|Sister Garaele]], and everyone's favorite coward, Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]]—who refused to open his door and slipped job notices beneath the timber threshold.

Over tankards at the [[Stonehill Inn]], the newcomers gathered local rumors from travelers and miners: word that veteran hunter **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]** welcomed guests to **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]** deep in Neverwinter Wood near the river's edge if they brought a bottle of wine for room and board, and grim tales of shipwrecks piling up beneath the cliffs of the **[[Tower of Storms]]**. The danger of the white wyrm became personal early on when **[[Cryovain]]** swept down over [[Phandalin]] in a brazen raid that claimed the life of **[[Turkos Doomheart]]**—a tragedy that left the townsfolk desperate for champions and underscored Harbin Wester's urgent push to find a fortified evacuation refuge.

In those early days on the frontier, the companions carried humble arms: [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] hefted a plain iron **Greataxe** and a bundle of **Javelins**; [[Rhynok]] carried a weathered **Longbow** and **Warhammer**; [[Myagkiy Znak]]—already a devoted priest of life and mercy—bore a **Mace** and wooden **Shield**; and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] wielded a slender Eastern-style ***Qiang* spear**.

### 2. [[Umbrage Hill]] & The Windmill Siege
Dispatched south to [[Umbrage Hill]], the party confronted and **drove off a hungry Manticore** besieging the stone windmill of midwife and Chauntea acolyte [[Adabra Gwynn]]. Though unable to convince the stubborn apothecary to abandon her hilltop home for the safety of Phandalin, the champions earned her gratitude and a **Potion of Healing**.

Hardened by their first frontier clash, Krageer began throwing himself into battle with reckless, all-out abandon while honing an uncanny sixth sense for imminent danger, and Rhynok awakened the primal magic of the wilds—learning to conjure nourishing *Goodberries* and volleys of piercing thorns (*Hail of Thorns*). *(Later in the campaign, the manticore and its mate returned to Umbrage Hill, only to be slain by [[Cryovain]] in a vicious aerial clash above the windmill.)*

### 3. [[Temple of Abbathor|The Dwarven Excavation]] & The Gem of Greed
Journeying into an isolated canyon in the Sword Mountains, the Order aided shield dwarf prospectors [[Dazlyn Grayshard]] and [[Norbus Ironrune]] at the newly uncovered [[Temple of Abbathor]]. Inside the ancient halls, the companions slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** (*"the blobs"*) haunting the sanctuary, uncovered the temple's hidden doors, and spent hours excavating a rubble-choked tunnel leading to the innermost chamber and the **Statue of Abbathor**.

Tempted by the glowing jewel set into the statue of the dwarven god of greed, Krageer pried at the gem and triggered its fiery explosive curse. Even though the Goliath's newly honed instincts warned him the instant the stone gave way, he could not twist clear in time and took the full brunt of the blast. Returning soot-stained to the surface to speak with Dazlyn and Norbus, the party could not persuade the stubborn prospectors to part with additional coin, but claimed their promised pair of ***Sending Stones***—a magical lifeline the Order would use across the remainder of their grand saga.

Upon exiting the canyon, the companions were ambushed by a pack of marauding **Orcs** charging aggressively down the defile. Against Rhynok's sworn hatred of orc-kind and his lightning-quick reflexes with bow and *Hail of Thorns*, the raiders were cut down before they could close the line. Emerging from the ruins, each champion stepped into their true calling:
* **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** bonded with the primal spirit of the **Bear Totem**, shrugging off nearly any blow while in the grip of his rage.
* **[[Rhynok]]** embraced the deep shadows of the wilderness and subterranean dark as an unseen **Gloom Stalker**.
* **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** mastered the swaying, unpredictable footwork and redirection of the **Drunken Master**.
* **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** deepened his sacred calling as a battlefield sustainer of the **Life Domain**.

### 4. [[Gnomengarde]] & The Barrel Room Mimic
Following a mountain stream up into the cliffside caverns of [[Gnomengarde]], the Order met all twenty-one rock gnome inhabitants of the recluse kingdom. Tensions flared early when manic inventor [[Facktoré]] opened fire with her spinning, autoloading heavy crossbow contraption and shot [[Rhynok]]—prompting a distrustful Krageer to rough up gnome sentries [[Pog]] and [[Ulla]] along the corridor on the way to chief artificers [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]].

Discovering that [[Korboz|King Korboz]] had locked himself and his husband [[Gnerkli|King Gnerkli]] away in terror after a shapeshifting monster began devouring gnomes inside the complex, the champions hunted down and slew the **Mimic in the barrel room**. With the royal sanity restored, the grateful gnomes rewarded the party with the ***Clockwork Amulet***, ***Pole of Collapsing***, ***Hat of Wizardry***, and ***Wand of Pyrotechnics***.

### 5. [[Butterskull Ranch]] — The Outhouse Ambush & Twelve Orcs Slain
Riding east along the Triboar Trail on the trail of marauding Orcs who had overrun [[Butterskull Ranch]], the Order approached the smoking homestead with stealth and precision—ambushing a lone orc guard right at the ranch **outhouse** before [[Rhynok]] crept up to peek through the farmhouse window and scout the occupiers inside. Storming the two-story ranch house, the champions **defeated twelve Orcs** in a fierce room-to-room brawl, rescued retired Conyberry sheriff **[[Alfonse Kalazorn|"Big Al" Kalazorn]]** from his cellar chains, rounded up his prized runaway dairy cow **[[Petunia (cow)|Petunia]]**, and took a well-earned long rest at the ranch before Big Al relocated to [[Phandalin]].

### 6. [[Loggers' Camp]], [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] & The Promise of Elven Boots
Returning to [[Phandalin]], the Order took charge of a provision wagon from [[Elmar Barthen]]—pulled by the stalwart draft ox **[[Vincent (ox)|Vincent]]**—bound for the [[Loggers' Camp]] on the Neverwinter River. Guided through the trackless boughs by [[Rhynok]]'s unerring woodcraft, the party spotted an eerie wild **boar** watching them from the brush—recognizing it as a shapeshifted **[[Anchorites of Talos|Anchorite of Talos]]** before the spy bolted toward the [[Woodland Manse]]. At the [[Loggers' Camp]], the champions discovered that hidden anchorite totems had drawn burrowing **Ankhegs** beneath the camp; slaying the acid-spitting insects—including a massive ankheg that erupted through the floorboards of **[[Tibor Wester]]**'s barricaded cabin—they rescued Harbin's shrewd half-brother and delivered the supplies.

Remembering the tavern rumor from Phandalin, the companions brought a **bottle of wine** deeper into the woods to **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]** for room and board. Arriving in the middle of a sudden aerial raid by **[[Cryovain]]**, the party leaped into the fray alongside master hunter **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]]** to drive the young white dragon from the palisade rooftops, and were welcomed inside by Falcon, his weary cook **[[Corwin]]**, mute stablehand **[[Pell]]**, and prized gray stallion **[[Baatorius]]**.

As the Order departed the lodge and started heading southeast toward the [[Woodland Manse]], **Falcon rode out after them on [[Baatorius]] and called out:**
> *"Wait! If you are headed where I think you are headed, then you would be doing me a service. If you can cull the evil that resides there, I have a pair of magical boots that might serve you on your journeys."*

One look at the silent ***Boots of Elvenkind*** was enough for the whole company to agree: those boots had **[[Rhynok]]**'s name written all over them (a prize that would later inspire the wood elf to hunt down a matching ***Cloak of Elvenkind***).

### 7. First Foray into the [[Woodland Manse]] — The Shaved One-Eyed Goat of Gruumsh & Desperate Retreat
Marching southeast toward the vine-choked [[Woodland Manse]] with a **shaved, one-eyed goat** in tow, [[Rhynok]] and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** concocted a legendary masterstroke of psychological warfare against the superstitious orc garrison. Before approaching the clearing, **Stoneface tattooed the following prophecy in Orcish directly onto the hide of the shaved one-eyed goat:**
> *"Take this goat as an offering of sustenance. For if you do not take it and flee from this mansion and cease your worship of false and weak gods then my wrath will come upon thee. My blood spear is unforgiving and though I only have one eye... it sees all. — He Who Watches, He Who Never Sleeps, The One Eyed God, Gruumsh"*

Setting the tattooed, one-eyed goat loose outside the estate in the name of **Gruumsh**, the ruse worked to perfection—terrifying a large contingent of the stationed orcs into abandoning the mansion on the spot as Rhynok opened the assault from cover!

When the party pushed into the central courtyard around the **Gulthias Tree**, however, they were rapidly surrounded by ritual leader **[[Grannoc]]**, waves of twig, needle, and vine blights, savage boars, and crackling *Lightning Bolts* from the **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**. Forced into a desperate fighting withdrawal, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] fell unconscious twice**—revived from the brink once in the heat of the melee, only to linger at the edge of the clearing as his companions broke into the trees and fall a second time under a withering hail of needle blight spines. Swooping back from his **flight** (*in both senses of the word*), the winged Aarakocra monk **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** hoisted the pin-cushioned Goliath and carried him away through the forest canopy to safety.

Hardened by their narrow survival at the Manse, the four champions surged into a new tier of martial and spiritual mastery: Krageer's greataxe now struck twice in the span of a single breath; Myagkiy learned to call down swirling radiant hosts of ***Spirit Guardians***; Tvyordiy unlocked nerve-locking ***Stunning Strikes*** alongside rapid multi-hit combinations; and Rhynok loosed rapid-fire volleys while mastering *Rope Trick* and *Pass without Trace*.

*(Shortly after the Order retreated from the Manse, the vengeful [[Anchorites of Talos]] conjured **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar** and led a warband of twenty orcs in a retaliatory assault on **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]**—smashing through the palisades, ransacking the compound, and driving Falcon, Corwin, and Pell south to shelter in [[Phandalin]].)*

### 8. [[Tower of Storms]] & The Awakened Crab's Gift
Turning west toward the jagged sea cliffs of the Sword Coast, the Order investigated the eerie green beacon of the **[[Tower of Storms]]**. On the shingle beach below the headland, the party gently calmed and befriended an **awakened giant crab**, who directed them into a tidal sea cave haunted by the sea elf banshee **[[Miraal]]**. Learning that the half-orc anchorite **[[Moesko]]** had murdered her and stolen her opalescent conch shell, the champions pledged to recover the heirloom.

Climbing the sheer cliffside ledge, several companions were momentarily entranced by the siren melody of a flock of **Harpies**, stumbling perilously toward the drop before shaking off the enchantment and cutting the winged predators down. Storming the lighthouse keep, [[Myagkiy Znak]] identified the desecrated storm-altar of **[[Talos]]**, and the party slew **[[Moesko]]** before ascending to the storm-lashed pinnacle to **pierce and destroy his disembodied, beating heart**, ending his undead curse forever. Returning the opalescent conch to [[Miraal]] laid her sorrowful spirit to rest, and true to its word, the awakened giant crab scuttled up from the sunken wreck of the *Star-Crossed Lover* bearing an enchanted **+1 Longbow**—which passed straight into the hands of **[[Rhynok]]**. Content with their prize, the companions never dove to explore the five sunken hulls (such as the *Orca*) and left the reef predator [[Daggermaw]] undisturbed beneath the waves.

### 9. [[Shrine of Savras]] & The 2,500 Gold Piece Bell
Returning to Phandalin to review the new frontier job postings—escorting **[[DonJon Raskin]]** to [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], securing the dwarven fortress of [[Axeholm]], and seeking the fabled dragon-slaying blade of [[Dragon Barrow]]—the Order first marched south of ruined Conyberry to the **[[Shrine of Savras]]**. Fighting shoulder-to-shoulder with the irrepressible, Cajun-drawling DonJon—and with Rhynok's newly acquired **+1 Longbow** singing from the rear—the party clashed with a massive warband of **Orcs** and towering **Ogres** in a brutal courtyard battle, felling every last marauder.

Inside the roofless sanctuary, **[[Rhynok]]** and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** laid their hands upon the uncovered stone altar of Savras and were swept into a prophetic vision—soaring high above the Sword Mountains to behold **[[Cryovain]]** slumbering atop the frozen fortress of **[[Icespire Hold]]**. Before departing, Krageer and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] scaled the crumbling belfry tower, pried loose the temple's **solid gold bell**, and hauled it back to **sell for a princely 2,500 gold pieces**!

### 10. [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] — The Wererat Lesson & [[Zeleen Varnaster]]'s Escape
Pressing into the western foothills with **[[DonJon Raskin]]** to inspect **[[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]**, the Order was not fooled for a moment by Whiskered Gang boss **[[Zeleen Varnaster]]** and her claim to be the legitimate mine overseer. Yet when steel was drawn against her pack of **wererats**, a grim realization struck the frontline: neither Krageer's mundane iron greataxe nor Tvyordiy's wooden *Qiang* could leave a lasting wound upon the lycanthropes' cursed hides!

With the two melee bruisers unable to harm the pack, the battle was carried entirely by **[[Rhynok]]**—whose enchanted **+1 Longbow** punched through their supernatural immunity—and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, whose swirling ***Spirit Guardians***, ***Spiritual Weapon***, and searing ***Sacred Flame*** burned through the wererat ranks. Routing the lycanthropes, the party **ran off the surviving wererats as [[Zeleen Varnaster]] escaped from the mine**, restoring the operation to DonJon, dividing the gang's coin stash and magic curios, and growing even deadlier from the hard-won experience.

### 11. Silvered Steel & The Purge of [[Axeholm]]
Returning to [[Phandalin]] after the humbling lesson of the gold mine, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** marched straight to the smithy and had his **Greataxe coated in alchemical silver**, swearing an oath never again to stand powerless before any foe. Beside him, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** had his ***Qiang* spear silvered**, and **[[Rhynok]]** silvered a **Shortsword** for close-quarters defense.

Tasked with opening the sealed dwarven mountain fortress of **[[Axeholm]]** as a refuge for Phandalin's townsfolk, the Order found the massive iron portcullis barred from within. Scaling the rocky mountainside to the natural rock **chimney** above the keep, all four companions rappelled smoothly down the slick stone flue under [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s prayerful ***Guidance***.

Dropping into the fortress, the heroes caught a nest of **Giant Spiders** completely off guard in a lethal surprise ambush—killing several spiders in the opening seconds as Krageer roared into a rage, Myagkiy summoned his floating *Spiritual Weapon*, and Tvyordiy unleashed a rapid *Flurry of Blows*. When the surviving giant spiders spat webs and scrambled fifteen feet up onto the stone ceiling, Rhynok methodically picked them off with six well-placed arrows. From there, the Order waged a methodical campaign through the haunted halls: purging packs of flesh-eating dwarven **Ghouls** and bloodsucking **Stirges** across the **West Hall** and **Smithy**, and marching into the **East Hall** and **Ghouls' Den** to exterminate the lower horde. Climbing the stone stairs to the upper **Haunted Hall**, they came face-to-face with the sorrowful moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**. When she loosed her soul-shattering *Wail*, the four champions stood like granite—not a single member of the Order faltering before her cry—and swiftly laid her spirit to rest with divine radiance and silvered steel.

Pushing into the **Castellan's Bedchamber**, the party cleared out another nest of **Stirges**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]'s sharp eyes** noticed a false stone back inside the soot-darkened fireplace. Opening the hidden iron vault within, **Myagkiy** claimed and attuned the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power***—infusing his arms with prodigious giant-kin might—while **Krageer** donned the menacing ***Dread Helm***, his eyes glowing like crimson embers beneath its shadowed steel visor. After putting down the undead **ghoul of the dwarf Castellan**, the Order left [[Axeholm]] completely cleansed—catching sight of [[Cryovain]] circling the mountain fortress on their way back to [[Phandalin]] and strafing the **Starmetal Hills** as they rode out of town again.

### 12. [[Dragon Barrow]], [[Circle of Thunder]] & Vengeance at the [[Woodland Manse]]
With only [[Dragon Barrow]], the unfinished [[Woodland Manse]], and [[Icespire Hold]] remaining before them, the Order rode past the [[Shrine of Savras]] and Conyberry (where [[Cryovain]] was spotted wheeling across the sky) to the grass-covered mounds of **[[Dragon Barrow]]**. Navigating the treacherous sarcophagus tunnels of Lady Tanamere Alagondar's tomb, the champions extinguished a lurking **Will-o'-Wisp** in the Adventurers' Sepulcher and slew the unseen **Invisible Stalker** guarding the bones of Azdraka, allowing **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** to lift the legendary ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** from its resting place. After exploring the remaining barrow passages, the companions forged an alliance outside the mound with the noble centaur **[[Xanth]]**, who pledged his hooves and spear to help them crush the anchorites of Neverwinter Wood.

Setting out into Neverwinter Wood with **[[Xanth]]** galloping at their side—and passing the ransacked ruins and heavy **orcsign** of **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]** along the river trail—the Order intercepted anchorite commander **[[Yargath]]** and her patrol of **ten Talosian Orcs** en route to the standing stones, annihilating Yargath and all ten warriors in a furious woodland clash.

Charging straight up the hill to the standing stones of the **[[Circle of Thunder]]**, the Order plunged into an apocalyptic storm battle against **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, **ten Twig Blights**, constricting **Vine Blights**, and the **two half-orc Anchorites of Talos** (**[[Flenz]]** and **[[Narux]]**). Amidst booming thunderclaps, crackling lightning tusks, and searing *Lightning Bolts*, the Order stood unbowed: Xanth trampled cultists underfoot, Krageer cleaved through boar and anchorite alike, Rhynok loosed lethal volleys of arrows through the downpour, Tvyordiy locked foes down with rapid *Stunning Strikes*, and Myagkiy's radiant *Spirit Guardians* shredded the blight throngs. With Gorthok, Flenz, and Narux slain, **[[Xanth]] the Centaur was satisfied**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**—as the company's sole shield-bearer—claimed the enchanted ***+1 Shield*** from the caves below.

With the Circle of Thunder broken, the champions marched back to the **[[Woodland Manse]]** to settle their unfinished score once and for all. Storming the courtyard, they fought a fierce five-round battle against **twelve Twig Blights**, **five Vine Blights** (pushing through their *Entangling Plants* while [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s ***Spirit Guardians*** tore the blights apart round after round), and **two [[Anchorites of Talos]]** including ritualist **[[Grannoc]]**. Sweeping through the upper halls and surrounding woods, the Order exterminated **twelve Stirges** in the ruined rooms and **eight Needle Blights** outside, purging the estate completely! Returning victorious from the Manse to **[[Phandalin]]**, the party reunited with the evacuated **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon]]**, who awarded **[[Rhynok]]** his hard-earned ***Boots of Elvenkind*** right before the Order departed for the high peaks.

### 13. Ascent to [[Icespire Hold]] & The Slaying of [[Cryovain]]
With the cult of Talos broken in the woods and Lady Alagondar's *Dragon Slayer* blade in hand, [[The Order of Znak]] began the perilous ascent up the frozen spurs of Icespire Peak toward **[[Icespire Hold]]**. Along the snow-choked mountain trails, a warband of **Hobgoblins** moved to bar their path, only to be cut down by the companions' silvered arsenal—[[Rhynok]] drawing his **Silvered Shortsword** alongside [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s **Silvered *Qiang*** and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s **Silvered Greataxe**.

Breaching the outer gatehouse and stone halls of [[Icespire Hold]], the party crossed paths with the treacherous mercenary company known as the **[[Stone-Cold Reavers]]** ([[Brakkis Elspaar]], [[Dobin Noreth]], [[Jabarl]], [[Runa Vokdottir]], and [[Syleen Wintermoon]]), dispatching the opportunists in their usual no-nonsense fashion before clearing a flock of **Stirges** from the lower keep.

Scaling the frost-sheathed stairs to the open fortress roof, the four champions stepped out into the howling gale to confront **[[Cryovain]]** and **four cackling Ice Mephits** lairing among the battlements:
* **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** invoked a prayer of ***Bless*** over his comrades and conjured his floating ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat the **four Ice Mephits** out of the freezing air.
* As the white wyrm unleashed blasts of glacial *Cold Breath* across the icy stones, the companions ducked and weathered the frost, closing the distance across the slick roof.
* Darting in with blinding speed, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] delivered a series of clutch *Stunning Strikes* that locked [[Cryovain]]'s nervous system in place**, leaving the massive predator reeling and helpless on the ice!
* Seizing the opening, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** roared into a Bear Totem rage and unleashed a devastating flurry of two-handed blows with his ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, its ancient runes blazing as it carved deep into the dragon's scales, while **[[Rhynok]]** drove deadeye arrows from his **+1 Longbow** straight into the beast's vitals.

With a final, shuddering roar, **[[Cryovain]]** collapsed lifelessly atop the stones of [[Icespire Hold]]. Returning down the mountain to [[Phandalin]] as the celebrated saviors of the frontier, the Order of Znak found their legend carried by bards and couriers down the High Road—reaching the ears of **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** in Neverwinter and the **Lords of Waterdeep**, who would soon call upon the four champions to defend the rebuilding town of **[[Leilon]]**.
