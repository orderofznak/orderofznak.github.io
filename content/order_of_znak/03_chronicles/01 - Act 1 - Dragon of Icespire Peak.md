# 🏔️ Act 1: Dragon of Icespire Peak

> **Chronicle Navigation:** 🏠 [[The Order of Znak Complete Campaign Chronicle|Campaign Chronicle Hub]] | ➡️ **Next:** [[02 - Act 2 - Storm Lord's Wrath|Act 2: Storm Lord's Wrath]]

## 📜 Overview of the First Act
When the young white dragon **[[Cryovain]]** descended upon the snow-capped spurs of **[[Icespire Hold]]**, his arrival sent a cascade of terror across the Sword Mountains and Neverwinter Wood—driving monstrous predators from the high peaks and displacing ferocious mountain orc tribes into a dark pact with the half-orc **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**. Answering the call for adventurers in the frontier boomtown of **[[Phandalin]]**, four sellswords—**[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]**, **[[Rhynok]]**, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, and **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]**—forged the brotherhood that would become **[[The Order of Znak]]**.

---

## ⚔️ The Frontier Quests

### 1. Arrival in [[Phandalin]] & The Frontier Tour
Arriving in the frontier settlement of [[Phandalin]] as untested sellswords, the four companions toured the town and met its colorful residents: innkeeper [[Toblen Stonehill]] at the [[Stonehill Inn]], provisioner [[Elmar Barthen]] and his clerks [[Ander (Phandalin)|Ander]] and [[Thistle (clerk)|Thistle]], Lionshield Coster master [[Linene Graywind]], Phandalin Miner's Exchange guildmaster [[Halia Thornton]], scholarly elf acolyte [[Garaele|Sister Garaele]], and everyone's favorite coward, Townmaster [[Harbin Wester]]—who refused to open his door and slipped job notices beneath the timber threshold.

In those early days on the frontier, the companions carried humble arms: [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] hefted a plain iron **Greataxe** and a bundle of **Javelins**; [[Rhynok]] carried a weathered **Longbow** and **Warhammer**; [[Myagkiy Znak]]—already a devoted priest of life and mercy—bore a **Mace** and wooden **Shield**; and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] wielded a slender Eastern-style ***Qiang* spear**.

### 2. [[Umbrage Hill]] & The Windmill Siege
Dispatched south to [[Umbrage Hill]], the party confronted and **drove off a hungry Manticore** besieging the stone windmill of midwife and Chauntea acolyte [[Adabra Gwynn]]. Though unable to convince the stubborn apothecary to abandon her hilltop home for the safety of Phandalin, the champions earned her gratitude and a **Potion of Healing**.

Hardened by their first frontier clash, Krageer began throwing himself into battle with reckless, all-out abandon while honing an uncanny sixth sense for imminent danger, and Rhynok awakened the primal magic of the wilds—learning to conjure nourishing *Goodberries* and volleys of piercing thorns (*Hail of Thorns*). *(Later in the campaign, prior to the party's first expedition to the [[Woodland Manse]], the manticore and its mate returned to Umbrage Hill, only to be slain by [[Cryovain]] in a vicious aerial clash above the windmill.)*

### 3. [[Temple of Abbathor|The Dwarven Excavation]] & The Gem of Greed
Journeying into an isolated canyon in the Sword Mountains, the Order aided shield dwarf prospectors [[Dazlyn Grayshard]] and [[Norbus Ironrune]] at the newly uncovered [[Temple of Abbathor]]. Inside the ancient halls, the companions slew the corrosive **Ochre Jellies** haunting the sanctuary, uncovered the temple's hidden doors, and spent hours excavating a rubble-choked tunnel leading to the innermost chamber and the **Statue of Abbathor**.

Tempted by the glowing jewel set into the statue of the dwarven god of greed, Krageer pried at the gem and triggered its fiery explosive curse. Even though the Goliath's newly honed instincts warned him the instant the stone gave way, he could not twist clear in time and took the full brunt of the blast. Returning soot-stained to the surface, the party could not persuade the stubborn prospectors to part with additional coin, but claimed their promised pair of ***Sending Stones***—a magical lifeline the Order would use across the remainder of their grand saga.

Upon exiting the canyon, the companions were ambushed by a pack of marauding **Orcs** charging aggressively down the defile. Against Rhynok's sworn hatred of orc-kind and his lightning-quick reflexes with bow and *Hail of Thorns*, the raiders were cut down before they could close the line. Emerging from the ruins, each champion stepped into their true calling:
* **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** bonded with the primal spirit of the **Bear Totem**, shrugging off nearly any blow while in the grip of his rage.
* **[[Rhynok]]** embraced the deep shadows of the wilderness and subterranean dark as an unseen **Gloom Stalker**.
* **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** mastered the swaying, unpredictable footwork and redirection of the **Drunken Master**.
* **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** deepened his sacred calling as a battlefield sustainer of the **Life Domain**.

### 4. [[Gnomengarde]] & The Barrel Room Mimic
Following a mountain stream up into the cliffside caverns of [[Gnomengarde]], the Order met all twenty-one rock gnome inhabitants of the recluse kingdom. Tensions flared early when manic inventor [[Facktoré]] opened fire with her spinning, autoloading heavy crossbow contraption and shot [[Rhynok]]—prompting a distrustful Krageer to rough up gnome sentries [[Pog]] and [[Ulla]] along the corridor on the way to chief artificers [[Fibblestib Pinebark]] and [[Dabbledob Plasterpatch]].

Discovering that [[Korboz|King Korboz]] had locked himself and his husband [[Gnerkli|King Gnerkli]] away in terror after a shapeshifting monster began devouring gnomes inside the complex, the champions hunted down and slew the **Mimic in the barrel room**. With the royal sanity restored, the grateful gnomes rewarded the party with the ***Clockwork Amulet***, ***Pole of Collapsing***, ***Hat of Wizardry***, and ***Wand of Pyrotechnics***.

### 5. [[Butterskull Ranch]] & The Outhouse Ambush
Riding east along the Triboar Trail after word reached Phandalin of an orc raid on [[Butterskull Ranch]], the Order approached the smoking homestead with stealth and precision—ambushing a lone orc guard right at the ranch **outhouse** before storming the farmhouse. Slaying the marauders occupying the upper and lower floors, the party rescued retired Conyberry sheriff **[[Alfonse Kalazorn|"Big Al" Kalazorn]]** from his cellar chains and rounded up his prized runaway dairy cow, **[[Petunia (cow)|Petunia]]**.

### 6. First Foray into the [[Woodland Manse]] — The Blood-Note of Gruumsh & Desperate Retreat
Venturing deep into Neverwinter Wood toward the vine-choked [[Woodland Manse]] before they knew the full strength of the cult gathered within, [[Rhynok]] drew upon his intimate knowledge of orcish superstition to stage a masterstroke of psychological warfare. Slaying a wild animal outside the clearing, the wood elf displayed its bloody carcass and scrawled a message in blood proclaiming that **Gruumsh One-Eye** was furious with the clan—terrifying a large contingent of the stationed orcs into abandoning the estate on the spot!

When the party pushed into the central courtyard around the **Gulthias Tree**, however, they were rapidly surrounded by ritual leader **[[Grannoc]]**, waves of twig, needle, and vine blights, savage boars, and crackling *Lightning Bolts* from the **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**. Forced into a desperate fighting withdrawal, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] fell unconscious twice**—revived from the brink once in the heat of the melee, only to linger at the edge of the clearing as his companions broke into the trees and fall a second time under a withering hail of needle blight spines. Swooping back from his **flight** (*in both senses of the word*), the winged Aarakocra monk **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** hoisted the pin-cushioned Goliath and carried him away through the forest canopy to safety.

### 7. [[Loggers' Camp]] & First Visit to [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]
Returning to [[Phandalin]] to recuperate, the Order took charge of a provision wagon from [[Elmar Barthen]]—pulled by the stalwart draft ox **[[Vincent (ox)|Vincent]]**—bound for the [[Loggers' Camp]] on the Neverwinter River. Guided through the trackless boughs by [[Rhynok]]'s unerring woodcraft, the party spotted an eerie wild **boar** watching them from the brush—recognizing it as a shapeshifted **[[Anchorites of Talos|Anchorite of Talos]]** before the spy bolted back toward the [[Woodland Manse]].

At the [[Loggers' Camp]], the champions discovered that hidden anchorite totems had drawn burrowing **Ankhegs** beneath the camp. Slaying the acid-spitting insects—including a massive ankheg that erupted through the floorboards of **[[Tibor Wester]]**'s barricaded cabin—the party rescued Harbin's shrewd half-brother and delivered the supplies.

Pressing deeper into the woods to **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]**, the companions arrived in the middle of a sudden aerial raid by **[[Cryovain]]**, leaping into the fray alongside master hunter **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]]** to drive the young white dragon from the palisade rooftops. Welcomed inside by Falcon, his weary cook **[[Corwin]]**, mute stablehand **[[Pell]]**, and prized gray stallion **[[Baatorius]]**, the party recounted their harrowing clash at the [[Woodland Manse]]. Falcon was deeply impressed by Rhynok's blood-note subterfuge and promised his finest trophy—a pair of ***Boots of Elvenkind***—if the Order returned to crush the woodland orcs and anchorites. Rhynok immediately set his heart on those silent elven boots (a prize that would later inspire him to hunt down a matching ***Cloak of Elvenkind***).

*(Unbeknownst to the companions, shortly after they departed the hunting lodge, the vengeful [[Anchorites of Talos]] conjured **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar** and led a warband of twenty orcs in a retaliatory assault that smashed through Falcon's palisades, ransacked the lodge, and forced Falcon, Corwin, and Pell to flee south to [[Phandalin]].)*

### 8. [[Tower of Storms]] & The Awakened Crab's Gift
Turning west toward the jagged sea cliffs of the Sword Coast, the Order investigated the eerie green beacon of the **[[Tower of Storms]]**. On the shingle beach below the headland, the party gently calmed and befriended an **awakened giant crab**, who directed them into a tidal sea cave haunted by the sea elf banshee **[[Miraal]]**. Learning that the half-orc anchorite **[[Moesko]]** had murdered her and stolen her opalescent conch shell, the champions pledged to recover the heirloom.

Climbing the sheer cliffside ledge, several companions were momentarily entranced by the siren melody of a flock of **Harpies**, stumbling perilously toward the drop before shaking off the enchantment and cutting the winged predators down. Storming the lighthouse keep, [[Myagkiy Znak]] identified the desecrated storm-altar of **[[Talos]]**, and the party slew **[[Moesko]]** before ascending to the storm-lashed pinnacle to **pierce and destroy his disembodied, beating heart**, ending his undead curse forever. Returning the opalescent conch to [[Miraal]] laid her sorrowful spirit to rest, and true to its word, the awakened giant crab scuttled up from the sunken offshore wreck bearing an enchanted **+1 Longbow**—which passed straight into the hands of **[[Rhynok]]**. Content with their prize, the companions left the sunken hulls and the reef predator [[Daggermaw]] undisturbed beneath the waves.

### 9. [[Shrine of Savras]] with [[DonJon Raskin]] & First Foray to [[Dragon Barrow]]
Hired in Phandalin to escort the irrepressible, Cajun-drawling troubleshooter **[[DonJon Raskin]]** to the troubled [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]], the Order first detoured south of ruined Conyberry to the **[[Shrine of Savras]]**. Fighting shoulder-to-shoulder with DonJon—and with Rhynok's newly acquired **+1 Longbow** singing from the rear—the party clashed with a massive warband of **Orcs** and towering **Ogres** in a brutal courtyard battle, felling every last marauder.

Inside the roofless sanctuary, **[[Rhynok]]** and **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** laid their hands upon the uncovered stone altar of Savras and were swept into a prophetic vision—soaring high above the Sword Mountains to behold **[[Cryovain]]** slumbering atop the frozen fortress of **[[Icespire Hold]]**. Before departing, Krageer and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] scaled the crumbling belfry tower and pried loose the temple's **solid gold bell** (worth a princely 2,500 gold pieces).

Journeying west across the Triboar Trail to the grass-covered mounds of **[[Dragon Barrow]]**, the companions made an initial foray into the subterranean tomb of Lady Tanamere Alagondar—trading blows with lurking **Will-o'-Wisps** and an unseen **Invisible Stalker**—before withdrawing to regroup and tend their wounds.

### 10. [[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]] & The Wererat Lesson
Tempered by weeks of relentless frontier combat, the four champions grew into the height of their martial and spiritual power: Krageer's mountain-born frame became nigh-unbreakable, his greataxe swinging twice in the span of a single breath; Myagkiy's physical fortitude and spiritual wisdom deepened as he learned to call down swirling radiant hosts of ***Spirit Guardians*** alongside his floating ***Spiritual Weapon***; Tvyordiy became ever more nimble and dexterous, his staff and spear weaving rapid combinations of concussive strikes; and Rhynok's marksmanship reached lethal, deadeye precision from hundreds of paces away.

Pressing into the Western foothills with **[[DonJon Raskin]]** to inspect **[[Mountain's Toe Gold Mine]]**, the Order was not fooled for a moment by Whiskered Gang boss **[[Zeleen Varnaster]]** and her claim to be the legitimate mine overseer. Yet when steel was drawn against her pack of **wererats**, a grim realization struck the frontline: neither Krageer's iron greataxe nor Tvyordiy's wooden *Qiang* could leave a lasting wound upon the lycanthropes' cursed hides! With the two melee bruisers unable to harm the pack, the battle was carried entirely by **[[Rhynok]]**—whose enchanted **+1 Longbow** punched through their supernatural immunity—and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**, whose swirling ***Spirit Guardians***, ***Spiritual Weapon***, and searing ***Sacred Flame*** burned the wererat gang into submission and restored the mine to DonJon.

### 11. Silvered Steel & The Purge of [[Axeholm]]
Returning to [[Phandalin]] after the humbling lesson of the gold mine, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** marched straight to the smithy and had his **Greataxe coated in alchemical silver**, swearing an oath never again to stand powerless before any foe. Beside him, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]]** had his ***Qiang* spear silvered**, and **[[Rhynok]]** silvered a **Shortsword** for close-quarters defense.

Tasked with opening the sealed dwarven mountain fortress of **[[Axeholm]]** as a potential refuge for Phandalin's townsfolk, the Order found the massive iron portcullis barred from within. Scaling the rocky mountainside to the natural rock **chimney** above the keep, all four companions rappelled smoothly down the slick stone flue under [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s prayerful ***Guidance***.

From the hearth floor, the Order waged a methodical campaign through the haunted halls: cutting through the **Giant Spiders** in the entry, purging packs of flesh-eating dwarven **Ghouls** and bloodsucking **Stirges** across the **West Hall** and **Smithy**, and marching into the **East Hall** and **Ghouls' Den** to exterminate the lower horde. Climbing the stone stairs to the upper **Haunted Hall**, they came face-to-face with the sorrowful moon elf banshee **[[Vyldara]]**. When she loosed her soul-shattering *Wail*, the four champions stood like granite—not a single member of the Order faltering before her cry—and swiftly laid her spirit to rest with divine radiance and silvered steel.

Pushing into the **Castellan's Bedchamber**, the party cleared out another nest of **Stirges**, and **[[Myagkiy Znak]]'s sharp eyes** noticed a false stone back inside the soot-darkened fireplace. Opening the hidden iron vault within, **Myagkiy** claimed the ***Gauntlets of Ogre Power***—infusing his arms with prodigious giant-kin might—while **Krageer** donned the menacing ***Dread Helm***, his eyes glowing like crimson embers beneath its shadowed steel visor. After putting down the undead **ghoul of the dwarf Castellan**, the Order left [[Axeholm]] completely cleansed.

### 12. Return to [[Dragon Barrow]], [[Circle of Thunder]] & Vengeance at the [[Woodland Manse]]
With [[Axeholm]] secured, the Order rode back to **[[Dragon Barrow]]** to finish what they had begun. Navigating the treacherous sarcophagus tunnels, they extinguished the remaining **Will-o'-Wisp** in the Adventurers' Sepulcher and overcame the **Invisible Stalker** guarding the bones of Azdraka, allowing **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** to lift Lady Tanamere Alagondar's legendary ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword** from its resting place. Outside the barrow, the companions forged an alliance with the noble centaur **[[Xanth]]**, who pledged his hooves and spear to help them root out the corruption festering in Neverwinter Wood.

Returning briefly to **[[Phandalin]]**, the party found **[[Gustaf Stellern|Falcon the Hunter]]**, [[Corwin]], and [[Pell]] sheltering in town. Falcon grimly recounted how [[Gorthok]] and a horde of twenty Talosian orcs had overrun and ransacked [[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]] right after the party's first departure—and true to his word, presented **[[Rhynok]]** with the coveted ***Boots of Elvenkind***.

Setting out into Neverwinter Wood with **[[Xanth]]** galloping at their side, the companions stopped over at the ruined compound of **[[Falcon's Hunting Lodge]]** along the river trail, surveying the tusk-shattered palisades and heavy **orcsign** left in the Thunder Boar's wake. Pressing south toward the storm-lashed hills, the Order and Xanth intercepted anchorite commander **[[Yargath]]** and her patrol of **ten Talosian Orcs** along the forest path—annihilating Yargath and all ten warriors in a furious woodland clash.

Charging straight up the hill to the standing stones of the **[[Circle of Thunder]]**, the Order plunged into an apocalyptic storm battle against **[[Gorthok]] the Thunder Boar**, half-orc anchorites **[[Flenz]]** and **[[Narux]]**, and swarms of **Twig Blights** and constricting **Vine Blights**. Amidst booming thunderclaps, crackling lightning tusks, and searing *Lightning Bolts*, the Order stood unbowed: Xanth trampled cultists underfoot, Krageer cleaved through boar and anchorite alike, Rhynok loosed lethal arrows from the gloom, Tvyordiy locked foes down with rapid *Stunning Strikes*, and Myagkiy's radiant *Spirit Guardians* shredded the blight throngs. With Gorthok and his summoners slain, **[[Myagkiy Znak]]**—as the company's sole shield-bearer—claimed the enchanted ***+1 Shield*** from the caves below.

From the smoking stones of the Circle, the champions and Xanth marched straight back to the **[[Woodland Manse]]** for their rematch. What had once been a desperate retreat was now a rout: sweeping through the upper halls and courtyard, they exterminated the **Stirges**, cut down **[[Grannoc]]** and the remaining **[[Anchorites of Talos]]**, and purged the Gulthias blights from the forest.

### 13. Ascent to [[Icespire Hold]] & The Slaying of [[Cryovain]]
With the cult of Talos broken in the woods and Lady Alagondar's *Dragon Slayer* blade in hand, [[The Order of Znak]] began the perilous ascent up the frozen spurs of Icespire Peak toward **[[Icespire Hold]]**. Along the snow-choked mountain trails, a warband of **Hobgoblins** moved to bar their path, only to be cut down by the companions' silvered arsenal—[[Rhynok]] drawing his **Silvered Shortsword** alongside [[Tvyordiy Znak]]'s **Silvered *Qiang*** and [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s **Silvered Greataxe**.

Breaching the outer gatehouse and stone halls of [[Icespire Hold]], the party crossed paths with the treacherous mercenary company known as the **[[Stone-Cold Reavers]]** ([[Brakkis Elspaar]], [[Dobin Noreth]], [[Jabarl]], [[Runa Vokdottir]], and [[Syleen Wintermoon]]), dispatching the opportunists in their usual no-nonsense fashion before clearing a flock of **Stirges** from the lower keep.

Scaling the frost-sheathed stairs to the open fortress roof, the four champions stepped out into the howling gale to confront **[[Cryovain]]** and a pack of cackling **Ice Mephits** lairing among the battlements:
* **[[Myagkiy Znak]]** invoked a prayer of ***Bless*** over his comrades and conjured his floating ***Spiritual Weapon*** to swat the **Ice Mephits** out of the freezing air.
* As the white wyrm unleashed blasts of glacial *Cold Breath* across the icy stones, the companions ducked and weathered the frost, closing the distance across the slick roof.
* Darting in with blinding speed, **[[Tvyordiy Znak]] delivered a series of clutch *Stunning Strikes* that locked [[Cryovain]]'s nervous system in place**, leaving the massive predator reeling and helpless on the ice!
* Seizing the opening, **[[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]** roared into a Bear Totem rage and unleashed a devastating flurry of two-handed blows with his ***Dragon Slayer* Longsword**, its ancient runes blazing as it carved deep into the dragon's scales, while **[[Rhynok]]** drove deadeye arrows from his **+1 Longbow** straight into the beast's vitals.

With a final, shuddering roar, **[[Cryovain]]** collapsed lifelessly atop the stones of [[Icespire Hold]]. Returning down the mountain to [[Phandalin]] as the celebrated saviors of the frontier, the Order of Znak found their legend carried by bards and couriers down the High Road—reaching the ears of **[[Dagult Neverember|Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]]** in Neverwinter and the **Lords of Waterdeep**, who would soon call upon the four champions to defend the rebuilding town of **[[Leilon]]**.
