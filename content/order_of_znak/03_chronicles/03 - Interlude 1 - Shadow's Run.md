## 📜 Overview of the First Interlude
In the tense lull between repelling the initial storm cult raids around [[Leilon]] and the full outbreak of two-front war along the Sword Coast, **[[The Order of Znak]]** ventured beneath the surface world into a lightless subterranean abyss where the boundary between the Material Plane and the Shadowfell had worn dangerously thin.

---

## ⚔️ Descent into the Shadow Caverns

### 1. Stalking the Lightless Depths
Descending into a sprawling network of pitch-black caverns choked with creeping gloom, the Order relied heavily on **[[Rhynok]]**'s Gloom Stalker mastery of total darkness. Moving unseen through the lightless tunnels while his own silhouette remained invisible to darkvision-reliant predators, the wood elf scouted ahead of [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], [[Myagkiy Znak]], and [[Tvyordiy Znak]] to pick off shadow-warped sentries from afar.

### 2. Clash with the Shadow Dragon
At the heart of the subterranean labyrinth, the champions confronted the master of the caverns: a lethal **Shadow Dragon** coiled amidst the darkness. Weathering blasts of necrotic shadow breath that threatened to drain the very life force from its victims, [[Myagkiy Znak]] anchored the company with radiant light and restorative prayers while [[Tvyordiy Znak]] darted through the gloom to harry the wyrm, [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]] traded ferocious blows at point-blank range, and [[Rhynok]] drove enchanted arrows through the dragon's shadowy scales—vanquishing the aberration and emerging triumphant back into the light of the Sword Coast.
