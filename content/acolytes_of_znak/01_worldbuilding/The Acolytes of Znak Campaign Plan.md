# 🏰 The Acolytes of Znak — Campaign Plan (Curse of Strahd)

## Overview
***The Acolytes of Znak*** is a planned D&D campaign set in Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*) using the **D&D 2024 / 5.5e ruleset**, DMed by Peter for Gary. The party consists of the grown-up orphaned wards (**Burnice** and **Smithwell**) and lizardfolk exchange allies (**Graxxoamo** and **Shriss**) of [[The Order of Znak]], along with **Yasmina**. Each PC is built as a Level 3 multiclass inheriting abilities and traits from their mentors/parents.
