# 🏰 The Acolytes of Znak — Campaign Plan (Curse of Strahd)

## 📜 The Second Generation: Legacy in Ravenloft
***The Acolytes of Znak*** chronicles the second generation of adventurers carrying the torch of [[The Order of Znak]]. Set within the mist-shrouded domain of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*), the party consists of the grown-up orphaned wards (**Burnice** and **Smithwell**) and lizardfolk exchange allies (**Graxxoamo** and **Shriss**) trained and mentored by the original champions, alongside rescued ward **Yasmina**.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Campaign & Engine Context:** DMed by Peter for Gary transitioning to the **D&D 2024 / 5.5e ruleset** in Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*).
* **Heritage Multiclass Design:** Each PC is designed as a Level 3 multiclass intentionally inheriting fighting styles, spell traditions, and personality traits from their adoptive parents or mentors in *The Order of Znak*.
