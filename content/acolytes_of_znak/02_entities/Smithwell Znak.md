# 🪓 Smithwell Znak

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Adoption:** Originally a 10-year-old child of local Leilon fishers during *Storm Lord's Wrath*. During the catastrophic raid on the town by the tiefling Kraken Priest **Nixoxious** (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), Smithwell and his best friend [[Burnice Znak]] went missing amidst the chaos. While [[The Order of Znak]] broke the ritual and rescued the children, their parents were tragically sacrificed to summon water elementals. Left orphaned and homeless, the two boys were formally adopted by the Order, taking the surname **Znak** and being raised within the Order's Leilon headquarters.
* **Heritage & Spirit:** Captivated by the boisterous laughter, campfire sagas, and devastating frontline ferocity of Goliath champion [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], Smithwell channeled both warrior rage and skaldic verse into a unique battle tradition.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Bard / Barbarian
* **Combat Style:** Frontline skald who weaves inspirational battlefield chants, cutting words, and unbridled primal barbarian rage.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player:** Played by Gary in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
* **Official D&D 5e Module Origin:** In Wizards of the Coast's *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Smithwell and Burnice are 10-year-old NPC children featured in the introductory quest *"A Normal Day in Leilon"*. The module tasks the party with rescuing them from the marshes after their fisher parents are taken by the kraken cult.
* **Table Emergence & Second-Generation Evolution:** Rather than leaving the children as nameless background NPCs, Gary and Peter chose to have The Order of Znak adopt them as wards. Years later, when launching the D&D 2024 / 5.5e *Curse of Strahd* campaign (*The Acolytes of Znak*), Gary selected Smithwell as his primary PC, playing him as a chaotic skald who grew up idolizing Krageer.
