# 🪓 Smithwell Znak

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Adoption:** Orphaned alongside his adoptive brother [[Burnice Znak]] when Kraken Priest cultists struck Leilon. Taken into the protective custody of [[The Order of Znak]] and raised within their headquarters.
* **Heritage & Spirit:** Captivated by the boisterous laughter, campfire sagas, and devastating ferocity of Goliath champion [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], Smithwell channeled both warrior rage and skaldic verse into a unique battle tradition.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Bard / Barbarian
* **Combat Style:** Frontline skald who weaves inspirational battlefield chants, cutting words, and unbridled primal barbarian rage.

---

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player:** Played by Gary in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
* **Character Concept:** Gary conceived Smithwell as a comedic yet potent skaldic barbarian directly inheriting [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]]'s love for tavern storytelling and frontline chaos.
