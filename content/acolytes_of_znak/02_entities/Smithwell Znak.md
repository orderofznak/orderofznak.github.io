# 🪓 Smithwell Znak

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Adoption:** Originally a 10-year-old child of local Leilon fishers during *Storm Lord's Wrath*. During the catastrophic raid on the town by the tiefling Kraken Priest **Nixoxious** (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), Smithwell and his best friend (and future adoptive sister) [[Burnice Znak]] went missing amidst the chaos. While [[The Order of Znak]] broke the ritual and rescued the children, their parents were tragically sacrificed to summon water elementals. Left orphaned and homeless, the two children were formally adopted by the Order, taking the surname **Znak** and being raised within the Order's Leilon headquarters as adoptive brother and sister (documented in [[Smithwell and Burnice|Young Smithwell & Burnice]]).
* **Heritage & Spirit:** Captivated by the boisterous laughter, campfire sagas, and devastating frontline ferocity of Goliath champion [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], Smithwell channeled both warrior rage and skaldic verse into a unique battle tradition.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Legacy Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Bard 2 / Barbarian 1 (or Barbarian 2 / Bard 1)
* **Combat Style:** Frontline skald who weaves inspirational battlefield chants, cutting words, and unbridled primal barbarian rage.

## 🛠️ 2024 Character Concept & Build Options
*Evaluated in full in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Barbarian — Path of the World Tree + Musician Feat — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass:* Manifests primal cosmic vitality; *Vitality of the Tree* grants Temp HP on entering rage and 1d6 Temp HP to an ally within 10 ft every turn (battlefield battle-singing). Level 6 spectral roots pull foes 30 ft into melee.
  * *Origin Feat:* **Musician** (plays war drums/horn to grant Heroic Inspiration to allies after every rest).
  * *Advantage:* Solves the 2014 Rage/Spellcasting lockout completely; *Primal Knowledge* uses Str for Perception, Survival, and Stealth during rage; immediate subclass at Level 3; Extra Attack at Level 5.
* **Path B (Pure 2024 Bard — College of Valor):**
  * *Subclass:* Medium armor, shields, martial weapons, Combat Inspiration damage/AC reactions.
  * *2024 Valor Power:* At Level 6, Extra Attack allows replacing one attack with a Cantrip (cast *Blade Ward* or *True Strike* while cleaving with a battleaxe!). Full spell progression with no Rage penalties.
* **Path C (Barbarian 1 / College of Valor Bard X):**
  * *Split:* Barbarian 1 / Bard 2 starting at Level 3.
  * *Concept:* Uses Rage strictly as a panic tank button for major boss fights; casts full spells and weaves Bardic Inspiration outside of rage.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player:** Played by Gary in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
* **Official D&D 5e Module Origin:** In Wizards of the Coast's *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Smithwell and Burnice are 10-year-old NPC children featured in the introductory quest *"A Normal Day in Leilon"*. The module tasks the party with rescuing them from the marshes after their fisher parents are taken by the kraken cult.
* **Table Emergence & Second-Generation Evolution:** Rather than leaving the children as nameless background NPCs, Gary and Peter chose to have The Order of Znak adopt them as wards. Years later, when launching the D&D 2024 / 5.5e *Curse of Strahd* campaign (*The Acolytes of Znak*), Gary selected Smithwell as his primary PC, playing him as a chaotic skald who grew up idolizing Krageer.
