## 📜 Backstory & Provenance: The Skald of Leilon & The Weight of Guilt

Smithwell was originally a ten-year-old boy living a humble life with his fisher parents in the coastal town of Leilon. When the calamitous kraken cult assault erupted across the town during *Storm Lord's Wrath* (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), Smithwell and his childhood companion [[Burnice Znak|Burnice]] were caught in the chaos and dragged into the marshes by tiefling Kraken Priest **Nixoxious** to be sacrificed for water elemental rituals. 

Although the champions of [[The Order of Znak]] shattered the cult line in a dramatic rescue, their parents were murdered before the heroes could reach them. While Burnice sought solace in prayer and devotion, Smithwell internalized the tragedy as personal failure—harboring a dark, gnawing guilt that a childish mistake or momentary hesitation had cost his parents their lives.

Adopted into the Order alongside Burnice and given the surname **Znak** (documented in [[Smithwell and Burnice|Young Smithwell & Burnice]]), Smithwell found an outlet for his turbulent emotions in Goliath champion **Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu**. Krageer's boisterous, room-shaking laughter, booming campfire battle sagas, and devastating, reckless ferocity on the frontline gave the orphaned boy a blueprint for survival. Smithwell apprenticed under Krageer, learning to channel grief and fury into reckless martial strikes while picking up the pan flute and war horn to rally companions.

As he reached adulthood, Smithwell worked as an **Urban Bounty Hunter** across the trade roads of the Sword Coast between Leilon, Neverwinter, and Waterdeep. Known for an impulsive streak and a refusal to follow strict tactical plans, Smithwell built an underground reputation as a Robin Hood figure—capturing high-value criminal bounties from greedy merchants and quietly redistributing the coin to impoverished refugees and struggling fisher families (his **Charity** ideal).

Now answering the call alongside Burnice and the second-generation acolytes, Smithwell steps into the fog-shrouded nightmare of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*). Armed with a massive greataxe, battle chants, and a reckless thirst for frontline combat, he fights with reckless abandon to ensure that no one under his watch ever dies because of a mistake again.

  * Before making a melee attack with a heavy weapon (e.g. Greataxe, Greatsword), can choose to take a **-5 penalty** to the attack roll; if it hits, add **+10 to the damage roll**!
* **Barbarian Features (Level 2):**

  * **Bardic Inspiration (d6):** 2 uses per long rest (Charisma modifier). As a bonus action, grant an inspiration die to an ally within 60 ft to add to an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw within 10 minutes.

## 🛠️ 2024 / 5.5e Redesign & Workshop Options
*Detailed evaluation in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*

With Smithwell's starting ability spread sitting at **STR 15 / DEX 12 / CON 14 / CHA 14**, the 2024 revised rules provide elegant solutions to the legacy Barbarian/Bard spell-lockout:

### 1. The 15 Str & Level 4 Feat Synergy
* With **STR 15**, taking a Strength half-feat at Level 4 (such as *Slasher*, *Crusher*, or the revised 2024 *Great Weapon Master* which now grants +1 Str natively) elevates Strength to **16 (+3)**.
* This increases his attack bonus to **+6** (+1 with GWM active) and melee damage to **1d12 + 3 (Str) + 2 (Rage) + 10 (GWM)**!

### 2. Path of the World Tree + Musician: The Ultimate Skald Solution
* In 2014, the Barbarian/Bard multiclass suffered because Rage prevented casting *Healing Word*, *Cure Wounds*, or *Sleep*.
* In 2024 rules, **Path of the World Tree Barbarian** + the **Musician Origin Feat** achieves the complete warrior-skald fantasy with **zero multiclass spell penalties**:
  * *Vitality of the Tree:* Entering rage grants Temporary HP, and at the start of each turn while raging grants 1d6 Temp HP to an ally within 10 ft (representing battle chants cheering allies on!).
  * *Musician Feat:* After each rest, plays horn or pan flute to grant **Heroic Inspiration** to PB allies.
  * *Primal Knowledge (2024 Barbarian core):* Uses **Strength** for Perception, Survival, and Stealth during Rage!

### 3. Build Pathways:
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Barbarian — Path of the World Tree + Musician Feat — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass at Level 3:* Unlocks **Path of the World Tree**.
  * *Milestones:* Level 4 (Str 16 Half-Feat) &rarr; Level 5 (Extra Attack on time, Fast Movement) &rarr; Level 6 (*Branches of the Tree* spectral roots pull foes 30 ft into melee as a reaction).
  * *Advantage:* Max HP tank (d12 hit die), constant party temporary HP, zero spellcasting lockouts.
* **Path B (Pure 2024 Bard — College of Valor):**
  * *Subclass at Level 3:* Medium armor, shields, martial weapons, Combat Inspiration damage/AC reactions.
  * *Level 6 Extra Attack:* Can replace one attack with a Cantrip (cast *Blade Ward* or 2024 *True Strike* while striking with a greataxe!). Full spellcaster without Rage penalties.
* **Path C (Barbarian 2 / Bard 1 &rarr; Bard X — Existing Hybrid):**
  * *Reckless GWM Core:* Relies on Reckless Attack + GWM for heavy burst damage. Uses Rage strictly as a panic tank button for major boss fights; casts utility and healing spells outside of rage.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player:** Played by Gary in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
