# 🌿 Shriss

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Alliance:** Lizardfolk mystic sent alongside [[Graxxoamo]] to [[The Order of Znak]] as part of the cultural exchange pact honoring [[Matriarch Qolzaz]].
* **Heritage & Calling:** Deeply attuned to primal earth spirits and swamp renewal, Shriss felt a profound reverence for [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s radiant life preservation, choosing to synthesize natural druidcraft with divine healing rites.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Race:** Lizardfolk
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Life Domain Cleric / Druid
* **Combat Style:** Primal battlefield battlefield control, area-of-effect natural hazards, and amplified Life Domain healing.
