## 📜 Backstory & Provenance: The Swamp Lorekeeper & Divine Synthesis

Unlike the fiercely athletic hunters of her clan, Shriss was drawn from youth to the esoteric mysteries of the **Mere of Dead Men**. In a harsh wetland where decay and rebirth are bound together in endless biological cycles, Shriss served as an intellectual mystic and tribal sage. She spent years observing the mosses, carnivorous flora, venomous reptiles, and necrotic miasmas of the swamp, recording her observations in handcrafted bone-bound tomes in an unending quest to unravel the fundamental laws governing life, decomposition, and souls.

When her wandering clan clashed with [[The Order of Znak]]—a tragedy that saw the death of [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Matron Quolzaz]]—Shriss was present on the field. Yet when the dreaded scourge of **rot trolls** ambushed both sides, Shriss witnessed a spectacle that challenged her entire worldview: the radiant miracles of dwarf cleric [[Myagkiy Znak]]. Where the rot trolls spread rotting necrosis and gangrene, Myagkiy's divine healing restored mangled flesh instantly without the slow, brutal survivalism of reptilian biology.

Fascinated by this miraculous synthesis of life preservation and primal renewal, Shriss joined [[Graxxoamo]] on their diplomatic journey north to **Leilon**. There, while Graxx trained in martial arts under Tvyordiy and Rhynok, Shriss immersed herself in the theological archives and spiritual mentorship of Myagkiy Znak.

Taking holy vows in the **Life Domain**, Shriss fused her deep attunement to primal swamp druidcraft with Myagkiy's radiant compassion. Clad in heavy chain mail and brandishing a bone-tipped spear, she became the intellectual and spiritual anchor of ***The Acolytes of Znak***. As the mists of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*) descend, Shriss views the domain of dread not merely as a horror, but as the ultimate biological and spiritual mystery waiting to be studied, survived, and cleansed.

## 🛠️ 2024 / 5.5e Redesign & Workshop Options
*Detailed evaluation in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*

With Shriss's starting ability spread sitting at **STR 15 / CON 15 / WIS 16**, the 2024 revised rules provide immense durability and healing throughput:

### 1. The 15 Str & 15 Con Frontline Engine
* **STR 15:** Allows Shriss to wear heavy armor (**Chain Mail + Shield = AC 18**) without suffering the 10 ft speed penalty, making her as durable as a Paladin on the frontline.
* **CON 15:** Taking **Resilient (Constitution)** at Level 4 immediately pushes Constitution to **16 (+3)** (+1 HP/level) and grants **proficiency in Constitution saving throws**—ensuring she never drops concentration on *Faerie Fire*, *Bless*, or *Spike Growth* when taking hits in melee.

### 2. 2024 Disciple of Life vs Circle of the Stars
* In 2024 rules, *Disciple of Life* specifically triggers when casting a spell of 1st level or higher that restores HP *on the turn it is cast*. 
* Meanwhile, **Circle of the Stars Druid** features the **Chalice Starry Form**: every time Shriss casts a healing spell, she or an ally within 30 ft heals an **additional 1d8 + Wisdom HP**! This matches or exceeds Disciple of Life while maintaining 100% pure Druid spell progression.

### 3. Build Pathways:
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Druid — Circle of the Stars — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass at Level 3:* Circle of the Stars. Free *Guiding Bolt* casts PB/day without spell slots. *Chalice Form* adds massive radiant healing bursts; *Archer Form* adds bonus-action radiant laser attacks.
  * *Milestones:* Level 4 (*Resilient: Con* &rarr; Con 16) &rarr; Level 5 (3rd-level spells on time: *Revivify*, *Call Lightning*, *Plant Growth*).
* **Path B (Druid 2 / Life Cleric 1 — Existing 2/1 Hybrid):**
  * *Frontline Tank:* AC 18 heavy armor, full spell slot progression (already has Level 2 slots!), Disciple of Life healing boost on all healing spells.
  * *Progression:* Druid 2 / Cleric 1 &rarr; Druid 3 (Swamp spells: *Darkness*, *Acid Arrow* at Lvl 4) &rarr; Druid 4 (Con 16 Feat at Lvl 5) &rarr; Druid 5 (3rd-level spells at Lvl 6).
* **Path C (Pure 2024 Life Domain Cleric with Druid Magic Initiate):**
  * *Protector Divine Order:* Heavy armor, *Preserve Life* Channel Divinity, *Spirit Guardians* on time at Level 5.
  * *Origin Feat:* *Magic Initiate (Druid)* provides *Shillelagh*, *Thorn Whip*, and *Goodberry* / *Entangle*.
