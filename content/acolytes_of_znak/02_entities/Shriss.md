# 🌿 Shriss

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Alliance:** Lizardfolk mystic sent alongside [[Graxxoamo]] to [[The Order of Znak]] as part of the cultural exchange pact honoring [[Matriarch Qolzaz]].
* **Heritage & Calling:** Deeply attuned to primal earth spirits and swamp renewal, Shriss felt a profound reverence for [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s radiant life preservation, choosing to synthesize natural druidcraft with divine healing rites.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Legacy Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Race:** Lizardfolk
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Life Domain Cleric 1 / Druid 2
* **Combat Style:** Primal battlefield battlefield control, area-of-effect natural hazards, and amplified Life Domain healing.

## 🛠️ 2024 Character Concept & Build Options
*Evaluated in full in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Druid — Circle of the Stars — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass:* Circle of the Stars. Free *Guiding Bolt* casts PB/day without spell slots. *Starry Form (Chalice)* adds **1d8 + Wis radiant healing** to every healing spell cast, equaling/surpassing Life Domain healing while keeping 100% Druid spell progression. *Archer* form adds a bonus-action radiant laser attack.
  * *Advantage:* Immediate subclass at Level 3, 3rd-level spells on time at Level 5 (*Revivify*, *Call Lightning*), Wild Resurgence spell-slot recovery.
* **Path B (Pure 2024 Life Domain Cleric with Druid Magic Initiate):**
  * *Subclass:* Heavy Armor (*Protector* Divine Order), *Preserve Life* Channel Divinity, enhanced healing spells.
  * *Origin Feat:* *Magic Initiate (Druid)* provides *Shillelagh*, *Guidance*, and *Goodberry* / *Entangle*.
* **Path C (Life Cleric 1 / Swamp Druid X):**
  * *Split:* Life Cleric 1 / Druid 2 starting at Level 3.
  * *Concept:* Heavy armor proficiency, Disciple of Life bonus on druid healing spells, and full spell slot progression.
