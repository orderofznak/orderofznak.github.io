# 🔮 Yasmina

## Profile & Character Sheet (Level 3)
* **Player / Role:** NPC / Party Member
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Cleric 1 / Fiend Warlock 2 (Legacy)
* **Origin:** Rescued as a young girl from a Night Hag by [[The Order of Znak]] during Gary's *"Keeping the Faith"* DM adventure.
* **Build & Lineage:** Carries a dark, witch/hag-influenced homebrew lineage, manifesting in Fiend Warlock pact magic and Cleric domain features.

## 🛠️ 2024 Character Concept & Build Options
*Evaluated in full in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Archfey Patron Warlock — The Coven Witch — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass:* Archfey Patron (the definitive 2024 hag/witch subclass). *Steps of the Fey* grants free *Misty Step* casts PB/day, triggering riders like *Taunting Step* (disadvantage on attacks), *Misty Slam* (2d6 psychic damage + push), or *Refreshing Step* (teleports an ally).
  * *Pact Boon:* *Pact of the Tome* (Book of Shadows with 3 cantrips + ritual casting) or *Pact of the Chain* (Quasit/Imp/Sprite hag familiar).
* **Path B (Pure 2024 Fiend Patron Warlock):**
  * *Subclass:* Fiend Patron (*Dark One's Blessing* temp HP on kills; *Dark One's Own Luck* adds 1d10 to ability checks/saves).
* **Path C (Cleric 1 / Archfey Warlock X):**
  * *Split:* Cleric 1 / Warlock 2 starting at Level 3.
  * *Concept:* Medium armor, shield, *Healing Word*, and hag pact magic progression.
