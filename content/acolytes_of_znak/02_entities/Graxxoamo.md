## 📜 Backstory & Provenance: From the Mere of Dead Men to Leilon

Graxxoamo was hatched into a wandering tribe of lizardfolk in the desolate, mist-choked wetlands of the **Mere of Dead Men**. At home in the swamp, Graxx learned from an early age to track, stalk, and hunt across treacherous mire and sucking muck that most soft-fleshed warm-bloods would drown in. Boasting natural athleticism and competitive drive, he regularly competed in tribal games, securing championship laurels in javelin throws, precision archery, distance mud-running, and the humorous (yet culturally revered) finger sandwich eating contests.

All seemed peaceful until the fateful day his band encountered a group of roving humans traversing the swamp. Misunderstanding and territorial defense led to violent skirmishes, drawing the swift and devastating wrath of [[The Order of Znak]]. In a stunning, surgical assault, the Order's warriors broke the lizardfolk line in an effort to rescue their associates, an encounter that tragically saw the death of several tribe members including [[Matriarch Qolzaz|Matron Quolzaz]].

Yet fate had a darker irony in store. **Rot trolls**—the ravenous, plague-ridden scourge of the Mere—had been stalking both parties through the fog and ambushed the battlefield at that very moment. Faced with annihilation by the undead trolls, [[The Order of Znak]] and the surviving lizardfolk formed an immediate, desperate shield wall. Fighting shoulder to shoulder, the holy light of [[Myagkiy Znak]], the thunderous rage of [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]], the avian fists of [[Tvyordiy Znak]], and the lethal sniping of [[Rhynok]] turned the tide. By the battle's end, shared blood and mutual survival forged an enduring alliance.

Determined to honor their fallen matriarch and bring glory back to their wandering tribe, Graxxoamo and mystic [[Shriss]] traveled north to **Leilon** as exchange envoys to study under the Order. Graxx apprenticed exclusively under **Tvyordiy Znak** and **Rhynok**, absorbing the disciplined martial philosophy of the fist, the predatory precision of the bow, and the arts of concealment in shadows.

Growing up alongside the Order's adopted orphan wards ([[Burnice Znak]] and [[Smithwell Znak]]) and rescued ward [[Yasmina]], Graxx formed the core of ***The Acolytes of Znak***. Together, the second generation ran regional defense errands, cleared roads along the High Road, and developed an instinctive combat synergy. Now, as the mists of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*) begin to gather, Graxx brings his reptilian discipline, swamp-honed survival instincts, and pocketful of questionable snacks to the fight against Strahd von Zarovich.

## 🛠️ 2024 / 5.5e Redesign & Workshop Options
*Detailed evaluation in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*

With Graxx's starting ability spread sitting at **DEX 17 / CON 15 / WIS 16**, the 2024 revised rules provide immediate mechanical solutions:

### The Level 4 Feat Synergy & "Chef / Finger Sandwich" Tech
* **Dexterity Half-Feat (Level 4):** Taking a Dex half-feat (*Athlete*, *Speedy*, or *Weapon Master*) immediately elevates Dexterity to **18 (+4)**, raising AC to **17**, to-hit to **+7**, and damage to **+4**.
* **The "Finger Sandwich" Chef Feat (2024 General Feat):** At Level 4 or 8, Graxx can take the **Chef Feat** (+1 Con). This pushes Constitution to **16 (+3)**, adds +1 HP per level, and allows Graxx to prepare special treats during short/long rests that grant **Temporary HP** to his companions—mechanically turning his signature *finger sandwich* habit into a tangible party survival buff!

### Build Pathways:
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Monk — Warrior of Shadow — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass at Level 3:* Immediately unlocks **Warrior of Shadow**. Casts *Darkness* with 1 Focus and **sees through it natively**, repositioning the darkness sphere every turn.
  * *2024 Core Monk Upgrades:* 1d6 Martial Arts die; free bonus-action unarmed strike (requires no Attack action); *Deflect Attacks* absorbs damage from **both melee and ranged** strikes (1d10 + Dex + Monk Level) and can counter-attack.
  * *Progression:* Monk 3 (Shadow) &rarr; Monk 4 (Dex 18 Half-Feat) &rarr; Monk 5 (Extra Attack, Stunning Strike, 1d8 damage) &rarr; Monk 6 (*Shadow Step* 60 ft at-will teleport with Advantage).
* **Path B (Monk 2 / Ranger 1 — The Existing Hybrid):**
  * *Ranger 1 (2024 PHB):* Provides 2 free castings of *Hunter's Mark* per long rest without consuming spell slots, 2 Weapon Masteries (e.g. Vex + Nick), and 2 Level 1 spell slots.
  * *Progression:* Monk 2 / Ranger 1 &rarr; Monk 3 (Shadow at Lvl 4) &rarr; Monk 4 (Dex 18 at Lvl 5) &rarr; Monk 5 (Extra Attack at Lvl 6).
* **Path C (Monk 2 / Fighter 1 — The Blindsight Stalker):**
  * *Fighter 1:* Provides *Blind Fighting* fighting style (10 ft blindsight inside magical darkness), Weapon Masteries, and *Second Wind* (with *Tactical Mind* adding 1d10 to failed Stealth checks).
