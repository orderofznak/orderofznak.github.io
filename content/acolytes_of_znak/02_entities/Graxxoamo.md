# 🦎 Graxxoamo

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Alliance:** Sent as a premier young lizardfolk exchange warrior to [[The Order of Znak]] from the Mere of Dead Men clans following the historic alliance honoring [[Matriarch Qolzaz]].
* **Training & Heritage:** Mentored under the dual guidance of avian monk [[Tvyordiy Znak]] and stealth sniper [[Rhynok]], synthesizing ancient tribal wetlands survival with disciplined monastic combat.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Legacy Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Race:** Lizardfolk
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Ranger 1 / Monk 2 (or Ranger 2 / Monk 1)
* **Combat Style:** Unarmed strikes and natural armor fused with hunter marks, rapid ambush maneuvers, and wilderness tracking.

## 🛠️ 2024 Character Concept & Build Options
*Evaluated in full in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Monk — Warrior of Shadow — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass:* Casts *Darkness* for 1 Focus and **sees through it natively**, moving the darkness sphere each turn. At Level 6, *Shadow Step* grants an at-will 60 ft bonus-action teleport between shadows with advantage on the next attack.
  * *2024 Monk Buffs:* 1d6 Martial Arts die, free bonus-action unarmed strike (requires no Attack action), *Deflect Attacks* reducing both melee and ranged attack damage by 1d10 + Dex + Monk Level.
  * *Advantage:* Solves the MAD problem completely (pure Dex/Wis); immediate subclass at Level 3; Extra Attack at Level 5.
* **Path B (Monk X / Fighter 1 Dip — The Blindsight Stalker):**
  * *Split:* Fighter 1 / Monk 2 starting at Level 3.
  * *2024 Synergy:* Gains *Blind Fighting* fighting style (10 ft blindsight inside magical darkness), Weapon Mastery (Nick/Vex), and *Second Wind* with *Tactical Mind* (+1d10 to failed Stealth checks).
  * *Progression:* Fighter 1 / Monk 2 &rarr; Monk 3 (Shadow at Lvl 4) &rarr; Monk 4 (ASI at Lvl 5) &rarr; Monk 5 (Extra Attack at Lvl 6).
* **Path C (Pure Gloom Stalker Ranger):**
  * *Concept:* Pure tracking and ambush predator (*Umbral Sight* invisibility to darkvision, *Dread Ambusher* burst damage and fear riders, full spell progression).
