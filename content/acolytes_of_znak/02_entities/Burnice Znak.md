# ⚔️ Burnice Znak

## 🛡️ Overview & Character Lore
* **Origin & Adoption:** Born the daughter of Leilon fishers, Burnice and her childhood companion (and future adoptive brother) [[Smithwell Znak]] were caught in the crossfire of the kraken cult assault on Leilon (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"* in *Storm Lord's Wrath*). The heroes of [[The Order of Znak]] intervened, driving back the tiefling Kraken Priest **Nixoxious** and rescuing the terrified children from the marshes—only to discover their parents had already been sacrificed in the water elemental summoning rites. The champions adopted both orphans into the Order, giving them the name **Znak** and raising them at their Leilon headquarters as adoptive brother and sister (documented in [[Smithwell and Burnice|Young Smithwell & Burnice]]).
* **Heritage & Vows:** Raised under the spiritual guidance of [[Myagkiy Znak]], Burnice grew up witnessing the severe cost of cosmic and primordial evil. She forged holy martial vows complemented by a shadowy patron blade to ensure no innocent child would suffer a similar fate.

## 📊 In-Game Mechanics (Legacy Level 3 Starting Build)
* **Class:** Level 3 Multiclass — Paladin 2 / Hexblade Warlock 1
* **Combat Style:** Frontline holy smites amplified by Hexblade curse bladecraft and dark patron incantations.

## 🛠️ 2024 Character Concept & Build Options
*Evaluated in full in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*
* **Path A (Pure Paladin — Oath of the Watchers / Vengeance):**
  * *Subclass:* Sworn to hunt extraplanar horrors (*Watcher's Will* saving throw advantage, extraplanar turning, *Moonbeam*, *Counterspell*).
  * *Origin Feat:* *Magic Initiate (Warlock)* for *Eldritch Blast* and *Hex*.
  * *Advantage:* Immediate subclass at Level 3, Extra Attack on time at Level 5, *Aura of Protection* (+Cha to all saves) at Level 6.
* **Path B (Paladin X / Warlock 1 Dip):**
  * *Split:* Paladin 2 / Warlock 1 starting at Level 3.
  * *2024 Synergy:* Warlock 1 grants *Pact of the Blade* (Charisma weapon attacks + Force damage) and short-rest spell slot.
  * *Progression:* Paladin 2 / Warlock 1 &rarr; Paladin 3 (Subclass at Lvl 4) &rarr; Paladin 4 (ASI at Lvl 5) &rarr; Paladin 5 (Extra Attack at Lvl 6).
* **Path C (Pure Celestial Blade Warlock):**
  * *Concept:* Full Warlock pact magic utilizing [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s radiant healing pool (*Healing Light*) and *Pact of the Blade*, with *Thirsting Blade* Extra Attack at Level 5.

## 💡 Behind the Screen & Trivia
* **Player:** Played by Peter in *The Acolytes of Znak*.
* **Official D&D 5e Module Origin:** In Wizards of the Coast's *Storm Lord's Wrath*, Burnice is one of two 10-year-old NPC children (alongside Smithwell) introduced in *"A Normal Day in Leilon"*. When the town fishery is attacked by a kraken priest, the missing children become the objective of an urgent rescue mission.
* **Character Concept & Second-Generation Evolution:** While Smithwell gravitated toward Krageer's chaotic skaldic traditions, Burnice was conceived as the moral and spiritual successor to [[Myagkiy Znak]], combining righteous conviction with hexblade darkness.
