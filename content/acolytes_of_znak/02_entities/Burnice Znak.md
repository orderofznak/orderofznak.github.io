## 📜 Backstory & Provenance: The Orphan of Leilon & The Roadside Bargain

Burnice was originally a ten-year-old child of local Leilon fishers during the turbulent era of *Storm Lord's Wrath*. When the catastrophic kraken cult raid struck the town fishery (*"A Normal Day in Leilon"*), Burnice and her childhood friend [[Smithwell Znak]] were abducted into the marshes to serve as sacrificial catalysts for water elemental summoning rites. While the champions of [[The Order of Znak]] broke the ritual in a furious assault and rescued the children, their parents had already been slain. Homeless and orphaned, Burnice and Smithwell were formally adopted into the Order, taking the surname **Znak** and being raised within the Order's fortified Leilon headquarters as adoptive sister and brother (documented in [[Smithwell and Burnice|Young Smithwell & Burnice]]).

Growing up under the roof of heroes, Burnice absorbed the teachings of each champion:
* She studied the priestly devotion, sacrificial healing, and moral clarity of dwarf cleric [[Myagkiy Znak]], whom she idolizes above all others.
* She learned frontline discipline and martial tenacity from Goliath warrior [[Krageer Stoneface Ogolakanu]].
* She mastered the precision of the spear and reach combat from avian monk [[Tvyordiy Znak]].
* She inherited the unrelenting, vigilant spirit against tyranny from wood elf scout [[Rhynok]].

Training alongside her fellow acolytes from youth, Burnice developed specialized combat synergies with her companions. Most notably, she and lizardfolk warrior [[Graxxoamo]] trained together extensively in sensory-deprivation combat, allowing Burnice to master the **Blind Fighting** style so that she could fight seamlessly alongside Graxx within magical darkness.

Charismatic, articulate, and fiercely driven, Burnice was tasked as a young adult with managing the Order's growing liaison network across the Sword Coast (operating as a **Faction Agent**). Traveling the roads between Leilon, Phandalin, Neverwinter, and Waterdeep, she gathered intelligence and forged supply lines. However, while traveling a lonely stretch of the High Road, Burnice was ambushed by ruthless brigands. Laid low, beaten, and robbed, she lay bleeding in the dirt, cursing her physical limitations to protect the innocent.

In that desperate moment of vulnerability, she was approached by a curious, otherworldly shadowy figure. Sensing her burning vow to protect the weak and punish the wicked, the patron offered her an eldritch bargain: dark power to curse her foes and bind magic into her bladecraft. Burnice accepted, forging a **Hexblade** pact to complement her holy vows. She views this dark magic not as corruption, but as a dangerous instrument consecrated in service of Znak.

Now stepping into the mists of Barovia (*Curse of Strahd*) alongside her adoptive brother and companions, Burnice stands as the party's moral vanguard—wielding holy smites, eldritch curses, and an unyielding glaive.

## 🛠️ 2024 / 5.5e Redesign & Workshop Options
*Detailed evaluation in [[The Acolytes of Znak - 2024 Character Concepts & Build Workshop]].*

With Burnice's starting ability spread sitting at **STR 16 / CON 15 / CHA 16**, the 2024 revised rules unlock formidable frontline power:

### 1. The Darkness & Blind Fighting Duo with Graxxoamo
* In 2024 rules, [[Graxxoamo]]'s *Warrior of Shadow* Monk casts *Darkness* for 1 Focus and **sees through it natively**.
* With Burnice's **Blind Fighting** (10 ft blindsight), Burnice and Graxx stand back-to-back inside the darkness sphere:
  * Burnice and Graxx gain **permanent Advantage on all attack rolls** against enemies inside the sphere.
  * Enemies attack them with **Disadvantage**.
  * Enemies cannot make Opportunity Attacks against them when moving.
  * Outside spellcasters cannot target either of them with spells requiring line of sight!

### 2. The 15 Con & Level 4 Feat Synergy
* With **CON 15**, taking **Resilient: Constitution** at Level 4 pushes Constitution to **16 (+3)**, adds +1 HP/level, and grants **proficiency in Constitution saving throws**—ensuring her concentration on *Bless*, *Protection from Evil*, or *Hex* rarely breaks under heavy damage.
* Alternatively, taking *Heavy Armor Master* pushes Strength to 17 or Con to 16 while reducing non-magical physical damage by 3.

### 3. Weapon Masteries (Paladin 2024 PHB):
* Paladins gain 2 Weapon Masteries natively at Level 1:
  * **Glaive (Graze Mastery):** If Burnice misses an attack roll, she still deals her Strength or Charisma ability modifier (+3) in damage!
  * **Spear (Sap Mastery):** When hitting with her spear, the target has **Disadvantage** on its next attack roll before the start of Burnice's next turn.

### 4. Build Pathways:
* **Path A (Pure 2024 Paladin — Oath of the Watchers — Recommended):**
  * *Subclass at Level 3:* Unlocks **Oath of the Watchers**. Sworn against extraplanar threats; Channel Divinity: *Watcher's Will* grants advantage on Int/Wis/Cha saving throws for herself and allies within 30 ft; *Abjure the Extraplanar* turns aberrations, fiends, elementals, and fey.
  * *Milestones:* Level 4 (Con 16 Feat) &rarr; Level 5 (Extra Attack on time, 2nd-level spells: *Moonbeam*, *Aid*) &rarr; Level 6 (*Aura of Protection* adds +3 to ALL party saving throws!).
* **Path B (Paladin X / Warlock 1 — Existing 2/1 Hybrid):**
  * *2024 Warlock 1:* Unlocks *Pact of the Blade* (Charisma weapon attacks dealing Radiant/Necrotic/Force damage) and 1 short-rest slot for *Armor of Agathys*.
  * *Progression:* Paladin 2 / Warlock 1 &rarr; Paladin 3 (Watchers or Vengeance at Lvl 4) &rarr; Paladin 4 (Feat at Lvl 5) &rarr; Paladin 5 (Extra Attack at Lvl 6).
* **Path C (Pure 2024 Celestial Blade Warlock):**
  * *Concept:* Full Warlock pact magic utilizing [[Myagkiy Znak]]'s radiant healing pool (*Healing Light*) and *Pact of the Blade*, gaining Extra Attack at Level 5 via *Thirsting Blade*.
